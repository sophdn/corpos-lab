## Research question

What does extended thinking text reveal about agent planning and design behavior, and can systematic multi-pass analysis of thinking traces reliably surface operational improvements and structural taboo candidates?

---

## Why this is a study, not an account

The ouija-researcher-analysis and technowizard-researcher-analysis journals apply two complementary analytical lenses to the same Researcher thinking trace — Protocol Tester's piece-by-piece extraction and Technowizard's structural synthesis. Together they constitute a methodology, not just a record of work done. The methodology generalizes: any future analysis of agent thinking traces during experimental design would use the same two-pass structure and extract the same categories of finding. No document currently records the methodology as methodology.

## What the full study would establish

- The two-pass methodology: piece-by-piece (extracts operational improvements and taboo candidates per segment) followed by whole-thing (extracts structural patterns and system vs. agent overhead)
- What each pass reliably surfaces and what each misses — the complementary coverage map
- Taxonomy of finding types: operational improvement / taboo opportunity / researcher behavior pattern / system design leverage
- The system-overhead vs. researcher-overhead distinction — which inefficiencies are fixed by form changes and which are fixed by researcher training
- Whether thinking-trace analysis and constructed-environment observation (ouija trap) surface overlapping or non-overlapping behavioral findings
- Whether planning structure is robust to form removal — reappearing in structural analogs (e.g. Declaration Block file ordering) when the explicit planning block is absent
