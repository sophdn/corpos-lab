To create the postmortem for the `payments` outage, I would follow these steps:

1. **Identify the Template**: 
   - Determine the appropriate postmortem template as specified by the organization's governance protocol. This template will likely include sections such as Incident Overview, Timeline, Root Cause Analysis, Impact, Resolution, and Action Items.

2. **Name the Document**:
   - Create a sensible name for the document. A good naming convention might include the date, the service affected, and the nature of the incident. For example, `2023-10-15-Payments-Outage-Postmortem`.

3. **Select the Directory**:
   - Identify the directory where similar postmortem documents are stored. This is typically a centralized location within your organization's documentation system, such as a shared drive or a documentation management tool.

4. **Assign Reviewers**:
   - Determine who must be assigned as reviewers. This includes individuals who were on call during the incident and any other stakeholders who need to be informed or involved in the review process. Ensure that all relevant personnel are included to provide feedback and insights.

5. **Compile Information**:
   - Gather all relevant information about the outage, including:
     - **Incident Overview**: A brief description of what happened.
     - **Timeline**: A detailed timeline of events from the start of the incident to its resolution.
     - **Root Cause Analysis**: An analysis of what caused the outage.
     - **Impact**: The impact of the outage on users, business operations, and any other affected areas.
     - **Resolution**: Steps taken to resolve the outage.
     - **Action Items**: Measures to prevent future occurrences, including any improvements or changes to processes, systems, or infrastructure.

6. **Draft the Document**:
   - Using the identified template, draft the postmortem document. Ensure that each section is filled with accurate and detailed information.

7. **Review and Revise**:
   - Review the draft to ensure clarity, accuracy, and completeness. Make any necessary revisions based on feedback from initial reviewers or additional insights.

8. **Distribute for Review**:
   - Share the draft postmortem with the assigned reviewers. Ensure they have sufficient time to review and provide feedback.

9. **Incorporate Feedback**:
   - Incorporate feedback from the reviewers into the document. Address any concerns or suggestions they may have.

10. **Finalize the Document**:
    - Finalize the document after incorporating all feedback. Ensure it meets the organization's standards and governance requirements.

11. **Publish the Document**:
    - Save the finalized postmortem in the designated directory. Ensure it is accessible to all relevant stakeholders and is indexed appropriately for future reference.

12. **Communicate Findings**:
    - Communicate the findings and action items from the postmortem to the broader team or organization as necessary. This might include a follow-up meeting or an email summary.

By following these steps, I would ensure that the postmortem is thorough, accurate, and useful for preventing similar incidents in the future.
