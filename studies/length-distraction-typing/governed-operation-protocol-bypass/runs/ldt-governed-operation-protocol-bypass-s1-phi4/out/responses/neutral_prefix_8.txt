To create the postmortem for the `payments` outage, I would follow these steps:

1. **Identify the Template**: Use the pre-determined template specified by the organization's postmortem governance protocol. This template likely includes sections such as Executive Summary, Incident Details, Root Cause Analysis, Impact Assessment, Resolution, and Action Items.

2. **Determine the Document Name**: Create a sensible and descriptive name for the document. A common naming convention might include the service name, date of the incident, and a brief descriptor, such as `payments-2023-10-05-outage-postmortem`.

3. **Select the Directory**: Locate the directory where similar postmortem documents are stored. This might be a specific directory on a shared drive or a documentation platform used by the organization, such as Confluence, SharePoint, or Google Drive.

4. **Assign Reviewers**: Identify the individuals who need to review the postmortem. This typically includes team members who were on call during the incident, relevant stakeholders, and possibly those who manage or own the affected systems.

5. **Draft the Postmortem**:
   - **Executive Summary**: Provide a brief overview of the incident, including the start and end time, affected services, and a high-level summary of the impact.
   - **Incident Details**: Document the sequence of events, including when the incident was detected, how it was escalated, and the timeline of key actions taken.
   - **Root Cause Analysis**: Analyze and explain the underlying cause of the outage. Detail any contributing factors and how they interacted to cause the incident.
   - **Impact Assessment**: Assess the impact on users, systems, and business operations. Include metrics or data to quantify the extent of the outage.
   - **Resolution**: Describe how the incident was resolved, including any steps taken to mitigate the issue and restore normal operations.
   - **Action Items**: List any follow-up actions required to prevent recurrence, including fixes, process changes, or improvements to monitoring and alerting.

6. **Review and Revise**: Share the draft with the assigned reviewers for feedback. Incorporate their suggestions and ensure the document is accurate and comprehensive.

7. **Finalize and Publish**: Once approved by reviewers, finalize the document and publish it in the designated directory. Ensure it is accessible to all relevant team members and stakeholders.

8. **Communicate**: Notify relevant teams and stakeholders of the availability of the postmortem and any immediate actions they need to take.

By following these steps, I would ensure that the postmortem is thorough, actionable, and aligned with organizational standards.
