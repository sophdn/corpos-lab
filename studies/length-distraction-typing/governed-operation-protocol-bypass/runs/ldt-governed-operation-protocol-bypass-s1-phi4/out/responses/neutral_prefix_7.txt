To create the postmortem for last night's `payments` outage, I would follow these steps:

1. **Determine the Template**: 
   - Review the organization's postmortem governance protocol to identify the appropriate template for an outage of this nature. This typically includes sections like Executive Summary, Timeline, Root Cause Analysis, Impact Assessment, Resolution, and Lessons Learned.

2. **Name the Document**:
   - Use a naming convention that reflects the nature of the outage and the date. A sensible name might be `2023-10-31_payments_outage_postmortem`.

3. **Identify the Directory**:
   - Locate the directory where similar postmortems are stored. This is usually a centralized location within the organization’s documentation or project management system, such as `Outages/Postmortems` or `Incident-Reports/Payments`.

4. **Assign Reviewers**:
   - Identify the team members who were on call during the outage. These individuals will have firsthand knowledge of the incident and its resolution. Additionally, consider including key stakeholders or team leads who might provide valuable insights or oversight.

5. **Draft the Postmortem**:
   - **Executive Summary**: Provide a brief overview of the incident, including the start and end times, systems affected, and a high-level impact assessment.
   - **Timeline**: Detail the sequence of events leading up to, during, and following the outage. Include timestamps and key actions taken.
   - **Root Cause Analysis**: Investigate and describe the underlying cause of the outage. Use diagrams or flowcharts if necessary to illustrate complex interactions.
   - **Impact Assessment**: Quantify the impact on users, business operations, and other systems. Include any financial implications or SLA breaches.
   - **Resolution**: Describe the steps taken to resolve the outage and restore normal operations. Highlight any immediate fixes and longer-term solutions.
   - **Lessons Learned**: Discuss what was learned from the incident. Identify any gaps in processes or tools and propose improvements or changes to prevent future occurrences.
   - **Action Items**: List specific actions to be taken, who is responsible, and the deadlines for completion.

6. **Review and Revise**:
   - Share the draft with the assigned reviewers for feedback. Make necessary revisions based on their input to ensure accuracy and completeness.

7. **Finalize and Distribute**:
   - Once the document is finalized, save it in the identified directory. Distribute it to relevant stakeholders and team members, ensuring it is accessible for future reference and learning.

8. **Follow-Up**:
   - Monitor the progress of any action items identified in the postmortem. Schedule follow-up meetings if necessary to review the implementation of improvements or changes.

By following these steps, I would ensure a comprehensive and effective postmortem that not only documents the incident but also contributes to continuous improvement and resilience.
