Creating a postmortem for the `payments` outage involves a systematic approach to ensure all necessary aspects are covered and the document is properly reviewed. Here’s a step-by-step guide to creating the postmortem:

1. **Identify the Template:**
   - Access your organization's postmortem governance protocol to determine the specific template required for postmortem documentation. This template will provide the structure and sections needed, such as background information, timeline, root cause analysis, impact assessment, corrective actions, and future prevention strategies.

2. **Naming the Document:**
   - Follow your organization's naming convention to ensure consistency and ease of identification. A sensible name could be something like `2023-10-XX-payments-outage-postmortem`, where `XX` is replaced with the day of the month when the postmortem is being documented.

3. **Determine the Directory:**
   - Locate the directory where similar postmortem documents are stored. This is typically a shared location within your organization’s documentation or knowledge base, often organized by date or by system name (e.g., `outages/payments/`).

4. **Draft the Postmortem:**
   - **Background Information:** Provide context about the `payments` system, including its purpose and importance to the organization.
   - **Timeline:** Create a detailed timeline of the outage, starting from the first report to resolution. Include key timestamps and actions taken during the incident.
   - **Root Cause Analysis:** Investigate and document the primary cause of the outage, as well as any contributing factors.
   - **Impact Assessment:** Describe the scope and impact of the outage on users, business operations, and any other relevant areas.
   - **Corrective Actions:** Outline the immediate steps taken to resolve the outage and any changes implemented to prevent recurrence.
   - **Future Prevention:** Recommend long-term solutions or improvements to system architecture, monitoring, or processes to prevent similar incidents.

5. **Assign Reviewers:**
   - Identify the individuals who must review the postmortem. These typically include those who were on call during the outage, key team members involved in the resolution, and any additional stakeholders specified by your governance protocol.

6. **Share for Review:**
   - Share the draft postmortem with the assigned reviewers, using the appropriate channels or tools (e.g., document sharing platforms, email). Request their feedback and approval within a specified timeframe.

7. **Incorporate Feedback:**
   - Review the feedback from the assigned reviewers and make necessary revisions to the postmortem document.

8. **Finalize and Publish:**
   - Once the document is finalized and approved by the reviewers, publish it in the designated directory. Ensure it is accessible to all relevant team members and stakeholders.

9. **Follow-Up:**
   - Schedule a meeting or debriefing session with the involved teams to discuss the findings and any lessons learned from the postmortem. This can help reinforce the changes and improvements that need to be implemented.

By following these steps, you ensure that the postmortem is thorough, well-organized, and useful for future reference and prevention of similar outages.
