To create the postmortem for last night's `payments` outage, I would follow these steps:

1. **Identify the Appropriate Template:**
   - Review the organization's postmortem governance protocol to select the correct template. The protocol should specify the template based on the nature or severity of the outage. In this case, the `payments` outage would likely have a predefined template for financial system outages.

2. **Name the Document:**
   - Use a clear and descriptive naming convention for the document. A sensible name might include the date of the outage, the affected system, and the term "postmortem." For example, "2023-10-15 Payments System Postmortem."

3. **Select the Directory:**
   - Determine the appropriate directory for storing the postmortem document. This directory is typically specified in the governance protocol and should contain similar documents for organizational consistency. It might be named something like `/postmortems/payments/` or `/outage-reports/2023/october/`.

4. **Assign Reviewers:**
   - Identify the individuals who must be assigned as reviewers. The protocol should specify who needs to review the postmortem, often including those who were on call, team leads, or relevant stakeholders. Assign the reviewers based on their roles and involvement in the outage resolution.

5. **Draft the Postmortem:**
   - Fill out the selected template with detailed information about the outage:
     - **Summary:** Provide a brief overview of what happened, including the time, duration, and impact of the outage.
     - **Timeline:** Detail the sequence of events leading up to, during, and after the outage.
     - **Root Cause Analysis:** Identify the primary cause of the outage and any contributing factors.
     - **Impact Assessment:** Describe the effects on users, systems, and business operations.
     - **Response and Resolution:** Outline the actions taken to address the outage and restore normal operations.
     - **Lessons Learned:** Highlight key takeaways and potential improvements to prevent similar issues in the future.
     - **Action Items:** List specific steps to be taken to implement the lessons learned, including responsible parties and deadlines.

6. **Review and Finalize:**
   - Circulate the draft postmortem to the assigned reviewers for feedback. Incorporate their suggestions to ensure accuracy and completeness.

7. **Publish the Document:**
   - Once finalized, save the postmortem in the designated directory with the chosen name, ensuring it is accessible to relevant teams and stakeholders.

8. **Communicate Outcomes:**
   - Share the key findings and action items with the broader organization as needed, ensuring transparency and fostering a culture of continuous improvement.

By following these steps, the postmortem will be comprehensive, informative, and aligned with organizational standards.
