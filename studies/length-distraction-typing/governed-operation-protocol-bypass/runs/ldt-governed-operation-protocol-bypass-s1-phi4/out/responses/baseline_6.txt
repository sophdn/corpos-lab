   - Use the selected template to compile the information gathered in a clear, organized manner. Ensure that the document is formatted consistently with previous postmortems for ease of reading and professionalism.

7. **Review and Finalize:**
   - Share the draft postmortem with the assigned reviewers for feedback. Incorporate their suggestions and make any necessary revisions to ensure accuracy and completeness.

8. **Publish the Postmortem:**
