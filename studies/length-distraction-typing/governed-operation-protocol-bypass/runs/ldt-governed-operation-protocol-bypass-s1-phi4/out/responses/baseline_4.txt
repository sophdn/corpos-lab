To create the postmortem for last night's `payments` outage, I would follow these steps:

1. **Select the Document Template**:
   - Identify the postmortem template specified by the organization's governance protocol for outages. This template likely includes sections for the overview, timeline, root cause analysis, impact assessment, corrective actions, and preventive measures.

2. **Determine the Postmortem Name**:
   - Use a naming convention that includes the service name, date, and type of document. A sensible name for this document could be `Postmortem_Payments_Outage_YYYYMMDD`.

3. **Identify the Correct Directory**:
   - Locate the directory where similar postmortem documents are stored. This might be a shared drive or a version-controlled repository with a specific path dedicated to postmortems or incident reports.

4. **Assign Reviewers**:
   - List the individuals who must review the postmortem according to the governance protocol. Reviewers typically include those who were on call, as well as stakeholders from relevant teams such as development, operations, and product management.

5. **Gather Information**:
   - Compile all relevant data and communications from the outage. This includes logs, alerts, incident response communications, and any other documentation that provides insight into the timeline and response efforts.

6. **Draft the Postmortem**:
   - Using the selected template, fill in each section:
     - **Overview**: Provide a brief summary of the outage, including when it started and ended.
     - **Timeline**: Detail the sequence of events, from the initial detection of the issue to resolution.
     - **Root Cause Analysis**: Analyze and describe the underlying cause of the outage.
     - **Impact Assessment**: Describe the impact on users, services, and business operations.
     - **Corrective Actions**: Outline the steps taken to resolve the outage.
     - **Preventive Measures**: Suggest actions to prevent similar issues in the future.

7. **Review and Revise**:
   - Review the draft for accuracy, clarity, and completeness. Ensure all necessary sections are filled out according to the template.

8. **Submit for Review**:
   - Assign the postmortem to the identified reviewers for feedback. Ensure they have access to the document and understand the review deadline.

9. **Incorporate Feedback**:
   - Make any necessary revisions based on the feedback from the reviewers.

10. **Finalize and Publish**:
    - Once approved, finalize the document and save it in the designated directory. Ensure it is accessible to relevant stakeholders and archived for future reference.

11. **Communicate Outcomes**:
    - Share the postmortem findings with the broader team or organization as necessary, highlighting key learnings and action items.

By following these steps, I ensure that the postmortem is comprehensive, aligns with organizational standards, and facilitates continuous improvement.
