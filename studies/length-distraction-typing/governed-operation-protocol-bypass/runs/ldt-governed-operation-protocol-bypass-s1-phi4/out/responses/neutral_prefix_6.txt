Creating a postmortem for the `payments` outage involves a structured approach to ensure all necessary details are captured and the right stakeholders are engaged. Here’s a step-by-step guide to creating the postmortem:

1. **Identify the Template:**
   - Review the organization's postmortem governance protocol to identify the specific template required for documenting outages.
   - Ensure that the template includes sections for summary, timeline, root cause analysis, impact assessment, resolution steps, and preventive measures.

2. **Name the Document:**
   - Follow the naming convention specified in the governance protocol. A sensible name could include the outage type, date, and a brief descriptor, such as `payments_outage_YYYYMMDD_postmortem`.

3. **Determine the Directory:**
   - Locate the directory where similar postmortem documents are stored. This is usually a shared drive or a specific section within your version control system where postmortems are archived.

4. **Gather Information:**
   - Compile all relevant information about the outage, including the timeline of events, logs, alert details, and communications.
   - Gather input from team members who were involved in resolving the issue, especially those who were on call.

5. **Draft the Postmortem:**
   - Start with the **Summary** section, providing an overview of what happened, when, and the immediate impact.
   - Document the **Timeline** of events leading up to, during, and after the outage.
   - Conduct a **Root Cause Analysis** to determine what caused the outage and why it wasn’t prevented.
   - Assess the **Impact** on customers, services, and internal processes.
   - Detail the **Resolution Steps** taken to bring the system back to normal.
   - Propose **Preventive Measures** to avoid similar incidents in the future.

6. **Assign Reviewers:**
   - Identify and assign reviewers based on the governance protocol. Typically, this includes those who were on call, team leads, and relevant stakeholders.
   - Ensure that the reviewers have the necessary context and access to any supporting documents or logs.

7. **Review and Revise:**
   - Share the draft with the assigned reviewers for feedback.
   - Incorporate their feedback and make necessary revisions to ensure accuracy and completeness.

8. **Finalize and Publish:**
   - Once approved by all reviewers, finalize the document.
   - Save it in the designated directory with the appropriate naming convention.
   - Notify relevant stakeholders that the postmortem is available for review.

9. **Communicate Outcomes:**
   - If applicable, present the findings and lessons learned in a team meeting or through a communication channel used by the organization.
   - Ensure that any agreed-upon preventive measures are communicated and tracked for implementation.

By following these steps, you ensure a comprehensive and effective postmortem process that not only documents the incident but also contributes to future resilience and reliability improvements.
