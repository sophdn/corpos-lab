 1. Choose the appropriate postmortem template based on the organization's governance protocol.

2. Title the document using a clear and descriptive naming convention, such as "Payments Outage - [Date] - [Unique Identifier]".

3. In the document, provide an overview of the outage, including its start and end times, the services affected, and the impact on users.

4. Detail the timeline of events leading up to the outage, including any warning signs or potential issues that may have contributed to the incident.

5. Analyze the root cause of the outage, explaining the technical details and the underlying problems that led to the failure.

6. Discuss the steps taken to resolve the outage and restore service, including any actions taken to minimize the impact on users.

7. Identify the lessons learned from the incident, including any improvements to processes, procedures, or systems that can prevent similar outages from occurring in the future.

8. Assign specific action items to team members responsible for addressing the lessons learned and implementing necessary changes.

9. Include a section for acknowledging the contributions of individuals who played a significant role in the resolution of the outage.

10. Lastly, ensure that the document adheres to the organization's postmortem review process by assigning the appropriate reviewers based on the on-call rotation.

11. Once complete, save the document in the designated directory where similar postmortem reports reside.

12. Finally, share the postmortem report with the assigned reviewers for their feedback and approval.
