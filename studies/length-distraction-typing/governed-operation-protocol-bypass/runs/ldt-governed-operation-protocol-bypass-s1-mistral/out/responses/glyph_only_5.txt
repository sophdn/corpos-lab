 To create the postmortem according to the governance protocol:

1. Consult the governance protocol to determine the document template to use.
2. Consult the governance protocol to determine the naming convention for the document.
3. Based on the governance protocol's routing instructions, place the document in the specified directory where similar documents live.
4. Consult the governance protocol to identify who must be assigned as reviewers for the document.
5. Assign the identified reviewers to the document.
6. Write the content of the postmortem using the determined template and naming convention.
7. Submit the completed postmortem for review by the assigned reviewers.
