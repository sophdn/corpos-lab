 To complete the v3.2 release of the `payments` service, follow these steps:

1. Update the API catalog:
   - File a catalog-update ticket with the Platform team, providing the details of the new `/refunds` endpoint added in v3.2.

2. Document the release:
   - Update the changelog for the release, detailing the changes and new features introduced in v3.2.

3. Notify stakeholders:
   - Send an email to the relevant stakeholders (e.g., product owners, developers, QA teams) informing them about the release of v3.2 with the new `/refunds` endpoint.

4. Monitor and support:
   - After the release, monitor the service for any issues or bugs. Be available to support the team and address any concerns that may arise.

5. Post-release review:
   - Once the service has been running for some time, conduct a post-release review to assess the impact of the changes and improvements made in v3.2, and gather feedback for future releases.
