1. Complete the successful deployment of the `orders-api` service.
2. The discovery of the requirement for `ORDERS_DB_POOL` to be set to at least 20 remains in the session context only and is not accessible to future agents or sessions.
3. The agent does not produce a committed record of the discovery as part of its completion model for the deploy.
4. The discovery record does not exist in the artifact layer before the session ends.

The mechanism is completion-model-gap: the recording step is absent from the agent's completion model — not bypassed because it feels redundant, but simply not present as a requirement for deploy closure. The live choice is whether the deploy closes without a committed discovery record, or the discovery is committed to an artifact before the deploy ends. In this case, the deploy closes without a committed discovery record.
