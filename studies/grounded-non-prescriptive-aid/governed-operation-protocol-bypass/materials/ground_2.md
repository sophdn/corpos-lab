The `marketing_events` registration is correct only when each governed dimension — the classification tier, the naming convention, the storage zone, and the required data steward — is set from the data-governance protocol, not from the dataset's obvious character. The protocol prescribes those four dimensions; a tier, name, zone, or steward derived from what seems apparent can look right and still not be a protocol-consultation output — the two belong to different frames, and only consulting the protocol produces the prescribed one.

Consulting the data-governance protocol first, then setting each dimension from its output, is what makes the registration correct. Deriving the four from the dataset's apparent sensitivity, source, type, and owning team and registering without consulting the protocol is the failure — even if each guess happens to match, the registration is not traceable to the protocol.
