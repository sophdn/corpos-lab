To register the `marketing_events` dataset in the organization's data catalog, I would follow these steps, adhering to the data-governance protocol:

1. **Determine Classification Tier:**
   - Review the dataset contents to assess its sensitivity. This involves identifying any personal identifiable information (PII), financial data, or other sensitive information.
   - Classify the dataset according to the organization's sensitivity tiers (e.g., public, internal, confidential, restricted).

2. **Establish Naming Convention:**
   - Identify the source system or origin of the dataset. This could be a specific marketing platform or database.
   - Apply the organization's naming convention, which might include elements like the source system name, dataset type, and version or timestamp.
   - For example, if the source is "PromoSys" and the convention is `[SourceSystem]_[DatasetType]_[Version]`, the name might be `PromoSys_MarketingEvents_v1`.

3. **Assign Storage Zone:**
   - Determine the type of data contained in the dataset (e.g., structured, unstructured, transactional).
   - Based on the data type, assign the appropriate storage zone as per organizational guidelines (e.g., data warehouse, data lake, operational database).

4. **Identify Required Data Steward:**
   - Identify the team responsible for the dataset. This could be the marketing team, the data engineering team, or another relevant group.
   - Confirm the data steward, who is typically someone from the owning team or department, responsible for maintaining data quality and compliance.

5. **Compile Metadata:**
   - Gather additional metadata such as dataset description, columns and their data types, and any relevant tags or categories.
   - Document the dataset's lineage, including how it is generated, transformed, and where it is sourced from.

6. **Create Registration Entry:**
   - Use the organization's data catalog tool or system to create a new entry for the dataset.
   - Input all gathered information: classification tier, dataset name, storage zone, data steward, metadata, and any other required fields.

7. **Review and Approve:**
   - Ensure all details are accurate and comply with organizational standards.
   - Submit the registration for review and approval by relevant stakeholders, if required by the protocol.

8. **Publish and Notify:**
   - Once approved, publish the dataset in the data catalog.
   - Notify relevant stakeholders, including the data steward and any teams that might use or be impacted by the dataset, about its availability.

By following these steps, I ensure that the `marketing_events` dataset is registered in compliance with the organization's data-governance protocol, facilitating proper management, access, and use within the organization.
