1. **Locate and Consult the Postmortem Governance Protocol**
   Before accessing any outage logs, ticket details, or existing documentation, the first action is to identify and read the official postmortem governance protocol. This step is mandatory to ensure the process is traceable to the governance source.

2. **Extract the Prescribed Template**
   From the protocol, determine the specific document template required for a `payments` outage. Do not select a template based on what seems sensible or obvious from the outage context; use only the template explicitly defined in the protocol.

3. **Determine the Naming Convention**
   Apply the naming convention specified in the governance protocol to generate the postmortem filename. This name must be derived strictly from the protocol’s rules, not from an intuitive or context-based guess.

4. **Identify the Correct Directory**
   Retrieve the designated directory path from the governance protocol. Place the postmortem in this specific directory, regardless of where similar documents are currently stored or where it might logically seem to belong.

5. **Assign the Protocol-Specified Reviewers**
   Identify the list of reviewers mandated by the governance protocol. Assign these specific individuals as reviewers. Do not assign reviewers based on who was on call or who was involved in the outage, unless the protocol explicitly defines the reviewer list in those terms.

6. **Create the Postmortem Document**
   Only after extracting the template, name, directory, and reviewer list from the protocol, create the postmortem document using these protocol-derived parameters. Populate the content based on the outage details, but ensure the structural and administrative dimensions (template, name, location, reviewers) remain strictly aligned with the protocol consultation.
