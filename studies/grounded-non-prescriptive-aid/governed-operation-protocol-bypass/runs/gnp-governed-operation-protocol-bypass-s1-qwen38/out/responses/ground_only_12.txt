The steps to create the postmortem are:

1.  **Locate and consult the postmortem governance protocol.** This is the mandatory first step to establish the source of truth for all governance dimensions.
2.  **Extract the prescribed document template** directly from the protocol. Do not rely on the template that seems obvious from the outage.
3.  **Extract the prescribed naming convention** directly from the protocol. Do not derive the name from the specific details of the `payments` outage.
4.  **Extract the prescribed directory location** directly from the protocol. Do not infer the directory based on where similar documents are stored.
5.  **Extract the assigned reviewers** directly from the protocol. Do not select reviewers based on who was on call or who seems clear from the context.
6.  **Create the postmortem document** using the template, name, directory, and reviewer list obtained exclusively from the protocol in steps 2–5.
