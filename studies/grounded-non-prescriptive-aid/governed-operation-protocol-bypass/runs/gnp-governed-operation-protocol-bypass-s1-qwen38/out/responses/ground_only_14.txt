    Assign the specific individuals or groups identified in Step 5 as the reviewers for the document.

8.  **Verify Protocol Traceability**
    Before finalizing, verify that every dimension of the created postmortem (template, name, directory, reviewers) can be directly traced back to a specific clause in the governance protocol. If any element was derived from the outage context rather than the protocol, correct it immediately.
