To create the postmortem for the `payments` outage, I would follow these steps:

1. **Identify the Postmortem Template**: 
   - Consult the organization's postmortem governance protocol to determine which template should be used. This template will likely include sections like Introduction, Timeline of Events, Root Cause Analysis, Impact Assessment, Resolution Steps, and Preventative Measures.

2. **Develop a Sensible Name**:
   - Create a name for the postmortem document that clearly identifies the outage. A suggested naming convention might be `2023-10-XX-payments-outage-postmortem`, where `XX` is replaced with the specific date of the outage.

3. **Determine the Appropriate Directory**:
   - Locate the directory where similar postmortem documents are stored. This is usually a shared location accessible to relevant stakeholders. The directory might be named something like `/outages/postmortems/` or `/incident-reports/`.

4. **Assign Reviewers**:
   - Identify who needs to review the postmortem. This typically includes the on-call personnel, engineering leads, and other stakeholders involved in the outage. Ensure these individuals are assigned as reviewers according to the governance protocol.

5. **Gather Information**:
   - Compile all relevant information about the outage, including incident reports, logs, communication records, and any other pertinent data that can help in understanding the timeline and root cause.

6. **Draft the Postmortem Document**:
   - Use the identified template to structure the document. Fill in the sections with the gathered information, ensuring clarity and thoroughness:
     - **Introduction**: Briefly describe the outage, including when it occurred and which services were affected.
     - **Timeline of Events**: Detail the sequence of events leading up to, during, and after the outage.
     - **Root Cause Analysis**: Analyze and explain the underlying cause of the outage.
     - **Impact Assessment**: Describe the impact on users, services, and the organization.
     - **Resolution Steps**: Outline the steps taken to resolve the issue.
     - **Preventative Measures**: Suggest actions to prevent similar outages in the future.

7. **Review and Edit**:
   - Review the draft for accuracy, clarity, and completeness. Make necessary edits to ensure the document meets organizational standards and provides valuable insights.

8. **Submit for Review**:
   - Share the draft postmortem with the assigned reviewers for feedback. Incorporate their suggestions and make any required revisions.

9. **Finalize and Publish**:
   - Once approved by reviewers, finalize the document. Save it in the determined directory with the appropriate naming convention.

10. **Communicate Findings**:
    - Share the findings with the broader team or organization as necessary. This might involve presenting the postmortem at a team meeting or distributing it via internal communication channels.

By following these steps, the postmortem will be comprehensive, actionable, and aligned with organizational protocols.
