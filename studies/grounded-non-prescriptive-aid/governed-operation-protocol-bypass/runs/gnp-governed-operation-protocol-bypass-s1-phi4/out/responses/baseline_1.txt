To create the postmortem for last night's `payments` outage, follow these steps:

1. **Identify the Template:**
   - Refer to your organization's postmortem governance protocol to select the appropriate template for an outage-related postmortem. Ensure it includes sections such as summary, timeline, root cause analysis, impact, and corrective actions.

2. **Name the Document:**
   - Use a naming convention that includes the service name, date, and type of document. A sensible name could be `Postmortem_Payments_Outage_YYYYMMDD`.

3. **Determine the Directory:**
   - Locate the directory where similar postmortem documents are stored. This is typically organized by service or team, such as `/postmortems/payments/`.

4. **Assign Reviewers:**
   - Identify the team members who were on call during the outage, as well as any key stakeholders involved in the incident response. Assign them as reviewers to ensure comprehensive feedback and accountability.

5. **Draft the Postmortem:**
   - Begin with a concise **Summary** of the outage, including the affected service, duration, and overall impact.
   - Create a detailed **Timeline** of events from the onset of the outage to resolution, noting key actions taken.
   - Conduct a **Root Cause Analysis** to identify the underlying issue that led to the outage.
   - Describe the **Impact** on users, business operations, and any financial implications.
   - Outline **Corrective Actions** and **Preventive Measures** to avoid future occurrences, including any changes to processes, infrastructure, or monitoring.

6. **Review and Revise:**
   - Circulate the draft among the assigned reviewers for feedback. Incorporate their suggestions to ensure accuracy and completeness.

7. **Finalize the Document:**
   - Make any necessary revisions based on reviewer feedback. Ensure the document is clear, concise, and free of technical jargon.

8. **Publish and Distribute:**
   - Save the finalized postmortem in the designated directory. Distribute it to relevant teams and stakeholders, ensuring it is accessible for future reference.

9. **Follow-Up:**
   - Schedule a follow-up meeting to discuss the postmortem findings and any action items with the involved teams to ensure continuous improvement.

By following these steps, you will create a comprehensive and useful postmortem document that adheres to your organization's governance protocols.
