To create the postmortem for the `payments` outage, I'll follow a structured approach based on the organizational governance protocol. Here are the steps I would take:

1. **Select the Appropriate Document Template**:
   - Identify the standard postmortem template that aligns with outages related to payment systems. This typically includes sections like Executive Summary, Incident Timeline, Root Cause Analysis, Impact Assessment, Corrective Actions, and Lessons Learned.

2. **Determine the Naming Convention**:
   - Use the established naming convention for postmortem documents. A sensible name might be: `payments_outage_YYYYMMDD_postmortem`, where `YYYYMMDD` is the date of the incident. This ensures clarity and easy retrieval of the document.

3. **Identify the Correct Directory**:
   - Determine the directory where similar postmortem documents are stored. This is usually within a centralized repository or shared drive, under a directory such as `Postmortems/Payments/`.

4. **Assign Reviewers**:
   - Identify the team members who were on call during the outage or those directly involved in incident management. These individuals should be assigned as reviewers. Typically, this includes system engineers, product managers, and potentially representatives from business units affected by the outage.

5. **Compile Incident Details**:
   - Gather all relevant data from the incident, including logs, incident response communications, and monitoring alerts. Document the timeline of events, starting from the first alert through resolution.

6. **Analyze and Document Root Cause**:
   - Conduct a root cause analysis to determine the underlying cause of the outage. Document this in the postmortem, ensuring clarity on why the incident occurred and what specific failure points were involved.

7. **Assess Impact**:
   - Evaluate the impact of the outage on both the technical systems and business operations. This should include metrics like downtime duration, number of affected users, and financial impact if applicable.

8. **Outline Corrective and Preventative Actions**:
   - Detail any immediate corrective actions taken to resolve the outage. Additionally, propose longer-term preventative measures to avoid recurrence of similar issues, such as code changes, infrastructure updates, or process improvements.

9. **Identify Lessons Learned**:
   - Reflect on the incident handling process itself and identify any areas for improvement. This could involve enhancements to alerting, communication protocols, or incident management tools.

10. **Draft the Postmortem**:
    - Using the selected template, draft the postmortem document incorporating all the collected information. Ensure it is clear, concise, and follows the format of previous postmortems for consistency.

11. **Review and Revise**:
    - Share the draft with the assigned reviewers for feedback. Make necessary revisions based on their input to ensure accuracy and completeness.

12. **Finalize and Submit**:
    - Once reviewed, finalize the document, ensuring all sections are complete and the document is well-organized. Submit it to the appropriate directory for organizational records and future reference.

13. **Communicate Findings**:
    - Plan a meeting or presentation to communicate the postmortem findings to relevant stakeholders. This can include lessons learned and action items to ensure transparency and collective learning within the organization.

Following these steps ensures a thorough, organized, and compliant creation of the postmortem document, facilitating learning and improvement from the outage incident.
