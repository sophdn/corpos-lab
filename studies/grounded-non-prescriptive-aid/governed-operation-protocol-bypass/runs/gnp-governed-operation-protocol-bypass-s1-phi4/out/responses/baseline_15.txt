To create the postmortem for last night's `payments` outage, I would follow these steps:

1. **Select the Template**: 
   - Identify the standard postmortem template used by the organization for outages. This template will include sections such as Executive Summary, Timeline of Events, Root Cause Analysis, Impact Analysis, Corrective Actions, and Preventative Measures.

2. **Define the Naming Convention**: 
   - Use the organization's naming convention for postmortems. A sensible name might be `2023-10-XX-Payments-Outage-Postmortem`, replacing `XX` with the specific date of the outage.

3. **Determine the Directory**: 
   - Locate the directory where similar postmortems are stored. This is typically a shared drive or document management system under a folder named something like `/Outages/Postmortems/` or `/Incident-Reports/`.

4. **Assign Reviewers**: 
   - Identify the individuals who need to review the document. This usually includes the team members who were on call during the outage, key stakeholders, and possibly representatives from related teams such as development, operations, and customer support.

5. **Gather Information**:
   - Collect all relevant data and logs related to the outage. This includes incident response communications, monitoring data, system logs, and any customer feedback received.

6. **Draft the Postmortem**:
   - **Executive Summary**: Write a brief overview of the outage, including what happened, when it happened, and the overall impact.
   - **Timeline of Events**: Detail the sequence of events leading up to, during, and after the outage.
   - **Root Cause Analysis**: Identify the root cause of the outage and explain it clearly.
   - **Impact Analysis**: Describe the impact on customers, services, and business operations.
   - **Corrective Actions**: Outline the immediate actions taken to resolve the issue.
   - **Preventative Measures**: Suggest long-term changes or improvements to prevent a recurrence.

7. **Review and Edit**:
   - Ensure the document is clear, concise, and free of technical jargon where possible. Make sure all sections are completed and accurate.

8. **Submit for Review**:
   - Assign the postmortem to the identified reviewers for their feedback and approval.

9. **Incorporate Feedback**:
   - Make necessary revisions based on the feedback received from reviewers.

10. **Finalize and Publish**:
    - Once approved, finalize the document and publish it in the designated directory, ensuring it is accessible to all relevant parties.

11. **Communicate Findings**:
    - Share the key findings and action items with the broader team and stakeholders as appropriate, possibly through a team meeting or a follow-up email.

12. **Schedule a Retrospective Meeting**:
    - Arrange a meeting with the involved teams to discuss the outage in detail, ensuring everyone understands the root cause and the steps being taken to prevent future occurrences.

By following these steps, the postmortem will be comprehensive, informative, and aligned with the organization's governance protocols.
