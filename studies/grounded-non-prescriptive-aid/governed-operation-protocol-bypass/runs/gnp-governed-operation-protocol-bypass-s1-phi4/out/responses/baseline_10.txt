To create the postmortem for the `payments` outage, I would follow these steps:

1. **Identify the Template:**
   - Determine the specific template required for postmortems within the organization. This template likely includes sections like Introduction, Impact, Root Cause, Timeline, Resolution, and Preventative Measures.

2. **Name the Document:**
   - Choose a sensible and consistent name for the document. A typical naming convention might include the service name, date, and type of document, such as `payments_outage_YYYYMMDD_postmortem`.

3. **Select the Directory:**
   - Determine the directory where postmortem documents are stored. This is usually a shared or version-controlled location where similar documents reside, often organized by date or service name.

4. **Assign Reviewers:**
   - Identify the individuals who were on call during the outage. These are typically the reviewers assigned to the postmortem. Additional stakeholders such as team leads or service owners might also be included.

5. **Gather Information:**
   - Collect all relevant data about the outage, including logs, incident reports, communications, and any other relevant documentation.

6. **Write the Postmortem:**
   - Fill out the template using the gathered information:
     - **Introduction:** Briefly describe the outage and its significance.
     - **Impact:** Detail the scope and effect of the outage on users and services.
     - **Root Cause:** Analyze and describe the underlying cause of the outage.
     - **Timeline:** Provide a chronological account of the incident from detection to resolution.
     - **Resolution:** Explain how the issue was resolved.
     - **Preventative Measures:** Suggest steps to prevent similar issues in the future.

7. **Review and Edit:**
   - Ensure the document is clear, concise, and free of errors. Make any necessary revisions for clarity and completeness.

8. **Submit for Review:**
   - Share the draft postmortem with the assigned reviewers for their input and approval.

9. **Finalize and Publish:**
   - Incorporate feedback from reviewers, finalize the document, and then publish it in the designated directory.

10. **Communicate Findings:**
    - Share the postmortem with relevant teams and stakeholders to ensure lessons learned are disseminated and discussed.

By following these steps, the postmortem will be comprehensive, align with organizational standards, and contribute to future improvements.
