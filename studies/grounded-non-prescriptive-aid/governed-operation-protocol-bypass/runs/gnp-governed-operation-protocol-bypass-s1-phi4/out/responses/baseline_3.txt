To create the postmortem for the `payments` outage, I would follow these steps:

1. **Select the Document Template:**
   - Identify the template specified by the organization's governance protocol for postmortems. This template will likely include sections such as summary, timeline, root cause analysis, impact, corrective actions, and lessons learned.

2. **Determine the Naming Convention:**
   - Use a sensible naming convention as outlined in the governance protocol. This might include the system name, date of the incident, and a brief description. For example, `Payments_Outage_2023-10-15`.

3. **Identify the Appropriate Directory:**
   - Locate the directory where similar postmortems are stored. This is usually determined by the governance protocol and may be based on the system or department affected by the outage.

4. **Assign Reviewers:**
   - Determine who must review the postmortem based on who was on call during the outage. This typically includes on-call engineers, system owners, and possibly other stakeholders like operations or customer support teams.

5. **Gather Information:**
   - Compile all relevant data from the incident, including logs, monitoring alerts, communication records, and any other documentation that can provide insights into what happened.

6. **Draft the Postmortem:**
   - Begin drafting the postmortem using the selected template. Fill in each section with detailed information:
     - **Summary:** Provide a brief overview of the incident.
     - **Timeline:** Outline the sequence of events leading up to, during, and following the outage.
     - **Root Cause Analysis:** Identify and explain the root cause(s) of the outage.
     - **Impact:** Describe the impact of the outage on users, services, and business operations.
     - **Corrective Actions:** List immediate actions taken to resolve the outage and any longer-term changes implemented to prevent recurrence.
     - **Lessons Learned:** Highlight key takeaways and recommendations for future improvements.

7. **Review and Edit:**
   - Review the draft for accuracy, completeness, and clarity. Ensure that all necessary information is included and that the document aligns with the organization's standards.

8. **Submit for Review:**
   - Assign the document to the identified reviewers for their feedback. Ensure that all necessary reviewers have access and are aware of their roles in the review process.

9. **Incorporate Feedback:**
   - Address any comments or suggestions from the reviewers, making necessary revisions to the document.

10. **Finalize and Publish:**
    - Once the document meets all criteria and has been approved by the reviewers, finalize it and save it in the designated directory.

11. **Communicate:**
    - Notify relevant stakeholders about the completion of the postmortem and share the document as appropriate, ensuring transparency and accountability.

By following these steps, I would ensure that the postmortem is thorough, accurate, and adheres to the organization's governance protocols.
