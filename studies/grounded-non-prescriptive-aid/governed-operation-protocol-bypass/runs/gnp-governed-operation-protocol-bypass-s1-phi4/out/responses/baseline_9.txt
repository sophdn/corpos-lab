To create the postmortem for the `payments` outage, I would follow these steps:

1. **Select the Appropriate Template:**
   - Identify the template designated for postmortems related to outages within the organization's governance protocol. This template will include sections like Overview, Incident Timeline, Root Cause Analysis, Impact Assessment, and Corrective Actions.

2. **Determine the Document Name:**
   - Use a sensible naming convention as specified by the protocol. Typically, this would include the service name, the date of the incident, and possibly a version number if applicable. For example, "Payments_Outage_Postmortem_2023-10-05_v1".

3. **Identify the Storage Directory:**
   - Locate the directory where similar postmortem documents are stored. This is often within a centralized repository or documentation folder, possibly organized by date or service name, such as `/postmortems/2023/payments`.

4. **Assign Reviewers:**
   - Determine who needs to be assigned as reviewers based on the governance protocol. This usually includes individuals who were on call, members of the incident response team, and any stakeholders impacted by the outage. Ensure all relevant parties are included.

5. **Draft the Postmortem:**
   - Fill out the template with detailed information:
     - **Overview:** Brief description of what happened.
     - **Incident Timeline:** A chronological account of the events leading up to, during, and after the outage.
     - **Root Cause Analysis:** In-depth analysis of why the outage occurred.
     - **Impact Assessment:** Details on the scope and effect of the outage on services and customers.
     - **Corrective Actions:** Steps taken to resolve the issue and prevent future occurrences, including any follow-up tasks.

6. **Review and Revise:**
   - Conduct an initial review of the draft to ensure accuracy and completeness. Make revisions as necessary.

7. **Submit for Review:**
   - Share the draft with the assigned reviewers for feedback. Ensure that all reviewers have access and time to provide their input.

8. **Incorporate Feedback:**
   - Gather feedback from reviewers and make any necessary adjustments to the postmortem document.

9. **Finalize and Publish:**
   - Once all feedback has been incorporated and the document is finalized, save it in the designated directory with the appropriate naming convention.

10. **Notify Stakeholders:**
    - Inform all relevant stakeholders that the postmortem is complete and available for review. This may involve sending an email or posting an announcement in a shared communication channel.

11. **Schedule a Follow-up Meeting:**
    - If necessary, organize a meeting to discuss the postmortem findings and any action items with the team to ensure accountability and implementation of corrective measures.

By following these steps, I ensure that the postmortem is comprehensive, adheres to organizational protocols, and facilitates learning and improvement from the incident.
