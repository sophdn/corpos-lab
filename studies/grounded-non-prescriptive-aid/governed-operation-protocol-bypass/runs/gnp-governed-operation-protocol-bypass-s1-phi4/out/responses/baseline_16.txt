To create the postmortem for the `payments` outage, I would follow these steps:

1. **Select the Template**: Use the specified template for postmortems as per the organization's governance protocol. This template will likely include sections such as an executive summary, timeline of events, root cause analysis, impact assessment, corrective actions, and preventive measures.

2. **Create a Sensible Name**: Name the postmortem document in a format that typically includes the date and a brief description of the incident. A sensible name could be something like `2023-10-15 Payments Outage Postmortem`.

3. **Identify the Correct Directory**: Determine the directory where similar postmortem documents are stored. This is usually a shared folder or repository where all postmortem documents are kept for organizational reference and future training. Ensure you have access to this directory.

4. **Assign Reviewers**: Identify and assign the reviewers who were on call during the outage. According to the governance protocol, these individuals are likely to be involved in the incident response and are responsible for reviewing the postmortem for accuracy and completeness.

5. **Draft the Executive Summary**: Begin with an executive summary that provides a high-level overview of the incident, including when it occurred, its duration, and the systems affected.

6. **Detail the Timeline of Events**: Construct a chronological timeline of the events as they unfolded. This should include when the outage was detected, the steps taken to address it, and when normal operations were restored.

7. **Conduct Root Cause Analysis**: Analyze and document the root cause of the outage. This involves identifying what went wrong, why it happened, and any contributing factors.

8. **Assess the Impact**: Evaluate the impact of the outage on business operations, customers, and other stakeholders. Include any service level agreement (SLA) breaches and financial implications if applicable.

9. **Outline Corrective Actions**: Describe the immediate actions taken to resolve the outage. This includes technical fixes, communication with stakeholders, and any other measures that were implemented.

10. **Propose Preventive Measures**: Suggest long-term actions to prevent similar incidents in the future. This might include changes to systems, processes, or monitoring tools.

11. **Review and Revise**: Share the draft postmortem with the assigned reviewers for feedback. Make necessary revisions based on their input to ensure accuracy and completeness.

12. **Finalize and Distribute**: Once the document is finalized, save it in the designated directory. Distribute it to relevant stakeholders and ensure it is accessible to those who may need it for future reference or training.

By following these steps, the postmortem will be comprehensive, aligned with organizational protocols, and useful for both immediate learning and long-term improvement.
