2. **Name the Document:**
   - Choose a sensible name for the postmortem document. A suitable format could be `Postmortem_Payments_Outage_YYYYMMDD`, where `YYYYMMDD` represents the date of the outage.

3. **Determine the Directory:**
   - Identify the directory where similar documents are stored. This is typically a centralized location for postmortems, such as `Outages/Postmortems/`, to ensure easy access and organization.

4. **Assign Reviewers:**
   - Determine who must review the document. Reviewers are usually those who were on call during the outage or have relevant expertise. This ensures that the document is accurate and comprehensive.

5. **Gather Information:**
   - Collect all relevant data about the outage, including timelines, logs, alerts, and any communications that occurred during the incident.

6. **Document the Incident:**
   - Fill out the template with the collected information. Include sections such as:
     - Summary of the incident
     - Impact analysis
     - Root cause analysis
     - Steps taken to resolve the issue
     - Timeline of events
     - Lessons learned
     - Preventative measures

7. **Review and Revise:**
   - Share the draft with the assigned reviewers. Incorporate their feedback to ensure accuracy and completeness.

8. **Finalize the Document:**
   - Once all feedback is addressed, finalize the document. Ensure it is clear, concise, and free of errors.

9. **Publish and Distribute:**
   - Save the finalized document in the designated directory. Distribute it to relevant stakeholders, ensuring that it is accessible to those who need it for future reference and learning.

By following these steps, you will create a thorough and useful postmortem that helps the organization learn from the outage and improve future response and prevention strategies.
