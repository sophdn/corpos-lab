To create the postmortem for last night's `payments` outage, I would follow these steps:

1. **Select the Document Template:**
   - Identify the correct postmortem template specified by the organization's governance protocol.
   - Ensure it includes sections such as Incident Overview, Impact, Root Cause Analysis, Resolution Steps, and Action Items.

2. **Create a Sensible Name for the Document:**
   - Construct a name that includes the outage date and the service affected. For example, "2023-10-25_Payments_Outage_Postmortem".

3. **Determine the Directory for the Document:**
   - Locate the directory where similar postmortem documents are stored. This is typically a shared drive or repository designated for incident reports.
   - Ensure you have the correct access permissions to add files to this directory.

4. **Compile Information for the Postmortem:**
   - **Incident Overview:** Describe the timeline of events, including when the outage started, when it was detected, and when it was resolved.
   - **Impact:** Detail the effects of the outage on users, systems, and business operations.
   - **Root Cause Analysis:** Analyze and document the underlying reasons for the outage, including any contributing factors.
   - **Resolution Steps:** Outline the steps taken to resolve the issue and restore normal operations.
   - **Action Items:** List any follow-up actions required to prevent future occurrences, including any changes to processes, infrastructure, or monitoring.

5. **Assign Reviewers:**
   - Identify team members who were on call during the outage, as well as other key stakeholders, such as system owners and team leads.
   - Assign these individuals as reviewers of the postmortem document to ensure accuracy and completeness.

6. **Draft the Postmortem Document:**
   - Use the selected template to draft the document, filling in each section with the compiled information.
   - Ensure clarity and conciseness, and include any relevant logs, screenshots, or data that support the analysis.

7. **Review and Revise:**
   - Share the draft with the assigned reviewers for feedback.
   - Incorporate their suggestions and verify all information is accurate and comprehensive.

8. **Finalize and Publish:**
   - Once approved by the reviewers, finalize the document.
   - Save and upload it to the designated directory, ensuring it is accessible to relevant parties.

9. **Communicate and Follow Up:**
   - Inform relevant teams and stakeholders about the completed postmortem.
   - Schedule any necessary meetings to discuss the action items and ensure accountability for follow-up tasks.

By following these steps, I would ensure a thorough and organized postmortem that meets the organization's standards and helps prevent future outages.
