 1. Choose the appropriate postmortem template for the outage based on the standard template used by the organization. This template will provide a structure for documenting the incident, its impact, root cause analysis, and action plan.

2. Compose a descriptive title for the postmortem, such as "Payments Service Outage on [Date] - Postmortem" or similar, following the organization's naming convention.

3. Populate the postmortem document with the following sections:
   - Summary: Briefly describe the incident, its impact, and the steps taken to resolve it.
   - Timeline: Detail the sequence of events leading up to the outage, the actions taken during the incident, and the recovery process.
   - Conclusions: Identify the root cause of the outage, analyze the contributing factors, and explain why the issue occurred.
   - Lessons Learned: Discuss the lessons learned from the incident, including any insights that may help prevent future similar outages.
   - Action Plan: Outline the steps that will be taken to address the root cause of the outage and improve the system's resilience.
   - Appendices: Include any relevant logs, screenshots, or other evidence that can help support the postmortem findings.

4. Review the postmortem document for clarity, completeness, and adherence to the organization's postmortem governance protocol.

5. Assign the appropriate reviewers as per the org's protocol. This could include those who were on call during the outage, subject matter experts, and/or management.

6. Share the postmortem document with the assigned reviewers for their feedback and approval.

7. Make any necessary revisions based on reviewer feedback, ensuring that the final document is accurate, informative, and actionable.

8. Once the document is approved, save it in the designated directory where similar postmortems are stored for future reference and analysis.

9. Share the final postmortem document with the wider organization, as per the org's protocol, to ensure transparency and accountability.

10. Schedule a follow-up meeting to discuss the action plan, address any open questions, and track the progress of remediation efforts.
