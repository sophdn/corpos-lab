 1. Consult the postmortem governance protocol to determine the prescribed document template to use.

2. Determine the naming convention for the postmortem document based on the protocol.

3. Identify the directory where the postmortem should be stored, based on where similar documents are located in the organization.

4. Identify the reviewers for the postmortem based on the protocol. These may be team members, managers, or other stakeholders as specified by the governance protocol.

5. Using the information gathered from the governance protocol, create the postmortem document using the prescribed template.

6. Name the document according to the naming convention from the protocol.

7. Save the postmortem in the identified directory.

8. Assign the identified reviewers to the postmortem document.

9. Review the postmortem to ensure it accurately reflects the outage and includes appropriate analysis, lessons learned, and recommended actions to prevent future outages.

10. Once the postmortem is complete, distribute it to the assigned reviewers for their feedback and approval.

11. After receiving feedback and making any necessary revisions, finalize the postmortem and make it available to the rest of the organization.
