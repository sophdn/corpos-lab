 1. Write the title of the postmortem document following the naming convention (e.g., YYYY-MM-DD_Service_Outage_Payments).

2. Fill in the body of the document using the template. The body should include:
   - A summary of the outage, including the start and end time, the impact on users, and any key performance metrics affected.
   - The root cause of the outage, detailing technical issues, human errors, or other factors that led to the incident.
   - The timeline of events during the outage, including the actions taken to mitigate the issue and restore service.
   - An analysis of the incident, discussing what was learned, any tools or processes that could have prevented the outage, and any areas for improvement in the system or team's response.
   - Recommendations for preventing similar outages in the future, including specific action items, changes to processes or procedures, and any needed resources or training.

3. Assign the reviewers according to the postmortem governance protocol and ensure they are aware that the document is ready for review.

4. Save the document in the designated directory where similar postmortem reports are stored.

5. Send an email to the team notifying them of the completed postmortem and providing a link or attachment to the document for review.

6. Schedule a meeting with the team to discuss the findings, lessons learned, and action items from the postmortem.
