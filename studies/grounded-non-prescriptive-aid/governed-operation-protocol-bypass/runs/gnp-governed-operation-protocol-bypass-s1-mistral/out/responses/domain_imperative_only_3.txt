 1. Consult the postmortem governance protocol to obtain the specifications for the document template, naming convention, directory, and assigned reviewers for the postmortem.

2. Use the specified document template to create the postmortem document, ensuring it includes all required sections such as summary, timeline, root cause analysis, resolution, and follow-up actions.

3. Apply the naming convention from the protocol to name the postmortem document.

4. Save the postmortem document in the directory specified by the protocol, where similar documents are typically stored.

5. Identify the reviewers assigned by the protocol and ensure they are notified about the postmortem document, asking for their feedback and approval.

6. Once all reviewers have provided their feedback and the document has been updated accordingly, the postmortem will be considered finalized and ready for distribution to the wider team.
