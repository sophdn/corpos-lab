 1. Choose the appropriate postmortem template from the designated repository. The template should include sections for incident summary, timeline, root cause analysis, resolution and recovery, and prevention and learning.

2. Title the postmortem document according to the naming convention. This might include the service affected (e.g., 'payments'), the date and time of the outage, and a unique identifier (e.g., '2022-03-15_payments_outage_001').

3. Populate the document with the following information:
   - Briefly summarize the incident, including the start and end times, the service affected, and the impact on users/customers.
   - Detail the timeline of the incident, including the sequence of events, the actions taken, and the responses from the team.
   - Conduct a root cause analysis to determine the underlying issue(s) that led to the outage. Explain how the issue(s) manifested and provide technical details as necessary.
   - Describe the resolution and recovery process, including the steps taken to restore the service and any mitigating actions.
   - Outline prevention measures to be implemented to minimize the likelihood of a similar incident in the future. This could include system updates, process improvements, or staff training.

4. Review the postmortem document for clarity, accuracy, and adherence to the organization's postmortem governance protocol.

5. Assign the designated reviewers (identified as those who were on call during the outage) to review the postmortem document. Request their feedback, suggestions, and any additional information they might provide.

6. Collaborate with the reviewers to incorporate their feedback and finalize the postmortem document.

7. Upload the completed postmortem document to the specified directory where similar documents live.

8. Share the postmortem document with relevant stakeholders, including team members, management, and customers (if appropriate). Ensure that lessons learned from the incident are communicated effectively and action is taken to prevent future occurrences.
