 1. Select the appropriate postmortem document template based on the pre-defined governance protocol.

2. Title the document using the suggested name that clearly describes the nature and impact of the outage (e.g., "Payments Service Outage on DATE - Postmortem Analysis").

3. Fill out the document using the following structure:
   - Executive Summary: Briefly summarize the incident, its impact, and the findings.
   - Timeline of the Incident: Detail the sequence of events that led to the outage and the recovery process.
   - Consequences: List the immediate and potential long-term effects of the outage.
   - Root Cause Analysis: Identify the primary and contributing factors that led to the outage.
   - Resolution and Recovery: Describe the steps taken to resolve the outage and restore normal operations.
   - Post-Mortem Analysis and Findings: Discuss the lessons learned, the actions taken to address the issue, and recommendations for preventing future occurrences.
   - Appendices (optional): Include any relevant logs, screenshots, or other supporting materials.

4. Assign the reviewers as specified by the on-call rotation and the governance protocol.

5. Save the document in the designated directory where similar postmortem analyses are stored.

6. Notify the reviewers of the completed postmortem, provide them with access to the document, and request their feedback and approval.

7. Once the reviewers have approved the postmortem, make any necessary revisions and finalize the document.

8. Distribute the finalized postmortem document to relevant stakeholders, including the team, management, and any customers affected by the outage.

9. Schedule a meeting to review the findings and discuss any necessary actions with the team and management.

10. Archive the completed postmortem analysis for future reference and continuous improvement.
