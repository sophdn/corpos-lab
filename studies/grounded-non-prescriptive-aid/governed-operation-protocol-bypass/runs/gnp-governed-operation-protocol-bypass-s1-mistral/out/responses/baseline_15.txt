 1. Choose the appropriate postmortem document template based on the organization's postmortem governance protocol.

2. Fill out the document using the following sections:
   - Title: Use a clear and descriptive title that includes the service affected (payments), the date and time of the outage, and a brief summary (e.g., "Payments Service Outage - 2022-03-01 23:00 - System Wide Failure").
   - Summary: Provide a concise summary of the outage, including its impact, duration, and key events.
   - Timeline: Detail the sequence of events leading up to, during, and after the outage, including relevant logs and system states.
   - Conclusions: Identify the root cause of the outage, the lessons learned, and any actions taken to prevent similar incidents in the future.
   - Action Items: List any ongoing or planned actions to address the root cause and prevent future occurrences.
   - Appendices: Include any additional relevant data, logs, or other supporting materials.

3. Save the document in the designated directory where similar postmortem documents live, following the naming convention specified by the governance protocol (e.g., yyyymmdd-service-outage-summary).

4. Assign the postmortem document to the designated reviewers, as specified by the on-call team during the outage. Ensure that each reviewer receives a copy of the document and is aware of their responsibility to provide feedback and sign off on the postmortem.

5. Circulate the postmortem document to the appropriate stakeholders, including management, engineering, operations, and support teams, for review and feedback.

6. Once all reviewers have provided their feedback and the document has been updated accordingly, finalize the postmortem and make it publicly available, according to the organization's postmortem publishing policy.

7. Schedule a retrospective meeting to discuss the postmortem findings, review action items, and establish any necessary follow-up actions or improvements.

8. Document any action items and assign responsibilities for their execution, following up as needed to ensure timely completion.

9. Close the loop with all relevant parties, updating them on the progress made in addressing the root cause and implementing any necessary changes to prevent future outages.
