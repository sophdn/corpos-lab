 1. Select the postmortem template that aligns with the organization's governance protocol. This template will provide a consistent structure for the postmortem document.

2. Write the title of the postmortem using the chosen naming convention, which should include a brief summary of the incident, such as "Payments System Outage - February 15th, 2023".

3. Fill in the sections of the template with detailed information about the outage, including:
   - Summary: Brief overview of the incident, its impact, and the timeline.
   - Conclusion: Summary of the root cause, the impact, and the lessons learned.
   - Timeline: A detailed chronology of the events that led to the outage, the actions taken to resolve it, and any follow-up actions.
   - Root Cause Analysis: Detailed explanation of the root cause of the outage, including any contributing factors.
   - Impact Analysis: Description of the business and customer impact, including data on affected users, revenue loss, and any other relevant metrics.
   - Lessons Learned: Identification of lessons learned and recommendations for preventing similar outages in the future.
   - Action Items: A list of specific actions to be taken to address the lessons learned and prevent future outages.
   - Appendices: Include any relevant supporting information, such as system logs, error messages, and screenshots.

4. Save the postmortem in the designated directory where similar documents live.

5. Assign the appropriate reviewers based on the org's governance protocol. These reviewers should be individuals who were on call during the outage, as well as relevant stakeholders such as team leads, managers, and executives.

6. Share the completed postmortem with the assigned reviewers for their feedback and approval.

7. After receiving feedback and making any necessary revisions, finalize the postmortem document and make it publicly available, following the organization's protocol for sharing postmortem documents.
