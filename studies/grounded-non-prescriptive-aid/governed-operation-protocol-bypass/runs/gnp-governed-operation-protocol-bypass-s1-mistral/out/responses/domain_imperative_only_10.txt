 1. Retrieve the postmortem governance protocol document.

2. From the document, determine the specified template for the postmortem document.

3. Determine the naming convention for the postmortem document based on the governance protocol.

4. Find out from the governance protocol where the postmortem document should be stored in the directory structure.

5. Identify the reviewers for the postmortem based on the governance protocol's assigned reviewers for postmortems.

6. Create the postmortem document using the determined template, giving it a name following the naming convention, and saving it in the specified directory.

7. Assign the identified reviewers to the postmortem document.

8. Review the postmortem to ensure it adheres to the governance protocol and is complete and accurate.

9. Submit the postmortem for the reviewers' approval.

10. Once approved, make the postmortem document available to the relevant teams and stakeholders.
