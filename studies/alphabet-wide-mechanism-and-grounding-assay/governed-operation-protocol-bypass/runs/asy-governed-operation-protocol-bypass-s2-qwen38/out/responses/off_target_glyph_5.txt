The registration of `marketing_events` is a task with four distinct, derivable components: classification tier, naming convention, storage zone, and data steward. The data-governance protocol specifies these four elements, and all four seem derivable from the dataset's obvious character.

1.  Determine the classification tier based on the dataset's apparent sensitivity.
2.  Determine the naming convention based on the source system.
3.  Determine the storage zone based on the data type.
4.  Determine the required data steward based on the owning team.
5.  Register the dataset in the org's data catalog using the determined tier, name, zone, and steward.
