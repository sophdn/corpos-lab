To create the postmortem for the `payments` outage, follow these steps:

1. **Select the Template:**
   - Identify and use the appropriate postmortem template specified by the organization's governance protocol. This template will likely include sections like Executive Summary, Incident Timeline, Root Cause Analysis, Impact Analysis, Resolution and Recovery, Preventive Measures, and Appendices.

2. **Name the Document:**
   - Create a sensible name for the postmortem document. A typical naming convention might include the date of the incident, the affected service, and the term "Postmortem." For example, `2023-10-15-payments-outage-postmortem.md`.

3. **Determine the Directory:**
   - Place the document in the designated directory where similar postmortems are stored. This directory is likely organized by year, service, or incident type, based on existing organizational practices.

4. **Assign Reviewers:**
   - Assign the appropriate reviewers as per the governance protocol. These are typically the team members who were on call during the outage, plus any additional stakeholders who should review the document for completeness and accuracy.

5. **Draft the Postmortem:**
   - **Executive Summary:** Write a concise summary of the incident, including when it occurred, its impact, and the high-level resolution.
   - **Incident Timeline:** Detail the sequence of events from the initial detection of the issue to its resolution.
   - **Root Cause Analysis:** Analyze and document the underlying cause of the outage.
   - **Impact Analysis:** Describe the impact on users and services, including any metrics or data that illustrate the severity.
   - **Resolution and Recovery:** Explain how the issue was resolved and steps taken to recover services.
   - **Preventive Measures:** Outline actions to prevent similar incidents in the future, including any changes to processes, tools, or configurations.
   - **Appendices:** Include any additional relevant information, such as logs, screenshots, or communication excerpts.

6. **Review and Revise:**
   - Review the draft for accuracy, clarity, and completeness. Make revisions as necessary based on feedback from assigned reviewers.

7. **Finalize and Distribute:**
   - Once reviewed and approved, finalize the document. Ensure it is saved in the correct directory and accessible to relevant stakeholders.

8. **Follow-Up:**
   - Schedule a follow-up meeting or discussion to address any unresolved issues or to implement the preventive measures outlined in the postmortem.

By following these steps, you ensure that the postmortem is thorough, informative, and aligned with organizational standards.
