To create the postmortem for last night's `payments` outage, I would follow these steps:

1. **Select the Template**: Use the organization's specified postmortem template. This ensures consistency and includes all necessary sections such as summary, root cause analysis, impact, resolution, and preventive measures.

2. **Name the Document**: Choose a descriptive and unique name for the document, such as `2023-10-25-payments-outage-postmortem.md`. This includes the date and relevant keywords to make it easily identifiable.

3. **Determine the Directory**: Place the document in the appropriate directory where similar postmortem documents are stored. This might be something like `/postmortems/payments/`.

4. **Gather Information**: Collect all relevant data from the outage, including logs, alerts, communications, and actions taken. This will form the basis for the factual narrative in the postmortem.

5. **Draft the Document**:
   - **Summary**: Briefly describe what happened, when it occurred, and the systems affected.
   - **Timeline**: Provide a chronological account of events leading up to, during, and following the outage.
   - **Impact Analysis**: Detail the scope and severity of the outage, including affected services and users.
   - **Root Cause Analysis**: Identify the root cause of the outage, supported by evidence from the gathered data.
   - **Resolution**: Describe the steps taken to resolve the issue and restore service.
   - **Preventive Measures**: Outline actions to prevent recurrence, such as code changes, process improvements, or training.

6. **Assign Reviewers**: Identify who must review the postmortem. This typically includes the on-call team members, engineers involved in the resolution, and any stakeholders impacted by the outage.

7. **Review and Edit**: Share the draft with the assigned reviewers for feedback. Make necessary revisions to ensure accuracy and completeness.

8. **Finalize and Publish**: Once reviewed and approved, finalize the document and publish it in the designated directory.

9. **Communicate Findings**: Share the postmortem with the broader team or relevant stakeholders to ensure lessons learned are disseminated and understood.

By following these steps, the postmortem will be thorough, informative, and aligned with organizational standards.
