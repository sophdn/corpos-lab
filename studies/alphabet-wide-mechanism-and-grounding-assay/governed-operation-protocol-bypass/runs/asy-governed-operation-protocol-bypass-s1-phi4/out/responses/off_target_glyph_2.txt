To create the postmortem for the `payments` outage according to your organization's governance protocol, you would follow these steps:

1. **Select the Document Template:**
   - Identify and open the specified template for postmortems. Ensure it aligns with the organization's standards and includes sections for problem description, impact analysis, root cause analysis, corrective actions, and future prevention strategies.

2. **Name the Document:**
   - Use the suggested naming convention to create a clear and descriptive file name for the postmortem. This might include the date of the outage, the service affected (e.g., `payments`), and the nature of the issue (e.g., `payments-outage-YYYYMMDD`).

3. **Determine the Directory:**
   - Navigate to the directory where similar documents are stored. This could be a shared drive or a specific folder within your organization's documentation system. Ensure the directory is accessible to all relevant stakeholders.

4. **Document the Outage Details:**
   - Begin by documenting the specifics of the outage, including the start and end times, the services affected, and the severity of the impact.
   - Provide a clear and concise problem description, outlining what went wrong and the immediate effects on users and systems.

5. **Conduct Impact Analysis:**
   - Assess the extent of the outage's impact on business operations, user experience, and any financial implications.
   - Include any customer communication that was necessary and any immediate mitigation steps taken during the outage.

6. **Perform Root Cause Analysis:**
   - Investigate and document the underlying cause of the outage. This may involve technical logs, system alerts, and collaboration with team members who were involved in the resolution.
   - Use methodologies like the "5 Whys" or fishbone diagrams to systematically identify the root cause.

7. **Outline Corrective Actions Taken:**
   - Detail the steps taken to resolve the outage and restore normal operations. Include any immediate fixes, system rollbacks, or patches applied.
   - Highlight any additional resources or tools that were utilized during the resolution process.

8. **Develop Future Prevention Strategies:**
   - Propose actionable recommendations to prevent similar incidents in the future. This could involve system improvements, process changes, or additional monitoring and alerting.
   - Suggest any training or awareness programs that could benefit the team.

9. **Assign Reviewers:**
   - Assign the document to the designated reviewers as per the governance protocol. These are typically team members who were on call or involved in the incident response.
   - Ensure all reviewers have access to the document and are aware of the expected timeline for their feedback.

10. **Finalize and Distribute:**
    - Incorporate feedback from reviewers and finalize the document.
    - Distribute the completed postmortem to all relevant stakeholders, including the wider team, management, and any other parties specified in the protocol.

By following these steps, you ensure that the postmortem is comprehensive, aligns with organizational standards, and serves as a valuable resource for learning and improvement.
