To create the postmortem for the `payments` outage, I would follow these steps:

1. **Select the Document Template**: Identify the appropriate postmortem template as specified by the organization's governance protocol. This template will guide the structure and content of the postmortem document.

2. **Draft the Document Name**: Use the naming convention outlined in the protocol to create a clear and descriptive name for the postmortem. This name should include relevant details such as the date of the outage and the service affected, e.g., `2023-10-15-payments-outage-postmortem`.

3. **Organize the Directory Structure**: Place the postmortem document in the designated directory where similar documents are stored. This ensures consistency and ease of access for future reference.

4. **Assign Reviewers**: Identify the individuals who must be assigned as reviewers according to the protocol. Typically, these would be the team members who were on call during the outage or those involved in the resolution process.

5. **Gather Information**: Collect all relevant data and insights related to the outage. This includes timelines, incident logs, communication records, root cause analysis, and any mitigations or corrective actions taken.

6. **Structure the Postmortem**: Using the selected template, organize the gathered information into the appropriate sections. Common sections include:
   - **Summary**: A brief overview of the incident, including the impact and duration.
   - **Timeline**: A detailed account of the events leading up to, during, and after the outage.
   - **Root Cause Analysis**: An in-depth examination of what caused the outage.
   - **Impact Assessment**: An evaluation of the outage's impact on customers and internal processes.
   - **Resolution**: Steps taken to resolve the issue.
   - **Lessons Learned**: Key takeaways and recommendations for preventing future occurrences.
   - **Action Items**: Specific tasks or initiatives to address the findings.

7. **Review and Revise**: Ensure the document is clear, accurate, and comprehensive. Make revisions as necessary to improve readability and completeness.

8. **Submit for Review**: Share the draft with the assigned reviewers for feedback. Incorporate their suggestions and finalize the document.

9. **Publish the Postmortem**: Once reviewed and approved, publish the postmortem in the designated directory, ensuring it is accessible to all relevant stakeholders.

10. **Follow-Up**: Monitor the implementation of any action items and track progress on recommendations to ensure continuous improvement.
