To create the postmortem for last night's `payments` outage, I would take the following steps:

1. **Select the Document Template:**
   - Identify and use the specific postmortem template mandated by the organization's governance protocol. This template will likely include sections such as an executive summary, incident timeline, root cause analysis, impact assessment, corrective actions, and preventive measures.

2. **Define the Document Naming Convention:**
   - Follow the prescribed naming convention to ensure consistency and ease of retrieval. The name should clearly reflect the incident, such as `2023-10-XX-payments-outage-postmortem`.

3. **Determine the Directory:**
   - Locate and select the appropriate directory where similar postmortem documents are stored, ensuring it aligns with organizational standards for document organization.

4. **Assign Reviewers:**
   - Assign the reviewers as specified in the governance protocol. This typically includes individuals who were on call, along with key stakeholders or subject matter experts relevant to the incident.

5. **Gather Incident Details:**
   - Collect all necessary information about the outage, including timestamps, affected systems, impacted services, and any initial observations or data points from monitoring systems or logs.

6. **Draft the Postmortem:**
   - Begin drafting the postmortem using the template. Include:
     - **Executive Summary:** A brief overview of what happened, when, and the impact.
     - **Incident Timeline:** A detailed timeline of events leading up to, during, and following the outage.
     - **Root Cause Analysis:** An investigation into the underlying causes of the outage.
     - **Impact Assessment:** An evaluation of how the outage affected services, customers, and operations.
     - **Corrective Actions Taken:** Immediate actions taken to resolve the outage.
     - **Preventive Measures:** Long-term solutions or changes to prevent recurrence.

7. **Review and Validate:**
   - Review the draft for accuracy and completeness. Validate the information with any team members involved in the incident response to ensure all perspectives are considered.

8. **Finalize the Document:**
   - Incorporate feedback from reviewers, make necessary revisions, and ensure the document adheres to the organization's standards and protocols.

9. **Submit for Review:**
   - Submit the finalized postmortem to the assigned reviewers for their approval and feedback.

10. **Publish and Archive:**
    - Once approved, publish the postmortem in the designated directory and ensure it is accessible to relevant team members and stakeholders. Archive it according to organizational policies for future reference.

By following these steps, I ensure that the postmortem is thorough, accurate, and aligned with organizational protocols, facilitating learning and improvement from the incident.
