Creating a postmortem for the `payments` outage involves several steps. Here's a structured approach to ensure that the process adheres to your organization's governance protocols:

1. **Identify the Template:**
   - Review your organization's documentation to find the specific template designated for postmortems. This template will include sections such as incident summary, timeline, root cause analysis, impact, corrective and preventative actions, and lessons learned.

2. **Name the Document:**
   - Follow your organization’s naming convention for postmortems. Typically, this might include the service name, the date, and possibly a unique identifier. For example, `payments_outage_YYYYMMDD_POSTMORTEM`.

3. **Determine the Directory:**
   - Locate the directory where similar postmortem documents are stored. This is often organized by service or department. Ensure you have the right permissions to create or edit files in this directory.

4. **Assign Reviewers:**
   - Identify the individuals who must review the postmortem. This usually includes the on-call team members, team leads, and any other stakeholders directly involved in the incident or affected by it.

5. **Document the Incident:**
   - **Incident Summary:** Provide a brief overview of what happened, including the start and end time of the outage.
   - **Timeline:** Create a detailed timeline of events, from the detection of the issue to its resolution. Include timestamps and key actions taken.
   - **Root Cause Analysis:** Identify and explain the root cause of the outage. Use techniques like the "5 Whys" to drill down to the fundamental issue.
   - **Impact:** Describe the impact of the outage on users, services, and the business. Include any metrics or data that quantify the impact.
   - **Corrective and Preventative Actions:** Outline the immediate actions taken to resolve the issue and any longer-term actions to prevent recurrence.
   - **Lessons Learned:** Summarize the key takeaways from the incident and any changes to processes or systems.

6. **Review and Edit:**
   - Share the draft with the assigned reviewers for feedback. Incorporate their suggestions to ensure accuracy and completeness.

7. **Finalize and Publish:**
   - Once reviewed, finalize the document. Ensure it is clear, concise, and free of errors. Publish it in the designated directory and distribute it to relevant stakeholders.

8. **Follow-Up:**
   - Schedule a follow-up meeting with the team to discuss the postmortem in detail, ensuring everyone understands the lessons learned and the actions to be taken.

By following these steps, you ensure that the postmortem is thorough, actionable, and aligns with your organization's protocols.
