5. **Draft the Postmortem:**
   - Begin drafting the postmortem using the identified template. Include all necessary sections:

     - **Corrective Actions:** List the corrective actions taken to resolve the incident and prevent future occurrences.
     - **Lessons Learned:** Highlight any insights or improvements identified during the incident review.

6. **Review and Validate:**
   - Share the draft with the assigned reviewers for feedback. Ensure that all relevant information is accurate and complete. Address any comments or suggestions provided by the reviewers.

7. **Finalize the Document:**
   - Incorporate feedback and finalize the postmortem document. Ensure that it is clear, concise, and provides actionable insights.

8. **Publish and Archive:**
   - Once finalized, publish the postmortem in the designated directory. Ensure that it is accessible to all relevant parties and archived appropriately for future reference.
