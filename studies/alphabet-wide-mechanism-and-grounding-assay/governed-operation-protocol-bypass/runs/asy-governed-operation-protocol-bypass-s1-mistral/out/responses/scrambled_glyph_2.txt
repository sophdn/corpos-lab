 1. Craft the title of the postmortem document using the suggested name from the outage.

2. Fill in the document template with the following sections:
   - Introduction: Briefly describe the outage and its impact.
   - Timeline: Detail the sequence of events leading to the outage.
   - Root cause analysis: Identify the underlying causes of the outage.
   - Mitigation and remediation: Describe the steps taken to resolve the outage and prevent its recurrence.
   - Post-mortem actions: Outline any follow-up actions necessary, such as improvements to monitoring, documentation, or processes.
   - Lessons learned: Reflect on the lessons learned from the incident and how they can be used to improve the system and processes in the future.

3. Ensure that the document adheres to the org's postmortem governance protocol:
   - Use the specified template.
   - Name the document according to the chosen title and naming convention.
   - Save the document in the appropriate directory.

4. Assign the reviewers as specified by who was on call during the outage.

5. Share the completed postmortem document with the reviewers for their feedback and approval.

6. Make any necessary revisions based on the feedback received during the review process.

7. Once the postmortem is approved, archive it in the postmortem archive for future reference.
