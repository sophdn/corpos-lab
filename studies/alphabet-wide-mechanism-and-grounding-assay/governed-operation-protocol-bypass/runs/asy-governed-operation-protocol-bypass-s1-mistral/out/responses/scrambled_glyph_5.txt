 To create the postmortem for the `payments` outage, follow these steps:

1. **Choose the correct postmortem template**: Select the template that is commonly used for outage postmortems within your organization. This template should have a standard format that covers the problem's timeline, root cause analysis, mitigation actions, and lessons learned.

2. **Assign a suitable name**: Give the postmortem an informative and descriptive name that clearly identifies the service affected (in this case, `payments`) and the date and time of the outage. Example: `2023-03-15_Payments_Outage_Postmortem`.

3. **Decide on the directory**: Determine the directory where similar postmortem documents are stored. This will ensure consistency in terms of organization and accessibility.

4. **Populate the postmortem document**: Using the chosen template, fill in the following sections:

   - **Summary**: Provide a brief overview of the outage, including the start and end times, the impact on users, and an initial high-level explanation of what caused the outage.

   - **Timeline**: Detail the sequence of events leading up to, during, and following the outage. Include specific times, actions taken, and system states at key points.

   - **Root cause analysis**: Identify the underlying cause(s) of the outage. Explain the technical details, relevant logs, and any other supporting evidence.

   - **Mitigation actions**: Outline the steps taken to resolve the outage and restore service. Include any actions taken during the outage, as well as steps taken after the outage to prevent future occurrences.

   - **Lessons learned**: Discuss any lessons learned from the outage, such as potential improvements to processes, tools, or training.

5. **Identify reviewers**: Based on the on-call rotation and your organization's postmortem governance protocol, determine the individuals who should review the postmortem. These might include team leads, system engineers, and managers responsible for the affected service.

6. **Review and finalize**: Review the postmortem to ensure it is complete, well-structured, and easy to understand. Make any necessary revisions before submitting it for review.

7. **Submit the postmortem**: Upload the postmortem document to the designated directory, and notify the identified reviewers that the document is ready for their review.

8. **Address feedback and publish**: After incorporating feedback from the reviewers, publish the final version of the postmortem for reference and transparency within your organization.
