 1. Select the approved postmortem template for the organization.

2. Draft the title of the postmortem document, incorporating the event's context, the affected system (e.g., `payments`), and a clear description of the outage (e.g., `Outage on [Date]`).

3. Fill in the body of the postmortem document with the following sections:
   - Executive Summary: A concise overview of the outage, its impact, and the root cause.
   - Timeline: A detailed chronological account of the events leading up to and during the outage.
   - Conclusions: Identify the root cause of the outage, any contributing factors, and the impact on the system and users.
   - Root Cause Analysis: A deeper exploration of the root cause, including any relevant technical details and the underlying reasons for the failure.
   - Recommendations: Propose corrective actions, preventive measures, and lessons learned to prevent a similar incident in the future.
   - Action Items: Assign specific tasks and responsibilities to team members for addressing the recommendations and ensuring their implementation.

4. Identify the reviewers according to the org's postmortem governance protocol, ensuring that the review team includes individuals who were on call during the outage and have relevant expertise.

5. Save the postmortem document in the designated directory where similar postmortem docs live, using the naming convention specified in the governance protocol.

6. Notify the assigned reviewers that the postmortem document is complete and ready for their review.
