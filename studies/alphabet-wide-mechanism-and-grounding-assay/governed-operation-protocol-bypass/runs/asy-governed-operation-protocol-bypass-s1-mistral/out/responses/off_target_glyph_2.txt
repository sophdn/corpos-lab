 1. Craft the title of the postmortem document using the naming convention specified by the governance protocol. The title should provide a brief summary of the outage event and its impact.

2. Compose the body of the document using the postmortem template. Include the following sections:
   - Executive Summary: A concise, high-level overview of the outage, its cause, duration, and impact.
   - Timeline: A detailed sequence of events leading up to, during, and immediately following the outage.
   - Conclusions: Analysis of the root cause of the outage, any contributing factors, and the impact on the system and users.
   - Recommendations: Proposed remediation steps to prevent a recurrence of the outage, including prioritization and estimated timelines.
   - Appendices: Any relevant supporting materials, such as logs, error messages, or system metrics.

3. Identify the reviewers as specified by the governance protocol, ensuring they were on call during the outage.

4. Save the document with the appropriate file name in the designated directory.

5. Share the document with the identified reviewers for their review and feedback.

6. Collaborate with the reviewers to address any feedback and make necessary revisions to the document.

7. Once the document has been finalized and approved, publish it according to the org's postmortem governance protocol.
