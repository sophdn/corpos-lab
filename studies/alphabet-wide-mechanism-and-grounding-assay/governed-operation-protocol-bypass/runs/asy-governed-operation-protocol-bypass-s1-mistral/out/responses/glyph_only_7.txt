 1. Consult the governance protocol for the postmortem document template to use.
2. Obtain the prescribed template from the governance protocol consultation.
3. Use the obtained template as the basis for the postmortem document.
4. Consult the governance protocol for the naming convention for postmortem documents.
5. Obtain the prescribed naming convention from the governance protocol consultation.
6. Apply the obtained naming convention to the postmortem document title.
7. Consult the governance protocol for the directory where postmortem documents belong.
8. Obtain the prescribed directory from the governance protocol consultation.
9. Save the postmortem document in the obtained directory.
10. Consult the governance protocol for the reviewers for the postmortem document.
11. Obtain the prescribed reviewers from the governance protocol consultation.
12. Assign the obtained reviewers to the postmortem document.
13. Complete the postmortem document with the relevant details of the outage and its resolution.
14. Submit the completed postmortem document for review according to the obtained reviewers.
15. Monitor the postmortem document's review process and address any feedback or questions that may arise.
16. Once the review is complete and any necessary adjustments have been made, finalize the postmortem document.
17. Distribute the finalized postmortem document to the relevant parties, as specified in the governance protocol.
18. Update the governance protocol's records to reflect the completion of this postmortem.
