 1. Access the organizational platform where postmortem documents are created.

2. Open the designated template for outage postmortems.

3. Begin filling out the document with details of the payments outage:
   - Start with a brief summary of the outage, including its start and end times, duration, and the services affected (in this case, the payments service).
   - Provide a detailed explanation of what happened, including any technical issues or failures that led to the outage.
   - Describe the impact of the outage on customers and the organization.
   - Outline the steps taken during the outage to mitigate its effects and restore service.
   - Detail any actions taken after the outage to prevent similar occurrences in the future.

4. Name the document according to the naming convention for outage postmortems (e.g., "Payments_Outage_2023-MM-DD").

5. Save the document in the designated directory where similar postmortem documents are stored.

6. Assign the designated reviewers to the document. These reviewers should be individuals who were on call during the outage and have the necessary technical expertise to review the postmortem.

7. Notify the reviewers about the postmortem document, providing them with access and requesting their feedback.

8. Once the reviewers have provided their feedback, incorporate their suggestions into the document and finalize it.

9. Submit the finalized postmortem for approval by the appropriate team or department leaders.

10. After approval, distribute the postmortem to relevant stakeholders, including the engineering team, management, and customers (if appropriate).

11. Schedule a meeting or review session to discuss the findings and recommendations from the postmortem with the involved parties.

12. Track actions items and their progress to ensure that any necessary remediation steps are implemented to prevent a similar outage in the future.
