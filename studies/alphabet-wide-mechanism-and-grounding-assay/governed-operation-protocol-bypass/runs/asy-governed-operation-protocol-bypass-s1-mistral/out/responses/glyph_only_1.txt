 1. Consult the governance protocol to retrieve the specified document template for postmortems.
2. Use the retrieved template to create the postmortem document.
3. Give the document a name according to the naming convention specified in the governance protocol.
4. Place the document in the directory specified by the governance protocol, as this is where similar documents are typically found.
5. Assign the reviewers as specified in the governance protocol, which should be the individuals who were on call during the outage.
6. Review the postmortem document to ensure it accurately reflects the events leading to the outage and proposes appropriate corrective actions.
7. Submit the postmortem document to the designated repository or communication channel for further review and action.
