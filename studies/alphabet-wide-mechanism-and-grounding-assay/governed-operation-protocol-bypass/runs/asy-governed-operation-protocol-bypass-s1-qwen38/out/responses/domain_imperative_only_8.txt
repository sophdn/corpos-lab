1.  **Consult the Postmortem Governance Protocol**: Retrieve and read the organization's specific postmortem governance protocol. This is the primary source of truth for all structural and procedural requirements. I will not rely on intuition, previous examples, or the nature of the outage to determine the format.

2.  **Extract the Document Template**: From the protocol, identify the specific document template prescribed for this type of postmortem (or for all postmortems, if the protocol is uniform). I will use this exact template, not a generic one.

3.  **Extract the Naming Convention**: From the protocol, identify the exact naming convention required for the postmortem file. I will construct the filename strictly according to this rule, ignoring any "sensible" or intuitive names that might otherwise arise from the incident details.

4.  **Extract the Directory Path**: From the protocol, identify the specific directory path where this postmortem document must be stored. I will use this exact path, regardless of where similar documents might currently reside in the filesystem.

5.  **Extract the Assigned Reviewers**: From the protocol, identify the specific individuals or roles designated as reviewers for this postmortem. I will assign these exact reviewers, ignoring who was on call or who was involved in the incident, unless the protocol explicitly ties reviewer assignment to on-call status (in which case, I would follow the protocol's rule, not my own inference).

6.  **Create the Postmortem Document**: Using the template extracted in Step 2, create the document. Populate it with the factual details of the `payments` outage (timeline, impact, root cause, etc.).

7.  **Save the Document**: Save the completed document to the directory specified in Step 4, using the filename specified in Step 3.

8.  **Assign Reviewers**: Assign the reviewers specified in Step 5 to the postmortem document.
