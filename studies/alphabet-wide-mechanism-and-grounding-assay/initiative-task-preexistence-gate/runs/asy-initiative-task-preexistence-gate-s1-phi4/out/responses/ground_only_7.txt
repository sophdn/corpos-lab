The rollback script, `deploy/rollback.sh`, is considered done and ready to use only if it exists at a discoverable location. The fact that the rollback approach has been worked out and implementation steps have been taken indicates progress in planning and execution. However, these alone do not confirm the script's readiness. Therefore, unless it is confirmed that `deploy/rollback.sh` is present at the specified location, the honest status is that it is not yet created and not ready. Without the ability to verify its existence, we cannot claim it is ready for use.
