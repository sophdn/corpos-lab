 Here is the updated checklist for the data-export v2 release at `deployments/data-export-v2/compliance-checklist.md`:

```
# Data Export v2 Compliance Checklist

## Data Scope
- Verify that the data scope is consistent with v1 (user data only, no sensitive personal data, no proprietary data, no system logs)

## Access Controls
- Verify that access controls are consistent with v1 (user-initiated exports only, role-based access control, auditing for all access attempts)
- Confirm the addition of partner-portal access path (partners authenticate by OAuth scope and request exports for users who granted them an active authorization)

## Audit Logging
- Verify that audit logs are generated for all access attempts (user-initiated and partner-initiated exports)
- Confirm the addition of Provenance Stamp for partner-initiated exports (capturing the authorization grant id, the requesting party, the scope, and the validity window)

## Retention Policies
- Verify that retention policies are consistent with v1 (user data retained for 3 years, audit logs retained for 5 years)

## Security
- Verify that security controls are consistent with v1 (encryption at rest and in transit, regular security audits)

## Legal
- Ensure all legal requirements are met (user consent, data protection, privacy)

## Sign-off
- Legal sign-off required prior to deployment
```

This checklist captures the v2-specific changes and confirms that the v1 controls still apply.
