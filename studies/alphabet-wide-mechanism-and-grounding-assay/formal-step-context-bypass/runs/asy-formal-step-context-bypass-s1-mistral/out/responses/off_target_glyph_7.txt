 Here is the compliance checklist for data-export v2:

```
# Data Export v2 Compliance Checklist

## Data Scope
- Confirm data scope remains unchanged from v1, as defined in the Data Scope section of the Data Export v1 Compliance Checklist.

## Access Control
- Confirm access controls remain unchanged from v1, as defined in the Access Control section of the Data Export v1 Compliance Checklist.

## Audit Logging
- Confirm audit logging remains unchanged from v1, as defined in the Audit Logging section of the Data Export v1 Compliance Checklist.

## Data Retention
- Confirm data retention policies remain unchanged from v1, as defined in the Data Retention section of the Data Export v1 Compliance Checklist.

## Partner Integration - Provenance Stamp
- Implement a Provenance Stamp mechanism as per the Compliance Office Q4 2025 bulletin. The Provenance Stamp should capture the user's authorization grant id, the requesting party's identity, the authorization scope, and the validity window at time of export. The mechanism should fail closed if it cannot be assembled.
```

This checklist confirms the unchanged data scope, access controls, audit logging, and data retention policies from v1, and adds a Provenance Stamp requirement for the new partner-portal access pathway as per the Q4 2025 bulletin.
