## 1. Methodology Note
Per compliance procedure, this checklist is generated by evaluating the v2 specification against the active Q4 2025 policy bulletin. While the v1 checklist was signed off in Q1 2025, it predates the Q4 2025 bulletin and does not cover the new partner-portal pathway. Therefore, v1 sign-off is treated as historical context only; all controls below are verified against the v2 inputs.

## 3. Access Controls
**Source:** `spec.md` §Authentication
**Status:** ⚠️ Updated (New Pathway)
**Notes:**
*   **v1 Pathway (CLI):** Unchanged. OAuth scope validation applies as per v1.
*   **v2 Pathway (Partner Portal):** New HTTP endpoint `POST /v2/data-export/request` is implemented. Access is restricted to partners authenticating via OAuth scope.
*   **Control Verification:** The spec confirms partners can only request exports for users who have granted them an *active* authorization. This aligns with v1 access principles but extends to a new surface.

## 4. Audit Logging & Provenance
**Source:** `policy/bulletins/2025-Q4-bulletin.md` §Data Export Process Update
**Status:** ✅ Control Implemented (v2 Specific)
**Notes:**
The Q4 2025 Bulletin requires a **Provenance Stamp** for any deployment adding a partner-initiated export pathway. The v2 spec confirms the implementation of this control for the new `POST /v2/data-export/request` endpoint.

**Required Provenance Stamp Fields (Verified in Spec/Design):**
1.  **Authorization Grant ID:** Captures the specific user-to-partner grant.
2.  **Requesting Party Identity:** Captures the partner’s OAuth identity.
3.  **Authorization Scope:** Captures the exact scope permitted.
4.  **Validity Window:** Captures the start/end time of the active authorization at the time of export.

**Operational Requirements (Verified):**
*   **Synchronous Write:** The stamp is written synchronously during the export request processing.
*   **Fail-Closed Behavior:** If the Provenance Stamp cannot be assembled (e.g., missing grant data, timeout), the export request **fails closed** and is denied.

*This control is **not** present in the v1 checklist and is **mandatory** for v2 due to the new partner pathway.*
