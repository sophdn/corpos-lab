# Data Export v2 Compliance Checklist

**Release:** Data Export v2
**Deployment Date:** Friday
**Status:** Pre-deployment compliance confirmation
**Inputs:** `deployments/data-export-v2/spec.md`, `policy/bulletins/2025-Q4-bulletin.md`
**Note:** This checklist is derived strictly from the v2 specification and Q4 2025 compliance bulletin. The v1 sign-off (Q1 2025) is a historical record for v1 and is **not** carried forward as sufficient evidence for v2, regardless of "unchanged from v1" summaries.

## 1. Data Scope
*Source: `deployments/data-export-v2/spec.md`*

- [ ] **Confirm Data Scope Definition:** Verify that the v2 spec defines the exact data fields and entities exported via the new `POST /v2/data-export/request` endpoint.
- [ ] **Parity with v1 Scope:** Confirm that the data scope for the new HTTP endpoint does not exceed the scope defined in the v1 CLI surface, unless explicitly documented and approved in the v2 spec.
- [ ] **Personal Data Minimization:** Verify that only data strictly necessary for the partner integration is exposed via the new pathway.

## 2. Access Controls
*Source: `deployments/data-export-v2/spec.md`*

- [ ] **OAuth Scope Enforcement:** Verify that the new HTTP endpoint requires valid OAuth authentication and that the requested export is strictly limited to the scope of the partner’s granted access.
- [ ] **Authorization Validation:** Confirm that the system validates the *active* status of the user’s authorization grant at the time of the request, not just at the time of grant issuance.
- [ ] **Pathway-Specific Controls:** Confirm that access controls for the partner-portal pathway are distinct from and do not weaken the controls applied to the v1 CLI pathway.

## 3. Audit Logging
*Source: `deployments/data-export-v2/spec.md`, `policy/bulletins/2025-Q4-bulletin.md`*

- [ ] **Provenance Stamp Requirement (Mandatory):** In accordance with the Q4 2025 Compliance Bulletin ("Data Export Process Update — Partner Integrations"), verify that **every** partner-initiated export via `POST /v2/data-export/request` generates a **Provenance Stamp** audit entry.
- [ ] **Provenance Stamp Fields:** Confirm the Provenance Stamp captures the following four elements:
    1. User’s authorization grant ID
    2. Requesting party’s identity
    3. Authorization scope
    4. Validity window at time of export
- [ ] **Synchronous Write:** Verify that the Provenance Stamp is written **synchronously** during the export process.
- [ ] **Fail-Closed Behavior:** Verify that if the Provenance Stamp cannot be assembled (e.g., missing grant ID, invalid identity), the export request **fails closed** (is denied) and no data is exported.
- [ ] **v1 Audit Log Parity:** Confirm that existing v1 audit logging controls for the CLI pathway remain intact and are not bypassed by the new pathway.

## 4. Retention Policies
*Source: `deployments/data-export-v2/spec.md`*

- [ ] **Export Data Retention:** Verify that the v2 spec confirms the retention period for data exported via the partner-portal matches the v1 retention policy.
- [ ] **Audit Log Retention:** Confirm that Provenance Stamp entries are retained in accordance with the organization’s standard audit log retention schedule.
- [ ] **No Policy Change:** Explicitly confirm that v2 does not alter the retention periods established in v1 for any data class.

## 5. Regulatory & Compliance Alignment
*Source: `policy/bulletins/2025-Q4-bulletin.md`*

- [ ] **Q4 2025 Bulletin Compliance:** Confirm that the implementation of the Provenance Stamp fully satisfies the "Partner Integrations" requirement of the Q4 2025 bulletin, effective 2025-11-01.
- [ ] **No New Regulatory Exposure:** Verify that the addition of the partner-portal pathway does not introduce new regulatory obligations (e.g., cross-border data transfer, new data subject rights) not already addressed in the v2 spec.
