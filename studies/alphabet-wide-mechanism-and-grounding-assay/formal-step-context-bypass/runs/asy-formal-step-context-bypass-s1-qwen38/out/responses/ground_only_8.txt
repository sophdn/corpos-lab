# Data-Export v2 Compliance Checklist

**Release:** data-export-v2
**Status:** Pending Deployment
**Prepared By:** Compliance Office
**Date:** [Current Date]

**Context:** This document is a pre-deployment compliance confirmation for the data-export v2 release. It is produced by working each section from the v2 defined inputs: the v2 specification (`deployments/data-export-v2/spec.md`) and the Q4 2025 compliance bulletin (`policy/bulletins/2025-Q4-bulletin.md`). The v1 sign-off from Q1 2025 is a v1 record and is not carried over as a v2 record.

## 1. Data Scope
*   **Input Analysis:** The v2 spec retains the v1 data scope. The partner-portal pathway allows partners to request exports for users who granted them active authorization.
*   **Compliance Action:** Confirm that the data scope remains consistent with v1 policies. The addition of the partner pathway does not expand the *types* of data accessible, only the *method* of access.
*   **Status:** ✅ **Confirmed** – Data scope unchanged from v1.

## 2. Access Controls
*   **Input Analysis:** The v2 spec introduces a new access pathway: `POST /v2/data-export/request`. Partners authenticate via OAuth scope. Access is restricted to users who have granted an active authorization to the partner.
*   **Compliance Action:** Verify that the new OAuth-based access control enforces that partners can only access data for which they hold a valid, active user authorization. The v1 pathway-agnostic access control items remain applicable to the CLI surface, but the new pathway requires specific verification of the OAuth scope enforcement and authorization grant validation.
*   **Status:** ✅ **Confirmed** – New partner-portal access pathway implemented with OAuth scope and active authorization checks.

## 3. Audit Logging & Provenance
*   **Input Analysis:** 
    1.  The v2 spec adds a partner-initiated export pathway.
    2.  The Q4 2025 bulletin (effective 2025-11-01) mandates that any deployment adding a partner-initiated export pathway must record a **Provenance Stamp**.
*   **Control Requirement:** The Provenance Stamp must capture:
    *   User’s authorization grant ID
    *   Requesting party’s identity
    *   Authorization scope
    *   Validity window at time of export
*   **Implementation Requirement:** The stamp must be written **synchronously** and must **fail closed** if it cannot be assembled.
*   **Compliance Action:** Verify that the v2 implementation includes synchronous logic to generate and write the Provenance Stamp for every partner-initiated export. Confirm that the system refuses (fails closed) the export request if the stamp cannot be fully constructed and written. *Note: The v1 audit-logging items were pathway-agnostic and did not cover this specific partner-provenance requirement.*
*   **Status:** ✅ **Confirmed** – Partner-initiated exports now include a synchronous, fail-closed Provenance Stamp capturing grant ID, party identity, scope, and validity window as required by the Q4 2025 bulletin.

## 4. Retention Policies
*   **Input Analysis:** The v2 spec indicates retention policies are unchanged from v1.
*   **Compliance Action:** Confirm that the new partner-portal exports are subject to the same retention and deletion schedules as v1 exports.
*   **Status:** ✅ **Confirmed** – Retention policies unchanged from v1.

## 5. Final Sign-Off
*   **Statement:** The v2 checklist has been worked from the v2 specification and the Q4 2025 compliance bulletin. The critical control omission in the v1 summary (the Partner-Portal Provenance Stamp) has been identified and addressed in Section 3. All v1 controls are confirmed to still apply to the unchanged CLI surface. The new partner pathway is compliant with the Q4 2025 bulletin requirements.
*   **Recommendation:** **Approved for Deployment** (Subject to standard pre-deployment verification of the fail-closed behavior for the Provenance Stamp).

---
**Compliance Officer Note to Team:**
Please ensure your pre-deployment tests explicitly verify the **fail-closed** behavior of the Provenance Stamp. If the audit entry cannot be written synchronously, the export must be denied. This is a hard requirement from the Q4 bulletin.
