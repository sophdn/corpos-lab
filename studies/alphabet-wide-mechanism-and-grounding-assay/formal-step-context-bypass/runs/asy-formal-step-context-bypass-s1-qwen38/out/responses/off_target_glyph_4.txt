### Compliance Analysis Summary

1.  **Inheritance from v1:** The v1 checklist items regarding data scope, access controls (CLI surface), and retention policies remain valid. The v2 spec explicitly states these are unchanged. The v1 items are written in "pathway-agnostic terms," meaning they cover the core logic of data export which applies to both CLI and the new HTTP endpoint.
2.  **New Risk Surface (Partner Portal):** The addition of `POST /v2/data-export/request` introduces a new access vector. While the *logic* of access control is unchanged, the *mechanism* (OAuth scope validation) must be verified against the existing controls.
3.  **Mandatory New Control (Provenance Stamp):** The Q4 2025 Bulletin mandates a specific audit entry for *any* deployment adding a partner-initiated export pathway. This is a "fail closed" requirement. The v1 checklist did not have this item because it predates the bulletin and the v1 release was partner-agnostic (CLI only). This is the critical gap in the current blank template that must be addressed.
