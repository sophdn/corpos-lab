## Executive Summary

This compliance checklist has been prepared for the Data-Export v2 release, which incorporates a new partner-portal export pathway. The checklist confirms that all existing controls from the v1 release remain applicable and identifies new controls required by the Q4 2025 compliance bulletin.

---

## Scope and Access Controls

- **Scope:** 
  - Confirmed unchanged from v1. The data scope remains consistent with the previous release.

- **Access Controls:**
  - Partner-portal access path added via `POST /v2/data-export/request`.
  - Partners authenticate via OAuth scope.
  - Exports requested for users with active authorization grants.
  - Existing access controls from v1 apply.

---

## Retention Policies

- Retention policies confirmed unchanged from v1.
- Ensure that retention periods are adhered to for data accessed via the new partner-portal pathway.

---

## Audit Logging

- **General Audit Logging:**
  - Existing audit logging controls from v1 apply, ensuring all data access and export activities are logged.

- **Provenance Stamp Requirement:**
  - **New Control:**
    - Implement a Provenance Stamp for every partner-initiated export.
    - Capture:
      - User's authorization grant ID
      - Requesting party's identity
      - Authorization scope
      - Validity window at the time of export
    - Ensure the Provenance Stamp is written synchronously and fails closed if it cannot be assembled.

---

## Security Measures

- Security measures from v1 remain applicable.
- Ensure that the new partner-portal integration does not introduce new vulnerabilities.

---

## Data Integrity

- Data integrity controls from v1 remain applicable.
- Verify that data integrity is maintained across both the CLI and new HTTP endpoint.

---

## Privacy and Compliance

- Ensure that the new partner-portal pathway adheres to privacy standards.
