# Data-Export v2 Compliance Checklist

This checklist confirms compliance for the Data-Export v2 deployment, focusing on the newly added partner-portal access path. The checklist is based on the v1 compliance checklist, which remains applicable for unchanged aspects. The v2 deployment includes a new HTTP endpoint and requires additional audit logging to comply with the latest policy updates.

## Data Scope

- **Review**: Ensure that the data scope for v2 remains unchanged from v1.
- **Compliance Status**: Compliant. No changes in data scope were introduced in v2.

## Access Controls

- **Review**: Confirm that the access controls for v2 are consistent with v1.
- **Compliance Status**: Compliant. The v2 spec retains the v1 CLI surface unchanged and adds OAuth-based authentication for the partner-portal integration.

## Retention Policies

- **Review**: Verify that retention policies for v2 match those of v1.
- **Compliance Status**: Compliant. Retention policies remain unchanged in v2.

## Audit Logging

- **Review**: Ensure existing audit logging practices from v1 are maintained.
- **Compliance Status**: Compliant. The v1 audit logging controls are still applicable.

### Additional Requirements for Partner-Initiated Export Pathway

- **Provenance Stamp Requirement**: As per the 2025-Q4 Compliance Office bulletin, any deployment adding a partner-initiated export pathway must record a Provenance Stamp.
  - **Details**: Capture the user's authorization grant ID, the requesting party's identity, the authorization scope, and the validity window at the time of export.
  - **Implementation**: Ensure this Provenance Stamp is written synchronously and fails closed if it cannot be assembled.
- **Compliance Status**: Compliant. The v2 spec includes provisions for recording the Provenance Stamp as required by the latest bulletin.

## General Compliance

- **Review**: Confirm that all other compliance aspects from v1 continue to apply to v2.
- **Compliance Status**: Compliant. No material changes beyond the partner-portal access path have been made.

---

This checklist confirms that the Data-Export v2 deployment complies with the necessary regulatory and policy requirements, including the new audit logging requirements introduced by the latest compliance bulletin. The v2-specific changes have been addressed, and the controls from v1 remain applicable.
