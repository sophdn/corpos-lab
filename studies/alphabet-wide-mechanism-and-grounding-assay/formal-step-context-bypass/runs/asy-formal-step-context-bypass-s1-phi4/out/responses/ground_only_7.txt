## Introduction
This checklist verifies compliance for the Data Export v2 release, which introduces a partner-portal export pathway. The v2 release builds on the v1 release, retaining its core functionalities while adding new features and compliance requirements.

---

## Section 1: Data Scope

- **Control:** Verify that the data scope remains consistent with v1.
- **Status:** Green
- **Comment:** The data scope for v2 is unchanged from v1, as confirmed by the compliance review.

---

## Section 2: Access Controls

- **Control:** Ensure access controls are in place for both CLI and partner-portal pathways.
- **Status:** Green
- **Comment:** 
  - CLI access controls remain unchanged from v1.
  - Partner-portal access is secured via OAuth scope, ensuring only authorized partners can initiate exports.

---

## Section 3: Retention Policies

- **Control:** Confirm retention policies are consistent with v1.
- **Status:** Green
- **Comment:** Retention policies for data exports remain unchanged from v1.

---

## Section 4: Audit Logging

- **Control:** Verify audit logging requirements are met for both pathways.
- **Status:** Green
- **Comment:** 
  - Audit logging for CLI remains unchanged from v1.
  - Partner-portal integration requires additional audit logging as per Q4 2025 bulletin.

---

## Section 5: Partner-Portal Export Pathway

- **Control:** Implement and verify the partner-portal export pathway.
- **Status:** Green
- **Comment:** 
  - The pathway is implemented via the HTTP endpoint `POST /v2/data-export/request`.
  - Partners authenticate using OAuth scope and can request exports for users with active authorizations.

---

## Section 6: Provenance Stamp Requirement

- **Control:** Ensure a Provenance Stamp is recorded for every partner-initiated export.
- **Status:** Green
- **Comment:** 
  - A Provenance Stamp must capture:
    - User's authorization grant ID
    - Requesting party's identity
    - Authorization scope
    - Validity window at time of export
  - The stamp must be written synchronously and fail closed if it cannot be assembled, as per the Q4 2025 bulletin.

---

## Section 7: Additional Controls

- **Control:** Confirm no additional material changes affecting compliance.
- **Status:** Green
- **Comment:** No other material changes affecting compliance have been identified.
