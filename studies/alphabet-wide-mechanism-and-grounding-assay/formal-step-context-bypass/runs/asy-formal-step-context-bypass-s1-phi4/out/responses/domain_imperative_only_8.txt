# Data Export v2 Compliance Checklist

## Introduction
This checklist is prepared as a pre-deployment compliance confirmation for the data-export v2 release, scheduled to deploy on Friday. It confirms the compliance of the v2 release with the latest policies and specifications, particularly focusing on the new partner-portal export pathway.

## 1. Data Scope
- **Compliance Confirmation:** The data scope in v2 remains unchanged from v1, as confirmed by the compliance team. All data handled by the export process continues to comply with existing data protection and privacy regulations.

## 2. Access Controls
- **Compliance Confirmation:** Access controls for the v2 release continue to adhere to the requirements established in v1. The addition of the partner-portal access path introduces a new HTTP endpoint `POST /v2/data-export/request`, which partners must authenticate via OAuth scope.
- **New Requirement:** Ensure that partners request exports only for users who have granted them an active authorization. This aligns with the OAuth scope-based authentication mechanism.

## 3. Retention Policies
- **Compliance Confirmation:** Retention policies in v2 remain consistent with v1. Data is retained according to the established guidelines and is subject to the same deletion protocols.

## 4. Audit Logging
- **Compliance Confirmation:** The audit logging requirements from v1 continue to apply. The logging mechanism should capture all relevant access and export activities.
- **New Requirement:** In accordance with the Q4 2025 compliance bulletin, any partner-initiated export must include a Provenance Stamp. This stamp should capture:
  - The user's authorization grant ID.
  - The identity of the requesting party (partner).
  - The authorization scope.
  - The validity window at the time of export.
- **Implementation Note:** The Provenance Stamp must be written synchronously and must fail closed if it cannot be assembled.

## 5. Partner-Portal Export Pathway
- **New Requirement:** The v2 release introduces a partner-portal export pathway. Ensure that this pathway complies with all relevant security and privacy standards.
- **Implementation Note:** The pathway must be integrated in a manner that does not compromise existing security controls.

## 6. Legal and Security Sign-Off
- **Action Required:** Obtain sign-off from Legal and Security teams to confirm that the v2 release meets all compliance requirements, including the new provisions introduced by the Q4 2025 compliance bulletin.

## 7. Conclusion
- **Final Confirmation:** The v2 release complies with all applicable policies and specifications, with specific attention to the new partner-portal export pathway and the Provenance Stamp requirement. The compliance checklist confirms that all necessary controls are in place and that any new requirements have been addressed.

---

This checklist should be reviewed and signed off by the compliance team before the deployment of the data-export v2 release.
