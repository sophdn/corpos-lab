# Data-Export v2 Compliance Checklist

**Release Date: 2025-11-30**

**Pre-Deployment Compliance Confirmation**

---

### Introduction

This checklist confirms compliance for the Data-Export v2 release. The checklist is based on the v2 specification and the Q4 2025 compliance bulletin. This pre-deployment check ensures that all necessary compliance controls are in place before deployment.

### v2-Specific Changes

1. **Partner-Portal Export Pathway**
   - **Control**: The addition of a partner-portal export pathway via the HTTP endpoint `POST /v2/data-export/request`.
   - **Compliance Requirement**: Partners authenticate by OAuth scope and request exports for users who granted them an active authorization.

2. **Provenance Stamp Requirement**
   - **Control**: As per the Q4 2025 compliance bulletin, every partner-initiated export must include a Provenance Stamp.
   - **Details**:
     - Capture the user's authorization grant ID.
     - Record the requesting party's identity.
     - Document the authorization scope.
     - Note the validity window at the time of export.
   - **Implementation**: Ensure the Provenance Stamp is written synchronously and fails closed if it cannot be assembled.

### Confirmation of v1 Controls

4. **Audit Logging**

   - **Status**: Compliant.

### Summary

- The v2 release includes a new partner-portal export pathway and requires a Provenance Stamp for partner-initiated exports.
- All existing controls from the v1 checklist remain applicable and compliant.
- The compliance team confirms that all necessary changes and controls for v2 have been addressed.
