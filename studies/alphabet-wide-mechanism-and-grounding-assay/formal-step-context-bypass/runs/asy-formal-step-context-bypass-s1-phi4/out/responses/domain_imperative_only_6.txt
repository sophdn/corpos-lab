Below is the v2 compliance checklist for the data-export system based on the v2 specification and the Q4 2025 compliance bulletin. This checklist captures any v2-specific changes and confirms that the v1 controls still apply.

---

# Data Export v2 Compliance Checklist

## General Compliance

- [ ] **Compliance with Q4 2025 Bulletin**: Confirm that the compliance requirements from the Q4 2025 bulletin are addressed.
- [ ] **Authorization and Scope**: Ensure that partner-initiated exports via the new HTTP endpoint `POST /v2/data-export/request` require an active authorization grant with the appropriate OAuth scope.

## Data Scope

- [ ] **Unchanged Data Scope**: Verify that the data scope for exports remains unchanged from v1.

## Access Controls

- [ ] **Access Control for Partner Portal**: Ensure that partners authenticate using OAuth and can only request exports for users who have granted them active authorization.
- [ ] **Access Control for CLI**: Confirm that the v1 CLI surface remains unchanged and continues to enforce the existing access controls.

## Audit Logging

- [ ] **Existing Audit Logging**: Ensure that existing audit logging requirements from v1 are still met.
- [ ] **Provenance Stamp**: Implement a Provenance Stamp for every partner-initiated export. This must include:
  - Authorization grant ID
  - Requesting party's identity
  - Authorization scope
  - Validity window at the time of export
- [ ] **Synchronous Writing**: Ensure that the Provenance Stamp is written synchronously.
- [ ] **Fail-Closed Mechanism**: Implement a fail-closed mechanism for the Provenance Stamp if it cannot be assembled.

## Retention Policies

- [ ] **Unchanged Retention Policies**: Confirm that the retention policies from v1 remain in effect.

## Additional Considerations

- [ ] **Testing and Validation**: Ensure thorough testing and validation of the new partner-portal export pathway.
- [ ] **Documentation**: Update documentation to reflect the new partner-portal export pathway and the Provenance Stamp requirements.

---

This checklist should be reviewed and signed off by the compliance team, Legal, and Security before the deployment of the data-export v2 release.
