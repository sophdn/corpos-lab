# Data-Export v2 Compliance Checklist

## Introduction
This checklist is prepared as a pre-deployment compliance confirmation for the data-export v2 release. It is based on the v2 specification and the Q4 2025 compliance bulletin. The checklist confirms that the v2 release adheres to the necessary compliance requirements, including any new controls introduced in v2.

## Compliance Inputs
- **v2 Specification:** `deployments/data-export-v2/spec.md`
- **Q4 2025 Compliance Bulletin:** `policy/bulletins/2025-Q4-bulletin.md`

## Checklist

### 1. Data Scope
- **Status:** Unchanged from v1
- **Details:** The data scope remains consistent with v1, ensuring no unauthorized data is exposed.

### 2. Access Controls
- **Status:** Unchanged from v1
- **Details:** Access controls are maintained as per v1, ensuring only authorized entities can initiate data exports.

### 3. Retention Policies
- **Status:** Unchanged from v1
- **Details:** Retention policies are consistent with v1, ensuring data is stored and deleted according to policy requirements.

### 4. Partner-Portal Export Pathway
- **Status:** Added in v2
- **Details:** 
  - A new HTTP endpoint `POST /v2/data-export/request` is introduced for partner-portal integration.
  - Partners authenticate using OAuth scope and request exports for users with active authorizations.

### 5. Provenance Stamp Requirement
- **Status:** New requirement in v2
- **Details:** 
  - As per the Q4 2025 bulletin, any deployment adding a partner-initiated export pathway must record a Provenance Stamp.
  - The Provenance Stamp must capture:
    - User's authorization grant ID
    - Requesting party's identity
    - Authorization scope
    - Validity window at the time of export
  - The Provenance Stamp must be written synchronously and must fail closed if it cannot be assembled.

### 6. Audit Logging
- **Status:** Unchanged from v1
- **Details:** Audit logging requirements remain as per v1, ensuring all necessary actions are logged.

### 7. Synchronous Failing Closed
- **Status:** New requirement in v2
- **Details:** 
  - The Provenance Stamp must be written synchronously and must fail closed if it cannot be assembled, ensuring data integrity and compliance.

## Sign-Off
This checklist confirms that the data-export v2 release complies with all relevant specifications and compliance requirements as of the date of this review. Any changes specific to v2 have been captured, and v1 controls remain applicable where unchanged.
