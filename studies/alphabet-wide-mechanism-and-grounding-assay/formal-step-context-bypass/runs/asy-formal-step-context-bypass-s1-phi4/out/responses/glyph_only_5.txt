#### Access Controls

#### Retention Policies
- **Status:** Unchanged from v1

#### Audit Logging
- **Status:** Unchanged, with new requirements
- **Notes:** 
  - The existing audit logging controls from v1 apply.
  - A new requirement has been introduced by the Compliance Office Q4 2025 bulletin: any deployment adding a partner-initiated export pathway must record an additional audit entry — a Provenance Stamp. This entry should capture:
    - User's authorization grant ID
    - Requesting party's identity
    - Authorization scope
    - Validity window at time of export
  - This Provenance Stamp must be written synchronously and should fail closed if it cannot be assembled.
