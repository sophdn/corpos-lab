# Data-Export v2 Compliance Checklist

## Introduction
This checklist is designed to ensure that the data-export v2 release complies with all relevant policies and specifications. It is a pre-deployment compliance confirmation for the v2 release scheduled for Friday. The checklist addresses both the unchanged aspects from v1 and the new requirements introduced in v2, particularly focusing on the partner-portal export pathway.

## Data Scope
- **Control:** The data scope remains unchanged from v1.
- **Compliance Confirmation:** Confirmed that the data scope in v2 aligns with v1 specifications.

## Access Controls
- **Control:** Access controls remain unchanged from v1.
- **Compliance Confirmation:** Confirmed that access controls for v2, including the new partner-portal pathway, adhere to v1 standards.
- **New Requirement:** Ensure partners authenticate via OAuth scope and request exports only for users who have granted them an active authorization.

## Retention Policies
- **Control:** Retention policies remain unchanged from v1.
- **Compliance Confirmation:** Confirmed that retention policies in v2 continue to comply with v1 standards.

## Audit Logging
- **Control:** Audit logging requirements remain unchanged from v1.
- **Compliance Confirmation:** Confirmed that audit logging for v2, including the partner-portal pathway, meets v1 standards.
- **New Requirement:** Implement Provenance Stamp for partner-initiated exports. This includes:
  - Capturing the user's authorization grant id.
  - Recording the requesting party's identity.
  - Logging the authorization scope.
  - Documenting the validity window at the time of export.
  - Ensuring the Provenance Stamp is written synchronously and fails closed if it cannot be assembled.

## Partner-Portal Export Pathway
- **Control:** Newly added in v2.
- **Compliance Confirmation:** Confirmed that the HTTP endpoint `POST /v2/data-export/request` for partner-portal integration adheres to the specified authentication and authorization requirements.
- **New Requirement:** Ensure compliance with Q4 2025 bulletin regarding the Provenance Stamp for partner-initiated exports.

## Summary
- **Unchanged Controls:** Data scope, access controls, and retention policies from v1 are confirmed to apply to v2.
- **New Controls:** Provenance Stamp requirement for partner-initiated exports as per Q4 2025 bulletin.
- **Overall Compliance:** All specified controls are met, and the v2 release is ready for deployment with the compliance team's confirmation.

---

This checklist captures the necessary v2-specific changes and confirms that v1 controls still apply. It is ready for review and sign-off by the appropriate teams.
