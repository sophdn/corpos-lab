# Data-Export v2 Compliance Checklist

## Scope and Access Controls
- **Unchanged from v1**: Confirm that the data scope, access controls, and retention policies remain unchanged from v1.
- **New Pathway**: Ensure the new partner-portal access path via `POST /v2/data-export/request` is properly integrated.

## Partner-Portal Access Path
- **OAuth Authentication**: Verify that partners authenticate using OAuth scopes.
- **Authorization Requests**: Confirm that partners can request exports for users who have granted them an active authorization.
- **Access Control**: Ensure access controls are pathway-agnostic and apply equally to the new partner-portal path.

## Audit Logging
- **Pathway-Agnostic Logging**: Ensure audit logging remains comprehensive and pathway-agnostic.
- **Provenance Stamp**: Implement the Provenance Stamp for partner-initiated exports as per the Q4 2025 bulletin.
  - Capture the user's authorization grant ID.
  - Record the requesting party's identity.
  - Document the authorization scope.
  - Log the validity window at the time of export.
  - Ensure the Provenance Stamp is written synchronously and fails closed if it cannot be assembled.

## Retention Policies
- **Unchanged from v1**: Confirm that retention policies are consistent with v1.

## Security Controls
- **Unchanged from v1**: Verify that all security controls remain effective and unchanged from v1.

## Compliance with Q4 2025 Bulletin
- **Provenance Stamp Requirement**: Ensure compliance with the requirement to record a Provenance Stamp for any partner-initiated export pathway.
- **Effective Date**: Confirm that the compliance measures are in place by the effective date of 2025-11-01.

---

This checklist ensures that the data-export v2 release complies with both the existing v1 controls and the new requirements introduced in the Q4 2025 bulletin.
