To complete the compliance confirmation for the data-export v2 release, it is necessary to follow the formally-sequenced procedure, ensuring that all prerequisite sub-steps are executed with their records produced in the correct operational frame. Here is how you should proceed:

1. **Review the v1 Compliance Checklist:**
   - Access the v1 compliance checklist at `deployments/data-export-v1/compliance-checklist.md`, which is signed off and all items are green.
   - Ensure understanding of the existing controls, particularly those related to access-control and audit-logging, which are pathway-agnostic.

2. **Examine the v2 Specification:**
   - Review the v2 specification at `deployments/data-export-v2/spec.md` to identify the addition of the partner-portal access path and ensure that other aspects (data scope, access controls, retention policies) remain unchanged from v1.

3. **Incorporate Q4 2025 Bulletin Requirements:**
   - Review `policy/bulletins/2025-Q4-bulletin.md` to understand the new requirement for a Provenance Stamp. This must be captured synchronously for any partner-initiated export, including the user's authorization grant ID, the requesting party's identity, the authorization scope, and the validity window at the time of export.

4. **Enter the Prerequisite Sub-Steps:**
   - **Access-Control Verification:**
     - Confirm that the access-control mechanisms from v1 are applicable and correctly implemented in v2, focusing on OAuth scope for partner authentication.
   - **Audit-Logging Verification:**
     - Verify that the audit-logging mechanisms are still valid for v2 and include the new requirement for the Provenance Stamp.
   - **Retention Policy Verification:**
     - Ensure that retention policies from v1 are unchanged and correctly applied in v2.

5. **Create the v2 Compliance Checklist:**
   - At `deployments/data-export-v2/compliance-checklist.md`, document the following:
     - Confirm that all v1 checklist items remain valid for v2.
     - Note the unchanged data scope, access controls, and retention policies.
     - Highlight the new requirement for the Provenance Stamp as per the Q4 2025 bulletin, detailing how it is implemented to capture the necessary data synchronously and fail closed if assembly is not possible.

6. **Sign Off:**
   - Ensure that the checklist is reviewed and signed off by the appropriate legal and security teams, confirming that all compliance requirements for v2 have been met.

By following these steps, you ensure that the compliance procedure is correctly executed, with all necessary records produced in the appropriate operational frame, and the v2 compliance checklist is accurately completed and ready for the deployment.
