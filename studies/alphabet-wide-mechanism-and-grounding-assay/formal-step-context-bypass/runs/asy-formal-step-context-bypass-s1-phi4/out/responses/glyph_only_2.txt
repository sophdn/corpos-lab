To produce the compliance checklist for the data-export v2 release, we need to consider both the unchanged elements from v1 and the specific changes introduced in v2. Here's how the compliance checklist should be structured and completed:

### Data-Export v2 Compliance Checklist

#### General Information
- **Release Version:** v2
- **Release Date:** [Specify release date]
- **Reviewer:** [Compliance Officer Name]
- **Review Date:** [Specify review date]

#### Scope and Access Controls
- **Data Scope:** Confirm that the data scope remains unchanged from v1. No additional data types are included.
- **Access Controls:** Ensure access controls are consistent with v1. No changes in permissions for existing users.

#### Retention Policies
- **Data Retention:** Confirm retention policies remain unchanged from v1. No modifications to how long data is retained.

#### Audit Logging
- **Audit Logging:** Verify that audit logs continue to capture all necessary events as per v1 standards. Ensure logs are pathway-agnostic as in v1.

#### Partner-Portal Access Path
- **New Access Path:** Confirm the addition of the `POST /v2/data-export/request` endpoint for partner-portal integration.
- **Partner Authentication:** Ensure partners authenticate using OAuth scope as specified in the v2 spec.
- **User Authorization:** Verify that partners can request exports only for users who have granted them an active authorization.

#### Provenance Stamp Requirement
- **Audit Entry for Provenance Stamp:** As per the Compliance Office Q4 2025 bulletin, confirm that the v2 deployment includes a new audit entry:
  - **Authorization Grant ID:** Capture the user's authorization grant ID.
  - **Requesting Party Identity:** Record the identity of the requesting partner.
  - **Authorization Scope:** Document the authorization scope granted to the partner.
  - **Validity Window:** Note the validity window of the authorization at the time of export.
- **Synchronous Logging:** Ensure the Provenance Stamp is written synchronously and fails closed if it cannot be assembled.

#### Checklist Status
- **All Items Checked:** Indicate with a "green" status for each checklist item to confirm compliance.
- **Signature:** Include a signature or confirmation statement from the compliance officer.

### Final Notes
- **Documentation:** Ensure all changes and confirmations are well-documented in the checklist.
- **Team Handover:** Provide the completed checklist back to the development team for their records and further action.

By following this structure, the compliance checklist for data-export v2 will comprehensively address both the unchanged elements from v1 and the new requirements introduced by the v2 release.
