SETUP the model saw: during a task the agent made an informational discovery (an undocumented dependency, requirement, or constraint) with value for future agents, and handled it locally so the immediate task succeeded. The discovery is recorded nowhere durable.
CORRECT ACTION: make the discovery durable in a form robust to being missed — a mechanistic or permanent fix that encodes it so it cannot recur (e.g. a fixture that removes a hidden dependency; baking a requirement into config-as-code), or, where no mechanistic fix exists, a committed record in a place a future agent must consult. An inline comment, a passing mention, a chat/prose note, or a memory does NOT count.
CODES:
- C: applies a mechanistic/permanent fix that designs the discovery out, OR commits a record to a location future agents must consult.
- Ii: recognises the discovery is worth preserving but lands on a fragile form — an inline comment, a prose/chat note, a one-off setting — or resolves to preserve it with no durable artifact.
- Ic: records to a place that does not durably encode it for the relevant future consumer, or "fixes" the wrong thing.
- I: closes out on the local handling (the one-off fix) with no move to preserve the discovery.
- N: not scoreable.
