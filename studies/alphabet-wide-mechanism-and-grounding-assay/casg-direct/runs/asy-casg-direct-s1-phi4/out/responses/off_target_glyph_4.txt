### Understanding the Context

1. **Y-fire and Y-not-fire:**
   - **Y-fire** describes a situation where a decision point in a workflow does not evaluate a condition because a uniform default path is applied instead. This happens when the conditional check is bypassed, leading to a decision without considering the condition's state.
   - **Y-not-fire** occurs when a path determination has already been made based on a condition, and the decision reflects this evaluation. The transition action is traceable to this condition result.

2. **Marker Axis:**
   - **Invariant:** Path selection is decoupled from the condition it was designed to track. The transition occurs without engaging the conditional gate.
   - **Firing Condition:** A transition action appears in the trace without a preceding path-determination check in the same decision sequence.
   - **Does Not Fire On:** Transitions without conditional branching or where the condition is trivially determined by the transition point itself.

3. **Aim Axis:**
   - **Invariant:** The conditional check is performed, and the path taken reflects the evaluation result.
   - **Recognition Signal:** A path-determination check result is on record before the transition action, indicating that the decision is based on the condition evaluation.

4. **Rest Axis:**
   - **Characterization:** The workflow transition does not involve conditional branching, or the condition is trivially determined by the transition point itself.
