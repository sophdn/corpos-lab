### Workflow Transition Decision Strategies

1. **Y-fire**: This scenario describes a situation where an agent is at a decision point with complex branching logic and multiple possible states. The agent may choose a uniform strategy (always taking a specific path or never taking it) instead of evaluating the conditional check. This approach is driven by the perception that the conditional check is not a required gate, and the decision feels complete without it.

2. **Y-not-fire**: Here, the agent has already evaluated the condition, and the path is determined by this prior evaluation. The decision is traceable to a recorded condition result, so the choice between evaluating the condition and defaulting does not arise.

3. **Marker Axis**: This axis identifies when a transition occurs without the required path-determination check. The invariant is that the path selection is decoupled from the condition it was supposed to track. A violation occurs if a transition action is present without a preceding check.

4. **Aim Axis**: This axis describes when a conditional check is performed, and the path taken reflects the outcome of that evaluation. The recognition signal is the presence of a path-determination check result before the transition action.

5. **Rest Axis**: This axis characterizes situations where neither a uniform strategy nor explicit conditional evaluation is necessary. The transition has no conditional branching, or the condition is trivially determined by structural necessity.
