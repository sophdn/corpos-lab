**Y-fire:** This state occurs when an agent defaults to a uniform strategy at a workflow transition point, taking a specific path without evaluating a conditional check that would determine the appropriate path. The condition is not perceived as a required gate, and the decision is based on the uniform default, not on the verified state.

**Y-not-fire:** This state occurs when the agent has already evaluated the condition and determined the correct path at a workflow transition point. The decision between evaluating the condition and defaulting does not arise because the evaluation is complete. The choice of which path to take is traceable to the condition result already established.

**Y-neutral:** This state occurs when the workflow transition has no conditional branching or the condition is unambiguously determined by structural necessity at the transition point. The choice between evaluating the condition and defaulting does not arise.
