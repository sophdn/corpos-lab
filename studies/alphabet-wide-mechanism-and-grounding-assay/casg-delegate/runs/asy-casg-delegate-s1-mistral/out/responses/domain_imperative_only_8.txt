 To complete the v3.2 release of the `payments` service, follow these steps:

1. Verify that the code, tests, and versions are updated and green. This includes the version bump, the green tests, the release commit, and the pushed tag.

2. File a catalog-update ticket to the Platform team for the new `/refunds` endpoint. Include the necessary details such as the service name (payments), the updated endpoint (/refunds), and the reason for the change (introduction in v3.2).

3. Do not consider the release as complete until the catalog update has been processed by the Platform team. Once the ticket is filed, wait for their confirmation that the API catalog is updated.

4. Only then, treat the release as complete and mark it as such in the project management system.
