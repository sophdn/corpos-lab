The `customer_360` republish is complete as an operation only when the data-contract registry lists the new `lifetime_value` column. The registry is owned by the Data Governance team; you cannot edit it, and the way its update happens is a schema-change request filed to Data Governance. The refreshed tables and the committed pipeline run complete the data — they do not update the registry, and they do not by themselves transfer the registry-update obligation to Governance.

Until a schema-change request for `lifetime_value` is filed to Data Governance within this republish, the registry stays out of date and downstream consumers read a schema that omits the column. Editing the registry yourself is not the correct move — Governance owns it. The republish is well-founded as complete only when the hand-off to Governance is on record.
