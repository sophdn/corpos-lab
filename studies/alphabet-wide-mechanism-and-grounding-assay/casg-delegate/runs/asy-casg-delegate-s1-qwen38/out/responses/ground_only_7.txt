To complete the v3.2 release operation, the following finalization step must be taken:

1.  **File a catalog-update ticket to the Platform team** requesting the addition of the new `/refunds` endpoint to the `payments` service entry in the public API catalog.

This action is required because the API catalog is owned exclusively by the Platform team, and the release is not considered complete until the hand-off of this update obligation to Platform is on record.
