The scenario describes a situation where the `RELEASES.md` file is at its maximum capacity (20 entries) and a new entry (v4.0) needs to be added. Adding the new entry directly would bring the count to 21, exceeding the specified ceiling of 20. According to the **Y-fire** condition, this triggers an architectural response (in this case, archiving/rotating) before the addition can proceed.

Therefore, the steps to add the v4.0 entry while respecting the structural constraint are:

1.  **Identify the oldest entry:** Locate the entry for the release that is currently the 20th most recent (the bottom-most entry in the list of the 20 most recent).
2.  **Archive the oldest entry:** Move this oldest entry from `RELEASES.md` to `RELEASES_ARCHIVE.md`. This reduces the entry count in `RELEASES.md` from 20 to 19, creating headroom.
3.  **Add the new entry:** Insert the entry for v4.0 into `RELEASES.md` as the most recent release. This brings the entry count back to 20, which is within the defined ceiling.

This sequence (Archive -> Add) ensures the architectural response (rotation/archiving) is applied before the direct addition, preventing the artifact from exceeding its size ceiling.
