The release of `payments` v3.2 is technically complete on the engineering side (version bumped, tests green, commit and tag pushed). To fully finalize the release according to org policy, the following steps must be taken:

1. **File a Catalog-Update Ticket with the Platform Team**:
   - Since the API catalog is owned exclusively by the Platform team, you cannot directly update it.
   - Create a ticket requesting the addition of the new `/refunds` endpoint to the `payments` service entry in the public API catalog.
   - Include details such as:
     - Service name: `payments`
     - Version: v3.2
     - New endpoint: `/refunds`
     - Method, request/response schema (if available), and any relevant documentation links.

2. **Verify Ticket Submission and Track Resolution**:
   - Confirm the ticket is properly assigned and visible to the Platform team.
   - Monitor the ticket until the Platform team confirms the catalog has been updated.

3. **Post-Release Validation (Optional but Recommended)**:
   - Once the Platform team confirms the catalog update, optionally verify that the new endpoint appears correctly in the public catalog.
   - Communicate to stakeholders (e.g., consumers of the `payments` API) that v3.2 is live and the new `/refunds` endpoint is now documented in the catalog.

These steps ensure compliance with org policy and complete the release finalization process.
