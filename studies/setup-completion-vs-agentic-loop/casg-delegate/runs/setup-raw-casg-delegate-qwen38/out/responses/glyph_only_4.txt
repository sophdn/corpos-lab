1.  **Verify Primary Artifact Completion:** Confirm that the version bump, green tests, and the push of the release commit and tag `v3.2` to the `payments` repository are successfully recorded in the trace.
2.  **Execute Delegation Action (The Critical Step):** File a **catalog-update ticket** to the **Platform team**. This ticket must explicitly request the addition of the new `/refunds` endpoint to the `payments` service entry in the public API catalog.

**Why this order matters:**
If you close the operation after step 1 without step 2, you trigger the **Violation signal**: the primary artifact is updated, but the companion update obligation is outstanding and the assigned role (Platform) has not been notified. By filing the ticket (step 2), you satisfy the **Aim axis** invariant, ensuring the hand-off is on record before the operation closes.
