**When this applies:** You are completing an operation on a primary artifact, and a companion artifact exists whose validity depends on reflecting the current state of the primary artifact set. A protocol assigns the companion artifact's update to another role — you do not hold direct authority to update it yourself. Completing the primary artifact action will feel like the end of the operation. You must decide whether to file the required hand-off to the assigned role before closing, or to treat the primary artifact action as the terminal step and close without filing.

**What to do:**
1. Before you close the operation, file the required hand-off to the role the protocol assigns the companion update to. Do this within the same operation scope as the primary artifact action.
2. Do not treat the primary artifact action as terminal while the hand-off is unfiled. Closing with the primary artifact updated and no hand-off on record leaves the companion update obligation outstanding and the assigned role un-notified.
3. Do not perform the companion update yourself — it is assigned to another role, and doing it directly bypasses the protocol. Your obligation here is the hand-off, not the update.
4. You have done this correctly when the hand-off to the assigned role is on record in the same operation, before it closes.

**When this does NOT apply:**
- No companion artifact exists whose validity depends on the primary artifact set state, or the operation creates no companion update obligation. Then there is no hand-off to file.
- You hold direct authority to update the companion artifact yourself (no protocol assigns it elsewhere). Then your obligation is the direct update, not a hand-off.

**Example:** You mark a work item complete in a tracked workflow. A companion record must be updated to reflect it, but by protocol only another role edits that record. You update the work item and close, filing no hand-off to that role. The obligation is left outstanding. Correct action is to file the hand-off to the assigned role in the same operation, before closing.
