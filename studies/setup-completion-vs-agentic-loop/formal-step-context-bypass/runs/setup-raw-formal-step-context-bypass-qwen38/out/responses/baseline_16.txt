**Reference:** Q4 2025 Bulletin, "Data Export Process Update — Partner Integrations"

### Executive Summary
The data-export v2 release adds a partner-portal access path (`POST /v2/data-export/request`) to the existing CLI surface. The compliance team has confirmed that data scope, access controls, and retention policies remain unchanged from v1. However, the Q4 2025 Compliance Bulletin mandates a new **Provenance Stamp** audit requirement for partner-initiated pathways. This checklist confirms v1 controls remain valid and specifies the new v2-specific audit controls required prior to Friday's deployment.

---

### v2 Compliance Checklist

#### 1. Data Scope & Retention
*   **Status:** ✅ **Inherited from v1 (No Change)**
*   **Verification:**
    *   Confirmed v2 spec retains v1 data scope definitions.
    *   Confirmed retention policies are unchanged.
    *   *Action:* No new controls required. V1 checklist items remain green.

#### 2. Access Controls
*   **Status:** ✅ **Inherited from v1 (No Change)** + ⚠️ **New Pathway Specifics**
*   **V1 Controls (Still Apply):**
    *   Pathway-agnostic access controls verified in Q1 2025 remain valid.
*   **V2-Specific Controls (Partner Portal):**
    *   **Authentication:** Partners must authenticate via OAuth.
    *   **Authorization:** Requests are only permitted for users with an **active authorization** granted to the partner.
    *   **Scope Enforcement:** The OAuth scope must match the requested export scope.
    *   *Action:* Security team to confirm OAuth scope validation logic is correctly implemented in the new endpoint.

#### 3. Audit Logging & Provenance (CRITICAL NEW REQUIREMENT)
*   **Status:** ❌ **NEW CONTROL REQUIRED (v2-Specific)**
*   **Reference:** Q4 2025 Bulletin, Item: "Data Export Process Update — Partner Integrations"
*   **Requirement:** Any deployment adding a partner-initiated export pathway must record a **Provenance Stamp**.
*   **Implementation Details:**
    1.  **Trigger:** Must be written **synchronously** during the `POST /v2/data-export/request` execution.
    2.  **Content:** The audit entry must capture:
        *   User's **Authorization Grant ID**
        *   Requesting Party's **Identity** (Partner ID/OAuth Client ID)
        *   **Authorization Scope**
        *   **Validity Window** at time of export
    3.  **Failure Mode:** **Fail Closed**. If the Provenance Stamp cannot be assembled or written, the export request **must be rejected**.
*   **V1 Comparison:** V1 checklist items are pathway-agnostic and do not capture partner-specific provenance. These new fields are additive and do not conflict with v1 controls.
*   *Action:* Engineering to confirm implementation of synchronous Provenance Stamp writing with fail-closed behavior. Security to verify log integrity.

#### 4. Legal & Security Sign-off
*   **Legal:** 
    *   V1 sign-off (Q1 2025) remains valid for data scope/retention.
    *   **New Action:** Legal to review Partner Terms of Service (if updated) to ensure the Provenance Stamp data collection aligns with partner agreements. *(Note: Typically low-risk, but confirm no new PII is exposed in logs.)*
*   **Security:**
    *   V1 sign-off remains valid for base access controls.
    *   **New Action:** Security to validate the OAuth flow and the fail-closed mechanism for the Provenance Stamp.
