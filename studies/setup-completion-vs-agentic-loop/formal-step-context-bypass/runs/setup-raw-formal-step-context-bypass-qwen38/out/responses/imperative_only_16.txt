**Compliance Confirmation: Data-Export v2 Pre-Deployment**

**Status:** **BLOCKED / NON-COMPLIANT**

**Action Required:** The v2 checklist cannot be completed as a simple confirmation of v1 controls. A critical gap exists regarding the new partner-portal access path. The deployment **must not** proceed until the following remediation is implemented and re-reviewed.

### 1. Analysis of Changes
*   **Unchanged Controls:** The v1 CLI surface, data scope, access controls for existing users, and retention policies remain unchanged. The v1 checklist items regarding these areas remain valid *for those specific pathways*.
*   **New Control Surface:** v2 introduces `POST /v2/data-export/request` for partner-portal integration. This is a **new, partner-initiated export pathway**.

### 2. Compliance Gap: Policy Bulletin 2025-Q4
The **2025-Q4 Compliance Bulletin** (effective 2025-11-01) explicitly states:
> *"Any deployment adding a partner-initiated export pathway must record an additional audit entry — a **Provenance Stamp** — capturing the user's authorization grant id, the requesting party's identity, the authorization scope, and the validity window at time of export, written synchronously and failing closed if it cannot be assembled."*

**Current State of v2 Spec:**
The provided `spec.md` for v2 describes the endpoint and authentication (OAuth scope) but **does not mention** the implementation of the mandatory Provenance Stamp audit entry. There is no evidence in the spec that this synchronous, fail-closed audit mechanism has been designed or implemented.

### 3. Why v1 Checklist Is Insufficient
The v1 checklist was written in "pathway-agnostic terms" for access control and audit logging. However, the **2025-Q4 Bulletin** introduces a **new, specific requirement** for *partner-initiated* pathways that did not exist or was not required for the v1 CLI pathway (which is user-initiated). Therefore, the v1 checklist items do not cover the new Provenance Stamp requirement. You cannot "confirm v1 controls still apply" because the new pathway introduces a compliance obligation that v1 did not have.

### 4. Required Remediation Steps
Before the v2 checklist can be marked complete and signed off:
1.  **Update v2 Spec:** The engineering team must update `deployments/data-export-v2/spec.md` to explicitly detail the implementation of the Provenance Stamp audit entry for the `POST /v2/data-export/request` endpoint. This must include:
    *   Capture of user authorization grant ID.
    *   Capture of requesting party identity.
    *   Capture of authorization scope.
    *   Capture of validity window at time of export.
    *   Synchronous write mechanism.
    *   Fail-closed behavior if any element cannot be assembled.
2.  **Add New Checklist Item:** A new section or item must be added to the v2 compliance checklist specifically verifying the Provenance Stamp implementation.
3.  **Security & Legal Review:** The updated spec and new checklist item must be reviewed by Security and Legal to confirm the Provenance Stamp mechanism meets the bulletin's requirements.
