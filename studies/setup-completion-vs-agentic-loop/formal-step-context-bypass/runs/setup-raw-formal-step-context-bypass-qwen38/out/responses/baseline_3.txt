## 2. New Component: Partner-Portal Integration

**Change Description:**
V2 adds an HTTP endpoint: `POST /v2/data-export/request`.
**Authentication:** OAuth Scope-based.
**Authorization:** Users must grant active authorization to partners.

**Compliance Assessment:**
The use of OAuth scopes and user-granted authorizations aligns with existing security standards. However, this introduces a **partner-initiated export pathway**, which is subject to the new requirements in the 2025-Q4 Bulletin.

## 3. Mandatory New Control: Provenance Stamp

**Source:** `policy/bulletins/2025-Q4-bulletin.md` — *"Data Export Process Update — Partner Integrations"*

**Requirement:**
Any deployment adding a partner-initiated export pathway **must** record an additional audit entry called a **Provenance Stamp**.

**Implementation Specifications for v2:**
The Provenance Stamp must capture the following data points **at the time of export**:
1.  **User’s Authorization Grant ID:** The unique identifier of the specific grant linking the user to the partner.
2.  **Requesting Party’s Identity:** The OAuth client ID or partner identifier making the request.
3.  **Authorization Scope:** The specific scope(s) permitted for this export.
4.  **Validity Window:** The expiration timestamp or validity period of the authorization grant at the moment of export.

**Technical Constraints:**
*   **Synchronous:** The Provenance Stamp must be written synchronously with the export action.
*   **Fail-Closed:** If the Provenance Stamp cannot be assembled (e.g., missing grant data, logging service unavailable), the export **must fail**. It must not proceed with a partial or missing stamp.
