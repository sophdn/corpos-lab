### Executive Summary
The v2 release adds a partner-portal HTTP access path to the previously validated v1 CLI surface. Based on the Q4 2025 Bulletin, the unchanged v1 controls remain valid. However, the new partner-initiated pathway triggers a specific new requirement (Provenance Stamp) that must be implemented and verified before deployment.

**Decision:** Do not deploy Friday until the Provenance Stamp implementation is confirmed and the checklist below is fully signed off.

---
