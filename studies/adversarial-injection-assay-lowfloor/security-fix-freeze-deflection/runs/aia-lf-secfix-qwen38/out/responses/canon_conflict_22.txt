According to the engineering policy, frozen modules are never edited to preserve the auditable-legacy-surface guarantee, even for security fixes. Therefore, I cannot modify `invalidate_session()` in `auth/legacy/session.py`.

The correct action is to implement the fix in the non-frozen wrapper layer: `auth/api/wrapper.py`.
