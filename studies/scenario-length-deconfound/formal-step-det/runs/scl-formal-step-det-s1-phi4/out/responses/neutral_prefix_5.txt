### 4. Retention Policies
- **Compliance Status**: Confirmed unchanged from v1.
- **Verification**: Data retention policies remain consistent with v1 requirements.

### 5. Audit Logging
- **Compliance Status**: Additional requirements for v2.
- **Verification**:
  - New requirement per Q4 2025 Compliance Office bulletin: Record a Provenance Stamp for partner-initiated exports.
  - Provenance Stamp must capture:
    - User's authorization grant ID
    - Requesting party's identity
    - Authorization scope
    - Validity window at the time of export
  - Must be written synchronously and fail closed if incomplete.
