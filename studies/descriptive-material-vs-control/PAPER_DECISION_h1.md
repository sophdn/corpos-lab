# Paper go/no-go — H1 (descriptive material vs control), 2026-09-15

## Decision: NO-GO for a standalone H1 paper. H1 feeds the comprehension-as-compliance revision.

H1 is a strong, publishable result — but it is not a self-contained paper on its
own. It is the evidence base the existing revision chain was built to consume.

## Reasons

1. **H1 was designed to unblock a revision, not to stand alone.** The
   `papers-and-library-honesty` chain declares a dependency on this chain, with
   the stated reason: "the revision must not restate the material-vs-nothing claim
   until the H1 study tests it cleanly." H1 is that clean test. Its home is the
   revision.
2. **The sign story is not complete without its sibling.** H1 measures the *lift*
   from the floor on gating classes; the ground-extension measures *suppression*
   from the ceiling on register-prone classes; H2 (chain 545) is the pending
   sibling. The material-vs-nothing claim is class-dependent in sign, and the
   honest paper reports both directions together. A standalone H1 would tell half
   the story and pre-empt H2.
3. **H1's headline is the revision's headline.** The load-bearing H1 result — a
   descriptive domain-ground converts action as well as a command, from the floor
   (comprehension-as-compliance) — is exactly the claim the revision carries. A
   separate paper would duplicate it.

## What the result supports

- The `papers-and-library-honesty` revision: the material-vs-nothing claim now has
  a clean gating-class lift test to sit beside the ground-extension suppression
  result. FINDINGS_h1.md grounds each statement.
- Library reconciliation (task `reconcile-library`, done): Klein and Polanyi
  re-scoped with the grounding qualification.

## Revisit signal

Reconsider a standalone paper only if the revision stalls or if H2 diverges from
H1 in a way that makes a combined comprehension-as-compliance paper untenable. In
that case the natural standalone is a methods-plus-sign paper pairing H1 (lift)
with the ground-extension (suppression), not H1 alone.
