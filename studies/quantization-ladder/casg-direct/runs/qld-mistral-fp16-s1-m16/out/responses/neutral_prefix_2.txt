Regarding the tides, the text explains that the gravitational forces of the Moon and the Sun cause the ocean's water to rise and fall twice a day, creating the semidiurnal pattern of high and low waters. Not all coastlines follow the twice-daily rhythm, with some experiencing diurnal or mixed tides.
