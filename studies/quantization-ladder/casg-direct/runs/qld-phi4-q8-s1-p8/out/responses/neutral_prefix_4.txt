Based on the provided text, here is a concise summary of the key points regarding ocean tides:

1. **Gravitational Influence**: Ocean tides are primarily caused by the gravitational pull from the Moon and the Sun. The Moon's gravitational influence on Earth's tides is about twice as strong as the Sun's, despite the Sun's larger size, due to its closer proximity to Earth.

2. **Tidal Bulges**: The key mechanism behind tides is the difference in gravitational pull across Earth's diameter. This causes two tidal bulges: one on the side of Earth facing the Moon and one on the opposite side. As Earth rotates, these bulges create high and low tides.

3. **Tidal Patterns**:
   - **Semidiurnal Tides**: Most shorelines experience two high and two low tides in a little over 24 hours.
   - **Spring and Neap Tides**: Spring tides occur during new and full moons when the Sun and Moon's gravitational pulls align, leading to higher high tides and lower low tides. Neap tides occur during the Moon's quarter phases when the pulls are perpendicular, resulting in a smaller tidal range.

4. **Tidal Range Variability**: The difference between high and low tide, known as the tidal range, varies significantly. It can be minimal in the open ocean but much larger near certain coastlines, such as the Bay of Fundy, which can experience ranges exceeding fifteen meters due to the basin's shape and resonance.

5. **Resonance**: When the natural oscillation period of a water body matches the tidal period, resonance can amplify the tidal range significantly.

6. **Tidal Patterns Variability**: Not all coastlines experience semidiurnal tides. Some have diurnal tides (one high and one low tide per day), while others experience mixed tides (two tides of differing heights).

7. **Tidal Currents**: The horizontal movement of water associated with tides is known as tidal currents. These include the flood (incoming tide) and the ebb (outgoing tide), with a period of slack water in between.

This summary captures the essential aspects of how ocean tides are generated and their various patterns and influences.
