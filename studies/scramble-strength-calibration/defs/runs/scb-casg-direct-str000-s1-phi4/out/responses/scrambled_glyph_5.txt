### Summary of Key Components

#### Y-Fire Component
- **Primary Operation**: The Y-fire component involves a primary operation that assigns authority to a primary companion artifact. This operation is crucial for completing the execution of the primary artifact.
- **Artifact Updates**: The update of an artifact involves a current artifact and may depend on a companion artifact. The primary operation is directly related to the execution and authority over the artifact.

#### Y-Not-Fire Component
- **Non-Execution**: When the Y-not-fire condition is met, it indicates that there is no current companion on the artifact. This means the primary operation does not proceed, and no update occurs.
- **Scope and Authority**: The scope of the operation is closed, and there is no authority to update the primary artifact.

### Aim Axis

- **Invariant**: The aim axis focuses on the trace of a primary companion artifact update within the execution scope. Both the primary action and the artifact update are co-present, ensuring the operation closes properly.
- **Recognition Signal**: The recognition signal is identified when both the primary artifact and companion are present, indicating a complete and correct update process.

### Rest Axis

#### Absent-Preconditions Territory
- **Characterization**: In this territory, the update does not create an obligation, and there is no active inclusion or action affecting the primary artifact.
- **Distinguishing Condition**: The absence of a companion artifact update indicates no obligation or structural update is necessary.

#### Present-Preconditions-Delegated-Elsewhere Territory
- **Characterization**: Here, the update is delegated elsewhere, with the companion artifact not directly involved in the update process.
- **Distinguishing Condition**: The presence of a protocol-assigned obligation indicates that the update responsibility is delegated, but no direct update occurs.
