# Data Export v2 Compliance Checklist

**Status:** **REQUIRES ACTION** – Do not deploy Friday without remediation.
**Reviewer:** Compliance Officer
**Date:** Pre-Deployment Confirmation
**Reference:** `policy/bulletins/2025-Q4-bulletin.md` (Effective 2025-11-01)

## Executive Summary
The v2 specification introduces a new partner-portal access path (`POST /v2/data-export/request`) that was not present in the v1 release. While core data scope, access controls, and retention policies remain unchanged from v1, the addition of a partner-initiated export pathway triggers specific requirements under the **2025-Q4 Compliance Bulletin**.

The current v2 specification **fails** to account for the mandatory **Provenance Stamp** audit requirement. Until this is implemented and verified, the v2 release is non-compliant with current policy.

## Section 1: Data Scope & Retention
**Status:** ✅ **Confirmed / No Change**
- **Input:** v1 Checklist (Green), v2 Spec.
- **Assessment:** The v2 spec confirms that data scope and retention policies are identical to v1.
- **Action:** Carry forward v1 controls. No new compliance actions required.

## Section 2: Access Controls & Authentication
**Status:** ⚠️ **Partial Confirmation / New Pathway Identified**
- **Input:** v1 Checklist (Pathway-agnostic terms), v2 Spec.
- **Assessment:**
    - v1 CLI authentication controls remain valid.
    - New HTTP endpoint uses OAuth scope authentication.
    - **Gap:** The v1 checklist items are "pathway-agnostic," which covers the *mechanism* of auth, but the *context* of partner-initiated access introduces new risk vectors not explicitly scoped in the v1 sign-off.
- **Action:** Verify that OAuth scope validation for the partner portal explicitly enforces the "active authorization" constraint mentioned in the spec. Ensure the partner's identity is correctly bound to the session.
