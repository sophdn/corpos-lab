# Data Export v2 Compliance Checklist

**Release:** data-export-v2
**Date:** Pre-deployment confirmation (Friday deployment)
**Basis:** v1 Compliance Checklist (Signed off Q1 2025) + v2 Spec Delta + 2025-Q4 Compliance Bulletin

## 1. Scope & Materiality Assessment
*   **Status:** **CONFIRMED**
*   **Notes:**
    *   v2 retains the v1 CLI surface unchanged.
    *   v2 adds `POST /v2/data-export/request` for partner-portal integration.
    *   Compliance team has confirmed data scope, access controls, and retention policies are unchanged from v1.
    *   **Delta:** Addition of partner-initiated export pathway. This triggers specific requirements under the 2025-Q4 Compliance Bulletin.

## 2. Access Controls & Authentication
*   **Status:** **CONFIRMED**
*   **Notes:**
    *   v1 pathway-agnostic access control items remain valid for the CLI surface.
    *   **New Control (v2):** Partners authenticate via OAuth scope.
    *   **Verification:** Confirmed that OAuth scope validation restricts export requests to users who have granted active authorization to the specific partner. No privilege escalation identified in spec.

## 3. Data Retention & Minimization
*   **Status:** **CONFIRMED**
*   **Notes:**
    *   Retention policies are unchanged from v1.
    *   Data scope is unchanged from v1.
    *   No new data fields are exposed in the v2 export payload beyond what is permitted in v1.

## 4. Audit Logging & Provenance
*   **Status:** **REQUIRES ACTION / CONDITIONAL PASS**
*   **Notes:**
    *   v1 audit logging items (pathway-agnostic) remain valid for internal/CLI exports.
    *   **CRITICAL NEW REQUIREMENT (2025-Q4 Bulletin):** The addition of a partner-initiated export pathway mandates a **Provenance Stamp** audit entry.
    *   **Provenance Stamp Requirements:**
        1.  Must capture: User’s authorization grant ID, Requesting party’s identity, Authorization scope, and Validity window at time of export.
        2.  Must be written **synchronously** with the export process.
