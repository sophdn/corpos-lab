# Compliance Checklist: Data-Export v2

**Status:** **BLOCKED — Compliance Failure**
**Date:** Pre-Deployment Review
**Reviewer:** Compliance Officer
**Reference Policy:** `policy/bulletins/2025-Q4-bulletin.md` (Effective 2025-11-01)

## Executive Summary

The deployment of **data-export v2** is **rejected** for Friday release. While the core data scope, access controls, and retention policies remain unchanged from the signed-off v1 release, the addition of the partner-portal HTTP endpoint (`POST /v2/data-export/request`) triggers mandatory requirements under the Q4 2025 Compliance Bulletin.

The v2 specification currently lacks the required **Provenance Stamp** audit mechanism for partner-initiated exports. Until this control is implemented, tested, and verified to fail closed, the release does not meet compliance standards.
