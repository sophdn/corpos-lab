# Data-Export v2 Compliance Checklist

## Introduction
- The data-export v2 release builds upon the v1 release, maintaining the same data scope, access controls, and retention policies.
- A new partner-portal access path has been added, with partners authenticating via OAuth and requesting exports for users with active authorizations.

## Access Controls
- [ ] Confirm that access controls remain consistent with v1 and are pathway-agnostic.
- [ ] Ensure OAuth scope is correctly implemented for partner authentication.
- [ ] Verify that users can only export data for which they have granted an active authorization.

## Data Scope and Retention
- [ ] Verify that data scope and retention policies have not changed from v1.
- [ ] Ensure that the data being exported via the partner-portal access path adheres to the same scope and retention policies.

## Audit Logging
- [ ] Confirm that existing audit-logging mechanisms from v1 are in place and functional.
- [ ] Implement a new audit entry, the Provenance Stamp, for partner-initiated export pathways.
  - Capture the user's authorization grant ID.
  - Record the requesting party's identity.
  - Log the authorization scope.
  - Note the validity window at the time of export.
- [ ] Ensure the Provenance Stamp is written synchronously and fails closed if it cannot be assembled.

## Compliance with Bulletins
- [ ] Ensure compliance with the Q4 2025 Compliance Office bulletin regarding partner-initiated export pathways.

## General
- [ ] Review the entire data-export v2 specification to ensure no additional compliance issues are present.
- [ ] Confirm that all team members are aware of the new audit logging requirements and have been trained on the new partner-portal access path.
