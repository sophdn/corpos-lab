# Data-Export v2 Compliance Checklist

This checklist confirms compliance for the Data-Export v2 deployment, focusing on the newly added partner-portal access path. The checklist is based on the v1 compliance checklist, with additional considerations for the new features introduced in v2.

## Access Controls

- **v1 Controls**: The access controls defined in the v1 checklist remain applicable. The CLI surface is unchanged, ensuring existing access control measures are intact.
- **v2 Specifics**:
  - The new HTTP endpoint `POST /v2/data-export/request` requires partners to authenticate via OAuth scope.
  - Ensure that only users who have granted an active authorization to partners can initiate exports.
  - Implement and verify access controls for the new partner-portal integration.

## Audit Logging

- **v1 Controls**: The audit logging requirements from the v1 checklist apply, ensuring all access and export activities are logged.
- **v2 Specifics**:
  - Implement an additional audit entry, the Provenance Stamp, for partner-initiated export pathways.
  - Capture the following details in the Provenance Stamp:
    - User's authorization grant ID
    - Requesting party's identity
    - Authorization scope
    - Validity window at the time of export
  - Ensure the Provenance Stamp is written synchronously and fails closed if it cannot be assembled.

## Data Scope and Retention Policies

- **v1 Controls**: The data scope and retention policies from v1 remain unchanged and continue to apply to v2.

## Partner-Portal Integration

- **New Pathway**: The addition of a partner-portal access path is the primary change in v2.
- **Compliance Considerations**:
  - Ensure the OAuth authentication mechanism is robust and secure.
  - Verify that the partner-portal integration complies with existing data protection and privacy regulations.

## Conclusion

The compliance checklist for Data-Export v2 confirms that all v1 controls remain applicable, with specific additions to address the new partner-portal access path. The checklist ensures that access controls, audit logging, data scope, and retention policies are compliant with the updated requirements.

---

This checklist should be reviewed and signed off by the compliance team, Legal, and Security before deployment.
