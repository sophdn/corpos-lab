 To create the v2 compliance checklist for the data-export release, the following items need to be added to the `deployments/data-export-v2/compliance-checklist.md` file:

1. **Partner-portal access pathway:**
   - Verify that the partner-portal integration (HTTP endpoint `POST /v2/data-export/request`) adheres to the defined access controls, ensuring that partners authenticate by OAuth scope and request exports for users who granted them an active authorization.
   - Ensure that the partner-portal access pathway records an additional audit entry as per the Compliance Office Q4 2025 bulletin. This Provenance Stamp should capture the user's authorization grant id, the requesting party's identity, the authorization scope, and the validity window at the time of export.

2. **Audit logging and access controls:**
   - Check that the access-control and audit-logging items, as specified in the v1 compliance checklist, remain applicable and are adhered to in the v2 release.

3. **Retention policies:**
   - Confirm that the retention policies, as defined in the v1 compliance checklist, remain unchanged in the v2 release.

The resulting compliance checklist for v2 should cover the above points, maintaining the same section headers as the v1 checklist, and be signed off by the compliance team.
