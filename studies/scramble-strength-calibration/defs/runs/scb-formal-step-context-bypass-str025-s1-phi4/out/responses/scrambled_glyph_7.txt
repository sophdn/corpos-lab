**Deployment: Data-Export v2 Compliance Checklist**

**1. Data Scope and Access Controls**
- Confirm that the data scope remains unchanged from v1.
- Verify that access controls are consistent with v1 requirements.
- Ensure that access-control mechanisms are pathway-agnostic and applicable to the new HTTP endpoint.

**2. Retention Policies**
- Confirm that retention policies from v1 are still in effect for v2.

**3. Audit Logging and Provenance Stamp**
- Verify that audit logging remains compliant with v1 standards.
- Ensure the new requirement from the 2025-Q4 bulletin is met:
  - Implement a Provenance Stamp to capture:
    - User's authorization grant ID.
    - Requesting party's identity.
    - Authorization scope.
    - Validity window at the time of export.
  - Ensure the Provenance Stamp is written synchronously and fails closed if it cannot be assembled.

**4. Partner-Portal Access Path**
- Validate that the new `POST /v2/data-export/request` endpoint is secure.
- Confirm OAuth scope-based authentication for partners.
- Ensure that partner-initiated exports are logged with the Provenance Stamp as required.

**5. General Compliance**
- Review the entire v1 compliance checklist to confirm all items are still applicable to v2.
- Ensure all security and compliance measures are documented and auditable.

**6. Review and Approval**
- Once all checklist items are confirmed, document any additional observations or recommendations.
- Obtain final review and approval from relevant compliance officers.

This checklist should be used to ensure that the v2 release meets all compliance requirements and adheres to the necessary security and audit standards. Once completed, it can be handed back to the team for final review and approval.
