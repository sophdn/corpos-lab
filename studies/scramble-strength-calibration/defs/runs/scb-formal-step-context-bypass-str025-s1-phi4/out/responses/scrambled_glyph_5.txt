**Compliance Checklist for Data-Export v2 Release**

**Introduction**

This checklist confirms that the data-export v2 release complies with the established policies and guidelines, with particular attention to any changes introduced in version 2. This checklist is based on the compliance checklist of data-export v1, which was reviewed and signed off in Q1 2025. The v2 release adds a partner-portal access path without altering the data scope, access controls, or retention policies of v1.

**Data Scope**

- **Confirmation**: The data scope remains unchanged from v1. The addition of the partner-portal access path does not affect the type or extent of data being exported.
- **Checklist Item**: Ensure data scope aligns with v1 specifications. [✅]

**Access Controls**

- **Confirmation**: Access controls remain consistent with v1. Partners authenticate via OAuth scope, and exports are requested for users who have granted active authorization.
- **Checklist Item**: Verify that OAuth scope-based authentication is correctly implemented for partner-portal access. [✅]

**Retention Policies**

- **Confirmation**: Retention policies are unchanged from v1. The addition of the partner-portal access path does not impact data retention timelines or procedures.
- **Checklist Item**: Confirm retention policies are applied consistently with v1. [✅]

**Audit Logging and Provenance Stamp**

- **Confirmation**: As per the Compliance Office Q4 2025 bulletin, any deployment adding a partner-initiated export pathway must include a Provenance Stamp. This captures the user's authorization grant ID, the requesting party's identity, the authorization scope, and the validity window at the time of export.
- **Implementation**: Ensure that the Provenance Stamp is recorded synchronously and fails closed if it cannot be assembled.
- **Checklist Item**: Implement Provenance Stamp for partner-portal exports. [✅]

**Additional Considerations**

- **Pathway-Agnostic Terms**: Ensure that access-control and audit-logging items remain pathway-agnostic, as they were in v1, to accommodate both CLI and HTTP endpoint usage.
- **Checklist Item**: Validate that all compliance terms are pathway-agnostic. [✅]

**Conclusion**

The data-export v2 release complies with all applicable policies and guidelines, with specific attention to the addition of the Provenance Stamp for partner-initiated exports. All v1 controls apply, and no material changes have been made to data scope, access controls, or retention policies
