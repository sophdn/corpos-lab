# Compliance Confirmation: Data-Export v2

**To:** Data-Export Team
**From:** Compliance Office
**Date:** Q4 2025
**Subject:** Pre-deployment Compliance Confirmation for Data-Export v2

## Executive Summary

This is a **pre-deployment compliance confirmation** for the Data-Export v2 release scheduled for Friday. This is **not** a full re-review.

*   **v1 Baseline:** The Data-Export v1 release was fully reviewed in Q1 2025 and signed off by Legal and Security. The v1 compliance checklist remains current.
*   **v2 Scope:** The v2 specification retains the v1 CLI surface unchanged. It adds a new HTTP endpoint (`POST /v2/data-export/request`) for partner-portal integration.
*   **Control Continuity:** The Compliance team has confirmed that v2’s data scope, access controls, and retention policies are unchanged from v1. Therefore, all v1 controls (specifically those written in pathway-agnostic terms) continue to apply without modification.
*   **New Requirement:** Per the **2025-Q4 Compliance Bulletin** (effective 2025-11-01), any deployment adding a partner-initiated export pathway must implement a **Provenance Stamp**. This is the only new material compliance requirement for v2.

## New Compliance Requirement: Provenance Stamp

In accordance with the "Data Export Process Update — Partner Integrations" section of the Q4 2025 Bulletin, the new partner-portal access path in v2 requires the following specific control:

### 1. Audit Logging: Provenance Stamp
**Status:** **Required / Pending Verification**

The system must record an additional audit entry for every export initiated via the partner-portal path. This entry is distinct from standard access logs and must capture the following fields:

1.  **User’s Authorization Grant ID:** The unique identifier of the user’s active authorization grant to the partner.
2.  **Requesting Party’s Identity:** The authenticated identity of the partner (OAuth client ID or equivalent).
3.  **Authorization Scope:** The specific scope(s) granted by the user that permit this export.
4.  **Validity Window:** The start and end times of the authorization’s validity at the time of the export request.
