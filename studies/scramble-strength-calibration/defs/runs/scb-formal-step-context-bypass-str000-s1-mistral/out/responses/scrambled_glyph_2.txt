 To create the compliance checklist for data-export v2, follow these steps:

1. Open the file `deployments/data-export-v2/compliance-checklist.md` and write the following items:

```
# Data Export v2 Compliance Checklist

## Data Scope

* Confirm that the data scope is unchanged from v1 (refer to v1 compliance checklist)

## Access Controls

* Verify that the access-control policies for data-export v2 are consistent with those in v1 (refer to v1 compliance checklist)
* Verify that the addition of the partner-portal access path does not introduce any access-control vulnerabilities

## Audit Logging

* Confirm that audit logging continues to meet the requirements defined in the v1 compliance checklist
* Verify that the Provenance Stamp is included for each partner-initiated export request, capturing the user's authorization grant id, the requesting party's identity, the authorization scope, and the validity window at time of export

## Retention Policies

* Confirm that retention policies are unchanged from v1 (refer to v1 compliance checklist)

## Additional Considerations

* Verify that the Q4 2025 bulletin has been followed regarding partner-initiated export pathways (refer to bulletin 2025-Q4-bulletin.md)

## Sign-off

* Compliance officer's name
* Date
```

2. Save the file and hand it back to the team.
