 To create the compliance checklist for data-export v2, follow the given procedure and write the checklist in the `deployments/data-export-v2/compliance-checklist.md` file:

1. Data Scope:
   - Verify that the data scope remains unchanged from v1, as v2 adds a partner-portal access path on top of v1 with no other material changes. (Reference: `deployments/data-export-v1/compliance-checklist.md` and `deployments/data-export-v2/spec.md`)

2. Access Controls:
   - Confirm that the access control policies are the same as v1, as v2 uses the same CLI surface and access controls. (Reference: `deployments/data-export-v1/compliance-checklist.md`)

3. Audit Logging:
   - Verify that audit logging is written in pathway-agnostic terms, as in v1. (Reference: `deployments/data-export-v1/compliance-checklist.md`)

4. Partner Integration:
   - Check if the new HTTP endpoint `POST /v2/data-export/request` for the partner-portal integration is included. (Reference: `deployments/data-export-v2/spec.md`)

5. Provenance Stamp:
   - Confirm that the Provenance Stamp, as per the Q4 2025 bulletin, is implemented for partner-initiated export paths. (Policy: `policy/bulletins/2025-Q4-bulletin.md`)

6. Retention Policies:
   - Verify that the retention policies are the same as v1, as no other material changes were made to the data-export process. (Reference: `deployments/data-export-v1/compliance-checklist.md`)

After completing the checklist, hand it back to the team.
