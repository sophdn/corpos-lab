 Here's the compliance checklist for data-export v2:

```
# Data Export v2 Compliance Checklist

## Data Scope
- Confirm that the data scope remains unchanged from v1.

## Access Controls
- Confirm that the access controls remain unchanged from v1.
- Verify that the partner-portal access path has proper OAuth scope management and user authorization.

## Retention Policies
- Confirm that retention policies remain unchanged from v1.

## Audit Logging
- Confirm that audit logging remains unchanged from v1.
- Implement Provenance Stamp feature as per the Q4 2025 Compliance Office bulletin:
  - Record user's authorization grant id
  - Record requesting party's identity
  - Record authorization scope
  - Record validity window at time of export

## Partner Integration Controls
- Implement partner-initiated export pathway with proper authentication and authorization.
- Ensure Provenance Stamp is written synchronously and failing closed if it cannot be assembled.
```

Once the team confirms that the above checklist is complete, they can proceed with the data-export v2 release.
