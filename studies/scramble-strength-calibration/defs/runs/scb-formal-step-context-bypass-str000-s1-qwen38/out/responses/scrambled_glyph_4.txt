## 1. Executive Summary
Data-Export v2 introduces a new partner-portal integration via `POST /v2/data-export/request`. The core data scope, access controls, and retention policies remain unchanged from v1 (signed off Q1 2025). However, per the **2025-Q4-bulletin** (effective 2025-11-01), the new partner-initiated pathway triggers a mandatory requirement for a **Provenance Stamp** audit entry. This checklist confirms that v1 controls persist and validates the implementation of the new Provenance Stamp requirement.
