Below is the compliance checklist for the data-export v2 release, taking into account the unchanged aspects of v1 and the new requirements introduced with v2:

# Data Export v2 Compliance Checklist

## Data Scope
- [ ] Confirm that data scope remains consistent with v1, with no changes in the types of data being exported.
- [ ] Verify that the data types and their respective fields align with the v1 specification.

## Access Controls
- [ ] Ensure access controls from v1 remain effective and unchanged.
- [ ] Confirm that the new HTTP endpoint `POST /v2/data-export/request` is protected by OAuth scope authentication.
- [ ] Validate that only partners with active authorization are allowed to initiate export requests.
- [ ] Confirm that user authorization is required before any export can be requested by a partner.

## Retention Policies
- [ ] Verify that data retention policies have not changed and continue to align with v1 specifications.
- [ ] Confirm compliance with all applicable data protection and privacy laws regarding data retention.

## Audit Logging
- [ ] Confirm that audit logging mechanisms from v1 are still in place and functioning.
- [ ] Ensure that new audit entries are captured for partner-initiated export pathways.
- [ ] Verify the implementation of the Provenance Stamp as per the Compliance Office Q4 2025 bulletin:
  - [ ] Capture the user's authorization grant ID.
  - [ ] Record the requesting party's identity.
  - [ ] Document the authorization scope.
  - [ ] Note the validity window at the time of export.
- [ ] Ensure Provenance Stamps are written synchronously and adhere to the "failing closed" principle if they cannot be assembled.

## Security
- [ ] Confirm that the security measures from v1 are intact and applicable to v2.
- [ ] Validate that the integration of the partner portal does not introduce new vulnerabilities.

## Partner Portal Integration
- [ ] Verify that the partner portal's access path is secure and complies with the defined security standards.
- [ ] Ensure that OAuth scope-based authentication is correctly implemented for partner requests.

## Overall Compliance
- [ ] Conduct a final review to ensure all compliance items are addressed and verified.
- [ ] Sign off by the compliance team, confirming that v2 meets all necessary compliance requirements.
