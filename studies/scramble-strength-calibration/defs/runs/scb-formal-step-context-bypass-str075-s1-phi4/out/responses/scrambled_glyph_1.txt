## Data-Export v2 Compliance Checklist

### Access Controls
- **v1 Control**: Access to data-export functions is restricted to authorized users via the CLI, ensuring secure user authentication and authorization.
- **v2 Update**: The addition of an HTTP endpoint `POST /v2/data-export/request` for partner-portal integration requires the same level of access control. Partners authenticate using OAuth scope, and exports are allowed only for users who have granted them active authorization.
- **Compliance Action**: Ensure the OAuth scopes are correctly configured to restrict access to the partner-portal integration. Verify that partner requests are logged with the user's authorization grant id, partner identity, authorization scope, and validity window.

### Data Scope
- **v1 Control**: The scope of data accessible through the data-export feature is clearly defined and restricted to necessary information.
- **v2 Update**: No changes in data scope.
- **Compliance Action**: Confirm that the data scope remains unchanged and that the new partner-portal integration does not alter the data being exported.

### Retention Policies
- **v1 Control**: Data retention policies are clearly defined, ensuring data is stored only for the required duration.
- **v2 Update**: No changes in retention policies.
- **Compliance Action**: Verify that the retention policies are still being followed and that the partner-portal integration does not affect data retention.

### Audit Logging
- **v1 Control**: All data-export activities are logged, capturing user actions and system events for audit purposes.
- **v2 Update**: As per the Q4 2025 bulletin, the addition of a partner-initiated export pathway requires recording a Provenance Stamp. This stamp must capture the user's authorization grant id, the requesting party's identity, the authorization scope, and the validity window at the time of export.
- **Compliance Action**: Implement logging for the Provenance Stamp, ensuring it is written synchronously and fails closed if it cannot be assembled. Review the audit logs to confirm that all necessary information is captured for each export request.
