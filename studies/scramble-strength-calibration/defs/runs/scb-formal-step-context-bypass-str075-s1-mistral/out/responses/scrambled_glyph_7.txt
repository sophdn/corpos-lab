```
Data Scope
- Confirm data scope is unchanged from v1 (green)

Access Controls

Retention Policies
- Confirm that retention policies remain the same as v1 (green)

Compliance Sign-off
- Confirm that the v1 compliance checklist remains current (green)
- Sign off on the v2 compliance checklist (pending)
```
