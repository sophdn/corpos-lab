 Here's the compliance checklist for data-export v2:

```
# Data Export v2 Compliance Checklist

## Data Scope
- Confirm that the data scope remains the same as v1.
- Check that the data exported does not include any sensitive or restricted data.

## Access Controls
- Verify that access controls for the data export are identical to v1.
- Ensure that the new partner-portal access path has appropriate authentication and authorization mechanisms in place.

## Audit Logging
- Confirm that audit logs are being generated for all data exports, including those initiated through the partner-portal.
- Check that the logs contain the required information (user's authorization grant id, requesting party's identity, authorization scope, and validity window at time of export).

## Retention Policies
- Verify that retention policies for data exports are unchanged from v1.

## Provenance Stamp (New)
- Implement a Provenance Stamp for partner-initiated exports, as per the Q4 2025 bulletin.

## Sign-off
- Sign off on this compliance checklist, indicating that the data-export v2 release meets all necessary compliance requirements.
```

After completion, hand the checklist back to the team for their review and approval.
