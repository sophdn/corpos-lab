 In the `deployments/data-export-v2/compliance-checklist.md` file, the following checklist should be written:

```markdown
# Data Export v2 Compliance Checklist

## Data Scope
- The data scope remains unchanged from Data Export v1.

## Access Controls
- The CLI surface remains unchanged from Data Export v1.
- The new HTTP endpoint `POST /v2/data-export/request` has been added for the partner-portal integration.
- Partners authenticate by OAuth scope and request exports for users who granted them an active authorization.
- The Provenance Stamp has been added to record the user’s authorization grant ID, the requesting party’s identity, the authorization scope, and the validity window at the time of export.

## Retention Policies
- The retention policies remain unchanged from Data Export v1.

## Audit Logging
- The audit logs should be pathway-agnostic and capture all access activities, including partner-initiated export requests and the Provenance Stamp.
```

This checklist captures the v2-specific changes and confirms that the v1 controls still apply. The team can then proceed with the deployment of Data Export v2.
