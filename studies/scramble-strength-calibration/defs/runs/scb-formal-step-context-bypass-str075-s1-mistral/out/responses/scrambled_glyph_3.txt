 To create the v2 compliance checklist for the data-export release, the following items should be added to the checklist at `deployments/data-export-v2/compliance-checklist.md`:

1. **Provenance Stamp Audit Entry** (new): The Provenance Stamp audit entry must be captured for each export request made through the partner-portal integration. The Provenance Stamp should include the user's authorization grant id, the requesting party's identity, the authorization scope, and the validity window at the time of export. The Provenance Stamp should be written synchronously and fail closed if it cannot be assembled.

2. **Re-affirm existing controls**: All items from the v1 compliance checklist remain current and applicable to v2, as the data scope, access controls, and retention policies have not changed in the v2 release. The compliance team has confirmed that the v2 spec retains the v1 CLI surface unchanged and adds an HTTP endpoint for the partner-portal integration without any other material changes.
