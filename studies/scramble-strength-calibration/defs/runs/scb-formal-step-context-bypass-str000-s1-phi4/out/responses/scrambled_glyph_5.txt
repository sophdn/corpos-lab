**Data-Export v2 Compliance Checklist**

---

**Access Controls**

1. **Scope and Permissions**  
   - The data scope and access controls remain unchanged from v1, as confirmed by the compliance review of the v2 specifications.
   - Partners authenticate via OAuth scope, ensuring access control is maintained.
   - User exports are requested only for users who have granted active authorization.

2. **Audit Logging**  
   - All access attempts and data export requests are logged.
   - Ensure existing audit logging items continue to capture relevant data in a pathway-agnostic manner.
   - New audit entries, specifically Provenance Stamps, must be recorded for partner-initiated export requests.

**Data Retention Policies**

1. **Retention Policies**  
   - Data retention policies from v1 are unchanged and remain compliant.
   - Ensure that data retention periods and deletion processes continue to adhere to established policies.

**Provenance Stamping**

1. **Provenance Stamp Requirements**  
   - As per the 2025-Q4 Compliance Bulletin, any deployment adding a partner-initiated export pathway must record a Provenance Stamp.
   - The Provenance Stamp must capture:
     - User's authorization grant ID
     - Requesting party's identity
     - Authorization scope
     - Validity window at the time of export
   - Ensure that Provenance Stamps are written synchronously and fail closed if they cannot be assembled.

**General Compliance**

1. **Compliance Checklist**  
   - All v1 checklist items remain applicable and are marked as compliant.
   - New v2-specific requirements, such as Provenance Stamping, have been addressed and documented.

2. **Review and Approval**  
   - This checklist confirms that the v2 release complies with all applicable policies and procedures.
   - Review by the compliance team is required to ensure all items are addressed and documented.
   - Legal and Security sign-off is recommended for final approval before deployment.

---

This checklist confirms that the v2 release maintains compliance with existing policies while incorporating necessary updates for the partner-portal integration. Ensure all items are reviewed and approved prior to deployment.
