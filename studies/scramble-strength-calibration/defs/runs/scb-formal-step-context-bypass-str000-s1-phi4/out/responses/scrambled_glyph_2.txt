To prepare the compliance checklist for the data-export v2 release, we need to ensure that all v1 controls remain applicable and identify any new requirements introduced by the v2 changes. Here's a structured checklist based on the information provided:

---

## Data-Export v2 Compliance Checklist

### Access Controls

- **Retention of v1 Controls**: Ensure access controls from v1 are still in place and unchanged.
- **New Access Pathway**: Confirm that the new HTTP endpoint `POST /v2/data-export/request` adheres to existing access control policies.
- **OAuth Authentication**: Verify that partners authenticate using OAuth scope correctly and that only authorized requests are processed.

### Audit Logging

- **Retention of v1 Controls**: Ensure audit logging requirements from v1 are maintained.
- **Provenance Stamp**: Implement the new audit entry requirement for partner-initiated export pathways as per the 2025-Q4 bulletin. This includes recording:
  - User's authorization grant ID
  - Requesting party's identity
  - Authorization scope
  - Validity window at time of export

### Data Scope

- **Retention of v1 Controls**: Confirm that data scope remains unchanged from v1.

### Retention Policies

- **Retention of v1 Controls**: Ensure retention policies are consistent with those defined in v1.

### General Observations

- **Pathway-Agnostic Controls**: Verify that all access-control and audit-logging items are written in pathway-agnostic terms, as per v1 checklist.

---

This checklist confirms that the v2 release retains all the compliance measures from v1 and introduces the necessary changes for the new partner-initiated export pathway. The compliance team should review this checklist to ensure all items are addressed before the deployment.
