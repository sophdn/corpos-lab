## 1. Executive Summary

The data-export v2 release introduces a new partner-portal access path via `POST /v2/data-export/request`. While the core data scope, access controls, and retention policies remain unchanged from v1 (which was signed off in Q1 2025), the addition of a partner-initiated export pathway triggers specific requirements under the **Compliance Office Q4 2025 Bulletin** (effective 2025-11-01).

**Critical Finding:** The v2 specification currently does not document the mandatory "Provenance Stamp" audit entry required by the Q4 2025 Bulletin for partner integrations. **Deployment is blocked until this control is implemented and verified.**
