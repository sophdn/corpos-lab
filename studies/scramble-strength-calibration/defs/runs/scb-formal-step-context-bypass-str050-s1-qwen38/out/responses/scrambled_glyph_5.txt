# Compliance Checklist: Data-Export v2

**Status:** **BLOCKED — Deployment Hold**
**Date:** Pre-Deployment Review
**Reviewer:** Compliance Officer
**Reference Policy:** `policy/bulletins/2025-Q4-bulletin.md` (Effective 2025-11-01)

## Executive Summary

The proposed deployment of `data-export-v2` is **non-compliant** with the current Q4 2025 Compliance Bulletin. While the core data scope, access controls, and retention policies remain unchanged from the signed-off v1 release, the addition of the `POST /v2/data-export/request` endpoint introduces a **partner-initiated export pathway**.

The Q4 2025 Bulletin mandates that any deployment adding a partner-initiated export pathway must implement a **Provenance Stamp**. The current v2 specification (`deployments/data-export-v2/spec.md`) describes the endpoint and OAuth authentication but **does not specify the generation or synchronous writing of the Provenance Stamp** (User Authorization Grant ID, Requesting Party Identity, Scope, Validity Window). Furthermore, the requirement to "fail closed" if the stamp cannot be assembled is not reflected in the specification.

**Action Required:** The v2 specification must be updated to include the Provenance Stamp logic, or the deployment must be blocked until the engineering team confirms that this logic is implemented and tested. The checklist below reflects the current state of the spec, which fails to meet the new regulatory requirement.

## Detailed Checklist

### 1. Data Scope
*   **Item:** Confirm data scope matches v1 baseline.
*   **Status:** ✅ **Pass**
*   **Note:** Spec confirms v1 CLI surface is unchanged. No new data fields are exposed beyond what was reviewed in Q1 2025.

### 2. Access Controls
*   **Item:** Confirm access controls are unchanged and pathway-agnostic terms apply.
*   **Status:** ⚠️ **Conditional Pass**
*   **Note:** V1 access controls (RBAC/ABAC) are retained. The new partner-portal path uses OAuth scopes. This is acceptable *provided* the OAuth scope validation strictly enforces the user’s active authorization. However, the audit trail for this specific pathway is missing (see Section 5).

### 3. Retention Policies
*   **Item:** Confirm retention policies match v1.
