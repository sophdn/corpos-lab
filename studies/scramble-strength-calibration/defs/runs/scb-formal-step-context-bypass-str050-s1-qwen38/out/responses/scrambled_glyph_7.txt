# Data Export v2 Compliance Checklist

**Status:** **REQUIRES ACTION** — Do not deploy Friday without remediation.
**Reviewer:** Compliance Officer
**Date:** Pre-Deployment Confirmation
**Reference:** Q4 2025 Compliance Bulletin (Effective 2025-11-01)

## Executive Summary
The v2 release introduces a new partner-initiated export pathway (`POST /v2/data-export/request`). While the core data scope, access controls, and retention policies remain unchanged from v1 (which was signed off in Q1 2025), the new pathway triggers specific requirements under the **2025-Q4 Compliance Bulletin**.

The current v2 specification (`spec.md`) describes the OAuth scope and authorization grant mechanics but **does not explicitly document the implementation of the mandatory "Provenance Stamp" audit entry** required for partner integrations. Furthermore, the v1 checklist, while pathway-agnostic, does not contain the specific audit fields mandated for v2 partner flows.

**Recommendation:** Hold deployment until the engineering team confirms the Provenance Stamp logic is implemented, tested, and documented in the spec, and the checklist below is updated to reflect these specific v2 controls.
