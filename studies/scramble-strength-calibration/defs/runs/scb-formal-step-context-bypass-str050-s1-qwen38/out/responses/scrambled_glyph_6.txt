Based on the provided context, specifically the Q4 2025 Compliance Bulletin and the v2 specification, here is the completed compliance checklist for `deployments/data-export-v2`.

### Compliance Checklist: Data Export v2

**Status:** Ready for Review
**Date:** [Current Date]
**Reference:** v1 Checklist (Signed Off Q1 2025), Q4 2025 Bulletin (Effective 2025-11-01)

#### 1. Access Controls
*   **v1 Controls (Unchanged):** CLI surface access controls remain active. Users must retain valid session credentials for direct CLI exports.
*   **v2 Specific Change (Partner Portal):**
    *   **Authentication:** Partner-initiated requests via `POST /v2/data-export/request` must validate OAuth scope.
    *   **Authorization:** The system must verify that the requesting partner holds an *active* authorization grant for the specific user whose data is being exported.
    *   **Verification:** Confirm that the OAuth scope validation logic is strictly enforced and that inactive/revoked authorizations result in immediate rejection.

#### 2. Audit Logging & Provenance
*   **v1 Controls (Unchanged):** Standard audit logs for CLI exports remain in effect.
*   **v2 Specific Change (Provenance Stamp - MANDATORY):**
    *   **Requirement:** Per Q4 2025 Bulletin ("Data Export Process Update — Partner Integrations"), all partner-initiated exports must generate a **Provenance Stamp**.
    *   **Data Fields:** The stamp must synchronously record:
        1.  User’s authorization grant ID.
        2.  Requesting party’s identity (Partner ID).
        3.  Authorization scope.
        4.  Validity window at the time of export.
    *   **Implementation Constraint:** The write must be **synchronous**.
    *   **Failure Mode:** The export process must **fail closed** (abort the export) if the Provenance Stamp cannot be assembled or written successfully.
    *   **Verification:** Code review confirms the `POST /v2/data-export/request` handler includes synchronous logging logic with a hard fail-state on logging errors.

#### 3. Data Scope & Retention
*   **v1 Controls (Unchanged):** Data scope definitions and retention policies are identical to v1.
