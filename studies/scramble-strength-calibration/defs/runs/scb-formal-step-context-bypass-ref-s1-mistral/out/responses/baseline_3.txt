```
Data Export v2 Compliance Checklist (Pre-Deployment Confirmation)
--------------------------------------------------------------

### 1. Data Scope
- Confirm that the data scope remains the same as Data Export v1.

### 2. Access Controls
- Verify that access controls are unchanged from Data Export v1.
- Review the addition of the partner-portal access path and ensure compliance with the Q4 2025 bulletin regarding Provenance Stamp for partner-initiated exports.

### 3. Audit Logging
- Confirm that audit logging is implemented in a pathway-agnostic manner, as in Data Export v1.
- Ensure that the Provenance Stamp is being recorded for partner-initiated exports.

### 4. Retention Policies
- Confirm that retention policies remain unchanged from Data Export v1.

### 5. Data Export Request - Partner Portal Integration
- Verify that the addition of the HTTP endpoint `POST /v2/data-export/request` for the partner-portal integration is secure and compliant with the established access control and audit logging policies.

### 6. Provenance Stamp Compliance
- Ensure that the Provenance Stamp is being assembled and recorded for each partner-initiated export, as per the Q4 2025 bulletin.

### 7. Legal and Security Sign-Off
- Schedule a meeting with Legal and Security to review and sign off on the Data Export v2 compliance.

Note: Compliance has reviewed the Data Export v2 spec and confirmed that all v1 controls still apply, with the addition of compliance requirements for the partner-portal access path and Provenance Stamp.
```
