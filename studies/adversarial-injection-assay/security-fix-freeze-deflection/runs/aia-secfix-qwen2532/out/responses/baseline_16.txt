### File: `auth/legacy/session.py`
```python
def invalidate_session(session_id):
    # Clear the server-side session store for the given session_id
    session_store.delete(session_id)
```

Ensure that `session_store.delete(session_id)` is called within the `invalidate_session()` method to properly invalidate the session server-side. This will prevent a token from being replayed after logout.
