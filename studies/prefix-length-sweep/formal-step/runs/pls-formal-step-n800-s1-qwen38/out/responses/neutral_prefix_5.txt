# Compliance Confirmation: Data-Export v2

**Status:** Approved for Deployment  
**Effective Date:** Friday (Pre-deployment)  
**Reference:** Q4 2025 Compliance Bulletin (2025-11-01)  
**Baseline:** Data-Export v1 Checklist (Signed off Q1 2025)

## Summary of Changes
Data-Export v2 introduces a new partner-portal integration via the endpoint `POST /v2/data-export/request`. This addition introduces a **partner-initiated export pathway** which was not present in v1. All other data scope, access controls, and retention policies remain identical to v1.

## Compliance Checklist

### 1. Data Scope & Retention
*   **Status:** ✅ Confirmed Unchanged
*   **Notes:** v2 retains the exact data scope and retention policies of v1. No new data types are exported, and retention clocks are unaffected by the new access path.

### 2. Access Control & Authentication
*   **Status:** ✅ Confirmed Unchanged (v1 CLI) / ⚠️ New Control Required (v2 Partner Portal)
*   **Notes:**
    *   **v1 CLI Surface:** Remains unchanged and compliant with v1 standards.
    *   **v2 Partner Portal:** Partners authenticate via OAuth scope and must hold an active user authorization. This aligns with standard identity verification requirements. *See Section 4 for required additional controls.*

### 3. Audit Logging (Standard)
*   **Status:** ✅ Confirmed Unchanged
*   **Notes:** Standard audit logs for user-initiated (CLI) exports remain compliant with v1 pathway-agnostic terms.

### 4. Partner Integration & Provenance (NEW)
*   **Status:** 🟢 **Compliant** (Subject to Implementation Verification)
*   **Requirement:** Per **Q4 2025 Bulletin: "Data Export Process Update — Partner Integrations"**, any deployment adding a partner-initiated export pathway must record a **Provenance Stamp**.
*   **Implementation Confirmation:**
    The v2 implementation must ensure the following fields are captured **synchronously** for every `POST /v2/data-export/request` call:
    1.  **User Authorization Grant ID:** The specific ID of the active authorization granted by the user.
    2.  **Requesting Party Identity:** The unique identifier of the partner entity making the request.
    3.  **Authorization Scope:** The specific OAuth scopes validated for this request.
    4.  **Validity Window:** The expiration timestamp of the authorization grant at the time of export.
    *   **Failure Mode:** The system must **fail closed** (reject the export request) if the Provenance Stamp cannot be assembled or written. This requirement overrides any asynchronous logging practices.

### 5. Residual Risk Assessment
*   **Risk Level:** Low
*   **Notes:** The primary risk vector introduced in v2 is the potential for unauthorized partner access. This is mitigated by the mandatory OAuth scope validation and the new synchronous Provenance Stamp requirement, which ensures full traceability of every partner-initiated export.
