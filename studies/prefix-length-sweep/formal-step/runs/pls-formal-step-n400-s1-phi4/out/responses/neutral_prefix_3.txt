## Scope and Data

- **Data Scope:** Unchanged from v1. No new data types or categories have been added.
- **Control Confirmation:** The data export process continues to adhere to the scope defined in v1, ensuring compliance with existing data privacy and protection regulations.

## Access Controls

- **Access Method:** 
  - v1 CLI interface remains unchanged.
  - New HTTP endpoint (`POST /v2/data-export/request`) added for partner-portal access.
- **Authentication:**
  - Partners authenticate via OAuth scope.
  - Requests require an active authorization grant from the user.
- **Control Confirmation:** Access controls from v1 remain applicable. The addition of the partner-portal access path has been reviewed to ensure it complies with the OAuth scope and active authorization requirements.

## Audit Logging and Provenance

- **Audit Logging:**
  - Existing audit logging mechanisms remain in place.
- **Provenance Stamp Requirement:**
  - As per the 2025-Q4 bulletin, a Provenance Stamp must be recorded for partner-initiated export pathways.
  - Required fields: User's authorization grant ID, requesting party's identity, authorization scope, and validity window.
  - Must be written synchronously and fail closed if not assembled.
- **Control Confirmation:** The addition of the Provenance Stamp for partner-initiated exports has been implemented in accordance with the compliance bulletin.

## Retention Policies

- **Policy Confirmation:** Data retention policies remain unchanged from v1. No new data is subject to different retention periods.

## Additional Considerations

- **Security Review:** Legal and Security have reviewed the v2 spec and confirmed that security measures align with organizational standards.
- **Compliance Review:** The compliance team has reviewed the v2 spec and confirmed that the data scope, access controls, and retention policies remain consistent with v1, with the noted addition of the partner-portal access path.

**Conclusion:** All compliance controls from v1 apply to v2, with the addition of the Provenance Stamp for partner-initiated exports. The deployment is ready for launch, pending final deployment procedures.
