# Data-Export v2 Compliance Checklist

## Data Scope
- Data scope remains unchanged from v1, covering only user data for which explicit authorization has been granted.

## Access Control
- Access controls remain unchanged from v1, with CLI access restricted to authorized users and partner access mediated through OAuth.
- Partner authentication and authorization scope is logged for each request.
- Compliance with Q4 2025 bulletin: Provenance Stamp is added for partner-initiated exports, capturing user's authorization grant id, requesting party's identity, authorization scope, and validity window.

## Audit Logging
- Audit logging remains unchanged from v1, logging all access and export events.
- Compliance with Q4 2025 bulletin: Provenance Stamp is written synchronously and fails closed if it cannot be assembled.

## Retention Policy
- Retention policy remains unchanged from v1, retaining logs for a minimum of 5 years.

## Security
- Security controls remain unchanged from v1, with user data encrypted at rest and in transit.

## Privacy
- Privacy controls remain unchanged from v1, with user data anonymized and aggregated where possible.

## Disaster Recovery
- Disaster recovery controls remain unchanged from v1, with regular backups and disaster recovery testing.

All items above are green, signed off Q1 2025.
