 Based on the information provided, here is the compliance checklist for the data-export v2 release:

```
deployments/data-export-v2/compliance-checklist.md

# Data Scope
- The data scope remains the same as v1, covering user data that has been explicitly granted for export by the user.

# Access Control
- The access controls remain the same as v1, with users maintaining control over their data and only granting access to partners through an active authorization.
- The new partner-portal access path (POST /v2/data-export/request) requires OAuth scope for partner authentication.

# Audit Logging
- The audit logging remains the same as v1, with all actions related to data export being logged.
- The Provenance Stamp requirement (user's authorization grant id, the requesting party's identity, the authorization scope, and the validity window at time of export) is added for partner-initiated export requests.

# Retention Policy
- The retention policy remains the same as v1.

# Sign-off
- Signed off by Compliance, Legal, and Security on Q1 2025 (v1 checklist).
- To be reviewed and signed off after the v2 deployment.
```
