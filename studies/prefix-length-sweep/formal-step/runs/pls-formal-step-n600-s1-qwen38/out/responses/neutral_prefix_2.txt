## 1. Executive Summary
This document serves as the pre-deployment compliance confirmation for Data-Export v2. It is not a full re-review. Based on the review of the v2 specification (`spec.md`) and the Q4 2025 Compliance Bulletin (`2025-Q4-bulletin.md`), the core data scope, access controls, and retention policies remain unchanged from v1. Therefore, all v1 controls carry over.

However, v2 introduces a new partner-initiated export pathway via `POST /v2/data-export/request`. Per the Q4 2025 Bulletin, this requires the implementation of a **Provenance Stamp** audit entry. This is the only material compliance addition for v2.

## 2. Carryover Controls (Unchanged from v1)

## 3. New v2-Specific Controls
The following controls are **new** or **modified** specifically for v2 due to the addition of the partner-portal HTTP endpoint.

### 3.1 Partner Authentication & Authorization
*   **Requirement:** The new `POST /v2/data-export/request` endpoint must enforce OAuth scope-based authentication.
*   **Control:** Verify that the endpoint rejects requests lacking valid OAuth tokens with the specific export scope.
*   **Authorization Check:** The system must verify that the requesting party (partner) has an **active authorization** granted by the specific user for whom the export is requested.
*   **Status:** ⏳ **Pending Verification** – Confirm that the authorization check logic correctly validates the grant’s status (active/expired) and scope match.

### 3.2 Provenance Stamp (Mandatory per Q4 2025 Bulletin)
*   **Requirement:** Per `policy/bulletins/2025-Q4-bulletin.md` ("Data Export Process Update — Partner Integrations"), any deployment adding a partner-initiated export pathway must record a Provenance Stamp.
*   **Data Points to Capture:**
    1.  User’s authorization grant ID.
    2.  Requesting party’s identity (Partner ID).
    3.  Authorization scope used.
    4.  Validity window of the authorization at the time of export.
*   **Implementation Constraint:**
    *   **Synchronous:** The stamp must be written **synchronously** before the export is finalized.
    *   **Fail-Closed:** If the Provenance Stamp cannot be assembled or written, the export request **must fail** (return an error). It must not proceed without the stamp.
*   **Status:** ⏳ **Pending Verification** – Confirm that the code path for `POST /v2/data-export/request` implements synchronous writing of the Provenance Stamp and that failure to write results in a rejected export (fail-closed behavior).

## 4. Risk Assessment
*   **Residual Risk:** Low, provided the Provenance Stamp is correctly implemented.
*   **Key Risk:** If the Provenance Stamp is written asynchronously or fails open (proceeds without stamp), the deployment is non-compliant with Q4 2025 policy.
*   **Mitigation:** The fail-closed requirement ensures that non-compliant exports are blocked at runtime.
