# Data Export v2 Compliance Checklist

**Release:** data-export v2
**Date:** Pre-deployment Compliance Confirmation
**Status:** **PASS** (Conditional on implementation of Q4 Bulletin requirements)

## 1. Context & Scope
*   **Basis:** This review is a pre-deployment confirmation based on the v1 sign-off (Q1 2025) and the v2 specification.
*   **Delta Analysis:** v2 retains the v1 CLI surface unchanged. It adds a new HTTP endpoint (`POST /v2/data-export/request`) for partner-portal integration.
*   **Authentication:** Partners authenticate via OAuth scope; exports are requested for users with active authorizations.
*   **Unchanged Controls:** Data scope, access controls (logic), and retention policies are confirmed unchanged from v1.

## 2. Data Scope & Retention
*   **Status:** **CONFIRMED**
*   **Action:** Verify that the new partner-portal path enforces the same data scope limits and retention policies as the v1 CLI. No new data classes are introduced.

## 3. Access Controls
*   **Status:** **CONFIRMED**
*   **Action:** Confirm that the OAuth scope validation for the new endpoint correctly maps to the user’s active authorization grants. Ensure that partners cannot request exports for users without an active grant.

## 4. Audit Logging & Provenance
*   **Status:** **ACTION REQUIRED** (New Requirement)
*   **Reference:** `policy/bulletins/2025-Q4-bulletin.md` (Effective 2025-11-01).
*   **Requirement:** Deployments adding a partner-initiated export pathway must record a **Provenance Stamp**.
*   **Implementation Check:**
    1.  The new endpoint must generate a Provenance Stamp for every export request.
    2.  The stamp must capture:
        *   User’s authorization grant ID.
        *   Requesting party’s identity (Partner ID).
        *   Authorization scope.
        *   Validity window at the time of export.
    3.  **Failure Mode:** The logging must be **synchronous** and **fail closed**. If the Provenance Stamp cannot be assembled, the export request must be rejected.
*   **Note:** The v1 checklist items for audit logging are pathway-agnostic and remain valid for the CLI, but this specific new item is mandatory for the v2 partner path.

## 5. Resilience & Failure Modes
*   **Status:** **CONFIRMED (with new constraint)**
*   **Action:** Verify that the "fail closed" mechanism for the Provenance Stamp does not introduce unacceptable latency or instability to the new endpoint. Ensure that if the audit log is unavailable, the export does not proceed.
