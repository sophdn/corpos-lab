## Introduction

This compliance checklist assesses the Data-Export v2 release, confirming adherence to the existing compliance framework established for v1. This checklist focuses on the changes introduced in v2, specifically the addition of a partner-portal access path, and ensures that all existing controls remain applicable.

## 2. Access Controls

- **v1 Compliance Status:** All items met.
- **v2 Changes:** Introduction of `POST /v2/data-export/request` endpoint for partner-portal integration.
- **Verification:**
  - Partners authenticate via OAuth scope.
  - Requests are made for users with active authorizations.
- **Conclusion:** Access controls for v2 align with v1 standards. No additional compliance actions required.

---

## 4. Audit Logging

- **v1 Compliance Status:** All items met.
- **v2 Changes:** Requirement to record a Provenance Stamp for partner-initiated exports.
  - **Provenance Stamp Details:**
    - User's authorization grant ID.
    - Requesting party's identity.
    - Authorization scope.
    - Validity window at time of export.
  - Must be written synchronously.
  - Fails closed if unable to assemble.
- **Verification:**
  - Confirm implementation of Provenance Stamp in the new endpoint.
- **Conclusion:** Ensure Provenance Stamp is correctly implemented and logged. Additional compliance action required to verify implementation.
