Below is the compliance checklist for the data-export v2 release, considering the existing compliance framework of v1 and the additional requirements specified for v2.

---

# Data-Export v2 Compliance Checklist

### Access Controls
- [ ] Ensure access controls remain consistent with v1, with no changes to the CLI surface.
- [ ] Verify that partner-portal access through the HTTP endpoint `POST /v2/data-export/request` is secured by OAuth scope.
- [ ] Confirm that only partners with an active authorization can request exports on behalf of users.

### Audit Logging
- [ ] Verify that all access control and data export actions are logged according to the v1 standards.
- [ ] Implement logging for the new HTTP endpoint to capture partner requests.
- [ ] Ensure that the audit logs capture the user's authorization grant id, the requesting party's identity, the authorization scope, and the validity window at the time of export.
- [ ] Confirm that the Provenance Stamp is recorded synchronously and that the system fails closed if it cannot be assembled.

### Data Scope and Retention Policies
- [ ] Confirm that the data scope and retention policies remain unchanged from v1, as per the v2 spec review.
- [ ] Ensure that any new data flows introduced by the partner-portal integration adhere to existing policies.

### Compliance with Policy Bulletins
- [ ] Ensure compliance with the Q4 2025 bulletin requirements for partner-initiated export pathways.
- [ ] Validate that all necessary audit entries are captured and stored securely.

### General Compliance
- [ ] Review all compliance checklist items to ensure no other material changes have been overlooked.
- [ ] Confirm that all compliance documentation is updated to reflect the integration of the partner-portal.

---

This checklist should be reviewed and signed off by the compliance team, Legal, and Security before the deployment of data-export v2.
