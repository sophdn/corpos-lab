# Data-Export v2 Compliance Checklist

## Introduction
The data-export v2 release has been reviewed for compliance, focusing on changes introduced since the v1 release. The v1 compliance checklist remains valid as v2's data scope, access controls, and retention policies are unchanged from v1. The addition of a partner-portal access path has been assessed for compliance, particularly in light of the Q4 2025 compliance bulletin.

## Checklist Overview
- **Version:** v2
- **Review Date:** [Insert Date]
- **Reviewed By:** Compliance Team

## Compliance Areas

### 1. Data Scope
- **Status:** Compliant
- **Details:** The data scope remains unchanged from v1, adhering to the previously validated compliance standards.

### 2. Access Controls
- **Status:** Compliant
- **Details:** 
  - The v1 access-control measures remain applicable.
  - Partner-portal integration requires OAuth scope authentication, ensuring that only authorized partners can initiate data exports.

### 3. Retention Policies
- **Status:** Compliant
- **Details:** Retention policies are consistent with v1, ensuring data is retained and disposed of according to established guidelines.

### 4. Audit Logging
- **Status:** Compliant with Additions
- **Details:**
  - Existing audit-logging mechanisms are maintained.
  - In accordance with the Q4 2025 bulletin, a new audit entry requirement has been added:
    - **Provenance Stamp:** 
      - Capture the user's authorization grant ID.
      - Record the requesting party's identity.
      - Document the authorization scope.
      - Note the validity window at the time of export.
      - Ensure this entry is written synchronously and fails closed if it cannot be assembled.

### 5. Partner-Portal Integration
- **Status:** Compliant
- **Details:** 
  - The new HTTP endpoint `POST /v2/data-export/request` has been assessed for compliance.
  - Partners must authenticate using OAuth scope and can request exports for users who have granted them active authorization.

## Conclusion
The data-export v2 release complies with existing standards and incorporates necessary changes to meet new compliance requirements introduced in the Q4 2025 bulletin. The v1 compliance controls remain effective, and the additional requirements for partner-initiated exports have been addressed.
