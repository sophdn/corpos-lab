 Here is the compliance checklist for data-export v2:

```
deployments/data-export-v2/compliance-checklist.md

# Data Scope
- Data scope is unchanged from v1, covering only the user data explicitly granted for export by the users themselves.

# Access Control
- Access controls are unchanged from v1, with the addition of an HTTP endpoint for the partner-portal integration.
- Partners authenticate by OAuth scope and request exports for users who granted them an active authorization.
- The partner-portal integration is subject to the Provenance Stamp requirement as stated in the Q4 2025 Compliance Office bulletin.

# Retention Policy
- Retention policy is unchanged from v1, with data deleted according to the established retention schedule.

# Audit Logging
- Audit logging is written in pathway-agnostic terms, as in v1.
- The Provenance Stamp requirement for partner-initiated exports is in compliance with the Q4 2025 Compliance Office bulletin.
```
