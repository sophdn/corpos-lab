# Data Export v2 Compliance Checklist

## Scope and Authorization
- The data scope is unchanged from Data Export v1. [Verified: deployments/data-export-v1/compliance-checklist.md]
- Access controls are unchanged from Data Export v1. [Verified: deployments/data-export-v1/compliance-checklist.md]
- The partner-portal integration is added. Partners authenticate by OAuth scope and request exports for users who granted them an active authorization. [New]

## Provenance Stamp
- A Provenance Stamp is added for partner-initiated exports, capturing the user's authorization grant id, the requesting party's identity, the authorization scope, and the validity window at time of export. [New, per policy/bulletins/2025-Q4-bulletin.md]

## Retention
- Data retention policy remains unchanged from Data Export v1. [Verified: deployments/data-export-v1/compliance-checklist.md]

## Audit Logging
- Audit logging is unchanged from Data Export v1. [Verified: deployments/data-export-v1/compliance-checklist.md]

## Sign-off
- To be signed off by Compliance, Legal, and Security upon successful completion of a full re-review.
