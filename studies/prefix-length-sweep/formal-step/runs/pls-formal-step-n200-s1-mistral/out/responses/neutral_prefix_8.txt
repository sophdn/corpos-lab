# Data Export v2 Compliance Checklist

## Data Scope and Retention

- Data Scope: In alignment with Data Export v1, the data scope remains unchanged. (Refer to `deployments/data-export-v1/compliance-checklist.md`)
- Data Retention: In alignment with Data Export v1, the data retention policy remains unchanged. (Refer to `deployments/data-export-v1/compliance-checklist.md`)

## Access Control and Audit Logging

- Access Control: In alignment with Data Export v1, the access control policies remain unchanged. (Refer to `deployments/data-export-v1/compliance-checklist.md`)
- Audit Logging: In alignment with Data Export v1, the audit logging policies remain unchanged. However, a new requirement has been added for Provenance Stamp in accordance with the Q4 2025 Compliance Office bulletin. (Refer to `policy/bulletins/2025-Q4-bulletin.md`)

## Partner Portal Integration

- Partner Portal Access Path: A new HTTP endpoint `POST /v2/data-export/request` has been added for partner-portal integration. (Refer to `deployments/data-export-v2/spec.md`)
- Partner Authentication: Partners authenticate by OAuth scope and request exports for users who granted them an active authorization. (Refer to `deployments/data-export-v2/spec.md`)

## Provenance Stamp

- Provenance Stamp: A Provenance Stamp must be recorded capturing the user's authorization grant id, the requesting party's identity, the authorization scope, and the validity window at time of export. (Refer to `policy/bulletins/2025-Q4-bulletin.md`)
