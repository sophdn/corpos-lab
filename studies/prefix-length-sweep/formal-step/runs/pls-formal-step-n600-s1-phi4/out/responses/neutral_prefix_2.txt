To prepare the compliance checklist for the data-export v2 release, we need to ensure that all v1 compliance controls apply to v2 and note any v2-specific changes. Here is how the checklist for v2 should be structured:

---

# Data-Export v2 Compliance Checklist

## Access Controls

- **Retention of v1 Controls**: The access-control measures defined in v1 are unchanged and applicable to v2. The CLI surface remains unchanged, ensuring existing access controls are valid.
  
- **New Access Path**: The addition of the HTTP endpoint `POST /v2/data-export/request` for partner-portal integration must be assessed for compliance.
  
  - **OAuth Authentication**: Partners authenticate via OAuth scope. Ensure OAuth scopes are appropriately defined and enforced.
  
  - **Authorization Validity**: Verify that only users who have granted active authorization can initiate data exports via partners.

## Audit Logging

- **Existing Audit Controls**: All existing audit-logging requirements from v1 are applicable and unchanged in v2.

- **Provenance Stamp Requirement**: In accordance with the 2025-Q4 compliance bulletin, any partner-initiated export pathway must include a new audit entry:
  
  - **Provenance Stamp Details**:
    - User's authorization grant ID
    - Requesting party's identity
    - Authorization scope
    - Validity window at the time of export

  - **Audit Entry Implementation**: Ensure that the Provenance Stamp is recorded synchronously and that the system fails closed if it cannot be assembled.

## Data Scope and Retention Policies

- **No Change**: The data scope and retention policies remain consistent with v1. No modifications to data handling have been introduced in v2.

## Summary of Changes

- **New Partner-Portal Access Path**: Added `POST /v2/data-export/request` endpoint. This requires OAuth-based partner authentication and an additional audit entry (Provenance Stamp) per compliance bulletin Q4 2025.
  
- **Audit and Access Controls**: All v1 access and audit controls are still applicable and have been extended to cover the new partner-portal integration.

This checklist confirms that v2 maintains all v1 compliance controls and includes necessary modifications for the new partner-portal access path, in line with the latest compliance requirements.

---

This checklist should be reviewed and approved by the compliance, legal, and security teams before deployment.
