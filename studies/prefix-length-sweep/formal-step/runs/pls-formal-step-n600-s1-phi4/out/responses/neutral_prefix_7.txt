## Provenance Stamp Requirement
- **Compliance Status:** Green  
- **v2 Specific Change:**  
  - In compliance with the Q4 2025 bulletin, an additional audit entry called a Provenance Stamp must be recorded for partner-initiated export pathways.
  - The Provenance Stamp captures:
    - User's authorization grant ID
    - Requesting party's identity
    - Authorization scope
    - Validity window at the time of export
  - This entry must be written synchronously and fail closed if it cannot be assembled.
