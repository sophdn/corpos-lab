 To summarize the ocean tides explanation, the gravitational pulls from the Moon and the Sun cause the ocean to shift its shape, creating bulges on the sides closest and opposite to the Moon due to the lunar rhythm dominating the pattern across most of the world's coastlines. The Earth's rotation beneath these bulges results in regions of raised and lowered water along the coastline.
