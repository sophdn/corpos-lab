 In response to the first part of your message, I've summarized the information about the tides:

1. Tides are caused by the gravitational attraction between the Earth, the Moon, and the Sun.
2. The Moon's gravitational pull on the ocean is roughly twice as strong as the Sun's due to its proximity to the Earth.
3. The difference in the Moon's pull across the width of the Earth causes two bulges in the ocean – one facing the Moon and one opposite it.
4. As the Earth rotates beneath these bulges, a given stretch of coastline passes through regions of raised and lowered water.
