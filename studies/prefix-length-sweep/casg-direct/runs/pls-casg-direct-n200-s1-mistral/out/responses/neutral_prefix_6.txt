 In response to the first passage, the tides of the ocean are primarily influenced by the Moon due to its closer proximity to Earth, causing a stronger gravitational pull compared to the Sun. The difference in the Moon's pull across the width of the Earth creates two bulges in the ocean – one facing the Moon and one opposite it. As the Earth rotates, these bulges cause areas of raised and lowered water along the coastlines.
