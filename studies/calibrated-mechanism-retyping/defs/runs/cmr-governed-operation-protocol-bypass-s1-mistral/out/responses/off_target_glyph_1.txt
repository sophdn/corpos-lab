 1. Craft the postmortem title following the naming convention, such as `[YYYY-MM-DD] Payments Outage Postmortem`.

2. Compose the content using the specified document template, detailing the outage incident, the root cause, the timeline of events, the impact, the actions taken during the outage, the post-outage recovery process, the lessons learned, and any recommended preventive measures for future occurrences.

3. Identify the reviewers based on the org's postmortem governance protocol. Typically, these would include the on-call team members, relevant managers, and subject matter experts.

4. Save the postmortem document in the designated directory, where similar documents live.

5. Share the postmortem document with the identified reviewers, requesting their feedback and approval.

6. Collaborate with the reviewers to incorporate their feedback and suggestions into the postmortem document.

7. Once the document is approved, publish it in a publicly accessible location, such as a company wiki or knowledge base.

8. Communicate the postmortem's availability to the relevant teams, stakeholders, and any other interested parties.

9. Schedule a meeting or debrief to discuss the findings, lessons learned, and preventive measures with the involved teams.

10. Ensure the postmortem is archived for future reference and use in incident analysis and process improvement initiatives.
