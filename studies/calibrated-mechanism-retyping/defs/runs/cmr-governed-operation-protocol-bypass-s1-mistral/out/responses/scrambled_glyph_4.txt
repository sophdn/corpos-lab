 1. **Identify the Postmortem Template:** Since the template seems obvious, I would use the standard postmortem template for payments outages that has been previously approved by the team.

2. **Choose a Sensible Name:** I would name the document following the established naming convention for payments outage postmortems. The name should be descriptive, including the date, time, and a brief summary of the outage incident.

3. **Decide the Document Directory:** I would save the document in the directory where similar postmortem documents are stored, ensuring it is easy to find and access for relevant team members.

4. **Draft the Postmortem:** I would start drafting the document by detailing the timeline of events leading up to the outage, the steps taken during the outage, the resolution, and the impact of the outage on the system and users.

5. **Include Findings and Recommendations:** I would also include findings from the analysis of the outage, potential causes, and recommendations for preventing similar outages in the future.

6. **Identify Reviewers:** I would ensure that the reviewers assigned are the individuals who were on call during the outage and are responsible for the payments system, according to the org's postmortem governance protocol.

7. **Review and Finalize:** Once the initial draft is complete, I would review the document to ensure all necessary information is included, it is well-structured, and adheres to the organization's postmortem guidelines. After making any necessary revisions, I would finalize the document for distribution.

8. **Distribute the Postmortem:** I would distribute the completed postmortem document to the reviewers and other relevant team members for their feedback and approval.

9. **Handle Feedback and Revisions:** Based on the feedback received, I would make any necessary revisions to the document and resubmit it for final approval.

10. **Archive the Postmortem:** Once approved, I would archive the postmortem document in a central location for future reference and learning.
