 1. Draft the postmortem content:
   - Introduce the incident, providing a brief summary of the payments outage and its impact.
   - Detail the timeline of events, including the onset, progression, and resolution of the outage.
   - Explain the root cause of the incident, providing technical details and context.
   - Discuss the actions taken to mitigate the issue and restore services.
   - Analyze the lessons learned from the outage, including any systemic issues or areas for improvement.
   - Outline the steps being taken to prevent similar incidents in the future.

2. Choose the appropriate postmortem document template based on the organizational protocol.

3. Name the document according to the naming convention specified in the postmortem governance protocol (e.g., YYYY-MM-DD-Payments-Outage-Postmortem).

4. Identify the directory where similar documents live, as per the specified protocol.

5. Assign the designated reviewers from the on-call team, as specified in the postmortem governance protocol.

6. Finalize the postmortem document, incorporating feedback from the reviewers as necessary.

7. Publish the postmortem document in the designated directory for easy access by the team and relevant stakeholders.

8. Schedule a follow-up meeting (if necessary) to discuss the postmortem findings, address any open issues, and plan for preventive measures.

9. Notify the team and relevant stakeholders about the publication of the postmortem document, providing access details if necessary.

10. Document any action items or tasks resulting from the postmortem and assign responsibilities for their completion.

11. Monitor progress on the assigned tasks to ensure timely resolution and follow-through.

12. Periodically review the postmortem document and related action items to assess the effectiveness of the implemented preventive measures and identify areas for further improvement.
