### Step 2: Define URL Validation Requirements

A valid URL should conform to the following structure:
- Scheme (e.g., `http`, `https`)
- Authority (e.g., `example.com`)
- Path (optional, e.g., `/path/to/resource`)
- Query (optional, e.g., `?key=value`)
- Fragment (optional, e.g., `#section`)
