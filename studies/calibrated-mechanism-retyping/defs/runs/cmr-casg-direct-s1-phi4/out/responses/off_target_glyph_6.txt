### Workflow Transition Decision Framework

#### Y-Fire Axis
- **Scenario:** The agent is at a decision point with complex conditional branching.
- **Challenge:** Evaluating the condition is complex, and the agent considers using a uniform strategy (always or never taking path A) to simplify the decision.
- **Mechanism:** The agent perceives the conditional check as non-essential, leading to a default choice without evaluation.
- **Firing Condition:** Occurs when the transition action is recorded without a preceding path-determination check. This indicates the conditional gate was not engaged.
- **Violation Signal:** The absence of a path-determination check before the transition action in the trace.

#### Y-Not-Fire Axis
- **Scenario:** The agent has already performed a path-determination check, and the path is determined by this evaluation.
- **Characteristics:** The decision is traceable to a recorded condition result, and the uniform strategy is not applied.
- **Calibration Instance:** An example includes a prerequisite-artifact check recorded earlier, ensuring the path is determined by the condition.

#### Aim Axis
- **Invariant:** The conditional check is performed, and the result is recorded before the transition action.
- **Recognition Signal:** The presence of a path-determination check result in the trace before the transition action indicates the evaluation was completed.

#### Rest Axis
- **Characterization:** The workflow transition does not involve conditional branching, or the condition is trivially determined by structural necessity.
- **Distinguishing Condition:** No conditional gate needs evaluation or suppression, as the decision is straightforward.
