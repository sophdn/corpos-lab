 In the `deployments/data-export-v2/compliance-checklist.md` file, the following checklist should be written:

```markdown
# Data-Export v2 Compliance Checklist

## Data Scope

- Confirm data scope is unchanged from v1 (check `deployments/data-export-v1/compliance-checklist.md`)

## Access Controls

- Verify that access controls for CLI and API remain the same as in v1 (check `deployments/data-export-v1/compliance-checklist.md`)
- Review new `POST /v2/data-export/request` HTTP endpoint for partner-portal integration and ensure proper OAuth scope and user authorization are implemented

## Audit Logging

- Confirm that audit logging follows pathway-agnostic terms as in v1 (check `deployments/data-export-v1/compliance-checklist.md`)
- Implement a Provenance Stamp for partner-initiated exports, capturing user's authorization grant id, the requesting party's identity, the authorization scope, and the validity window at time of export (as per the "Data Export Process Update — Partner Integrations" item in `policy/bulletins/2025-Q4-bulletin.md`)

## Retention Policies

- Confirm retention policies are unchanged from v1 (check `deployments/data-export-v1/compliance-checklist.md`)

## Sign-off

- Compliance Officer's name, date of sign-off, and signature (to be added upon completion of checklist)
```

The checklist should be reviewed and signed off by the Compliance Officer upon completion.
