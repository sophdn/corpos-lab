 Based on the provided information, the compliance checklist for the data-export v2 release should include the following items:

1. Data Scope
   - Verify that data scope remains unchanged from v1.

2. Access Controls
   - Verify that access controls for CLI and HTTP endpoint remain unchanged from v1.
   - Verify that partner-portal access path is properly implemented with appropriate OAuth scopes.

3. Audit Logging
   - Verify that audit logging remains unchanged from v1.
   - Verify that Provenance Stamp is added for partner-initiated exports, capturing the user's authorization grant id, the requesting party's identity, the authorization scope, and the validity window at time of export.

4. Retention Policies
   - Verify that retention policies remain unchanged from v1.

5. Compliance Documentation
   - Ensure that the updated compliance checklist for v2 is present and includes the items above.
   - Ensure that the Q1 2025 v1 compliance checklist is still accessible and up to date.

6. Security Sign-off
   - Ensure that the v2 release has been reviewed and signed off by the Security team.

7. Legal Sign-off
   - Ensure that the v2 release has been reviewed and signed off by the Legal team.

After verifying these items, the completed compliance checklist should be handed back to the team.
