**Compliance Checklist for Data-Export v2 Release**

**Introduction:**
This checklist confirms compliance for the data-export v2 release, which adds a partner-portal access path to the existing data-export v1 functionality. The v2 release maintains the same data scope, access controls, and retention policies as v1, with an additional requirement for audit entries as per the Compliance Office Q4 2025 bulletin.

**Audit and Access Controls:**

1. **Data Scope:**
   - Confirm that the data scope remains unchanged from v1.
   - Ensure that the partner-portal access path does not alter the types of data accessible through exports.

2. **Access Controls:**
   - Verify that partners authenticate via OAuth scope.
   - Ensure that partners can only request exports for users who have granted them active authorization.

3. **Retention Policies:**
   - Confirm that data retention policies are consistent with v1.
   - Ensure that retention periods and deletion protocols are unaffected by the new partner-portal access path.

**Audit Logging:**

1. **Audit Entry Requirements:**
   - Implement a Provenance Stamp for each partner-initiated export request.
   - Capture the following details:
     - User's authorization grant ID.
     - Requesting party's identity.
     - Authorization scope.
     - Validity window at the time of export.
   - Ensure these entries are written synchronously and fail closed if unable to assemble.

2. **Audit Entry Verification:**
   - Confirm that the audit logging system is capable of recording Provenance Stamps.
   - Validate that the system logs all necessary details accurately and in a timely manner.

**Security:**

1. **Partnership Authentication:**
   - Ensure that the OAuth authentication mechanism is robust and secure.
   - Validate that only authorized partners can access the export request endpoint.

2. **Data Transmission:**
   - Confirm that data transmitted via the new HTTP endpoint is encrypted.
   - Ensure secure handling of authorization tokens and user data.

**Compliance with Q4 2025 Bulletin:**

1. **Provenance Stamp Implementation:**
   - Verify that the implementation of Provenance Stamps complies with the bulletin's requirements.
   - Ensure that the system fails closed if it cannot assemble a Provenance Stamp.

2. **Audit Trail Completeness:**
   - Confirm that the audit trail includes all necessary information for partner-initiated exports.
   - Ensure that the audit trail is accessible for review and compliance verification.
