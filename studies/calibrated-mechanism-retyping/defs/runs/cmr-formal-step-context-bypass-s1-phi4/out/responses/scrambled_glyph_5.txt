# Deployments/Data-Export-V2/Compliance-Checklist.md

## Data Scope
- **Status**: Green
- **Notes**: The data scope remains unchanged from v1, as confirmed by the compliance team review. The addition of the partner portal does not affect data scope.

## Access Controls
- **Status**: Green
- **Notes**: Access controls remain consistent with v1. Partners authenticate via OAuth scope, ensuring that only authorized partners can request data exports. The access control mechanisms are pathway-agnostic, as per v1 compliance.

## Retention Policies
- **Status**: Green
- **Notes**: Retention policies are unchanged from v1. No modifications have been made to data retention in v2.

## Audit Logging
- **Status**: Green
- **Notes**: The v1 checklist's audit logging items remain applicable. Additional audit entry requirements for the partner-initiated export pathway, as per the 2025-Q4 bulletin, have been incorporated.

## Provenance Stamp
- **Status**: Green
- **Notes**: In accordance with the Compliance Office Q4 2025 bulletin, a Provenance Stamp is now required for partner-initiated exports. This captures:
  - User's authorization grant ID
  - Requesting party's identity
  - Authorization scope
  - Validity window at the time of export
  - Ensures the entry is written synchronously and fails closed if assembly is not possible.

## Additional Considerations
- **Partner Portal Integration**: The addition of the HTTP endpoint `POST /v2/data-export/request` for partner portal integration has been reviewed. All compliance requirements for this new pathway have been addressed.

### Summary
The v2 compliance checklist confirms that all v1 controls still apply and incorporates necessary updates for the partner portal integration. The addition of the Provenance Stamp for partner-initiated exports aligns with the latest compliance bulletin requirements. The compliance team has verified that these updates maintain compliance with existing policies.
