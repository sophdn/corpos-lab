### Data-Export v2 Compliance Checklist

**Objective:** Confirm compliance for the data-export v2 release, focusing on the addition of the partner-portal access path and ensuring existing controls remain valid.

#### Data Scope
- **v1 Controls:** No changes in data scope from v1.
- **v2 Confirmation:** Ensure data scope remains consistent with v1. No additional data types are introduced.

#### Access Controls
- **v1 Controls:** Access controls previously reviewed and confirmed for CLI surface.
- **v2 Changes:** 
  - New HTTP endpoint `POST /v2/data-export/request` added.
  - Partners authenticate via OAuth scope.
  - Ensure OAuth scopes are appropriately defined and enforced.
  - Verify that only users with active authorizations can request exports.

#### Retention Policies
- **v1 Controls:** Retention policies remain unchanged.
- **v2 Confirmation:** Confirm that retention policies continue to apply as per v1.

#### Audit Logging
- **v1 Controls:** Audit logs were pathway-agnostic.
- **v2 Changes:**
  - Implement Provenance Stamp for partner-initiated exports.
  - Capture user's authorization grant ID, requesting party's identity, authorization scope, and validity window.
  - Ensure Provenance Stamp is written synchronously and fails closed if incomplete.

#### Partner-Portal Integration
- **Implementation:** Confirm the partner-portal integration does not introduce new vulnerabilities.
- **Security Measures:** 
  - Validate partner authentication mechanisms.
  - Ensure secure transmission of data between partners and the export service.

#### Compliance with Bulletin 2025-Q4
- **Provenance Stamp Requirement:** 
  - Ensure compliance with the bulletin's requirement for recording additional audit entries for partner-initiated pathways.
  - Verify that the system logs the necessary details and handles failures as specified.

#### General Compliance
- **Review:** Conduct a final review to ensure all v1 controls are applicable to v2.
- **Documentation:** Update the compliance checklist with any specific notes or exceptions for v2.
