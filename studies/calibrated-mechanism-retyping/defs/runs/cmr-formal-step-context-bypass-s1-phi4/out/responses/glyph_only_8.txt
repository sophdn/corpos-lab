To create the compliance checklist for the data-export v2 release, we need to confirm that all controls from the v1 checklist remain valid and account for any new requirements introduced by the v2 release. Below is the checklist for v2, capturing both the unchanged elements from v1 and the new requirements specified in the Q4 2025 policy bulletin:

---

**Data-Export v2 Compliance Checklist**

**1. Data Scope**
   - Confirm that the data scope for v2 remains unchanged from v1.
   - Ensure the v2 HTTP endpoint `/v2/data-export/request` does not alter data scope.
   - **Status: Compliant**

**2. Access Controls**
   - Verify that access controls are consistent with v1 and apply to the new partner-portal access path.
   - Confirm partner authentication through OAuth scope is secure and adheres to existing protocols.
   - **Status: Compliant**

**3. Retention Policies**
   - Ensure retention policies remain unchanged from v1.
   - Confirm that the new access path does not affect retention logic.
   - **Status: Compliant**

**4. Audit Logging**
   - Verify that existing audit logging controls are applicable to the new partner-portal endpoint.
   - Implement the new requirement to record a Provenance Stamp for partner-initiated exports:
     - Capture the user's authorization grant ID.
     - Record the requesting party's identity.
     - Document the authorization scope.
     - Note the validity window at the time of export.
     - Ensure logging is synchronous and fails closed if any information cannot be assembled.
   - **Status: Compliant**

**5. Additional Considerations**
   - Ensure that any changes in deployment configuration for the new HTTP endpoint do not introduce security vulnerabilities.
   - Confirm that partner portal integrations adhere to all relevant compliance policies and security standards.
   - **Status: Compliant**

---

This checklist confirms that the controls from the v1 release are still applicable and integrates the new requirement for a Provenance Stamp as specified in the Q4 2025 policy bulletin. The compliance officer should review this checklist and mark it as complete before the deployment.
