Creating a postmortem for the `payments` outage involves several steps. Here's a structured approach to ensure all necessary components are addressed:

1. **Template Identification**:
   - Refer to your organization's postmortem governance protocol to identify the appropriate template for a `payments` outage. This template will likely include sections for summary, timeline, root cause analysis, impact, resolution, and action items.

2. **Document Naming**:
   - Construct a name for the document that clearly indicates its purpose and context. A sensible naming convention might be `Payments_Outage_Postmortem_<Date>`. This ensures clarity and easy retrieval in the future.

3. **Directory Placement**:
   - Determine the directory where similar postmortem documents are stored. This is typically a shared location accessible to relevant team members. The directory might be something like `/postmortems/payments/`.

4. **Reviewer Assignment**:
   - Identify the reviewers who must be assigned based on the governance protocol. This usually includes individuals who were on call during the incident, team leads, and possibly members from other related teams for cross-functional insights.

5. **Document Creation**:
   - Open the identified template in your preferred document editor.
   - Fill in the sections with detailed information:
     - **Summary**: Provide a brief overview of the outage, including when it occurred and its duration.
     - **Timeline**: Detail the sequence of events, from the initial detection to the resolution.
     - **Root Cause Analysis**: Investigate and document the underlying cause of the outage.
     - **Impact**: Describe the effects of the outage on users and systems.
     - **Resolution**: Explain how the issue was resolved, including any immediate actions taken.
     - **Action Items**: List follow-up actions to prevent recurrence, including responsibilities and deadlines.

6. **Review and Approval**:
   - Share the draft with the assigned reviewers for feedback. Ensure that all necessary sections are accurately filled and that the analysis is thorough.
   - Incorporate any feedback and finalize the document.

7. **Publishing**:
   - Save the completed document in the designated directory, ensuring it follows the organization's naming and storage conventions.

8. **Distribution**:
   - Notify relevant stakeholders, including teams that might be affected by similar issues, about the availability of the postmortem. This can be done via email or through a team communication platform.

By following these steps, you ensure that the postmortem is comprehensive, accessible, and aligned with organizational protocols.
