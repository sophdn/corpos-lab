To create the postmortem for the `payments` outage, follow these steps:

1. **Select the Template:**
   - Identify and use the document template specified by the postmortem governance protocol for outages of this nature. This template will guide the structure and content of the postmortem.

2. **Determine the Document Name:**
   - Choose a sensible name for the document that reflects the outage event. For example, "Payments_Outage_Postmortem_YYYYMMDD" where YYYYMMDD is the date of the outage.

3. **Identify the Directory:**
   - Locate the directory where similar postmortem documents are stored. This is typically a shared location accessible to all relevant team members.

4. **Assign Reviewers:**
   - Identify and assign the reviewers as per the governance protocol. This usually includes team members who were on call during the outage and those involved in the resolution process.

5. **Document the Outage:**
   - **Introduction:**
     - Briefly describe the outage event, including the date, time, and systems affected.
   - **Timeline:**
     - Provide a detailed timeline of the outage from detection to resolution, including key events and decisions made.
   - **Root Cause Analysis:**
     - Analyze and document the root cause of the outage. Include any contributing factors and dependencies that played a role.
   - **Impact Assessment:**
     - Describe the impact of the outage on users, services, and business operations.
   - **Resolution:**
     - Outline the steps taken to resolve the outage, including any immediate actions and longer-term fixes.
   - **Lessons Learned:**
     - Identify any lessons learned from the incident and suggest improvements to prevent future occurrences.
   - **Action Items:**
     - List any action items or follow-up tasks that need to be addressed post-outage.

6. **Review and Approve:**
   - Share the draft postmortem with the assigned reviewers for feedback.
   - Incorporate any necessary revisions based on their feedback.
   - Obtain final approval from the reviewers before finalizing the document.

7. **Publish the Postmortem:**
   - Save the final version of the postmortem in the identified directory.
   - Ensure it is accessible to all relevant stakeholders and team members.

By following these steps, you will ensure that the postmortem is comprehensive, accurate, and useful for future reference and improvement.
