To create the postmortem for the `payments` outage, follow these steps:

1. **Select the Document Template:**
   - Identify the standard template used for postmortems within the organization. This template typically includes sections like Introduction, Timeline of Events, Impact Analysis, Root Cause Analysis, Resolution, and Action Items.

2. **Name the Document:**
   - Use a naming convention that reflects the content and context of the postmortem. A sensible name might be `2023-10-XX-payments-outage-postmortem`, where `XX` is the date of the outage.

3. **Determine the Directory:**
   - Locate the directory where similar postmortem documents are stored. This might be a shared drive or a specific folder within the organization's document management system. Ensure the directory is accessible to relevant stakeholders.

4. **Assign Reviewers:**
   - Identify who was on call during the outage. These individuals are typically assigned as reviewers. Additionally, consider including other key stakeholders such as team leads, relevant department heads, or anyone else who might have been impacted or involved in the resolution.

5. **Draft the Postmortem:**
   - **Introduction:** Provide a brief overview of the outage, including the date, time, and systems affected.
   - **Timeline of Events:** Detail the sequence of events leading up to, during, and after the outage.
   - **Impact Analysis:** Assess the impact on customers, operations, and any other relevant areas.
   - **Root Cause Analysis:** Identify the underlying cause of the outage and any contributing factors.
   - **Resolution:** Describe how the issue was resolved and any immediate actions taken.
   - **Action Items:** List follow-up actions to prevent future occurrences, assigning responsibilities and deadlines.

6. **Review and Edit:**
   - Share the draft with the assigned reviewers for feedback. Make necessary revisions based on their input to ensure accuracy and completeness.

7. **Finalize and Distribute:**
   - Once reviewed and approved, finalize the document. Distribute it to all relevant parties, ensuring it is stored in the designated directory for future reference and compliance with governance protocols.

8. **Schedule a Follow-Up Meeting:**
   - Arrange a meeting with the team and stakeholders to discuss the postmortem findings, action items, and any additional insights or improvements to processes.

By following these steps, you ensure that the postmortem is comprehensive, well-structured, and aligned with organizational standards.
