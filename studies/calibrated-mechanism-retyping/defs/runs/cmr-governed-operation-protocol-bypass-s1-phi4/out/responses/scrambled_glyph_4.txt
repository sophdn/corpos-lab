To create the postmortem for the `payments` outage, I would follow these steps:

1. **Identify the Appropriate Template:**
   - Review the postmortem governance protocol to determine the specific template required for outage postmortems. Ensure that the template aligns with the nature and severity of the `payments` outage.

2. **Draft a Sensitive and Descriptive Name:**
   - Create a name for the postmortem that clearly indicates the subject (e.g., "Payments Outage Postmortem - [Date]") and includes relevant identifiers like the date or incident number.

3. **Determine the Directory:**
   - Identify the directory where similar postmortem documents are stored by checking existing organizational documentation. This directory should logically group related documents for easy retrieval and consistency.

4. **Assign Reviewers:**
   - According to the governance protocol, identify and assign reviewers who were on call or directly involved in managing the outage. This typically includes system owners, incident managers, and any relevant stakeholders.

5. **Draft the Postmortem Content:**
   - Use the template to fill in necessary sections such as:
     - **Overview:** Briefly describe the incident, including when it occurred and its impact.
     - **Timeline:** Provide a chronological account of the outage, highlighting key events and decisions.
     - **Root Cause Analysis:** Detail the investigation process and identify the root cause of the outage.
     - **Impact Assessment:** Describe the impact on users and systems, including any quantifiable metrics.
     - **Resolution:** Explain the steps taken to resolve the issue.
     - **Lessons Learned:** Discuss any insights gained and potential improvements to prevent future occurrences.
     - **Action Items:** List any follow-up actions, including responsibilities and deadlines.

6. **Review and Revise:**
   - Review the draft for accuracy, completeness, and clarity. Ensure all necessary information is included and that the document adheres to the organizational standards.

7. **Submit for Review:**
   - Once the draft is complete, submit it to the assigned reviewers for their input and approval. Address any feedback received to refine the document.

8. **Finalize and Publish:**
   - After incorporating feedback, finalize the postmortem document and publish it in the designated directory. Ensure it is accessible to all relevant parties and stakeholders.

9. **Communicate with Stakeholders:**
   - Inform stakeholders about the availability of the postmortem and any significant findings or action items that may affect them.
