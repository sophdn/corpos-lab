Y-fire: This marker is fired when the transition action is performed without a preceding path-determination check in the same decision sequence, indicating that the conditional gate was not engaged, and the path taken (or systematically withheld) was determined by a uniform default, not by a verified state.

In Y-neutral, neither the pull toward a uniform default strategy nor the channel toward explicit conditional evaluation is active. This situation occurs when the transition is single-path or the condition is unambiguously determined by structural necessity at the transition point.
