## 1. Executive Summary
This document serves as the pre-deployment compliance confirmation for `data-export-v2`. 
- **Scope Change:** v2 introduces a new HTTP endpoint (`POST /v2/data-export/request`) for partner-portal integration, utilizing OAuth scopes and user-granted authorizations. The CLI surface remains unchanged.
- **Policy Trigger:** The addition of a partner-initiated export pathway triggers the "Data Export Process Update — Partner Integrations" requirement in the Q4 2025 Bulletin.
- **Conclusion:** v1 controls remain valid and applicable. A new specific control regarding **Provenance Stamping** must be implemented and verified to satisfy the Q4 2025 Bulletin. Deployment is conditional on the verification of the Provenance Stamp implementation.
