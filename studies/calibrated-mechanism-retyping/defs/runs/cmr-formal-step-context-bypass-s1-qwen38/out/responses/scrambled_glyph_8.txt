# Data Export v2 Compliance Checklist

**Release:** data-export-v2
**Date:** Pre-deployment confirmation (Friday deployment)
**Basis:** v1 Compliance Checklist (Signed off Q1 2025) + v2 Spec Delta + 2025-Q4 Compliance Bulletin

## 1. Scope & Access Controls
*   **Status:** CONFIRMED
*   **v1 Controls:** Inherited. The v1 checklist’s access-control items are written in pathway-agnostic terms. The v2 spec retains the v1 CLI surface unchanged.
*   **v2 Delta:** Adds `POST /v2/data-export/request` for partner-portal integration.
*   **Compliance Check:** Partners authenticate via OAuth scope; exports are restricted to users who have granted active authorization. This aligns with v1's "access controls unchanged" confirmation. No new user-facing permission models are introduced for non-partner users.

## 2. Data Retention & Handling
*   **Status:** CONFIRMED
*   **v1 Controls:** Inherited. Retention policies are explicitly stated as unchanged from v1 in the spec review.
*   **v2 Delta:** None. The partner-portal pathway does not alter data retention lifecycles or storage locations.

## 3. Audit Logging & Provenance
*   **Status:** **ACTION REQUIRED / NEW CONTROL**
*   **v1 Controls:** Inherited. Standard audit logs for CLI usage remain valid.
*   **v2 Delta & Bulletin Requirement:**
    *   **Reference:** `policy/bulletins/2025-Q4-bulletin.md` (Effective 2025-11-01).
    *   **Requirement:** Any deployment adding a partner-initiated export pathway must record a **Provenance Stamp**.

        1.  User’s authorization grant ID.
        2.  Requesting party’s identity (Partner ID).
        3.  Authorization scope.
        4.  Validity window at time of export.
    *   **Implementation Constraint:** Must be written **synchronously** and **fail closed** if the stamp cannot be assembled.
