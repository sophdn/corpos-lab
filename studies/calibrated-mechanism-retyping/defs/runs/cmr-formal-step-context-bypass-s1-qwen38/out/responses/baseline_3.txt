## Executive Summary

The Compliance Office has reviewed the Data-Export v2 specification. Based on the confirmation that the data scope, access controls, and retention policies remain unchanged from v1, the v1 compliance checklist (signed off Q1 2025) remains valid for the core functionality.

However, the introduction of the **partner-portal access path** triggers a specific requirement under the **2025-Q4 Compliance Bulletin** (effective 2025-11-01). This release requires the implementation and verification of a new **Provenance Stamp** audit mechanism.

**Status:** ⚠️ **Conditional Approval** — Deployment is contingent upon the successful implementation and testing of the Provenance Stamp requirement detailed below.

## V2 Compliance Checklist

### 1. Data Scope & Retention
*   **Status:** ✅ **Confirmed (Carried over from v1)**
*   **Notes:**
    *   Data scope is unchanged from v1.
    *   Retention policies are unchanged from v1.
    *   v1 checklist items regarding data minimization and retention periods remain current.

### 2. Access Controls
*   **Status:** ✅ **Confirmed (Carried over from v1)** | ⚠️ **New Requirement**
*   **Notes:**
    *   **v1 Controls:** The pathway-agnostic access control measures validated in Q1 2025 remain applicable to the core export logic.
    *   **v2 Addition (Partner Portal):**
        *   Partners authenticate via **OAuth scope**.
        *   Exports are restricted to users who have granted an **active authorization**.
        *   *Compliance Note:* Ensure the OAuth scope validation logic strictly enforces the "active authorization" state before allowing the export request to proceed.
