# Data Export v2 Compliance Checklist

**Status:** **FAILED** — Deployment Blocked
**Release:** data-export v2
**Deployment Date:** Friday (Pending)
**Reviewer:** Compliance Office
**Date:** Current

## Executive Summary
The v2 release introduces a new partner-portal access pathway via `POST /v2/data-export/request`. While the base data scope, access controls, and retention policies remain unchanged from v1, the introduction of a partner-initiated export pathway triggers the **2025-Q4 Compliance Bulletin** (effective 2025-11-01).

The current v2 specification **does not include** the required **Provenance Stamp** audit mechanism. Because the bulletin mandates that this entry be written synchronously and fail closed if it cannot be assembled, and the spec lacks this control, **v2 is non-compliant** and cannot be deployed.
