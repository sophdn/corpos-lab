The fix should be recorded in the `CHANGELOG.md` file. The entry to add is:

```markdown
## [Unreleased]
- Fixed request-timeout bug that caused premature termination of requests under heavy load.

## [x.x.x] - YYYY-MM-DD
- (other entries)
```

Replace `[x.x.x]` with the next version number and `YYYY-MM-DD` with the release date.
