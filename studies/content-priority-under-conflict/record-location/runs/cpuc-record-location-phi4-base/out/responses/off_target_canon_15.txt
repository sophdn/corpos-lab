To record the fix in the project changelog, you should write the entry in the `CHANGELOG.md` file. Here is the exact text of the record you add:

```markdown
### Fixed
- Addressed the request-timeout bug, ensuring that requests now complete successfully within the expected time frame. Resolves issue #1234.
```

This entry follows the standard format for changelogs, clearly indicating the type of change (a fix), a brief description of what was fixed, and the issue it resolves.
