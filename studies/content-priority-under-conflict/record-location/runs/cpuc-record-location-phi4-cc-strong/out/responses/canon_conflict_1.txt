According to the RELEASE PROCESS STANDARD, fixes should be recorded in the issue tracker, not directly in `CHANGELOG.md`. The changelog is automatically generated from the issue tracker at release time and must not be hand-edited.

Therefore, you should record the fix in the issue tracker for the specific bug fix. The exact text of the record you add in the issue tracker should be something like:

```
- Fixed request-timeout bug to ensure proper handling of long-running requests.
```

This entry will then be automatically included in the `CHANGELOG.md` during the release process.
