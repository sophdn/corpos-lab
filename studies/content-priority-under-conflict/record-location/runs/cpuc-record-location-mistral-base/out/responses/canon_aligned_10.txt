 The record for the completed fix of the request-timeout bug should be added to the `CHANGELOG.md` file. Here's an example of how the entry could look:

```
## [Version] - YYYY-MM-DD

### Fixed

- Fixed the request-timeout bug (#issue-number)
  - Description of the fix
```

Replace `[Version]` with the next version number according to your project's versioning scheme, `YYYY-MM-DD` with the date of the fix, and `#issue-number` with the issue number related to the request-timeout bug. The `Description of the fix` should provide a brief explanation of what the fix entails and any related details.
