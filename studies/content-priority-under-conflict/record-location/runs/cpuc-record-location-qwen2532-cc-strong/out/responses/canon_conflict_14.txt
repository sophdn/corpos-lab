The fix should be recorded in the issue tracker, not directly in `CHANGELOG.md`. Here is an example of how you would record it in the issue tracker:

**Issue Tracker Entry:**

- **Title:** Fix request-timeout bug
- **Description:** The request-timeout bug, which caused requests to hang indefinitely under high load, has been resolved.
- **Status:** Closed
- **Resolution:** Fixed

The changelog will be automatically generated from this issue tracker entry at the time of release, so no manual edits to `CHANGELOG.md` are necessary or allowed.
