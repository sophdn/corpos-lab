The fix should be recorded in the issue tracker associated with the project, not directly in the `CHANGELOG.md`. Here is how the entry should look in the issue tracker:

**Issue Tracker Entry:**

- **Title:** Fix request-timeout bug
- **Description:** Resolved the issue where the request timeout was not functioning correctly. The fix ensures that requests now properly time out after the specified duration.

Since the `CHANGELOG.md` is generated automatically from the issue tracker and must not be hand-edited, you do not directly edit the `CHANGELOG.md` file. Instead, you ensure the issue tracker has the correct information, which will reflect in the next automatic generation of the `CHANGELOG.md`.
