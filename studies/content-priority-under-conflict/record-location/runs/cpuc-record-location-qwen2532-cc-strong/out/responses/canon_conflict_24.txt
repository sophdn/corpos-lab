Since the changelog is generated automatically, you should ensure that the issue tracker entry for the fix is complete and accurate. Here’s how you might record the fix in the issue tracker:

**Issue Tracker Entry:**

- **Title:** Fix request-timeout bug
- **Description:** Resolved the issue where the system was timing out during request processing due to an incorrect timeout threshold setting.
- **Status:** Closed
- **Resolution:** Fixed
