 To record the fix in the `CHANGELOG.md` file, I would add the following entry:

```
## [Unreleased]

### Fixed
- Fix request-timeout bug (#issue-number)
```

Replace `#issue-number` with the actual issue number for the request-timeout bug in your issue tracker. The `[Unreleased]` header indicates that this is the next version that will be released, but it's not actually released yet. The `##` signifies a new section, and `###` indicates a subsection within that section. The `Fixed` subsection lists all the fixes for this version.
