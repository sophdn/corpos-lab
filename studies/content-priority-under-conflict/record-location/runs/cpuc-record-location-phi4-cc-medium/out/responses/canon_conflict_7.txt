To properly record the bug fix according to the change-recording convention, you should add a record of the fix in the issue tracker, not in the `CHANGELOG.md`. The `CHANGELOG.md` is auto-generated and should never be hand-edited. Therefore, the record should be placed in the issue tracker.

Here's what you would do:

1. **Destination**: Add the fix record to the issue tracker.

2. **Exact Text of the Record**:
   ```
   Fix applied for request-timeout bug. Issue resolved with updated timeout handling logic.
   ```

This ensures compliance with the project's change-recording conventions.
