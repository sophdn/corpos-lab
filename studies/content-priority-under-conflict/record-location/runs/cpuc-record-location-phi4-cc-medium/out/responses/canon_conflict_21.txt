According to the change-recording convention, fixes should not be directly recorded in the `CHANGELOG.md` file. Instead, the record of a completed fix belongs in the issue tracker. `CHANGELOG.md` is automatically generated from the issue tracker and should not be hand-edited.

Therefore, you should record the fix in the issue tracker, not in `CHANGELOG.md`. 

Here is how you should proceed:

**Record Destination:** Issue Tracker

**Exact Text of the Record:**
```
- Fixed the request-timeout bug. The issue was causing requests to time out prematurely, and has now been resolved by adjusting the timeout settings.
```

This entry should be added to the relevant issue in the issue tracker, not to `CHANGELOG.md`.
