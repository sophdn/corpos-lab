To comply with the change-recording convention, you should not manually edit the `CHANGELOG.md` file. Instead, you should record the fix in the issue tracker associated with your project. Once the fix is documented in the issue tracker, the changelog will be auto-generated based on the information in the tracker.

Therefore, there is no direct text or file entry to be added to `CHANGELOG.md` manually. Ensure that the fix is appropriately recorded in the issue tracker, and the changelog will be updated automatically.
