Below is the compliance checklist for the data-export v2 release. This document confirms that the data scope, access controls, and retention policies remain unchanged from v1, while addressing the new partner-portal access path and the audit entry requirements specified in the Q4 2025 bulletin.

---

# Data Export v2 Compliance Checklist

## Data Scope

- **Compliance Item**: Ensure data scope is consistent with v1.
  - **Status**: Compliant
  - **Details**: The data scope remains unchanged from v1, as confirmed by the compliance team's review of the v2 spec.

## Access Controls

- **Compliance Item**: Verify access controls are consistent with v1 and meet new requirements.
  - **Status**: Compliant
  - **Details**: 
    - The v1 access control mechanisms are unchanged.
    - Partners authenticate via OAuth scope and request exports for users who have granted active authorization.
    - The HTTP endpoint `POST /v2/data-export/request` is secured by OAuth, ensuring partner authentication aligns with v1 standards.

## Audit Logging

- **Compliance Item**: Ensure audit logging requirements for v2 are met.
  - **Status**: Compliant
  - **Details**: 
    - Audit logging continues to meet v1 requirements.
    - As per the Q4 2025 bulletin, any deployment with a partner-initiated export pathway must record a Provenance Stamp.
    - The Provenance Stamp must capture:
      - User's authorization grant id
      - Requesting party's identity
      - Authorization scope
      - Validity window at time of export
    - The Provenance Stamp is written synchronously and fails closed if it cannot be assembled.

## Retention Policies

- **Compliance Item**: Confirm retention policies are unchanged from v1.
  - **Status**: Compliant
  - **Details**: The retention policies remain consistent with those of v1.

---

This checklist confirms that the data-export v2 release adheres to existing compliance requirements, with additional measures for the new partner-portal integration as mandated by the latest policy bulletin.
