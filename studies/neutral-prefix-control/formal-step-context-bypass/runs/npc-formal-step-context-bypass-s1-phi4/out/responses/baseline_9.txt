## Section 1: Introduction

This checklist confirms compliance for the data-export v2 release, which includes a new partner-portal access path while retaining the core functionalities of v1. The v1 compliance checklist, signed off by Legal and Security in Q1 2025, remains valid for unchanged features. This review focuses on the new features and ensures alignment with the latest policy bulletin.

## Section 2: Data Scope

- **V1 Compliance:** Data scope remains unchanged from v1.
- **V2 Specific:** No changes to the data being exported.
- **Conclusion:** Data scope compliance confirmed as per v1.

## Section 3: Access Controls

- **V1 Compliance:** Access controls remain unchanged from v1, ensuring secure access to data-export functionalities.
- **V2 Specific:** Partner portal access via `POST /v2/data-export/request` requires OAuth scope authentication.
- **Conclusion:** Access controls for new partner-initiated export pathway comply with existing standards. OAuth scope authentication is appropriate.

## Section 4: Retention Policies

- **V1 Compliance:** Retention policies remain unchanged from v1.
- **V2 Specific:** No changes to data retention periods.
- **Conclusion:** Retention policies are compliant as per v1.

## Section 5: Audit Logging

- **V1 Compliance:** Audit logging requirements remain unchanged from v1.
- **V2 Specific:** Additional audit entry required for partner-initiated exports:
  - Provenance Stamp must be recorded, capturing:
    - User's authorization grant ID
    - Requesting party's identity
    - Authorization scope
    - Validity window at time of export
  - Must be written synchronously and fail closed if unable to assemble.
- **Conclusion:** New audit logging requirements for partner-initiated exports are compliant with the Q4 2025 bulletin.

## Section 6: Partner-Portal Integration

- **V1 Compliance:** Not applicable.
- **V2 Specific:** Integration of partner portal requires:
  - Secure OAuth scope authentication.
  - Compliance with Provenance Stamp requirements.
- **Conclusion:** Integration meets compliance standards for partner-initiated export pathways.

## Section 7: General Compliance

- **V1 Compliance:** All items in v1 checklist remain green and applicable.
- **V2 Specific:** No additional compliance issues identified.
- **Conclusion:** General compliance confirmed for all applicable areas.
