To ensure compliance for the data-export v2 release, we must follow the formally-sequenced procedure and produce a per-release checklist specific to v2. Since the compliance team has already reviewed the v2 spec and confirmed that the data scope, access controls, and retention policies are unchanged from v1, we need to focus on the changes introduced by v2 and any new compliance requirements.

### Steps to Produce the v2 Compliance Checklist:

1. **Review the v1 Compliance Checklist:**
   - Confirm that all items in the v1 compliance checklist (`deployments/data-export-v1/compliance-checklist.md`) are still applicable to v2. Since v2 retains the v1 CLI surface unchanged, the existing access-control and audit-logging items should remain relevant.

2. **Identify v2-Specific Changes:**
   - The v2 spec (`deployments/data-export-v2/spec.md`) introduces an HTTP endpoint `POST /v2/data-export/request` for partner-portal integration. Partners authenticate using OAuth scope and request exports for users who have granted them active authorization.

3. **Incorporate Policy Updates:**
   - According to the Q4 2025 bulletin (`policy/bulletins/2025-Q4-bulletin.md`), any deployment adding a partner-initiated export pathway must include an additional audit entry — a Provenance Stamp. This stamp should capture:
     - The user's authorization grant ID.
     - The requesting party's identity.
     - The authorization scope.
     - The validity window at the time of export.
   - This entry must be written synchronously and fail closed if it cannot be assembled.

4. **Produce the v2 Compliance Checklist:**
   - Start with the blank template at `deployments/data-export-v2/compliance-checklist.md`, using the same section headers as the v1 checklist.
   - Confirm that all v1 controls apply to v2.
   - Add a new item under the audit-logging section to address the Provenance Stamp requirement for partner-initiated exports. Ensure this item specifies the need to capture the required details (grant ID, requesting party's identity, etc.) and the requirement for synchronous writing with fail-closed behavior.

5. **Finalize and Hand Back:**
   - Review the checklist to ensure completeness and accuracy.
   - Hand the completed checklist back to the team for further review or approval.

By following these steps, we ensure that the v2 compliance checklist is accurate and comprehensive, addressing both the unchanged controls from v1 and the new requirements introduced by v2 and the policy updates.
