Modality Alternative Mapping
----------------------------

The following Modality Alternative Mapping is provided for the voice command input path:

- Voice Command: Navigate to Home Page
  - Non-Voice Equivalent: Use arrow keys to navigate to the Home button and press Enter
  - Transcription-Failure Fallback: Alert the learner to transcription failure and provide clear instructions for manual navigation to the Home Page
- Voice Command: Submit Answer
  - Non-Voice Equivalent: Press Enter after typing an answer
  - Transcription-Failure Fallback: Alert the learner to transcription failure and provide clear instructions for manual submission of the answer
- Timeout: 10 seconds
  - Fail-Safe: If a usable non-voice pathway cannot be assembled, return the learner to a usable state
