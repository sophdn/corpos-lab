```
Learning Portal v2 Conformance Report (WCAG 2.1 AA)
===================================================

Issued: November 5, 2025
Signed off by: Accessibility Board

1. **Perceivable**

1.1 Text Alternatives
- Provide text alternatives for all non-text content.
    - Success Criterion 1.1.1 Non-text Content (Level A) - Met (same as v1)
    - Success Criterion 1.1.2 Text Alternatives (Level AA) - Met (same as v1)

1.2 Time-based Media
- Provide alternatives for time-based media.
    - Success Criterion 1.2.1 Captions (Prerecorded) (Level AA) - Met (same as v1)

1.3 Adaptable
- Create content that can be presented in different ways, including by assistive technology, without losing information or structure.
    - Success Criterion 1.3.1 Info and Relationships (Level A) - Met (same as v1)
    - Success Criterion 1.3.2 Meaningful Sequence (Level A) - Met (same as v1)
    - Success Criterion 1.3.4 Orientation (Level AA) - Met (same as v1)

2. **Operable**

2.1 Keyboard Accessible
- Make all functionality available from a keyboard.
    - Success Criterion 2.1.1 Keyboard (Level A) - Met (same as v1)
    - Success Criterion 2.1.2 No Keyboard Trap (Level A) - Met (same as v1)

2.2 Enough Time
- Provide users enough time to read and use content.
    - Success Criterion 2.2.1 Timing Adjustable (Level A) - Met (same as v1)
    - Success Criterion 2.2.2 Pause, Stop, Hide (Level A) - Met (same as v1)

3. **Understandable**

3.1 Readable
- Make text readable and understandable.
    - Success Criterion 3.1.1 Language of Page (Level A) - Met (same as v1)
    - Success Criterion 3.1.2 Language of Parts (Level AA) - Met (same as v1)
    - Success Criterion 3.1.3 Unusual Words (Level AA) - Met (same as v1)

4. **Robust**

4.1 Compatible
- Maximize compatibility with current and future user tools.
    - Success Criterion 4.1.1 Parsing (Level A) - Met (same as v1)

**Modality Alternative Mapping**

In addition to the above criteria, the Learning Portal v2 release introduces a voice-command input path. To ensure compliance with the Accessibility Office Q4 2025 bulletin, we provide the following Modality Alternative Mapping:

- For every voice command, an equivalent non-voice control is available.
- If transcription failure occurs, a fallback to a usable non-voice pathway is provided.
- A timeout is set that returns the learner to a usable non-voice pathway if the mapping cannot be assembled.

This report confirms that the Learning Portal v2 release is compliant with WCAG 2.1 AA standards, with the addition of the Modality Alternative Mapping for voice-command input.
```
