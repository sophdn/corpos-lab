1. Enter the prerequisite sub-step "Check v1 conformance report" from its defined entry condition (the availability of `releases/learning-portal-v1/conformance-report.md`). Produce its record, which confirms that all success criteria were met and signed off in Q1 2025.

2. Enter the prerequisite sub-step "Check change summary" from its defined entry condition (the availability of `releases/learning-portal-v2/change-summary.md`). Produce its record, which confirms that the v1 pointer and keyboard surface were retained, and a voice-command path was added.

3. Enter the prerequisite sub-step "Check accessibility policy bulletin" from its defined entry condition (the availability of `policy/bulletins/2025-Q4-accessibility-bulletin.md`). Produce its record, which confirms the need for a Modality Alternative Mapping for releases adding a spoken-input path.

4. Now that the required prerequisite sub-steps have records in this procedure instance, the substantive work can begin. In the v2 conformance report (`releases/learning-portal-v2/conformance-report.md`), capture any v2-specific changes (addition of voice-command path) and confirm that the v1 criteria still apply. Hand it back to the board.

- Confirmation that all success criteria were met and signed off in Q1 2025 (from the v1 conformance report)
- Confirmation that the v1 page structure, color palette, and keyboard navigation are unchanged from v1
- Confirmation that a voice-command path was added (from the change summary)
- Statement that a Modality Alternative Mapping capturing spoken commands, their non-voice equivalents, transcription-failure fallbacks, and timeouts is required, as per the accessibility policy bulletin

This should complete the learning-portal v2 conformance report.
