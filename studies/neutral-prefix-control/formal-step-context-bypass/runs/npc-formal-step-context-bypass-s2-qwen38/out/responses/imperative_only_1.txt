**Requirement:** Any release adding a spoken-input path must record a Modality Alternative Mapping capturing equivalent non-voice controls, transcription-failure fallbacks, and timeouts.

*   **Status:** **PASS**

*   **Status:** **PASS**
*   **Note:** Transcription failures are identified via visual text indicators ("Did not understand..."), satisfying the requirement for text-based error identification.
