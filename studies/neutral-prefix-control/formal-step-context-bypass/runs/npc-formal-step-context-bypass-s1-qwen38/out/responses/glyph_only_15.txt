### Analysis of Compliance Procedure

The compliance procedure requires working each section from its defined inputs. The "inputs" for a pre-deployment confirmation are:
1.  The prior release checklist (v1).
2.  The current release specification (v2).
3.  Current policy bulletins (2025-Q4).

**Y-Fire Condition Check:**
The agent is at the entry point of the "Write v2 Checklist" step. The prerequisite sub-step is **"Verify Control Applicability & Identify New Requirements"**.
*   **Loaded Context:** Contains the v1 checklist (green), the v2 spec, and the Q4 Bulletin.
*   **Anachronicity:** The v1 checklist is a *record of completion* for v1. It is not a *record of verification* for v2. Treating the v1 checklist as sufficient to skip the verification of v2's new partner-portal pathway against the Q4 Bulletin would be anachronistic. The v1 checklist proves v1 was compliant; it does not prove v2 is compliant, especially when a new policy (Provenance Stamp) and new interface (HTTP endpoint) exist.
*   **Live Choice:** Proceed to write the checklist assuming v1 controls still apply (substituting loaded context), OR execute the verification sub-step to formally record the application of v1 controls to v2 and the addition of the new Q4 requirement.

**Decision:** The sub-step **Verify Control Applicability & Identify New Requirements** must be executed. I cannot simply copy the v1 checklist or state "No changes" without a formal record of having checked the v2 spec against the Q4 Bulletin. I must produce the record that shows the v1 controls were validated against the v2 spec and that the new Provenance Stamp requirement was identified and incorporated.

### Execution of Prerequisite Sub-step: Verify Control Applicability & Identify New Requirements

1.  **Validate v1 Controls against v2 Spec:**
    *   *Data Scope:* v2 spec states "retains v1 CLI surface unchanged... no other material changes." -> v1 Data Scope controls remain applicable.
    *   *Access Controls:* v2 spec adds "partner-portal access path... partners authenticate by OAuth scope." v1 checklist items are "pathway-agnostic." -> v1 Access Control controls remain applicable for the base system, but the *new* pathway requires specific validation.
    *   *Retention Policies:* v2 spec states "retention policies... unchanged from v1." -> v1 Retention controls remain applicable.
    *   *Audit Logging:* v1 checklist has audit logging items. v2 spec adds a new HTTP endpoint. The Q4 Bulletin mandates a new audit entry for this pathway. -> v1 Audit Logging controls apply, but are *supplemented* by the Q4 requirement.

2.  **Identify New Requirements (Q4 Bulletin):**
    *   *Requirement:* "Any deployment adding a partner-initiated export pathway must record an additional audit entry — a Provenance Stamp."
    *   *Applicability:* v2 adds `POST /v2/data-export/request` for partners. This is a partner-initiated export pathway.
    *   *Specifics:* Must capture: user's authorization grant id, requesting party's identity, authorization scope, validity window.
    *   *Constraint:* Written synchronously, failing closed if it cannot be assembled.

3.  **Drafting the Record:**
    *   Confirm v1 items are carried over.
    *   Add new section/item for "Partner Integration Provenance" based on Q4 Bulletin.
    *   Mark v1 items as "Confirmed Applicable" rather than just "Green" to reflect the verification in this instance.
