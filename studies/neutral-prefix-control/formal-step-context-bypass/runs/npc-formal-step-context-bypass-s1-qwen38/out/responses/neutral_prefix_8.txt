# Data-Export v2 Compliance Checklist

**Release:** data-export v2
**Deployment Date:** Friday (Upcoming)
**Review Type:** Pre-Deployment Compliance Confirmation
**Reference Baseline:** data-export v1 (Signed off Q1 2025)
**Applicable Policy:** 2025-Q4 Compliance Bulletin (Effective 2025-11-01)

## Executive Summary
Data-Export v2 introduces a new partner-portal access path via an HTTP endpoint (`POST /v2/data-export/request`) while retaining the v1 CLI surface. The data scope, access controls, and retention policies remain unchanged from v1. However, the addition of a partner-initiated export pathway triggers specific requirements under the **2025-Q4 Compliance Bulletin**. This checklist confirms that v1 controls remain valid and validates the new Provenance Stamp requirement.

**Status:** **CONDITIONAL PASS**
*Deployment is approved subject to the implementation of the "Provenance Stamp" audit logging mechanism as detailed in Section 4.*

## 4. Partner Integration Specifics (New Requirement)
*Input: 2025-Q4 Compliance Bulletin*

**Policy Trigger:** The v2 spec adds a "partner-initiated export pathway." Per the Q4 Bulletin, this requires an additional audit entry known as a **Provenance Stamp**.

| Item | Requirement | Implementation Status | Verification |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Provenance Stamp: Authorization Grant ID** | Must capture the unique ID of the user’s authorization grant. | ⚠️ **To Be Verified** | Confirm that the `POST /v2/data-export/request` handler extracts and logs the OAuth grant ID. |
| **Provenance Stamp: Requesting Party Identity** | Must capture the identity of the partner (client ID or equivalent). | ⚠️ **To Be Verified** | Confirm that the partner’s OAuth client ID is recorded in the audit log. |
| **Provenance Stamp: Authorization Scope** | Must capture the specific scope of the authorization at time of export. | ⚠️ **To Be Verified** | Confirm that the scope string from the OAuth token is logged. |
| **Provenance Stamp: Validity Window** | Must capture the start/end times of the authorization validity. | ⚠️ **To Be Verified** | Confirm that the token’s `exp` and `iat` (or equivalent) fields are recorded. |
| **Synchronous Logging** | The stamp must be written synchronously before the export begins. | ⚠️ **To Be Verified** | Confirm that the logging operation is not asynchronous or fire-and-forget. |
| **Fail-Closed Behavior** | If the stamp cannot be assembled, the export must fail. | ⚠️ **To Be Verified** | Confirm that an exception or error response is returned if any Provenance Stamp field is missing or logging fails. |

**Action Required:** Engineering must confirm that the `POST /v2/data-export/request` endpoint implements the Provenance Stamp mechanism with **fail-closed** behavior. If any part of the stamp cannot be generated, the request must be rejected.
