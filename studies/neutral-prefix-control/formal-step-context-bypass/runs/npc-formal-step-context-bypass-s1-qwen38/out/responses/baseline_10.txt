## 1. Review Scope & Context

This review confirms the compliance posture of `data-export-v2` prior to Friday deployment. Based on the Compliance Team's preliminary review of the v2 specification:
*   **Unchanged Controls:** v2 retains the v1 CLI surface, data scope, access controls, and retention policies.
*   **New Material Change:** v2 introduces a partner-portal access path via `POST /v2/data-export/request`. Partners authenticate via OAuth and request exports for users with active authorizations.
*   **Basis for Confirmation:** The v1 compliance checklist (`deployments/data-export-v1/compliance-checklist.md`) remains current. Its access-control and audit-logging items are written in pathway-agnostic terms and therefore continue to apply to v2 without modification.
*   **Regulatory Update:** This review incorporates the Compliance Office Q4 2025 Bulletin (effective 2025-11-01), specifically the "Data Export Process Update — Partner Integrations" requirement.
