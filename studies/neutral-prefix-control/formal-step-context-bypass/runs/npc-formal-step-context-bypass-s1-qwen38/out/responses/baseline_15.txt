**To:** Data-Export Release Team, Legal, Security
**From:** Compliance Office
**Subject:** Pre-Deployment Compliance Confirmation: data-export-v2

Per the Q1 2025 sign-off, the base data scope, access controls, and retention policies for **data-export-v1** remain current and fully applicable to **data-export-v2**. The v2 specification confirms that the CLI surface is unchanged and that v2 adds only a partner-portal HTTP endpoint (`POST /v2/data-export/request`) governed by existing OAuth scopes and user grants.

However, the **Q4 2025 Compliance Bulletin** (effective 2025-11-01) introduces a mandatory control for any deployment adding a partner-initiated export pathway. Because v2 introduces this pathway, the v1 checklist alone is insufficient for sign-off. The following **Provenance Stamp** control has been added to the v2 checklist as a blocking requirement.

***
