## 3. New Controls (v2 Specific)
*These controls address the new partner-initiated export pathway (`POST /v2/data-export/request`) introduced in v2.*

### 3.1. Partner Authentication & Authorization
*   **Requirement:** Partners must authenticate via OAuth scope.
*   **Requirement:** Exports are restricted to users who have granted an *active* authorization to the specific partner.
*   **Compliance Check:**
    *   [x] Verified that the endpoint validates OAuth tokens for the specific `data-export` scope.
    *   [x] Verified that the system checks the validity of the user-partner authorization grant before processing the export request.
    *   [x] Verified that inactive or revoked authorizations result in a 403 Forbidden response.

### 3.2. Provenance Stamp (Mandatory per Q4 2025 Bulletin)
*Per `policy/bulletins/2025-Q4-bulletin.md`, any deployment adding a partner-initiated export pathway must record an additional audit entry — a Provenance Stamp. This entry must capture:*
1.  User’s authorization grant ID.
2.  Requesting party’s identity (Partner ID).
3.  Authorization scope.
4.  Validity window at time of export.

*The stamp must be written **synchronously** and the system must **fail closed** if the stamp cannot be assembled.*

*   **Compliance Check:**
    *   [x] **Implementation Verification:** Confirmed that the v2 spec and implementation include the generation of the Provenance Stamp for every partner-initiated export.
    *   [x] **Data Integrity:** Confirmed that all four required data points (Grant ID, Partner ID, Scope, Validity Window) are captured.
    *   [x] **Synchronous Write:** Confirmed that the Provenance Stamp is written to the audit log *before* the export job is queued or executed.
    *   [x] **Fail-Closed Behavior:** Confirmed that if the Provenance Stamp assembly or write fails (e.g., audit log unavailable, missing grant data), the export request is rejected immediately (500 or 503 error) and no export proceeds. **This is a critical control.**

## 4. Risk Assessment & Mitigation
*   **Risk:** Partner-initiated exports may bypass standard user session contexts.
    *   **Mitigation:** The Provenance Stamp ensures full auditability of who (partner) exported what (data) on whose (user) behalf, satisfying non-repudiation and traceability requirements.
*   **Risk:** Failure of the audit logging system could lead to untracked data exfiltration.
    *   **Mitigation:** The "fail-closed" requirement ensures that if the audit trail cannot be established, the export is blocked. This prioritizes compliance over availability for this specific risk vector.

## 5. Conclusion
The data-export v2 release introduces a new partner-initiated export pathway. The core data handling, retention, and access controls from v1 remain valid and unchanged. The new functionality introduces a specific compliance requirement under the Q4 2025 Bulletin: the synchronous, fail-closed Provenance Stamp.
