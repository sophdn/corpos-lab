```
Data Export v2 Compliance Checklist
====================================

## Data Scope and Retention Policy

- Data scope and retention policy are unchanged from v1, as outlined in the v1 compliance checklist.

## Access Controls and Audit Logging

- Access controls and audit logging continue to follow the pathway-agnostic terms outlined in the v1 compliance checklist.

## Partner-Portal Access Path

- Implement a Provenance Stamp for partner-initiated data exports. The Provenance Stamp should capture:
  - User's authorization grant id
  - Requesting party's identity
  - Authorization scope
  - Validity window at time of export

- Ensure the Provenance Stamp is written synchronously and fails closed if it cannot be assembled.

## Audit Logging and Reporting

- Ensure all data export requests (both CLI and HTTP) are logged and can be reported upon in accordance with the audit logging procedures.

## Security

- Ensure that the OAuth scope for partner authentication is properly defined and enforced.

## Legal

- Ensure all legal requirements for data handling and user consent are met, as outlined in the v1 compliance checklist.
```
