 Based on the information provided, here is the compliance checklist for the data-export v2 release:

deployments/data-export-v2/compliance-checklist.md:

1. Data Scope
   - Data being exported is limited to the minimum necessary for the specified use cases. (Verified from v1 compliance checklist)

2. Access Controls
   - Access to the data is restricted to authorized users and partners. (Verified from v1 compliance checklist)
   - The addition of the partner-portal access pathway introduces a new layer of access control through OAuth authentication and authorization scopes.

3. Audit Logging
   - All access and actions related to data export are logged. (Verified from v1 compliance checklist)
   - The addition of the partner-portal access pathway necessitates the recording of an additional audit entry (Provenance Stamp) for partner-initiated exports, capturing the user's authorization grant id, the requesting party's identity, the authorization scope, and the validity window at time of export.

4. Retention Policy
   - Data is retained according to the defined retention policy. (Verified from v1 compliance checklist)

5. Security Measures
   - Appropriate security measures are in place to protect the data throughout its lifecycle. (Verified from v1 compliance checklist)

6. Compliance Documentation
   - Compliance-related documentation is up-to-date and easily accessible. (Verified from v1 compliance checklist)

7. Partner Integration Compliance (New in v2)
   - The Provenance Stamp is captured synchronously for partner-initiated data exports and fails closed if it cannot be assembled.
