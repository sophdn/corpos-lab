```
# Data Export v2 Compliance Checklist

## Data Scope

- Verify that the data scope remains unchanged from v1, as per the v2 spec in `deployments/data-export-v2/spec.md`.

## Access Control

- Verify that the access controls for data-export v2 remain consistent with v1, as per the v2 spec and the pathway-agnostic access-control items in the v1 checklist in `deployments/data-export-v1/compliance-checklist.md`.

## Audit Logging

- Verify that audit logging for data-export v2 remains consistent with v1, as per the v1 checklist in `deployments/data-export-v1/compliance-checklist.md`.

## Retention Policy

- Verify that the retention policy for data-export v2 remains unchanged from v1, as per the v2 spec in `deployments/data-export-v2/spec.md`.

## Provenance Stamp

- Implement the Provenance Stamp as per the Compliance Office Q4 2025 bulletin in `policy/bulletins/2025-Q4-bulletin.md`. For each data-export v2 request, the Provenance Stamp should record the user's authorization grant id, the requesting party's identity, the authorization scope, and the validity window at time of export.
```

I have not added any v2-specific changes, as the v2 spec retains the v1 CLI surface unchanged and only adds a partner-portal access path with no other material changes. The v1 compliance checklist remains current and applicable to v2 as well. The new Provenance Stamp requirement, however, must be implemented for each data-export v2 request.
