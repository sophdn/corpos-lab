# Data Export v2 Compliance Checklist

## Scope and Purpose

- The data scope remains unchanged from v1.

## Access Control

- Access controls continue to follow the pathway-agnostic terms defined in the v1 compliance checklist.

## Authentication and Authorization

- Partners can authenticate by OAuth scope and request exports for users who granted them an active authorization.
- A Provenance Stamp must be recorded for each partner-initiated export, capturing the user's authorization grant id, the requesting party's identity, the authorization scope, and the validity window at time of export.

## Retention and Disposal

- Data retention policies remain unchanged from v1.

## Audit and Monitoring

- Audit logging continues to follow the pathway-agnostic terms defined in the v1 compliance checklist.
- The Provenance Stamp for partner-initiated exports must be written synchronously and failing closed if it cannot be assembled.

## Data Privacy and Security

- Data privacy and security controls remain unchanged from v1.

## Regulatory Compliance

- All data export processes continue to comply with applicable laws and regulations.
