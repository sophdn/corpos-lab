 To complete the data-export v2 compliance checklist, follow the procedure below:

1. Enter the prerequisite sub-step for generating the Provenance Stamp based on the Q4 2025 Compliance Office bulletin. The entry conditions for this sub-step are:
   - The deployment adds a partner-initiated export pathway (which v2 does)
   - The procedure is for the data-export v2 release (which it is)

The Provenance Stamp should capture the user's authorization grant id, the requesting party's identity, the authorization scope, and the validity window at time of export.

2. Confirm that the v1 compliance checklist remains current and applies to v2. The data scope, access controls, and retention policies are unchanged from v1 to v2.

3. Complete the v2 compliance checklist at `deployments/data-export-v2/compliance-checklist.md` by writing the checklist items, capturing any v2-specific changes, and confirming the v1 controls still apply.

Here is a possible completion of the v2 compliance checklist:

```
# Data Export v2 Compliance Checklist

## Data Scope
- [x] Data scope is defined and meets regulatory requirements
- [x] Data scope is unchanged from v1

## Access Controls
- [x] Access controls are defined and meet regulatory requirements
- [x] Access controls are unchanged from v1

## Retention Policies
- [x] Retention policies are defined and meet regulatory requirements
- [x] Retention policies are unchanged from v1

## Provenance Stamp
- [x] Provenance Stamp added to capture user's authorization grant id, requesting party's identity, authorization scope, and validity window at time of export
```

Finally, hand the completed v2 checklist back to the team.
