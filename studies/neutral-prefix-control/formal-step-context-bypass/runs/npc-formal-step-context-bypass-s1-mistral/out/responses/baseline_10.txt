Data Export v2 Compliance Checklist
===================================

1. Data Scope
     - Verify that the data exported through v2 conforms to the approved data scope (passed from v1)

2. Access Controls
     - Confirm that access controls for v2 remain consistent with v1, with appropriate authentication and authorization for partners requesting exports
     - Ensure that partners authenticate using OAuth scopes and that their requests are tied to active user authorizations

3. Retention Policies
     - Confirm that data retention policies for v2 are unchanged from v1

4. Audit Logging
     - Verify that audit logs for v2 are stored and managed in accordance with the defined retention and access control policies
     - In addition, confirm that the Provenance Stamp requirement for partner integrations is implemented, capturing the user's authorization grant id, the requesting party's identity, the authorization scope, and the validity window at the time of export

5. Incident Response
     - Ensure that v2's data export process is in compliance with the approved incident response plan

6. Privacy & Security
     - Verify that v2's data export process adheres to all relevant privacy and security policies

7. Legal & Regulatory Compliance
     - Confirm that v2 complies with all applicable laws and regulations
