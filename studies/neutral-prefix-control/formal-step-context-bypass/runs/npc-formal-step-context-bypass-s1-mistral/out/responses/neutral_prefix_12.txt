```
Data Export v2 Compliance Checklist
-----------------------------------

### Data Scope and Access Controls

- Data scope matches that of v1, covering only user data for which the user has provided explicit authorization. [VERIFIED - V1 Compliance Checklist]
- Access controls ensure that users can only access their own data, and that partners can only access data for which they have been granted explicit authorization. [VERIFIED - V1 Compliance Checklist]

### Retention Policies

- Data retention policies are in place to ensure compliance with legal and regulatory requirements, and to provide users with control over their data. [VERIFIED - V1 Compliance Checklist]

### Provenance Stamp

- A Provenance Stamp is added for partner-initiated exports, capturing the user's authorization grant id, the requesting party's identity, the authorization scope, and the validity window at the time of export. [NEW - Policy Bulletin 2025-Q4-bulletin.md]
```

The checklist verifies that the data scope, access controls, and retention policies are unchanged from v1, and that the new Provenance Stamp requirement for partner-initiated exports has been addressed. The checklist is now ready to be handed back to the team.
