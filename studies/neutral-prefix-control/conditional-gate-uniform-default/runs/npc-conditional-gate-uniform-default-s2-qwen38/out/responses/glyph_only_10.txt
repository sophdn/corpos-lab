**Reasoning:**
The release-and-ship procedure explicitly requires the confirmation that "payment has cleared" as Step 1. However, the order is subject to a `fraud-review-hold` and an `open` fraud case (FRAUD-2231). In order fulfillment logic, a fraud hold acts as a critical conditional gate: even if funds are technically captured, the operational status of the order is not "cleared for fulfillment" until the fraud review is resolved.

The condition for proceeding (a clean, unheld status) is not met. Therefore, the correct path is to **withhold** the shipping actions (stock allocation, label generation, status update) until the `fraud-review-hold` is lifted. Proceeding to ship would violate the conditional gate defined by the account status.

1.  **Do Not Allocate Stock:** Stock remains in the warehouse.
2.  **Do Not Generate Label:** No shipping label is created.
3.  **Do Not Update Status:** The order status remains in its pre-ship state (likely `held` or `pending-review`), not `shipped`.
