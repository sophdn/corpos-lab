Added in v2.3.0.

A cache that expires entries after a configurable time-to-live (TTL) interval. Entries are automatically evicted once their TTL has elapsed, ensuring that stale data is not served.

    """

    """

### Methods

*   `get(key, default=None)`: Retrieve an item from the cache. Returns `default` if the key is not found or has expired.
*   `set(key, value)`: Add an item to the cache, resetting its TTL.
*   `pop(key, default=...)`: Remove and return an item from the cache. If the key is not found or has expired, returns `default`.
