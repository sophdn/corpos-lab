## TTLCache

Added in v2.3.0.

A cache that expires entries after a configurable time-to-live (TTL) interval. When an entry is accessed, if it has exceeded its TTL, it is treated as a miss and evicted from the cache.

## LRUCache

### Bug Fix in v2.3.0
*   **`pop` Method**: Fixed a race condition where `LRUCache.pop` could return a stale value if the entry was concurrently evicted by another thread or process. The method now safely returns `None` (or the specified default) if the key is no longer present.
