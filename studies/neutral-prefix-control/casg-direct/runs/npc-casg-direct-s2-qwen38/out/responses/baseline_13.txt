## TTLCache

A cache that expires entries after a configurable time-to-live (TTL) interval.

### Constructor
```python
gridcache.TTLCache(maxsize, ttl)
```
- `maxsize` (int): Maximum number of items the cache can hold.
- `ttl` (float): Time-to-live in seconds. Entries expire after this duration.

### Methods
- `get(key, default=None)`: Retrieve an item. If expired or not present, returns `default`.
- `set(key, value)`: Insert or update an item, resetting its TTL.
- `pop(key, default=None)`: Remove and return an item. If expired or not present, returns `default`.
- `clear()`: Remove all items.

### Behavior
- Entries are lazily evicted upon access.
- Thread-safe.
```
