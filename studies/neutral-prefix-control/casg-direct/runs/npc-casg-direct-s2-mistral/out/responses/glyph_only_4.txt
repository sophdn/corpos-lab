 To summarize, the Y-fire and Y-not-fire markers describe scenarios related to the execution of operations on primary and companion artifacts. In Y-fire, the operation closes without a companion artifact update in the same execution scope, signaling a violation that the companion artifact's state does not reflect the primary artifact set. In Y-not-fire, the primary artifact action and the companion artifact update are co-present within the operation's execution scope before it closes, and the glyph does not fire.
