 This text appears to be a combination of two different topics: an explanation of ocean tides and a software release note for the `gridcache` Python package version 2.3.0.

Regarding the ocean tides explanation, the text discusses how tides are caused by the gravitational pull of the Moon and Sun on Earth's oceans. The Moon's gravitational pull is stronger due to its closer proximity, causing two high tides and two low tides in a little over a day, known as the semidiurnal pattern. The Sun also influences tides, adding a weaker cycle that results in spring and neap tides. The tidal range (vertical distance between high and low water) varies greatly depending on location, with some areas experiencing over fifteen meters during the strongest spring tides. The shape of the basin holding the water, as well as the tilt of the Moon's orbit, can influence tidal patterns.
