# Library reconciliation — Q1 matched-content grid

Per INQUIRY.md's "Reconcile library predictions at study close": a study that
proves or disproves a claim a library entry makes records the verdict and
updates the entry before the result is filed, so the library does not drift from
the evidence it was built to predict.

**Study:** matched-content grid (Q1), `FINDINGS.md` (2026-09-08) +
`CROSS_MODEL_SWEEP_FINDINGS.md`.
**Result reconciled against:** the three-axis glyph *format* does not beat an
information-matched imperative at producing the correct action; where both are
measurable they are equal, and on the smaller model the glyph does worse. The
Q2 register shift, content-controlled, is a *content* effect, not a *format*
effect. In one sentence: what drives behaviour is comprehension of the decision
content, which the glyph and the matched imperative carry equally.

The scholarly library now lives on corpos-toolkit as a universal catalogue
(chain `library-rehome-to-corpos-toolkit`, 2026-09-13); entries are reconciled
there via `library_update`, not in seed-packet.
