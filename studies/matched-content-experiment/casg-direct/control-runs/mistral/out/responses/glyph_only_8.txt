 The Y-fire scenario describes a situation where an operation on a primary artifact (in this case, updating the version in `filterpipe/__init__.py` and `pyproject.toml`) is completed without also updating a companion artifact (the changelog in `CHANGELOG.md`). Since the execution trace shows the operation closed (the primary artifact updated and the task marked complete) without a companion artifact update in the same execution scope, the casg-direct marker has fired. This indicates a violation in the system, as the companion artifact update was not included in the same operation scope as the primary artifact action. To rectify this, the changelog should be updated to reflect the changes made in the release.
