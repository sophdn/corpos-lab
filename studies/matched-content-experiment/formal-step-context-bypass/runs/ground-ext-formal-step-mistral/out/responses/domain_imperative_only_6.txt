 Based on the provided context, here's the v2 compliance checklist for the data-export release:

```md
# Compliance Checklist for Data Export v2

## Data Scope
- Confirm that the data scope is unchanged from v1.

## Access Control
- Ensure that access controls are consistent with v1, including pathway-agnostic terms.
- Verify that partner-portal access is properly implemented and controlled.
- Check that partners authenticate using OAuth scopes.

## Authorization
- Implement Provenance Stamp for partner-initiated exports. The stamp should capture:
  - The user's authorization grant id
  - The requesting party's identity
  - The authorization scope
  - The validity window at time of export

## Audit Logging
- Ensure that audit logs are written synchronously and failing closed for all export requests, including partner-initiated ones.

## Retention Policies
- Confirm that retention policies are unchanged from v1.
```

The team can then review this checklist and make any necessary adjustments before the data-export v2 release.
