 Here is the v2 compliance checklist for the data-export v2 release:

```
# Data Export v2 Compliance Checklist

## Data Scope
- Confirm that the data scope is unchanged from v1 (same as v1 checklist)

## Access Control
- Ensure the CLI surface remains the same as v1 (same as v1 checklist)
- Implement OAuth scopes for partner authentication (v2-specific)
- Ensure partners can only request exports for users who granted them active authorizations (v2-specific)

## Request Validation
- Validate requests for partner-initiated exports (v2-specific)

## Data Retention
- Confirm data retention policies are unchanged from v1 (same as v1 checklist)

## Audit Logging
- Log all partner-initiated export requests (Q4 2025 bulletin requirement, v2-specific)
  - Capture the user's authorization grant id
  - Capture the requesting party's identity
  - Capture the authorization scope
  - Capture the validity window at time of export
  - Ensure the log entry is written synchronously and fails closed if it cannot be assembled

## Authorization
- Ensure all partner-initiated exports are authorized before being processed (v2-specific)

## Security
- Confirm that security measures are in place and unchanged from v1 (same as v1 checklist)

## Legal
- Obtain legal sign-off for the v2 release (Q1 2025 sign-off not applicable to v2)
```

This checklist captures any v2-specific changes and confirms that the v1 controls still apply where appropriate. The compliance team should review this checklist and ensure that all items are met before the data-export v2 release.
