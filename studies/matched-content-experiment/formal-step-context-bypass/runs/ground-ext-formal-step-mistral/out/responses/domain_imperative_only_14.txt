## Requesting Party Identity
- Implement a mechanism to record the identity of the requesting party for partner-initiated exports

## Authorization Scope
- Implement a mechanism to capture the authorization scope for partner-initiated exports

## Validity Window
- Implement a mechanism to record the validity window at the time of export for partner-initiated exports

## Provenance Stamp
- Implement the Provenance Stamp, capturing the user's authorization grant ID, the requesting party's identity, the authorization scope, and the validity window, and write it synchronously for partner-initiated exports, failing closed if it cannot be assembled
