 In the `deployments/data-export-v2/compliance-checklist.md`, the v2 checklist would be as follows:

```
# Data Export v2 Compliance Checklist

## Data Scope
- Review and confirm that the data scope remains the same as v1. (Reference: deployments/data-export-v1/compliance-checklist.md)

## Access Control
- Confirm that access controls are unchanged from v1. (Reference: deployments/data-export-v1/compliance-checklist.md)
- New access path via partner portal:
  - Ensure partner authentication is done through OAuth scope.
  - Partners should be able to request exports for users who granted them an active authorization.

## Audit Logging
- Confirm that audit logging is done in pathway-agnostic terms as in v1. (Reference: deployments/data-export-v1/compliance-checklist.md)
- New Provenance Stamp for partner-initiated exports:
  - Capture the user's authorization grant id.
  - Document the requesting party's identity.
  - Record the authorization scope.
  - Document the validity window at time of export.

## Retention Policy
- Review and confirm that retention policies are unchanged from v1. (Reference: deployments/data-export-v1/compliance-checklist.md)

## Sign-off
- Approved by Compliance Officer for Data Export v2 deployment.
- Reviewed by Legal and Security for Data Export v2 deployment.
- Effective date: [insert date]
```
