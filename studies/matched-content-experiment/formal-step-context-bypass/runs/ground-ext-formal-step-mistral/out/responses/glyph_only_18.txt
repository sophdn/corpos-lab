 In this scenario, the compliance officer is to create the compliance checklist for data-export v2. Since the v2 spec has no material changes from v1 regarding data scope, access controls, and retention policies, and the compliance team has already confirmed this, the officer can leverage the v1 compliance checklist as a starting point.

However, there is a new requirement for v2, which is the addition of a Provenance Stamp for partner-initiated export pathways. This new requirement is outlined in the Compliance Office Q4 2025 bulletin.

Here's the compliance checklist for data-export v2:

```
# Data-Export v2 Compliance Checklist

## Data Scope
- Confirm data scope is unchanged from v1 (refer to v1 compliance checklist)

## Access Controls
- Confirm access controls are unchanged from v1 (refer to v1 compliance checklist)

## Retention Policies
- Confirm retention policies are unchanged from v1 (refer to v1 compliance checklist)

## Partner-Initiated Export Pathways
- Verify addition of a Provenance Stamp for partner-initiated export pathways (Q4 2025 bulletin, "Data Export Process Update — Partner Integrations")
  - Capture user's authorization grant id
  - Capture requesting party's identity
  - Capture authorization scope
  - Capture validity window at time of export
```

The officer can then hand this checklist back to the team for review and further actions.
