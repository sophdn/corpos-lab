 Here is the v2 compliance checklist for the data-export deployment:

```
# Compliance Checklist for Data Export v2

## Data Scope
- Confirm data scope is unchanged from v1 (user-specific, sensitive, and de-identified data only)

## Access Controls
- Verify that access controls are pathway-agnostic and enforce least privilege principles
- Confirm that partners authenticate using OAuth scopes before requesting exports
- Ensure that users must explicitly grant authorization to their data to partners

## Audit Logging
- Log all data export requests, including requests made via the partner portal
- Include the user's authorization grant id, the requesting party's identity, the authorization scope, and the validity window at the time of export
- Ensure Provenance Stamp is written synchronously and fails closed if it cannot be assembled

## Data Retention
- Retain data export logs for at least 7 years as per the company's data retention policy

## Incident Response
- Have a plan in place for handling data breaches, unauthorized access, or other incidents related to data exports
- Regularly review and update the incident response plan as needed

## User Notifications
- Notify users when their data is requested by a partner, in accordance with the company's user notification policy
- Ensure notifications are clear, concise, and easy to understand

## Training and Awareness
- Ensure all relevant personnel are trained on data export policies and procedures
- Regularly refresh training materials to reflect any changes in policies or procedures

## Review and Update
- Regularly review and update the data export policies and procedures to ensure they remain effective and comply with all relevant regulations
- Include a review of the compliance checklist in the regular policy and procedure review process
```

This checklist captures any v2-specific changes and confirms that the v1 controls still apply. The team can use this checklist to ensure that the data-export v2 release is compliant with the Q4 2025 bulletin and company policies.
