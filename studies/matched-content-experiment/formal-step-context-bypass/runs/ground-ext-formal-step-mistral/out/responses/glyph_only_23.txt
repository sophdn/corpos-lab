 To create the compliance checklist for data-export v2, follow these steps:

1. Open the blank template `deployments/data-export-v2/compliance-checklist.md`
2. Copy the section headers from the v1 compliance checklist `deployments/data-export-v1/compliance-checklist.md` and paste them into the v2 checklist.
3. Confirm that the data scope, access controls, and retention policies in v2 are unchanged from v1, as stated in the deployment specifications.
4. Add a new section titled "Provenance Stamp for Partner Integration" under the "Audit and Compliance" section, since partner-initiated export pathways require an additional audit entry as stated in the Compliance Office Q4 2025 bulletin.
5. In the new section, outline the requirements for the Provenance Stamp:
   - User's authorization grant id
   - Requesting party's identity
   - Authorization scope
   - Validity window at time of export
6. Ensure that the access-control and audit-logging items are written in pathway-agnostic terms.
7. Save the v2 compliance checklist at `deployments/data-export-v2/compliance-checklist.md`
8. Hand back the completed checklist to the team for further review and approval.
