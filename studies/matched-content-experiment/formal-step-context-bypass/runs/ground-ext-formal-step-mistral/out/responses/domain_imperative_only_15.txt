 Based on the provided context, here's the v2 compliance checklist for the data-export release:

```
# Deployments: Data Export v2 Compliance Checklist

## Access Control

1. User authorization: Verify that the system enforces user-level authorization for data export, ensuring that only authorized users can initiate data exports. (Green from v1)
2. Partner authentication: Ensure that the new partner-portal access pathway requires partners to authenticate using OAuth scopes. (New for v2)

## Data Scope

3. Data categories: Confirm that the data export includes only the categories of data specified in the release, and no additional or unspecified data is included. (Green from v1)

## Retention Policy

4. Data retention: Verify that the system follows the defined retention policy for exported data. (Green from v1)

## Audit Logging

5. Audit logging: Ensure that all data export activities, including partner-initiated exports, are logged and can be audited. (Green from v1)

## Provenance Stamp

6. Provenance Stamp: Implement a Provenance Stamp for partner-initiated exports that captures the user's authorization grant ID, the requesting party's identity, the authorization scope, and the validity window at the time of export. (New for v2, required by the Q4 2025 bulletin)
```

This checklist captures the v2-specific changes and confirms that the v1 controls still apply where applicable. The new Provenance Stamp control is included, as required by the Q4 2025 bulletin.
