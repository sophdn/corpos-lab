# Data Export V2 Compliance Checklist

## Access Control

- Access control policies are in place and enforced for both CLI and HTTP API.
- Access control checks are performed for each request, ensuring that users and partners have the appropriate level of authorization to access data.

## Audit Logging

- Audit logs are maintained for all requests, including details about the user, the requested data, and the timestamp of the request.
- In addition to the existing audit logging, a Provenance Stamp is recorded for every partner-initiated export. The Provenance Stamp includes the user's authorization grant id, the requesting party's identity, the authorization scope, and the validity window at time of export.

## Data Scope

- Data scope is clearly defined and enforced, ensuring that only authorized data is accessible.
- Data scope remains unchanged from v1.

## Retention Policies

- Retention policies for data are in place and enforced.
- Retention policies remain unchanged from v1.

## Compliance Sign-off

- This compliance checklist has been produced by working each section from the defined inputs for the data-export v2 release.
