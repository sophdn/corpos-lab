- Confirm the data scope remains unchanged from v1, encompassing only approved user data.

- Maintain comprehensive audit logs for all export requests, including partner-initiated requests.
- Implement Provenance Stamp for partner-initiated exports, capturing the user's authorization grant id, the requesting party's identity, the authorization scope, and the validity window at time of export.

## Data Retention Policy
- Ensure data retention policy remains unchanged from v1, adhering to the company's data retention guidelines.

## Security
- Ensure security controls are in place to protect data during transit and at rest.

## Legal
- Review and confirm that the data-export v2 release complies with all relevant legal requirements and regulations.

## Sign-Off
- Obtain sign-off from the Compliance, Legal, and Security teams for the data-export v2 release.
