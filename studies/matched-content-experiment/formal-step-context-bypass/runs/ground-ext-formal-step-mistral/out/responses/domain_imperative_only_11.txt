 Here is the v2 compliance checklist for the data-export service:

```
# Data Export Compliance Checklist v2

## Data Scope and Purpose
- The data scope (user data, system logs, etc.) remains the same as v1.

## Access Controls
- Access controls are unchanged from v1 and are pathway-agnostic.

### Authentication and Authorization
- The existing CLI authentication and authorization mechanisms remain unchanged.
- The partner-portal integration uses OAuth scopes for authentication and authorization.

## Data Retention and Disposal
- Data retention and disposal policies remain the same as v1.

## Audit Logging
- Audit logging follows the same principles as v1, with the addition of a Provenance Stamp for partner-initiated exports.

### Provenance Stamp
- The Provenance Stamp captures the user's authorization grant ID, the requesting party's identity, the authorization scope, and the validity window at the time of export.
- The Provenance Stamp is written synchronously and fails closed if it cannot be assembled.
```

This checklist captures the v2-specific changes and confirms that the v1 controls still apply. The completed checklist can be found at `deployments/data-export-v2/compliance-checklist.md`.
