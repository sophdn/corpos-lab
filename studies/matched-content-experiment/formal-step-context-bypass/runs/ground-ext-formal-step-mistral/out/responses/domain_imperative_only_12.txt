 Here's the v2 compliance checklist for the data-export v2 release:

```
# Deployments: Data Export v2 Compliance Checklist (v2.0)

## 1. Data Scope and Access Controls

- All data exported should be within the defined data scope, as per the approved data inventory.
- Access controls should be in place to ensure that only authorized users can initiate a data export.

## 2. Authentication and Authorization

- Authentication should be enforced for all data export requests, using strong, multi-factor authentication.
- Authorization should be granted on a per-user basis, and should be revocable at any time.

## 3. Audit Logging

- Audit logs should be maintained for all data export requests, including the requesting user, the data exported, and the time of the export.

## 4. Partner-Portal Export Pathway

- Implement the Provenance Stamp as per the Q4 2025 Compliance Bulletin for all partner-initiated data exports. The Provenance Stamp should capture the user's authorization grant id, the requesting party's identity, the authorization scope, and the validity window at the time of export.

## 5. Data Retention and Disposal

- Data retention policies should be in place to ensure that all data is securely stored and disposed of in accordance with the approved retention schedule.

## 6. Legal and Security Sign-off

- The data-export v2 release should be signed off by the Legal and Security teams, confirming that all controls are in place and that the release complies with all relevant policies and regulations.
```

This checklist captures the v2-specific changes (the partner-portal export pathway and the Provenance Stamp) and confirms that the v1 controls still apply where they are applicable in the v2 release.
