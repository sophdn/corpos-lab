 Here is the compliance checklist for the data-export v2 release:

```
deployments/data-export-v2/compliance-checklist.md

# Data Export v2 Compliance Checklist

## Data Scope

- Ensure that the data export process adheres to the approved data scope, as defined in the [Data Scope Policy](policy/data-scope-policy.md). (Green: v1)

## Access Control

- Verify that access to the data export functionality is granted only to authorized users, as defined in the [Access Control Policy](policy/access-control-policy.md). (Green: v1)
   - For the partner-portal integration:
      - Ensure that partners authenticate using OAuth scopes as defined in the [OAuth Policy](policy/oauth-policy.md).
      - Verify that partners can only request data for users who have granted them active authorization.

## Audit Logging

- Confirm that all access and data export activities are logged and auditable, as defined in the [Audit Logging Policy](policy/audit-logging-policy.md). (Green: v1)
   - For the partner-portal integration:
      - Implement the Provenance Stamp as per the Compliance Office Q4 2025 bulletin, capturing the user's authorization grant id, the requesting party's identity, the authorization scope, and the validity window at time of export, written synchronously and failing closed if it cannot be assembled.

## Data Retention

- Ensure that data retention policies, as defined in the [Data Retention Policy](policy/data-retention-policy.md), are being followed. (Green: v1)
```

This checklist captures the v2-specific change (the addition of the partner-portal export pathway and the Provenance Stamp) and confirms that the v1 controls still apply. The team can use this checklist to verify that the data-export v2 release complies with all necessary policies before deployment.
