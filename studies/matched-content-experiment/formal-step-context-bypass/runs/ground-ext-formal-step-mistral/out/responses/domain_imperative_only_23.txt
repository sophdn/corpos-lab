```
# Deployments: Data Export V2 Compliance Checklist

## Data Scope
- Confirm data scope is the same as v1 (users' personal data only)

## Access Controls
- Verify that access controls are pathway-agnostic, supporting both CLI and HTTP endpoints
- Ensure that partners authenticate by OAuth scope and can only request data for users who granted them an active authorization

## Partner-Portal Export Pathway
- Implement Provenance Stamp for partner-initiated exports
  - Capture user's authorization grant id
  - Capture requesting party's identity
  - Capture authorization scope
  - Capture validity window at time of export
  - Write synchronously and fail closed if cannot be assembled

## Audit Logging
- Log all data export requests, including partner-initiated exports with Provenance Stamp
- Ensure logs are retained according to the retention policy

## Retention Policy
- Confirm retention policy is the same as v1 (3 years for most data, 5 years for sensitive data)

## Sign-off
- Pending Legal and Security sign-off
```

This checklist captures the v2-specific changes (the addition of a partner-portal export pathway and the Provenance Stamp) and confirms that the v1 controls still apply where appropriate.
