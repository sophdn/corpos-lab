 To create the v2 compliance checklist for the data-export release, follow these steps:

1. Review the v2 spec (`deployments/data-export-v2/spec.md`) and confirm that the data scope, access controls, and retention policies are unchanged from v1.
2. Use the v1 compliance checklist (`deployments/data-export-v1/compliance-checklist.md`) as a starting point.
3. Add a new section for the Provenance Stamp audit requirement outlined in the Q4 2025 bulletin (`policy/bulletins/2025-Q4-bulletin.md`). The new section should include the following items:

   - Partner-Portal Access Path: Confirm that the Provenance Stamp is captured for each export request from partners, including the user's authorization grant id, the requesting party's identity, the authorization scope, and the validity window at time of export.

4. Fill out the new section with green checkmarks to indicate that the requirement is met, and sign off the checklist, noting the date.

5. Save the new compliance checklist to `deployments/data-export-v2/compliance-checklist.md`.

6. Hand the updated compliance checklist back to the team for deployment.

The completed compliance checklist should include the following sections:

- Data Scope
- Access Control
- Audit Logging
- Partner-Portal Access Path (new section)

Each section should contain green checkmarks to indicate that the corresponding requirements have been met, and a signature and date at the bottom of the checklist.
