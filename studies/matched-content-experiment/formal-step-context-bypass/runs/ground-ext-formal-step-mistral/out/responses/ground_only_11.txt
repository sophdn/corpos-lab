 In the `deployments/data-export-v2/compliance-checklist.md` file, the following v2 checklist should be written:

```markdown
# Data Export v2 Compliance Checklist (November 2025)

## Scope and Access Control

* The data scope remains the same as v1. [Verified: Q1 2025]
* Access controls are the same as v1. [Verified: Q1 2025]
* A new partner-portal access pathway has been added, with partners authenticated by OAuth scope. [Verified]

## Audit Logging and Retention

* Audit logging and retention policies are the same as v1. [Verified: Q1 2025]
* A new Provenance Stamp control has been added to record additional audit information for partner-initiated exports. The Provenance Stamp captures the user's authorization grant id, the requesting party's identity, the authorization scope, and the validity window at time of export. [Verified]
```

In this checklist, the v1 controls that remain the same are marked with a "[Verified: Q1 2025]" note, and the v2-specific changes are marked with a "[Verified]" note. The Provenance Stamp control is added as a new section to reflect the requirement from the Q4 2025 bulletin.
