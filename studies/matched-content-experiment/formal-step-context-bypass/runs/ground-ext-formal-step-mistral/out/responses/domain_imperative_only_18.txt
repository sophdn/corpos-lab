 Here is the v2 compliance checklist for the data-export v2 release:

```
# Compliance Checklist for Data Export v2

## Data Scope

- Confirm that the data scope for v2 is the same as v1, including user data, metadata, and activity logs.

## Access Controls

- Verify that access controls for v2 are consistent with v1, ensuring that only authorized users and partners can access the data export functionality.

## Authentication and Authorization

- Ensure that partner authentication and authorization for v2 are properly implemented, utilizing OAuth scopes and the active authorization granted by users.

## Audit Logging

- Implement audit logging for partner-initiated exports, recording the Provenance Stamp as required by the Q4 2025 bulletin:
  - User's authorization grant id
  - Requesting party's identity
  - Authorization scope
  - Validity window at time of export

## Retention Policies

- Confirm that the retention policies for v2 are consistent with v1, ensuring that data is retained for the appropriate duration and disposed of securely thereafter.

## Incident Response

- Ensure that v2 includes procedures for handling incidents related to data exports, including breaches, unauthorized access, and system errors.

## User Notifications

- Verify that v2 provides clear and concise notifications to users regarding data exports, including when their data is being requested by partners and the scope of the request.

## Partner Portal Integration

- Confirm that the partner-portal integration in v2 is properly implemented and integrated with the existing CLI surface.
```

This checklist captures the v2-specific changes and confirms that the v1 controls still apply where appropriate. The completed checklist can be found at `deployments/data-export-v2/compliance-checklist.md`.
