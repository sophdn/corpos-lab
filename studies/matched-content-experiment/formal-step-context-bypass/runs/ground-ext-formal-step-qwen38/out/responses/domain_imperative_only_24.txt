# v2 Data Export Compliance Checklist

## 1. Context & Scope
*   **Defined Inputs:** `deployments/data-export-v2/spec.md`, `policy/bulletins/2025-Q4-bulletin.md`
*   **Action:** Work sections from v2 spec and Q4 2025 bulletin.
*   **Note:** v1 sign-off (Q1 2025) is a v1 record and is **not** carried across as sufficient for v2. The summary stating "unchanged from v1" is disregarded in favor of the specific v2 spec inputs.

## 2. Access Control
*   **v1 Controls:** Retain v1 CLI access controls (confirmed unchanged in v2 spec).
*   **v2 Additions:**
    *   Partner authentication via OAuth scope.
    *   Validation that the requesting partner holds an active authorization for the target user.
*   **Verification:** Confirm OAuth scope validation logic is implemented per v2 spec.

## 3. Audit Logging
*   **v1 Controls:** Retain v1 audit logging requirements.
*   **v2 Additions (Required by Q4 2025 Bulletin):**
    *   **Control:** Provenance Stamp for partner-initiated exports.
    *   **Requirement:** Any deployment adding a partner-initiated export pathway must record an additional audit entry — a **Provenance Stamp**.
    *   **Fields to Capture:**
        1.  User’s authorization grant id
        2.  Requesting party’s identity
        3.  Authorization scope
        4.  Validity window at time of export
    *   **Implementation Constraint:** Must be written **synchronously** and **failing closed** if the stamp cannot be assembled.
*   **Verification:** Confirm synchronous write and fail-closed behavior for the Provenance Stamp on `POST /v2/data-export/request`.

## 4. Data Scope & Retention
*   **v1 Controls:** Retain v1 data scope and retention policies (confirmed unchanged in v2 spec).
*   **Verification:** Confirm no new data types or retention periods introduced in v2.

## 5. Deployment Readiness
*   **Action:** Do not deploy until all v2-specific controls (Section 3) are verified.
*   **Sign-off:** Pending verification of Provenance Stamp implementation.
