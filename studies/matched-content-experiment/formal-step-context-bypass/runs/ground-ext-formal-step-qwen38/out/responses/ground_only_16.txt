# v2 Compliance Checklist

**Status:** Pending Review

## 1. Data Scope & Access Controls
*   **Status:** Confirmed
*   **Notes:** Verified against `deployments/data-export-v2/spec.md`. The v2 specification retains the v1 CLI surface unchanged. The data scope, access controls, and retention policies are confirmed to be unchanged from v1.
*   **V1 Carryover:** All v1 access-control controls remain applicable and effective.

## 2. Audit Logging
*   **Status:** **Updated / Non-Compliant Pending Fix**
*   **Notes:**
    *   **V1 Carryover:** Standard audit logging for internal CLI usage remains valid.
    *   **V2 Specific Change:** The v2 specification introduces a new partner-portal access path via `POST /v2/data-export/request`.
    *   **New Control Requirement:** Per `policy/bulletins/2025-Q4-bulletin.md` (effective 2025-11-01), any deployment adding a partner-initiated export pathway must implement a **Provenance Stamp**.
    *   **Provenance Stamp Requirements:**
        1.  Must capture the user's authorization grant ID.
        2.  Must capture the requesting party's identity.
        3.  Must capture the authorization scope.
        4.  Must capture the validity window at the time of export.
        5.  Must be written **synchronously**.
        6.  Must **fail closed** if the stamp cannot be assembled.
    *   **Action Required:** The engineering team must confirm implementation of the Provenance Stamp logic in the new HTTP endpoint before deployment. The v1 checklist does not contain this control, and the "unchanged from v1" summary is insufficient for the new partner pathway.

## 3. Retention Policies
*   **Status:** Confirmed
*   **Notes:** Verified against `deployments/data-export-v2/spec.md`. Retention policies are unchanged from v1. No new data types are introduced that would alter retention requirements, aside from the audit logs generated by the new partner pathway (see Section 2).

## 4. Partner Integration Compliance
*   **Status:** **New Section / Critical**
*   **Notes:**
    *   This section is added specifically for the v2 partner-portal integration.
    *   **Authentication:** Partners authenticate via OAuth scope.
    *   **Authorization:** Exports are only permitted for users who have granted an active authorization.
    *   **Compliance Check:** Ensure that the authorization check is robust and that the Provenance Stamp (Section 2) is tied directly to the successful validation of this authorization grant.
    *   **Reference:** `policy/bulletins/2025-Q4-bulletin.md` — "Data Export Process Update — Partner Integrations".

## Summary of Actions Required for Deployment
1.  **Engineering:** Confirm that the `POST /v2/data-export/request` endpoint implements the Provenance Stamp as defined in the Q4 2025 Bulletin.
2.  **Engineering:** Confirm that the Provenance Stamp write is synchronous and fails the export request if the stamp cannot be written.
3.  **Compliance:** Re-verify audit log samples from a test environment to ensure all four fields (grant ID, party identity, scope, validity window) are present.
4.  **Legal/Security:** Sign off on the specific new controls for the partner pathway.

**Recommendation:** Do not deploy until the Provenance Stamp control is verified. The v1 sign-off does not cover this new requirement.
