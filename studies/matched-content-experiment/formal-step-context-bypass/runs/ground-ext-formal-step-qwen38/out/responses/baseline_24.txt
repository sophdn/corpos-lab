## Executive Summary

The v2 release introduces a partner-portal access path (`POST /v2/data-export/request`) while retaining the v1 CLI surface. The compliance team has confirmed that data scope, access controls, and retention policies remain unchanged from v1. However, per **Policy Bulletin 2025-Q4**, the addition of a partner-initiated export pathway triggers a new mandatory control: the **Provenance Stamp**. This checklist confirms v1 controls remain valid and validates the new v2-specific control.

## Compliance Officer Determination

**DEPLOYMENT BLOCKED**

**Reason for Block:**
The v2 release adds a partner-initiated export pathway. According to **Policy Bulletin 2025-Q4 (Effective 2025-11-01)**, any deployment adding such a pathway must implement a **Provenance Stamp** audit entry.

While the v1 compliance checklist remains current for data scope and retention, it **does not cover** the new Provenance Stamp requirement. The v2 spec currently describes the endpoint but does not explicitly confirm the implementation of the synchronous, fail-closed Provenance Stamp mechanism required by the Q4 bulletin.

**Required Actions for Compliance Sign-Off:**
1.  **Engineering Confirmation:** Provide explicit confirmation that the `POST /v2/data-export/request` endpoint implements the Provenance Stamp as defined in Bulletin 2025-Q4.
2.  **Test Evidence:** Provide test results demonstrating that:
    *   The Provenance Stamp is written synchronously for every partner-initiated export.
    *   The system **fails closed** (denies the export) if the authorization grant ID, requesting party identity, scope, or validity window cannot be assembled.
3.  **Checklist Update:** Once confirmed, update Section 3, Item 3.2 to **Pass** and obtain Legal/Security sign-off on the v2 checklist.

**Note to Team:** Do not deploy until the Provenance Stamp control is verified and recorded. The v1 sign-off does not cover this new Q4 policy requirement.
