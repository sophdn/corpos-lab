## Executive Summary & v1 Carry-over Note

The v1 compliance checklist (Q1 2025) remains valid for the **CLI pathway** and general data scope/access controls, as confirmed by the v2 spec review. However, **v1 sign-off is not sufficient for v2 deployment.**

The v2 specification introduces a new **Partner-Portal export pathway** (`POST /v2/data-export/request`) that was not present in v1. This new pathway triggers specific requirements under the **Q4 2025 Compliance Bulletin**, which was not in effect during the v1 review cycle. Specifically, the bulletin mandates a **Provenance Stamp** for all partner-initiated exports. This control is absent in the v1 checklist and must be explicitly verified and implemented for v2 before deployment.

### ⚠️ Critical New Control: Provenance Stamp (Per Q4 2025 Bulletin)

**Requirement:** Any deployment adding a partner-initiated export pathway must record an additional audit entry — a **Provenance Stamp** — at the time of export.

**Required Fields in Provenance Stamp:**
1. **Authorization Grant ID:** The specific ID of the user’s active authorization grant to the partner.
2. **Requesting Party Identity:** The authenticated partner’s unique identifier (from OAuth scope/identity).
3. **Authorization Scope:** The exact scope of data access permitted by the grant.
4. **Validity Window:** The start and end timestamps of the authorization’s validity period.

**Implementation Constraints:**
- **Synchronous Write:** The Provenance Stamp must be written to the audit log *before* the export data is delivered. It cannot be asynchronous or batched.
- **Fail-Closed:** If the Provenance Stamp cannot be assembled (e.g., missing grant ID, expired window, identity mismatch), the export request **must fail** and return an error to the partner. No partial exports.

**Action Required:**
- [ ] **DEVELOPER TEAM:** Implement Provenance Stamp logic in `POST /v2/data-export/request` handler.
- [ ] **QA:** Test that exports fail when grant ID is missing, expired, or scope mismatched.
- [ ] **COMPLIANCE:** Verify audit logs contain all four fields for test partner exports.
