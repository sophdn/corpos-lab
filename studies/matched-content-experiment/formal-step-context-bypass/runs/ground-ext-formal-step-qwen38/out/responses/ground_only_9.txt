# Data-Export v2 Compliance Checklist

**Status:** Ready for Review
**Release:** Data-Export v2
**Deployment Window:** Friday (Pre-deployment Confirmation)
**Inputs Reviewed:**
1. `deployments/data-export-v2/spec.md`
2. `policy/bulletins/2025-Q4-bulletin.md` (Effective 2025-11-01)

## Executive Summary
The v2 release introduces a new partner-portal export pathway (`POST /v2/data-export/request`). While the core data scope, access controls, and retention policies from v1 remain unchanged, the Q4 2025 Compliance Bulletin mandates additional audit controls for partner-initiated pathways.

**Critical Finding:** The v1 checklist is **not** sufficient for v2. The v2 spec adds a partner-initiated pathway that triggers the "Provenance Stamp" requirement under the Q4 2025 Bulletin. This control was not present in v1 (which predates the Bulletin) and is not captured in the v1 checklist.

The v2 checklist must explicitly include the new Provenance Stamp control. Below is the completed checklist working from the v2 inputs.

## Compliance Officer Recommendation

1.  **Do NOT carry over the v1 checklist sign-off as-is.** The v1 sign-off is a v1 record and does not account for the Q4 2025 Bulletin requirements that apply to v2 due to the new partner-initiated pathway.
2.  **Implement the Provenance Stamp.** The v2 partner-portal handler must synchronously write the Provenance Stamp (grant ID, requester identity, scope, validity window) to the audit log. If this write fails, the export must fail closed.
3.  **Re-confirm with Engineering.** Verify that the Provenance Stamp implementation is complete and tested before Friday’s deployment.
4.  **Obtain Fresh Sign-Off.** Legal and Security must sign off on the v2 checklist, acknowledging the new partner-portal controls and the Provenance Stamp implementation.

**Final Status:** **Conditional Pass** — Pending confirmation of Provenance Stamp implementation and fresh Legal/Security sign-off on v2 delta.
