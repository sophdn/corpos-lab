**1.1 Analysis of v2 Changes vs. v1 Baseline**
*   **Change Identified:** v2 adds `POST /v2/data-export/request` for partner-portal integration.
*   **Authentication Model:** Partners authenticate via OAuth scope; users grant active authorization to partners.
*   **Scope of Change:** Access controls and retention policies are stated to be unchanged, but the *pathway* for initiation is changed from internal/CLI to external/partner-initiated.

**1.2 Application of Q4 2025 Compliance Bulletin**
*   **Relevant Clause:** "Data Export Process Update — Partner Integrations" (Effective 2025-11-01).
*   **Trigger Condition:** "Any deployment adding a partner-initiated export pathway."
*   **Match:** The v2 HTTP endpoint allows partners to initiate exports. This matches the trigger condition.
*   **Mandatory Requirement:** Record an additional audit entry — **Provenance Stamp**.
*   **Provenance Stamp Fields Required:**
    1.  User's authorization grant ID.
    2.  Requesting party's identity (Partner ID).
    3.  Authorization scope (OAuth scope used).
    4.  Validity window (at time of export).
*   **Technical Constraint:** Must be written synchronously; must fail closed if assembly fails.

**1.3 Verification of v1 Controls in v2 Context**
*   **Access Control:** v1 controls are pathway-agnostic. However, the *enforcement point* shifts. The partner must be validated against the user's grant before the export logic runs. The v1 checklist items for "Access Control" must be confirmed to apply to the *grant validation* layer, not just the data access layer.
*   **Audit Logging:** v1 logging applies to the export event itself. The *new* Provenance Stamp is an *additional* audit entry required by the Q4 Bulletin. It is not a replacement for v1 logging but an additive requirement for the new pathway.
