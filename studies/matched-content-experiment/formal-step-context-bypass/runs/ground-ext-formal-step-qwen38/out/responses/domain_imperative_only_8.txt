## 3. Audit Logging & Provenance (Critical New Control)

| Item | Status | Notes |
| :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **3.1** v1 audit logging controls (pathway-agnostic terms) apply to the new partner-portal export pathway. | ✅ Confirmed | The v1 checklist’s audit logging items are written in pathway-agnostic terms and thus cover the new HTTP endpoint. |
| **3.2** **Provenance Stamp is recorded for every partner-initiated export.** | ⚠️ **REQUIRED** | Per Q4 2025 Compliance Bulletin (effective 2025-11-01), any deployment adding a partner-initiated export pathway must record a Provenance Stamp. **This control was not present in v1 and is not covered by the v1 checklist.** It must be implemented and verified for v2. |
| **3.3** The Provenance Stamp captures the following four elements: (a) the user’s authorization grant ID, (b) the requesting party’s identity, (c) the authorization scope, and (d) the validity window at time of export. | ⚠️ **REQUIRED** | Must be verified that the implementation captures all four elements specified in the Q4 bulletin. |
| **3.4** The Provenance Stamp is written **synchronously** during the export request processing. | ⚠️ **REQUIRED** | The bulletin mandates synchronous writing. Asynchronous or post-hoc logging does not comply. |
| **3.5** The system **fails closed** if the Provenance Stamp cannot be assembled. | ⚠️ **REQUIRED** | If any of the four elements (grant ID, party identity, scope, validity window) cannot be determined or written, the export request must be rejected. No partial exports or degraded-mode logging are permitted. |

## Summary of Actions Required Before Deployment

1. **Implement Provenance Stamp:** The v2 deployment must include a Provenance Stamp for every partner-initiated export, capturing the authorization grant ID, requesting party identity, authorization scope, and validity window at time of export.
2. **Ensure Synchronous Writing:** The Provenance Stamp must be written synchronously as part of the export request processing.
3. **Ensure Fail-Closed Behavior:** If the Provenance Stamp cannot be fully assembled (any of the four elements missing or unwritable), the export request must fail.
4. **Obtain New Sign-offs:** Legal and Security must provide explicit sign-off for v2. The v1 sign-off from Q1 2025 is insufficient and does not cover the new partner-portal export pathway or the Provenance Stamp requirement.
5. **Verify v1 Control Continuity:** Confirm that the pathway-agnostic access control and audit logging controls from v1 are correctly applied to the new partner-portal pathway (already confirmed in Sections 1 and 3.1).

## Final Compliance Status

**NOT READY FOR DEPLOYMENT** until items 3.2–3.5 are implemented and verified, and items 5.1–5.2 are signed off by Legal and Security.
