## 4. Audit Logging & Provenance (CRITICAL)

*   **Requirement:** Per `policy/bulletins/2025-Q4-bulletin.md`, Section "Data Export Process Update — Partner Integrations."
*   **Trigger:** Deployment adds a partner-initiated export pathway (`POST /v2/data-export/request`).
*   **Control: Provenance Stamp**
    *   **Status:** ❌ **MISSING / NOT YET IMPLEMENTED**
    *   **Mandatory Fields:**
        1.  User’s authorization grant ID
        2.  Requesting party’s identity
        3.  Authorization scope
        4.  Validity window at time of export
    *   **Implementation Requirement:**
        *   Must be written **synchronously** during the export request processing.
        *   Must **fail closed**: If the Provenance Stamp cannot be assembled, the export request **must be rejected**. No export may proceed without a complete, synchronous Provenance Stamp.
    *   **V1 Gap:** V1 checklist items were pathway-agnostic and did not require this specific synchronous, fail-closed provenance stamp for partner-initiated flows. **V1 controls are NOT sufficient.**
