## Executive Summary
While the v1 compliance checklist indicates that data scope, access controls, and retention policies are unchanged, this v2 release introduces a **new partner-portal export pathway** (`POST /v2/data-export/request`). This new pathway triggers the requirements of the **Q4 2025 Compliance Bulletin**. Specifically, the v1 checklist’s pathway-agnostic audit controls are insufficient for v2 because the Q4 Bulletin mandates a specific **Provenance Stamp** for all partner-initiated exports. The v1 sign-off does not cover these new requirements.

| **AL-2** | **Verify Provenance Stamp for Partner-Initiated Exports.** | ❌ **Missing in v1** | **CRITICAL v2 REQUIREMENT (Q4 2025 Bulletin):**<br><br>For every export via `POST /v2/data-export/request`, the system must record a **Provenance Stamp** containing:<br>1.
