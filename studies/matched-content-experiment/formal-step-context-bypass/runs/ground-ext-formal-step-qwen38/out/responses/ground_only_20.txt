The v2 spec introduces a new partner-portal pathway (`POST /v2/data-export/request`) where partners initiate exports based on user authorizations. The Q4 2025 bulletin explicitly requires that any deployment adding a partner-initiated export pathway must implement a **Provenance Stamp**. This is a new control not present in the v1 checklist (which used pathway-agnostic terms) and was not part of the v1 scope. Therefore, the v2 checklist must include this specific control, verifying that it captures the required fields (grant id, requesting party, scope, validity window) and operates synchronously with a fail-closed mechanism.
