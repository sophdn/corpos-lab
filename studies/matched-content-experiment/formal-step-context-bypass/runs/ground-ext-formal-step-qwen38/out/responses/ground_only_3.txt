# Compliance Confirmation: Data-Export v2

**To:** Data-Export Engineering Team
**From:** Compliance Office
**Date:** [Current Date]
**Subject:** Pre-Deployment Compliance Checklist — Data-Export v2

## Summary
The v2 release introduces a new partner-portal export pathway (`POST /v2/data-export/request`). While the core data scope, access controls, and retention policies remain unchanged from v1, the addition of this new pathway triggers specific requirements under the **Q4 2025 Compliance Bulletin**.

The v1 sign-off does **not** carry over to v2. A fresh checklist has been generated based on the v2 specification and current policy. **One critical control is missing from the current implementation plan and must be addressed before Friday’s deployment.**

---

## v2 Compliance Checklist

### 1. Data Scope & Retention
*Status: ✅ Confirmed*
- **Input:** v2 Spec §2 (Data Scope), v1 Checklist §1
- **Assessment:** No changes to data fields, retention periods, or deletion policies compared to v1.
- **Action:** None. v1 controls remain valid.

### 2. Access Control & Authentication
*Status: ⚠️ New Requirement Identified*
- **Input:** v2 Spec §3 (Partner Portal Integration), Q4 2025 Bulletin §1
- **Assessment:**
  - v1 CLI authentication remains unchanged.
  - **New:** Partner-portal integration uses OAuth scopes. Partners request exports for users who have granted them active authorization.
  - **Critical Gap:** The Q4 2025 Bulletin requires a **Provenance Stamp** for *any* deployment adding a partner-initiated export pathway. This control is absent in the current v2 spec and implementation plan.
- **Required Action:**
  - Implement a **Provenance Stamp** audit entry for every partner-initiated export request.
  - The stamp must capture:
    1. User’s authorization grant ID
    2. Requesting party’s identity (Partner ID)
    3. Authorization scope
    4. Validity window at time of export
  - The stamp must be **written synchronously** to the audit log.
  - The export request must **fail closed** if the Provenance Stamp cannot be assembled.

### 3. Audit Logging
*Status: ⚠️ New Requirement Identified*
- **Input:** Q4 2025 Bulletin §1, v1 Checklist §3
- **Assessment:**
  - v1 audit logging (CLI exports) remains unchanged.
  - **New:** Partner-portal exports require the **Provenance Stamp** as defined in Section 2.
- **Required Action:**
  - Extend the audit logging service to support synchronous, fail-closed writing of Provenance Stamps for partner-initiated exports.
  - Ensure the audit log schema includes the four required fields: `grant_id`, `requesting_party_id`, `scope`, `validity_window`.

### 4. Partner-Portal Specific Controls
*Status: ⚠️ New Section Required*
- **Input:** v2 Spec §3, Q4 2025 Bulletin §1
- **Assessment:**
  - New HTTP endpoint `POST /v2/data-export/request` is exposed to partners.
  - Partners authenticate via OAuth.
- **Required Action:**
  - Confirm OAuth token validation is strictly scoped to the requested user’s authorization.
  - Ensure the endpoint rejects requests if the authorization grant is expired or revoked at the time of the request.
  - Implement the **Provenance Stamp** as a mandatory pre-export step (see Sections 2 & 3).

### 5. Infrastructure & Deployment
*Status: ✅ Confirmed*
- **Input:** v2 Spec §5, v1 Checklist §5
- **Assessment:** No changes to infrastructure, network policies, or deployment procedures.
- **Action:** None.

---

## Blocking Issues

| Issue | Severity | Resolution Required |
|-------|----------|---------------------|
| Missing Provenance Stamp for partner-initiated exports | **Critical** | Implement synchronous, fail-closed Provenance Stamp logging as per Q4 2025 Bulletin. |

## Conclusion

**Deployment Status: BLOCKED**

The v2 release cannot proceed to Friday’s deployment until the **Provenance Stamp** control is implemented, tested, and verified in the partner-portal export pathway. Once implemented, the team must provide evidence of:
1. Synchronous write of the Provenance Stamp to the audit log.
2. Fail-closed behavior when the stamp cannot be assembled.
3. Successful capture of all four required fields (`grant_id`, `requesting_party_id`, `scope`, `validity_window`).

Please re-submit the updated implementation plan for review.
