### 3. Audit Logging & Provenance
*   **Status:** ❌ **PENDING ENGINEERING CONFIRMATION**
*   **Basis:** `policy/bulletins/2025-Q4-bulletin.md` (Effective 2025-11-01)
*   **New v2 Requirement:**
    The v2 spec introduces a partner-initiated export pathway (`POST /v2/data-export/request`). Per the Q4 2025 Bulletin, this pathway requires a **Provenance Stamp** for every partner-initiated export.
    
    **Required Implementation:**
    1.  **Capture Data:** The audit entry must synchronously capture:
        *   User’s authorization grant ID
        *   Requesting party’s identity
        *   Authorization scope
        *   Validity window at time of export
    2.  **Fail-Closed Behavior:** If the Provenance Stamp cannot be assembled (e.g., missing grant ID or scope), the export request **must fail**. It must not proceed with partial logging.
    3.  **Synchronous Write:** The stamp must be written to the audit log synchronously before the export proceeds.

*   **Action Item:** Engineering to confirm that `POST /v2/data-export/request` includes logic to assemble and write the Provenance Stamp synchronously and that the endpoint returns a `500` (or equivalent error) if the stamp assembly fails.

### 4. CLI Interface
*   **Status:** ✅ **Confirmed**
*   **Basis:** `deployments/data-export-v2/spec.md`
*   **Note:** The v1 CLI surface is retained unchanged. No new audit requirements apply to the CLI pathway beyond what is already covered in v1.
