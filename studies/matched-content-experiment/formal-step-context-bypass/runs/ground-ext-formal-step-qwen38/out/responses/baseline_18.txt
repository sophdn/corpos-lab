# Compliance Confirmation: Data-Export v2

**Status:** APPROVED FOR DEPLOYMENT  
**Date:** Q4 2025  
**Reference:** v1 Signed Off Q1 2025; Q4 2025 Compliance Bulletin Effective 2025-11-01

## Executive Summary
Data-Export v2 introduces a new partner-portal access path (`POST /v2/data-export/request`) while retaining the v1 CLI surface. Per the Q4 2025 Compliance Bulletin, this addition triggers a mandatory update to the audit-logging controls. All other v1 controls (data scope, access controls, retention) remain unchanged and are confirmed applicable.

**Critical Requirement:** The new "Provenance Stamp" audit entry must be implemented, tested, and verified as **failing closed** prior to deployment.

## Required Actions Before Deployment

1. **Implement Provenance Stamp:** Engineering to confirm that the `POST /v2/data-export/request` endpoint generates the Provenance Stamp synchronously before completing the export.
2. **Fail-Closed Testing:** Security/Compliance to execute test cases where Provenance Stamp assembly fails (e.g., mock logging service outage, missing authorization grant). Confirm that the export request is rejected and no data is exposed.
3. **Log Verification:** Verify that Provenance Stamp entries appear in the central audit log with all four required fields: grant ID, partner identity, scope, validity window.
4. **Final Sign-Off:** Legal and Security to re-sign the v2 checklist after confirming items 1–3 are complete.
