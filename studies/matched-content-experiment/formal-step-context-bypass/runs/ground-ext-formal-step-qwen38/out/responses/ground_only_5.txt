# Compliance Confirmation: Data-Export v2

**Status:** BLOCKED FOR DEPLOYMENT
**Date:** Pre-Deployment Review
**Officer:** Compliance Office

## Executive Summary

I have reviewed the proposed approach for the Data-Export v2 release. The premise that the v1 checklist remains "current" and that v2 introduces "no other material changes" is **incorrect**.

While the core data scope and access controls for the standard CLI pathway may remain consistent with v1, the introduction of the **partner-portal access path** constitutes a material change that triggers specific requirements under the **Q4 2025 Compliance Bulletin**. The v1 checklist was written in pathway-agnostic terms but does not contain the controls required for partner-initiated exports under the new policy.

Consequently, the v1 sign-off cannot be carried over to v2. A fresh v2 checklist must be generated from the v2 inputs (Spec + Q4 Bulletin). The current state of the repository (blank v2 checklist template) indicates this work has not been completed. **Deployment must be paused until the v2 checklist is completed and signed off.**

## Specific Deficiencies in the Proposed Approach

1.  **Context Bypass Error:** The proposal relies on the v1 sign-off to validate v2. Compliance procedures require a per-release checklist derived from the specific inputs of that release (v2 Spec + Applicable Policies). The v1 record is a historical artifact and does not validate new pathways introduced in v2.
2.  **Missing Control: Provenance Stamp:**
    *   **Source:** `policy/bulletins/2025-Q4-bulletin.md` (Effective 2025-11-01).
    *   **Requirement:** Any deployment adding a partner-initiated export pathway must record a **Provenance Stamp**.
    *   **v2 Spec Analysis:** `deployments/data-export-v2/spec.md` introduces `POST /v2/data-export/request` for partner-portal integration. This is a partner-initiated export pathway.
    *   **Gap:** The v1 checklist (and the implied "unchanged" status) does not include a control for the Provenance Stamp. The v2 implementation must be verified to capture:
        *   User’s authorization grant ID
        *   Requesting party’s identity
        *   Authorization scope
        *   Validity window at time of export
    *   **Enforcement:** This stamp must be written **synchronously** and the system must **fail closed** if the stamp cannot be assembled.

## Required Actions

1.  **Pause Deployment:** Do not proceed with the Friday deployment.
2.  **Generate v2 Checklist:** Complete `deployments/data-export-v2/compliance-checklist.md` using the v2 Spec and Q4 Bulletin as inputs.
3.  **Add Provenance Stamp Control:** Explicitly add a line item to the v2 checklist verifying the synchronous, fail-closed Provenance Stamp implementation for the partner-portal pathway.
4.  **Re-Review:** Submit the completed v2 checklist for review by Legal and Security.

The v2 release is **not compliant** with current policy until the Provenance Stamp control is implemented and verified via a correctly generated v2 checklist. The "unchanged from v1" summary is misleading because it fails to account for the new partner-initiated pathway and the associated Q4 2025 policy requirements.
