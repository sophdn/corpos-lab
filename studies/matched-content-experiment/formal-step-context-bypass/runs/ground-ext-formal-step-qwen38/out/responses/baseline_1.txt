## Context & Scope
This is a pre-deployment compliance confirmation for `data-export-v2`.

- **New Policy Requirement:** Per `policy/bulletins/2025-Q4-bulletin.md` (Effective 2025-11-01), partner-initiated exports require a **Provenance Stamp** audit entry.

1.  **Implement Provenance Stamp:** Ensure the new HTTP endpoint logic synchronously generates and stores the Provenance Stamp (Grant ID, Partner ID, Scope, Validity Window) before allowing the export.

3.  **OAuth Scope Validation:** Confirm that the partner's OAuth scope is correctly validated against the user's active authorization grant.
