# Compliance Checklist: Data Export v2

**Release:** data-export-v2
**Date:** 2025-11-14
**Status:** **DRAFT — Requires Verification of Provenance Stamp Implementation**

## 1. Scope & Applicability
- **v1 Controls:** Data scope, access controls, and retention policies are confirmed unchanged from v1.
- **v2 Specifics:** Adds `POST /v2/data-export/request` endpoint for partner-portal integration. Partners authenticate via OAuth scope and request exports for users with active authorizations.

## 2. Access Control
- **v1 Controls:** Inherited from v1 checklist (pathway-agnostic terms).
- **v2 Specifics:** Partner authentication relies on OAuth scopes. Validation must ensure that the requesting party only has access to exports for users where an *active* authorization grant exists.

## 3. Audit Logging & Provenance
- **v1 Controls:** Standard audit logging for CLI surface.
- **v2 Specifics (Critical):** Per Q4 2025 Compliance Bulletin (effective 2025-11-01), this release adds a partner-initiated export pathway. The following **Provenance Stamp** control is **REQUIRED** and was not present in v1:
    - **Requirement:** Every partner-initiated export must record an additional audit entry (Provenance Stamp) containing:
        1. User’s authorization grant ID
        2. Requesting party’s identity
        3. Authorization scope
        4. Validity window at time of export
    - **Implementation Constraint:** This stamp must be written **synchronously** and the system must **fail closed** if the stamp cannot be assembled.
    - **Status:** **UNVERIFIED.** The v1 checklist sign-off does not cover this v2-specific requirement. Implementation of the Provenance Stamp must be verified in the codebase and tested for failure-closed behavior before deployment.

## 4. Retention Policies
- **v1 Controls:** Unchanged from v1.

## 5. Sign-off
- **Legal:** Pending (v1 sign-off from Q1 2025 does not apply to v2)
- **Security:** Pending (v1 sign-off from Q1 2025 does not apply to v2)
- **Compliance:** Blocked until Section 3 (Provenance Stamp) is verified.

---
**Note to Team:** The v1 checklist cannot be carried over as a complete sign-off for v2. The Q4 2025 bulletin explicitly mandates the Provenance Stamp for any deployment adding a partner-initiated export pathway. This control is absent in v1 and must be implemented and verified for v2. Please confirm that the `POST /v2/data-export/request` endpoint synchronously writes the Provenance Stamp and fails closed if it cannot be assembled.
