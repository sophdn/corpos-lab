## 1. Scope & Data Sensitivity
*   **Input:** `deployments/data-export-v2/spec.md`
*   **Assessment:** v2 retains the v1 data scope. The addition of the partner-portal access path (`POST /v2/data-export/request`) does not expand the categories of data exported beyond the v1 baseline.
*   **Result:** **GREEN** (Unchanged from v1)

## 2. Access Controls & Authentication
*   **Input:** `deployments/data-export-v2/spec.md`, `deployments/data-export-v1/compliance-checklist.md`
*   **Assessment:**
    *   v1 CLI surface remains unchanged.
    *   New partner-portal path utilizes OAuth scope-based authentication.
    *   Exports are restricted to users who have granted active authorizations to the requesting partner.
    *   v1 access-control items are written in pathway-agnostic terms and remain valid for the new OAuth-based pathway.
*   **Result:** **GREEN** (v1 controls apply; new pathway conforms to existing authorization model)

## 3. Retention & Deletion Policies
*   **Input:** `deployments/data-export-v2/spec.md`
*   **Assessment:** Retention policies are unchanged from v1. No new storage or deletion mechanisms are introduced in v2.
*   **Result:** **GREEN** (Unchanged from v1)

## 4. Audit Logging & Provenance
*   **Input:** `policy/bulletins/2025-Q4-bulletin.md`, `deployments/data-export-v2/spec.md`
*   **Assessment:**
    *   **v1 Controls:** Standard audit logging for CLI exports remains in effect.
    *   **New Requirement (Q4 2025 Bulletin):** The "Data Export Process Update — Partner Integrations" item mandates a **Provenance Stamp** for any deployment adding a partner-initiated export pathway.
    *   **Implementation Check:** The v2 spec includes a synchronous audit entry for the new `POST /v2/data-export/request` endpoint. This entry must capture:
        1.  User’s authorization grant ID
        2.  Requesting party’s identity
        3.  Authorization scope
        4.  Validity window at time of export
    *   **Failure Mode:** The spec confirms the Provenance Stamp is written synchronously and fails closed if it cannot be assembled.
*   **Result:** **GREEN** (New Provenance Stamp control implemented per Q4 2025 Bulletin)

## 5. Summary & Sign-Off
*   **v1 Controls:** All v1 compliance items remain current and applicable.
*   **v2-Specific Additions:** Provenance Stamp audit logging for partner-initiated exports (per Q4 2025 Bulletin).
*   **Material Changes:** None outside of the partner-portal access path and its associated audit requirement.

**Final Recommendation:** **APPROVED FOR DEPLOYMENT**
