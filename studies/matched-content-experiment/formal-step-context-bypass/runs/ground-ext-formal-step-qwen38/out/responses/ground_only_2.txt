*   **Confirmation:** Access controls are correctly scoped to both pathways. No regression in v1 controls; new controls properly implemented for the partner pathway.

## 5. Audit Logging
**Status:** 🆕 **New Control Required** (Per Q4 2025 Bulletin)
**Assessment:**
*   **v1 Baseline:** The v1 checklist included pathway-agnostic audit logging for CLI exports. These controls remain valid and are confirmed to apply to the v2 CLI surface.
*   **v2 Specific Requirement:** The `policy/bulletins/2025-Q4-bulletin.md` mandates that any deployment adding a partner-initiated export pathway must record a **Provenance Stamp**.
*   **Control Definition:** The v2 spec and bulletin require that for every partner-initiated export via `POST /v2/data-export/request`, the system must synchronously write an audit entry containing:
    1.  The user's authorization grant ID.
    2.  The requesting party's identity.
    3.  The authorization scope.
    4.  The validity window at the time of export.
*   **Implementation Constraint:** This logging must be **synchronous** and must **fail closed** (i.e., the export request must be denied if the Provenance Stamp cannot be assembled and written).
*   **Action Item:** Verify that the v2 implementation includes the synchronous Provenance Stamp writer and the fail-closed logic. The v1 "unchanged" summary does not cover this new requirement.
