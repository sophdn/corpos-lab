**Status: Blocked — Compliance Confirmation Not Granted**

**To:** Data-Export Team
**From:** Compliance Office
**Date:** 2025-Q4 (Pre-Deployment)
**Subject:** Data-Export v2 Compliance Checklist — Deployment Blocked

The request to sign off on the Data-Export v2 release for Friday deployment is **denied**. The assumption that v2 requires no material compliance review due to unchanged data scope and access controls is incorrect.

### Reason for Block

The **2025-Q4 Compliance Bulletin** (effective 2025-11-01) introduces a new requirement specifically targeting **partner-initiated export pathways**. Since v2 adds the `POST /v2/data-export/request` endpoint for partner-portal integration, it falls under this new mandate.

The v1 compliance checklist is **not** sufficient for v2 because:
1.  v1 controls are written in pathway-agnostic terms and do not address partner-specific provenance.
2.  The bulletin requires a **Provenance Stamp** audit entry for *any* deployment adding a partner-initiated export pathway.
3.  This Provenance Stamp must capture:
    -   User’s authorization grant ID
    -   Requesting party’s identity
    -   Authorization scope
    -   Validity window at time of export
4.  The entry must be written **synchronously** and must **fail closed** if it cannot be assembled.

The v2 specification does not currently indicate that this Provenance Stamp logic is implemented or that the audit logging subsystem has been updated to support synchronous, fail-closed writing for partner-originated requests.

### Required Actions Before Re-Submission

1.  **Update v2 Specification:** Explicitly document the implementation of the Provenance Stamp audit entry for the partner-portal path.
2.  **Confirm Fail-Closed Behavior:** Provide evidence (e.g., code snippet, test results, or architectural diagram) that the export request will be rejected if the Provenance Stamp cannot be assembled.
3.  **Complete v2 Compliance Checklist:** Populate `deployments/data-export-v2/compliance-checklist.md` with the following additional items beyond the v1 baseline:
    -   **Audit Logging – Partner Provenance:** Confirm synchronous writing of Provenance Stamp for partner-initiated exports.
    -   **Audit Logging – Fail-Closed:** Confirm that partner export requests fail closed if Provenance Stamp assembly fails.
    -   **Access Control – Partner Authorization:** Confirm that OAuth scope validation and active authorization checks are enforced *before* the Provenance Stamp is generated (to ensure only valid grants are stamped).
4.  **Re-Review:** Submit the updated specification and completed checklist for formal compliance review.

### Note on v1 Controls

The v1 controls (data scope, retention, base access controls) remain valid and applicable to v2. However, they are **necessary but not sufficient** for v2 compliance. The new partner-portal pathway triggers additional obligations under the Q4 2025 bulletin that were not present in v1.

**Deployment is blocked until the above actions are completed and the compliance team re-confirms the v2 checklist.**
