 To create the compliance checklist for data-export v2, follow these steps:

1. Open `deployments/data-export-v2/compliance-checklist.md` and write a new checklist based on the v1 checklist, which is available at `deployments/data-export-v1/compliance-checklist.md`.

2. In the new checklist, confirm that the data scope, access controls, and retention policies are unchanged from v1, as stated in `deployments/data-export-v2/spec.md`.

3. Since v2 introduces a partner-portal access path, add a new item to the checklist to ensure that the Provenance Stamp is being recorded as per the Q4 2025 Compliance Office bulletin in `policy/bulletins/2025-Q4-bulletin.md`. This should capture the user's authorization grant id, the requesting party's identity, the authorization scope, and the validity window at the time of export.

Here's an example of what the completed compliance checklist might look like:

```
# Data Export v2 Compliance Checklist

## Scope

- Data Scope: Unchanged from v1, as per `deployments/data-export-v2/spec.md`

## Access Controls

- Access Controls: Unchanged from v1, as per `deployments/data-export-v2/spec.md`
- Audit Logging: Unchanged from v1, as per `deployments/data-export-v2/spec.md`
- Provenance Stamp: Confirm that the Provenance Stamp is being recorded for partner-initiated data exports, as per "Data Export Process Update — Partner Integrations" in `policy/bulletins/2025-Q4-bulletin.md`

## Retention Policies

- Data Retention Policies: Unchanged from v1, as per `deployments/data-export-v2/spec.md`
```

Once you've completed the checklist, hand it back to the team.
