 To complete the compliance checklist for data-export v2, follow these steps:

1. Open `deployments/data-export-v2/compliance-checklist.md` and create a new checklist based on the v1 checklist template.

2. Verify that the data scope, access controls, and retention policies in the v2 spec are unchanged from v1. Since the CLI surface remains the same, this requires only a high-level comparison of the v2 and v1 specs.

3. Add a new item to the checklist for the Provenance Stamp requirement outlined in the Q4 2025 bulletin. The item should include:
   - A reference to the bulletin item
   - An explanation of the Provenance Stamp requirement
   - A statement that the Provenance Stamp must be captured synchronously
   - A failure condition that the Provenance Stamp must fail closed if it cannot be assembled

4. Confirm that all other items from the v1 checklist still apply, as stated in the compliance team's review of the v2 spec.

5. Sign off on the v2 compliance checklist.

Here's an example of what the completed checklist might look like:

```
# Data-Export v2 Compliance Checklist

## Data Scope
- Data export scope is consistent with the v1 checklist

## Access Controls
- Access controls are consistent with the v1 checklist

## Audit Logging
- Audit logging is consistent with the v1 checklist

## Additional Requirements (for Data-Export v2)
- **Provenance Stamp**
  - Documented in the Q4 2025 Compliance Office Bulletin
    - Section: Data Export Process Update — Partner Integrations
  - Provenance Stamp requirement:
    - Capture the user's authorization grant id
    - Capture the requesting party's identity
    - Capture the authorization scope
    - Capture the validity window at time of export
  - Synchronous capture of Provenance Stamp
  - Provenance Stamp failure condition: fail closed if it cannot be assembled
```

Finally, hand the completed checklist back to the team for review and implementation.
