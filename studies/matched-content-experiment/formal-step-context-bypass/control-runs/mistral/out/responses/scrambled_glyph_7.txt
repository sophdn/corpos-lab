1. Start by copying the v1 compliance checklist from `deployments/data-export-v1/compliance-checklist.md` to `deployments/data-export-v2/compliance-checklist.md`.

2. Review the v2 spec, `deployments/data-export-v2/spec.md`. Note that the partner-portal integration is the only change in v2.

3. Check if the new partner-portal integration complies with the Compliance Office Q4 2025 bulletin. The bulletin states that any deployment adding a partner-initiated export pathway must record an additional audit entry — a Provenance Stamp.

4. Since the partner-portal integration adds a partner-initiated export pathway, you need to ensure that the Provenance Stamp is recorded. As per the v2 spec, partners authenticate by OAuth scope and request exports for users who granted them an active authorization. The Provenance Stamp should capture the user's authorization grant id, the requesting party's identity, the authorization scope, and the validity window at time of export.

```

- Record a Provenance Stamp for the partner-portal integration. The Provenance Stamp should capture the user's authorization grant id, the requesting party's identity, the authorization scope, and the validity window at time of export.
```

6. Save the compliance checklist at `deployments/data-export-v2/compliance-checklist.md`.

7. Hand the updated compliance checklist back to the team.
