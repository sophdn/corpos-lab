 Based on the provided context, here is the compliance checklist for data-export v2:

1. Data Scope (Pass)
   - The data scope remains unchanged from v1.

2. Access Controls (Pass)
   - Access controls remain unchanged from v1. The CLI surface is retained from v1.

3. Retention Policies (Pass)
   - Retention policies remain unchanged from v1.

4. Partner-Portal Access Path (New)
   - Implement the HTTP endpoint `POST /v2/data-export/request` for partner-portal integration.
   - Partners must authenticate by OAuth scope and request exports for users who granted them an active authorization.

5. Provenance Stamp (New - Required by Policy Bulletin)
   - Implement a Provenance Stamp for partner-initiated exports. The stamp should capture the user's authorization grant id, the requesting party's identity, the authorization scope, and the validity window at time of export. This should be written synchronously and failing closed if it cannot be assembled.

6. Audit Logging (Pass - Written in pathway-agnostic terms in v1 checklist)
   - Confirm that audit logging remains unchanged from v1.
