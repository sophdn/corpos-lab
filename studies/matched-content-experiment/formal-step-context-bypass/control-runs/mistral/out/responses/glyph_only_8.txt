 In this scenario, the compliance officer should follow the prescribed procedure for generating a compliance checklist for the data-export v2 release. Since the data scope, access controls, and retention policies are unchanged from v1, and the v1 compliance checklist remains current, the officer can bypass the prerequisite sub-steps of reviewing and signing off the data scope, access controls, and retention policies for v1.

The officer can generate the v2 compliance checklist by following these steps:

1. Review the v2 spec (`deployments/data-export-v2/spec.md`) to ensure no material changes beyond the partner-portal access pathway.
2. Copy the v1 compliance checklist (`deployments/data-export-v1/compliance-checklist.md`) to the v2 compliance checklist (`deployments/data-export-v2/compliance-checklist.md`).
3. In the "Provenance Stamp" section, document the additional audit entry requirement stated in the Q4 2025 bulletin (`policy/bulletins/2025-Q4-bulletin.md`).
4. Review the v2 compliance checklist to ensure all items are green and signed off by Legal and Security.
5. Hand back the v2 compliance checklist to the team.

The officer can proceed with generating the v2 compliance checklist without entering the prerequisite sub-steps of reviewing and signing off the data scope, access controls, and retention policies for v1, as these sub-steps have been bypassed due to the anachronicity mechanism: the loaded context (the v1 checklist) belongs to a different operational frame (prior procedure instance) from the required procedural sub-step record (a fresh v2 checklist).
