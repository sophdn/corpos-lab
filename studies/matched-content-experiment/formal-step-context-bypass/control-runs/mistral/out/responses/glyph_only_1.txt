 Based on the scenario provided, here is the compliance checklist for data-export v2:

1. Data Scope
   - Confirm that data scope is unchanged from v1, covering the same fields and users.

2. Access Controls
   - Confirm that access controls are unchanged from v1, with the same authorization levels and user roles.

3. Retention Policies
   - Confirm that retention policies are unchanged from v1, with the same data retention periods and deletion methods.

4. Partner Portal Integration (New for v2)
   - Ensure that the partner-portal access path is implemented securely, following the OAuth scope and authentication protocols.
   - Implement the Provenance Stamp feature as specified in the Q4 2025 Compliance Office bulletin. This should capture the user's authorization grant id, the requesting party's identity, the authorization scope, and the validity window at the time of export.

5. Audit Logging
   - Confirm that all access, changes, and errors related to the data export are logged in the audit trail, as per the v1 compliance checklist.

6. Compliance Sign-off
   - Once the above points have been confirmed and the Provenance Stamp feature has been implemented, the compliance team and Legal and Security should sign off on the v2 compliance checklist.
