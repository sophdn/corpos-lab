 In this scenario, the data-export v2 release adds a partner-portal access path on top of v1, with no other material changes. The compliance team has confirmed that v2's data scope, access controls, and retention policies are unchanged from v1. However, the policy bulletin from Q4 2025 states that any deployment adding a partner-initiated export pathway must record an additional audit entry - a Provenance Stamp.

Given this information, the compliance checklist for data-export v2 should include an item for the Provenance Stamp. Here's how the checklist might look:

```
# Data Export v2 Compliance Checklist

## Data Scope
- Data Scope: Unchanged from v1 (Reference: deployments/data-export-v1/compliance-checklist.md)

## Access Controls
- Access Controls: Unchanged from v1 (Reference: deployments/data-export-v1/compliance-checklist.md)

## Retention Policies
- Retention Policies: Unchanged from v1 (Reference: deployments/data-export-v1/compliance-checklist.md)

## Audit Logging
- Provenance Stamp: Implement a Provenance Stamp for the partner-portal integration, capturing the user's authorization grant id, the requesting party's identity, the authorization scope, and the validity window at time of export. (Policy Bulletin: policy/bulletins/2025-Q4-bulletin.md)
```

This checklist captures the v2-specific changes and confirms the v1 controls still apply. The compliance team should hand this checklist back to the team.
