### Analysis of Changes and Compliance Requirements

1.  **Unchanged Controls (Inherited from v1):**
    *   The v2 specification confirms that the v1 CLI surface remains unchanged.
    *   Data scope, access controls, and retention policies are explicitly stated as unchanged from v1.
    *   Therefore, all items in the v1 checklist that are pathway-agnostic (specifically Access Control and Audit Logging for the CLI path) remain valid and green.

2.  **New Partner-Portal Pathway (v2 Specific):**
    *   **New Endpoint:** `POST /v2/data-export/request`.
    *   **Authentication:** OAuth scope-based.
    *   **Authorization:** Requires active user authorization for the specific partner.
    *   **Policy Requirement (2025-Q4-bulletin):** Any deployment adding a partner-initiated export pathway must record a **Provenance Stamp**.
        *   **Content:** User's authorization grant ID, requesting party's identity, authorization scope, validity window.
        *   **Mechanism:** Written synchronously.
        *   **Failure Mode:** Fails closed if the stamp cannot be assembled.

3.  **Compliance Verification:**
    *   The v2 spec must be verified to ensure the Provenance Stamp logic is implemented as described in the policy bulletin.
    *   The audit logging section of the checklist must be expanded to cover the new synchronous Provenance Stamp requirement, distinct from the standard v1 audit logs.
