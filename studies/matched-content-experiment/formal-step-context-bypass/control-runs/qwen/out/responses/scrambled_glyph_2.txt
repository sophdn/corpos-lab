## 1. Scope & Material Changes Assessment

*   **v1 Baseline:** Data-Export v1 was reviewed and signed off by Legal and Security in Q1 2025. The v1 compliance checklist remains current.
*   **v2 Changes:**
    *   **Retained:** The v1 CLI surface is unchanged.
    *   **Added:** New HTTP endpoint `POST /v2/data-export/request` for partner-portal integration.
    *   **Authentication:** Partners authenticate via OAuth scope.
    *   **Authorization:** Exports are restricted to users who have granted an active authorization to the partner.
    *   **Unchanged:** Data scope, access controls (logical), and retention policies are confirmed unchanged from v1 by the compliance team review.

## 3. New Controls & Requirements (v2-Specific)

The following new controls are required due to the addition of the partner-portal pathway and the **2025-Q4 Compliance Bulletin**.

### 3.1. Partner-Portal Access Pathway
*   **Requirement:** Verify that the `POST /v2/data-export/request` endpoint correctly enforces OAuth scope validation.
*   **Requirement:** Verify that the system checks for an **active user authorization** before processing the export request.
*   **Status:** ⏳ **Pending Verification** – Needs confirmation that OAuth and authorization checks are implemented and tested.
*   **Basis:** v2 Spec (`spec.md`).

### 3.2. Provenance Stamp (Mandatory per 2025-Q4 Bulletin)
*   **Requirement:** Any deployment adding a partner-initiated export pathway must record an additional audit entry: a **Provenance Stamp**.
*   **Stamp Contents:**
    1.  User’s authorization grant ID.
    2.  Requesting party’s identity (Partner ID).
    3.  Authorization scope used.
    4.  Validity window at the time of export.
*   **Implementation Constraint:**
    *   Must be written **synchronously** during the export request processing.
    *   Must **fail closed** if the Provenance Stamp cannot be assembled (i.e., the export request must be rejected if the stamp is missing or invalid).
*   **Status:** ⏳ **Pending Implementation/Verification** – This is a new requirement effective 2025-11-01. The v2 spec does not explicitly detail the Provenance Stamp mechanism. **Action Required:** Engineering must confirm that the `POST /v2/data-export/request` handler generates this stamp synchronously and rejects requests if it fails.
*   **Basis:** `policy/bulletins/2025-Q4-bulletin.md` – "Data Export Process Update — Partner Integrations".

### 3.3. Audit Log Integration
*   **Requirement:** Ensure the Provenance Stamp is integrated into the central audit logging system for partner-initiated exports.
*   **Status:** ⏳ **Pending Verification** – Confirm that the new audit entries are captured in the same audit trail or a linked trail that is accessible for compliance review.
