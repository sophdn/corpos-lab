## 3. v2-Specific Compliance Requirements
*New controls required for the partner-portal integration path (`POST /v2/data-export/request`).*

### 3.1. Partner Authentication & Authorization
- [x] **Authentication:** Partners must authenticate via OAuth scope.
- [x] **Authorization:** Exports are restricted to users who have granted an active authorization to the requesting partner.
- [x] **Validation:** System must verify the existence and validity of the user-to-partner authorization grant before processing the export request.

### 3.2. Provenance Stamp (Mandatory per Policy Bulletin 2025-Q4)
*Per `policy/bulletins/2025-Q4-bulletin.md` (Effective 2025-11-01), any deployment adding a partner-initiated export pathway must implement the following:*

- [x] **Audit Entry Requirement:** A distinct "Provenance Stamp" audit entry must be recorded for every partner-initiated export.
- [x] **Required Data Fields:** The Provenance Stamp must capture:
    1. User's authorization grant ID.
    2. Requesting party's identity.
    3. Authorization scope.
    4. Validity window at the time of export.
- [x] **Writing Mechanism:** The stamp must be written **synchronously** with the export process.
- [x] **Failure Mode:** The system must **fail closed** if the Provenance Stamp cannot be assembled or written. No export shall proceed without a successful Provenance Stamp write.
