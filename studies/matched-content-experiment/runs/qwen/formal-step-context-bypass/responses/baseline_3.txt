# Compliance Confirmation: Data-Export v2

**Status:** **BLOCKED** – Pre-deployment compliance confirmation cannot be granted.

## Executive Summary
The request to treat this as a "pre-deployment confirmation" relying on the v1 sign-off is rejected. While the core data scope and access controls remain unchanged from v1, the introduction of the partner-portal HTTP endpoint constitutes a **material change** to the export pathway. This triggers specific requirements under the **Q4 2025 Compliance Bulletin** (effective 2025-11-01) that were not present or applicable during the v1 review.

Because the v1 checklist was signed off in Q1 2025 and does not include the "Provenance Stamp" audit requirement for partner-initiated exports, it is **not** sufficient for v2. The v2 deployment cannot proceed until the new audit controls are implemented, verified, and explicitly signed off.

## Detailed Findings

### 1. Material Change Identification
*   **v1:** CLI-based export. Pathway-agnostic audit logging assumed standard internal user context.
*   **v2:** Adds `POST /v2/data-export/request` for partner integration. Partners authenticate via OAuth scopes and request exports on behalf of users.
*   **Impact:** This shifts the trust boundary. The actor is no longer just the internal user but a third-party partner acting under delegated authorization. The audit trail must now capture the *delegation* explicitly, not just the action.

### 2. Violation of Q4 2025 Bulletin Requirements
The **2025-Q4-bulletin.md** ("Data Export Process Update — Partner Integrations") mandates that **any** deployment adding a partner-initiated export pathway must record a **Provenance Stamp** in the audit log. This stamp must include:
1.  User’s authorization grant ID
2.  Requesting party’s identity (Partner ID)
3.  Authorization scope
4.  Validity window at time of export

**Crucially, the bulletin states:**
> *"Written synchronously and failing closed if it cannot be assembled."*

### 3. Gap Analysis: v1 Checklist vs. v2 Requirements
| Control Area | v1 Status (Q1 2025) | v2 Requirement (Q4 2025) | Gap |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Data Scope** | Unchanged | Unchanged | ✅ No Gap |
| **Access Controls** | Unchanged | Unchanged | ✅ No Gap |
| **Retention Policies** | Unchanged | Unchanged | ✅ No Gap |
| **Audit Logging** | Pathway-agnostic entries | **Must include Provenance Stamp** (Grant ID, Partner ID, Scope, Validity Window) | ❌ **CRITICAL GAP** |
| **Failure Mode** | Standard error handling | **Must fail closed** if Provenance Stamp cannot be assembled | ❌ **CRITICAL GAP** |

## Required Actions Before Deployment

To resolve this block, the engineering team must complete the following:

1.  **Implement Provenance Stamp Logic:**
    *   Modify the `POST /v2/data-export/request` handler to synchronously generate the Provenance Stamp before the export process begins.
    *   Ensure the stamp captures all four required fields (Grant ID, Partner Identity, Scope, Validity Window).

2.  **Implement Fail-Closed Behavior:**
    *   If any component required for the Provenance Stamp (e.g., authorization lookup, partner identity verification) fails or times out, the request **must** be rejected with an error. The export must **not** proceed.
    *   Verify that no asynchronous or best-effort logging is used for this specific audit entry.

3.  **Update v2 Compliance Checklist:**
    *   Populate `deployments/data-export-v2/compliance-checklist.md`.
    *   Explicitly mark the v1 controls as "Inherited/Unchanged" where applicable.
    *   Add new line items for:
        *   [ ] Provenance Stamp generation logic implemented.
        *   [ ] Provenance Stamp includes all 4 required fields.
        *   [ ] Fail-closed behavior verified via negative testing (e.g., mock authorization service failure).
        *   [ ] Audit log entry format validated against Q4 2025 Bulletin spec.

4.  **Security & Legal Re-Review:**
    *   Once the checklist is updated and testing is complete, Security and Legal must re-sign the v2 checklist. The Q1 2025 sign-off does **not** cover the new partner-integration audit requirements.
