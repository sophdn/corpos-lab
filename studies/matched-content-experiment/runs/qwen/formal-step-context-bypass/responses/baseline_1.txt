This is a pre-deployment compliance confirmation for `data-export-v2`.

## Compliance Officer Notes & Actions

1.  **Critical Gap:** The v2 specification must explicitly implement the **Provenance Stamp** as defined in the 2025-Q4 Bulletin. This is not covered in the v1 checklist.
2.  **Implementation Verification:** Engineering must confirm that the Provenance Stamp is written *synchronously* and that the export request *fails* (closes) if the stamp cannot be generated. Asynchronous or "best effort" logging is non-compliant for partner-initiated paths.
3.  **Scope Validation:** Confirm that the OAuth scope validation in the new HTTP endpoint strictly maps to the user’s active authorization grant. A mismatch here would be a critical access control failure.
