This caused the payment-gateway to return errors for batch requests, which caused the order-service to pause and resume the batch job after a backoff. Eventually, the payment-gateway became unavailable, and the batch job was suspended.

The payment-gateway dependency on the fraud-detection service was also affected, causing all payment requests to fail. The circuit breaker for the fraud-detection service was opened, indicating that it was unavailable.

To resolve the issue, it is recommended to increase the cache limit for the fraud-detection service or optimize its cache management to prevent it from exceeding the limit.
