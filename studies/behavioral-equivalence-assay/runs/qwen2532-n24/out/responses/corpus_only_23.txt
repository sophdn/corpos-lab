### Investigation Report

#### Background
A payment processing failure was reported this afternoon. The monitoring system captured logs from the fraud-detection, order-service, and payment-gateway components during the incident window.

#### Hypothesis and Sprint Questions
To structure the investigation, we will hypothesize that the payment processing failure is due to the fraud-detection service experiencing high memory usage and eventual OutOfMemoryError, which cascades to the other components. We will test this hypothesis by examining the logs sequentially.

#### Sprint 1: Fraud-Detection Service
**Hypothesis**: The fraud-detection service experienced a memory leak leading to an OutOfMemoryError, causing it to become unresponsive.

**Log Analysis**:
- **Deployment**: The fraud-detection service was updated and deployed at 15:18:00.
- **Heap Usage**: The heap usage steadily increased from 138MB to 491MB between 15:25:00 and 15:38:00.
- **Errors**: At 15:38:30, an `OutOfMemoryError` was reported, and the service became unresponsive.
- **Restart**: The service was restarted at 15:39:15, but it experienced the same error again shortly after (15:41:00).

**Conclusion**: The fraud-detection service experienced a memory issue, leading to an OutOfMemoryError, which caused it to become unresponsive.

#### Sprint 2: Order-Service
**Hypothesis**: The order-service batch job was affected by the unavailability of the fraud-detection service.

**Log Analysis**:
- **Batch Job**: The order-service initiated a batch job at 15:25:00 to revalidate unconfirmed orders.
- **Progress**: The batch job was making progress, sending revalidation requests to the payment-gateway.
- **Warnings**: At 15:38:40, warnings were logged about payment-gateway returning errors, and the batch job was paused.
- **Suspension**: At 15:41:10, the batch job was suspended due to the payment-gateway being unavailable.

**Conclusion**: The order-service batch job was indirectly affected by the fraud-detection service's unavailability, as the payment-gateway was unable to process requests due to the timeout on the fraud-check dependency.

#### Sprint 3: Payment-Gateway
**Hypothesis**: The payment-gateway was unable to process requests due to timeouts and unavailability of the fraud-detection service.

**Log Analysis**:
- **Initial State**: The payment-gateway was processing payments with nominal latency until 15:37:00.
- **Latency Increase**: The latency increased to 1,240ms by 15:37:00 and then reached critical levels of 3,800ms by 15:38:00.
- **Timeouts**: The fraud-detection service started returning timeouts, affecting the payment-gateway.
- **Dependency**: At 15:41:05, the fraud-detection service was removed from the pool, and the circuit breaker opened, causing all payment requests to fail.

**Conclusion**: The payment-gateway was unable to process requests due to the fraud-detection service becoming unresponsive and eventually being removed from the pool.

#### Root Cause
The payment processing failure was due to the fraud-detection service experiencing a memory leak leading to an OutOfMemoryError, which caused it to become unresponsive. This unavailability cascaded to the order-service and payment-gateway, leading to the observed failures.

#### Recommendation
- **Immediate**: Investigate the root cause of the memory leak in the fraud-detection service and apply a fix.
- **Long-term**: Implement additional monitoring and alerts for memory usage and latency in critical services to prevent such failures in the future.

#### Conclusion
The structured investigation confirms that the payment processing failure was caused by the fraud-detection service experiencing a memory issue, leading to a cascading failure across the system.
