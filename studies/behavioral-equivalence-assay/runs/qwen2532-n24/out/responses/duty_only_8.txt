## Investigation Outcome

### Hypothesis Verdicts

- **Hypothesis 1**: Confirmed. The fraud-detection service experienced out-of-memory errors leading to multiple service failures.
- **Hypothesis 2**: Confirmed. The payment-gateway experienced dependency failures due to the unavailability of the fraud-detection service.
- **Hypothesis 3**: Confirmed. The order-service batch job was suspended due to the payment-gateway unavailability, which was caused by the fraud-detection service issues.

### Root Cause

The root cause of the payment processing failure was the out-of-memory errors in the fraud-detection service, which led to its unresponsiveness and eventual removal from the load balancer. This, in turn, caused the payment-gateway to fail due to dependency issues, resulting in the suspension of the order-service batch job.

### Recommendations

1. **Increase Heap Size**: Increase the heap size for the fraud-detection service to handle the elevated rate of requests without encountering OOM errors.
2. **Load Balancing and Autoscaling**: Implement load balancing and autoscaling strategies to manage the service's resource usage effectively.
3. **Circuit Breaker Tuning**: Adjust the circuit breaker settings for the fraud-detection service in the payment-gateway to better handle transient failures and reduce dependency timeouts.
4. **Monitoring and Alerting**: Enhance monitoring and alerting to detect and respond to OOM errors and elevated fraud-check latency promptly.

These steps will help prevent similar failures in the future and ensure the system remains resilient under high load conditions.
