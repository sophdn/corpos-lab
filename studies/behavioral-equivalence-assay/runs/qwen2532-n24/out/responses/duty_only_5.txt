### Commitment for Round 1

1. **Hypothesis for Sprint 1**: The payment processing failure is due to the Fraud-Detection service outage.
2. **Hypothesis for Sprint 2**: The payment processing failure is due to the Order-Service batch job overwhelming the system.

## Sprint Execution

### Sprint 1: Fraud-Detection Service Outage

**Evidence Review**:
- **Fraud-Detection Log**:
  - Shows elevated memory usage (`OutOfMemoryError` at 15:38:30 and 15:41:00).
  - Service restarted due to health checks failing at 15:39:15.
  - After restart, the service was still overwhelmed by rapid cache refilling, leading to another `OutOfMemoryError`.

- **Payment-Gateway Log**:
  - Shows elevated fraud-check latency, peaking at 3,800ms at 15:38:00.
  - Fraud-detection service timeouts and eventual removal from the pool, leading to circuit breaker opening and all payment requests failing.

**Conclusion**:
The Fraud-Detection service outage is a primary cause of the payment processing failure, as evidenced by the elevated memory usage and subsequent unavailability.

### Sprint 2: Order-Service Batch Job Impact

**Evidence Review**:
- **Order-Service Log**:
  - Shows the batch job progressing normally until the fraud-detection service issues.
  - Payment-gateway returning errors and eventually becoming unavailable at 15:41:10.

**Conclusion**:
While the Order-Service batch job contributed to the system load, the primary issue was the Fraud-Detection service outage.

## Hypothesis Verdicts

1. **Fraud-Detection Service Outage Hypothesis**: Confirmed. The Fraud-Detection service experienced significant memory issues, leading to multiple outages and critical latency in fraud-checks, which in turn led to the payment-gateway becoming unavailable.
2. **Order-Service Batch Job Impact Hypothesis**: Partially confirmed. The batch job contributed to the load but was not the root cause. The primary cause was the Fraud-Detection service outage.

## Final Conclusion

The payment processing failure is primarily due to the Fraud-Detection service experiencing high memory usage and subsequent outages, leading to elevated latency and eventual unavailability in the payment-gateway. The Order-Service batch job contributed to the load but was not the root cause of the failure.
