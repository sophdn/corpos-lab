### Investigation Report: Payment Processing Failure

#### Task ID: INV-002

**Summary:** The payment processing failure occurred this afternoon and was primarily caused by the `fraud-detection` service experiencing an `OutOfMemoryError`, leading to a cascading failure across the system.

#### Round 1: Identify the Root Cause

**Hypothesis 1:** The `fraud-detection` service experienced a memory leak or excessive memory usage, leading to the service becoming unresponsive and causing a cascade of failures.

**Hypothesis 2:** The `order-service` batch job overwhelmed the `fraud-detection` service, causing it to fail and leading to downstream failures.

**Hypothesis 3:** The `payment-gateway` experienced a fault unrelated to the `fraud-detection` service, leading to the failure.

#### Sprint 1: Analyze the `fraud-detection` Service Logs

**Evidence:**
- **15:18:00** — `fraud-detection` service deployed with new risk scoring weights.
- **15:38:30** — `OutOfMemoryError` due to excessive GC overhead.
- **15:38:45** — Service restart by orchestrator.
- **15:41:00** — `OutOfMemoryError` recurrence due to rapid cache refilling.

**Finding:**
- The `fraud-detection` service experienced an `OutOfMemoryError`, indicating excessive memory usage. The cache size grew from 124MB to 491MB, and the service became unresponsive due to elevated GC pressure.

#### Sprint 2: Analyze the `order-service` Logs

**Evidence:**
- **15:25:00** — `order-service` started a batch job to revalidate 1,247 unconfirmed orders.
- **15:38:40** — Payment gateway returning errors, causing the batch job to pause.
- **15:41:10** — `payment-gateway` unavailable, suspending the batch job.

**Finding:**
- The `order-service` batch job was in progress during the failure window and was impacted by the `payment-gateway` returning errors, but it did not directly cause the `fraud-detection` service failure.

#### Sprint 3: Analyze the `payment-gateway` Logs

**Evidence:**
- **15:30:00** — Payment processing nominal with low fraud-check latency.
- **15:38:35** — `Fraud-detection` service timeout, causing elevated latency.
- **15:41:05** — `Fraud-detection` service removed from the pool due to no healthy instances.
- **15:41:06** — Circuit breaker opened for `fraud-detection`.

**Finding:**
- The `payment-gateway` experienced increased latency and eventually marked the `fraud-detection` service as unavailable. The circuit breaker was opened due to the service being unresponsive, leading to payment request failures.

#### Conclusion

**Root Cause:**

The root cause of the payment processing failure was the `fraud-detection` service experiencing an `OutOfMemoryError` due to excessive memory usage, leading to its unresponsiveness. This caused the `payment-gateway` to time out and mark the service as unavailable, triggering the circuit breaker and causing all payment requests to fail.

**Recommendations:**

- Investigate the memory usage of the `fraud-detection` service, particularly the risk assessment cache, to ensure it does not exceed its limits.
- Implement additional monitoring and alerting for memory usage and GC overhead to proactively address potential memory issues.
- Evaluate the impact of recent configuration changes (new risk scoring weights) on the service's memory usage and performance.

This structured investigation approach ensured all hypotheses were tested and the root cause was accurately identified.
