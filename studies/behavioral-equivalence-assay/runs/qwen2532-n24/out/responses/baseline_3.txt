### Payment Processing Failure Investigation Report

A payment processing failure occurred on [date] between 15:38 and 15:41. #### **Root Cause:**
The root cause of the failure was the `fraud-detection` service reaching its memory limit and experiencing an `OutOfMemoryError`, which led to the service becoming unresponsive and being marked permanently unhealthy. This resulted in the `payment-gateway` failing to process payment requests due to the unavailability of the `fraud-detection` service.

#### **Recommendations:**
1. **Increase Memory Allocation:**
   - Increase the memory limit for the `fraud-detection` service to accommodate higher loads, or optimize the service to reduce memory usage.
   
2. **Caching Strategy:**
   - Evaluate and optimize the caching strategy to prevent rapid heap growth during high load periods.
   
3. **Load Balancing and Circuit Breaker Tuning:**
   - Review and tune the load balancing and circuit breaker configurations to handle transient failures more gracefully.
   
4. **Monitoring and Alerts:**
   - Implement more granular monitoring and alerts for heap usage and GC overhead to proactively identify and address potential memory issues.

#### **Conclusion:**
The payment processing failure was primarily due to the `fraud-detection` service reaching a memory limit and becoming unresponsive. Immediate steps should be taken to address memory allocation and caching strategies, and to implement proactive monitoring to prevent future occurrences.
