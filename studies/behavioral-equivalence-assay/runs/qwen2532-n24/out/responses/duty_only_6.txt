### Investigation Report: Payment Processing Failure

#### Round 1: Hypothesis Testing

**Hypothesis 1: Fraud Detection Service Overload**
- **Commitment:** The fraud-detection service experienced an overload due to a high number of entries in the risk assessment cache, leading to out-of-memory errors and service unresponsiveness.
- **Sprint 1:** Review logs from the fraud-detection service.
  - **Observation:** The fraud-detection service showed increasing cache entries and heap usage, reaching critical levels and eventually failing due to out-of-memory errors.
  - **Conclusion:** Confirmed. The service became unresponsive due to excessive cache usage and heap pressure.
- **Sprint 2:** Check for any recent configuration changes that might have exacerbated the issue.
  - **Observation:** A new configuration update (new risk scoring weights) was deployed just before the failure.
  - **Conclusion:** Confirmed. The update may have contributed to the increased load on the cache.

**Hypothesis 2: Batch Processing Interference**
- **Commitment:** The batch reconciliation job running in the order-service might have contributed to the high load on the fraud-detection service.
- **Sprint 1:** Review logs from the order-service.
  - **Observation:** The order-service was in the middle of a batch reconciliation job, sending a large number of revalidation requests to the payment-gateway.
  - **Conclusion:** Confirmed. The batch job was active during the incident and may have increased the load on the fraud-detection service.
- **Sprint 2:** Check if the payment-gateway experienced any issues due to the batch job.
  - **Observation:** The payment-gateway reported elevated fraud-check latency and eventually marked the fraud-detection service as unavailable.
  - **Conclusion:** Confirmed. The high fraud-check latency and eventual service unavailability align with the batch job's activity.

#### Round Summary

- **Hypothesis 1 (Fraud Detection Service Overload):** Confirmed.
- **Hypothesis 2 (Batch Processing Interference):** Confirmed.

#### Root Cause

The root cause of the payment processing failure was the combined effect of the fraud-detection service experiencing an overload due to a high number of entries in the risk assessment cache and a large batch reconciliation job running in the order-service. The new configuration update to the fraud-detection service likely exacerbated the issue. This overload led to out-of-memory errors and service unresponsiveness, ultimately causing the payment-gateway to mark the fraud-detection service as unavailable.

#### Recommendations

1. **Cache Management:** Investigate and implement better cache management strategies to prevent the risk assessment cache from reaching critical levels.
2. **Configuration Review:** Review recent configuration updates to ensure they do not inadvertently increase the load on the service.
3. **Batch Job Optimization:** Optimize batch jobs to avoid overwhelming dependent services during critical times.
4. **Monitoring and Alerts:** Enhance monitoring and alerting to detect cache pressure and service unresponsiveness early, allowing for proactive intervention.

By addressing these areas, the system can better handle high load scenarios and prevent similar failures in the future.
