## Investigation Plan

### Round 1: Initial Hypothesis Testing
#### Sprint 1: Fraud Detection Service
**Commitment:** The fraud detection service experienced a critical issue due to a high volume of risk assessments, leading to an `OutOfMemoryError` and subsequent service unavailability.

#### Sprint 2: Order Service Batch Processing
**Commitment:** The order service attempted to reconcile a large batch of unconfirmed orders, which coincided with the fraud detection service's unavailability, resulting in payment gateway errors.

#### Sprint 3: Payment Gateway Service
**Commitment:** The payment gateway service experienced elevated fraud-check latencies and eventually failed due to the unavailability of the fraud detection service.

### Execution and Findings

#### Sprint 1: Fraud Detection Service
- **Log Analysis:**
  - At 15:38:00, the fraud detection service encountered an `OutOfMemoryError` due to excessive heap usage.
  - The service was restarted at 15:39:15 but faced a recurrence of the `OutOfMemoryError` at 15:41:00.
  - The service was marked as permanently unhealthy and removed from the load balancer.

#### Sprint 2: Order Service Batch Processing
- **Log Analysis:**
  - The order service initiated a reconciliation batch at 15:25:00, attempting to revalidate 1,247 orders.
  - By 15:38:00, 1,005 out of 1,247 orders were processed.
  - The order service encountered payment gateway errors at 15:38:40 and was suspended at 15:41:10 due to the gateway's unavailability.

#### Sprint 3: Payment Gateway Service
- **Log Analysis:**
  - The payment gateway service observed elevated fraud-check latencies starting at 15:37:00.
  - The fraud-check dependency became critical at 15:38:00, with the fraud detection service timing out and then being removed from the pool.
  - The payment gateway service was unable to process requests due to the unavailability of the fraud detection service.

### Conclusion
All three hypothesis sprints have been completed and confirm the following:
- The fraud detection service experienced a critical issue due to high memory usage and was unable to recover.
- The order service batch processing was interrupted due to the unavailability of the fraud detection service.
- The payment gateway service failed due to the unavailability of the fraud detection service, causing payment processing failures.

The root cause of the payment processing failure is the unavailability of the fraud detection service, which was unable to handle the high volume of risk assessments leading to an `OutOfMemoryError`.

**Root Cause:** The fraud detection service experienced a critical issue due to high memory usage, leading to service unavailability and subsequent failures in the order service and payment gateway.
