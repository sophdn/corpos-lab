### Investigation Report: Payment Processing Failure (INV-002)

#### Hypothesis Round 1: Fraud-Detection Service Failure

**Hypothesis**: The payment processing failure is due to the fraud-detection service experiencing an OutOfMemoryError, leading to unavailability.

**Sprint 1: Fraud-Detection Service Logs Analysis**

**Commitment**: Analyze the fraud-detection service logs to identify the cause of the OutOfMemoryError and its impact on the service availability.

**Evidence**:
- The fraud-detection service deployed a new version `v3.8.1` at 15:18:00, which included new risk scoring weights.
- At 15:38:30, the service encountered a `java.lang.OutOfMemoryError: GC overhead limit exceeded`, leading to an unresponsive state and subsequent restart.
- After the restart, the service experienced another `OutOfMemoryError` at 15:41:00, causing it to be marked as permanently unhealthy and removed from the load balancer.

**Conclusion**: The fraud-detection service failed due to an OutOfMemoryError, which caused it to become unresponsive and eventually led to its removal from the load balancer.

**Sprint 2: Order-Service Logs Analysis**

**Commitment**: Analyze the order-service logs to determine if the payment-gateway was impacted by the fraud-detection service failure.

**Evidence**:
- The order-service initiated a batch reconciliation job at 15:25:00, submitting revalidation requests to the payment-gateway at a rate of 50/sec.
- The order-service encountered errors from the payment-gateway at 15:38:40 and again at 15:41:10, indicating that the payment-gateway was unable to process requests due to the unavailability of the fraud-detection service.

**Conclusion**: The order-service was unable to complete its batch job due to the unavailability of the payment-gateway, which in turn was caused by the failure of the fraud-detection service.

**Sprint 3: Payment-Gateway Logs Analysis**

**Commitment**: Analyze the payment-gateway logs to confirm the dependency on the fraud-detection service and the impact of its failure.

**Evidence**:
- The payment-gateway experienced elevated fraud-check latency starting at 15:37:00, reaching critical levels at 15:38:00.
- At 15:38:35, the payment-gateway reported timeouts with the fraud-detection service, and at 15:41:05, the fraud-detection service was removed from the pool due to no healthy instances.
- The payment-gateway encountered a circuit breaker opening for the fraud-detection service at 15:41:06, leading to all payment requests failing.

**Conclusion**: The payment-gateway failed to process requests due to the unavailability of the fraud-detection service, which was marked as unhealthy and removed from the load balancer.

#### Root Cause

The payment processing failure was caused by the fraud-detection service experiencing an OutOfMemoryError, leading to its unresponsiveness and removal from the load balancer. This unavailability cascaded to the payment-gateway and subsequently impacted the order-service, causing the payment processing failure.

### Recommendations

1. **Memory Leak or High Memory Usage**: Investigate the new risk scoring weights deployment in the fraud-detection service to identify potential memory leaks or high memory usage patterns.
2. **Heap Size Configuration**: Review and potentially increase the heap size limit for the fraud-detection service to handle higher loads.
3. **Caching Strategy**: Evaluate the risk assessment cache strategy and consider optimizing it to prevent excessive memory usage.
4. **Monitoring and Alerts**: Enhance monitoring and alerts to detect and mitigate memory issues early, before they cause service unavailability.

By addressing these issues, the risk of similar failures can be reduced, ensuring the stability and reliability of the payment processing system.
