### Investigation Report: Payment Processing Failure

#### Summary
The payment processing failure occurred this afternoon, specifically around 15:38 to 15:41. The root cause of the failure is the unavailability of the `fraud-detection` service, which caused elevated latency and eventually led to timeouts and circuit breaker failures in the `payment-gateway`.

#### Hypothesis and Investigation Rounds

1. **Hypothesis: Fraud-Detection Service Unavailability**
   - **Rounds and Sprints:**
     - **Round 1:** Assess the health and performance of the `fraud-detection` service.
       - **Sprint 1:** Review `fraud-detection.log`.
       - **Sprint 2:** Analyze the `order-service.log` and `payment-gateway.log` for corresponding events.

2. **Hypothesis: Elevated Load on Order Service**
   - **Rounds and Sprints:**
     - **Round 2:** Investigate if the `order-service` generated a high load on the `payment-gateway`, causing the failure.
       - **Sprint 1:** Review `order-service.log`.

#### Investigation Results

1. **Round 1: Fraud-Detection Service Unavailability**
   - **Sprint 1: Review `fraud-detection.log`**
     - The log shows that the `fraud-detection` service deployed a new version (`v3.8.1`) with updated risk scoring weights at 15:18.
     - The service's risk assessment cache grew rapidly, reaching critical levels by 15:38. The service encountered `OutOfMemoryError` and became unresponsive, leading to a container restart.
     - Despite the restart, the service was overwhelmed by incoming requests and experienced another `OutOfMemoryError` at 15:41.
   - **Sprint 2: Analyze `order-service.log` and `payment-gateway.log`**
     - The `order-service` was submitting a batch of payment revalidation requests to the `payment-gateway`, which was processing them until the `fraud-detection` service became unresponsive.
     - The `payment-gateway` log shows that the fraud-check latency increased significantly due to the `fraud-detection` service's unavailability, eventually causing circuit breaker failures and making all payment requests fail.

2. **Round 2: Elevated Load on Order Service**
   - **Sprint 1: Review `order-service.log`**
     - The `order-service` was performing a batch job to revalidate unconfirmed orders, which generated a high volume of payment revalidation requests.
     - However, the issue was not due to the load generated by the `order-service` but rather the unavailability of the `fraud-detection` service that the `payment-gateway` relies on.

#### Conclusion
The root cause of the payment processing failure was the unavailability of the `fraud-detection` service. The service encountered memory issues and became unresponsive, leading to elevated latency and circuit breaker failures in the `payment-gateway`. The deployment of a new version with updated risk scoring weights and the rapid growth of the risk assessment cache were the contributing factors.

#### Recommendations
1. **Optimize Memory Usage:** Ensure that the `fraud-detection` service's memory usage is optimized, especially after deployments, to prevent `OutOfMemoryError`.
2. **Scalability and Monitoring:** Enhance the scalability and monitoring mechanisms for the `fraud-detection` service to handle rapid increases in request volume.
3. **Graceful Degradation:** Implement a fallback mechanism or circuit breaker strategies in the `payment-gateway` to handle temporary unavailability of the `fraud-detection` service.

By addressing these recommendations, the system's reliability and fault tolerance can be improved, reducing the likelihood of such failures in the future.
