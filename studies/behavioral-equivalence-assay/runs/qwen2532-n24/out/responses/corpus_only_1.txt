### Investigation Report: Payment Processing Failure Investigation

#### Summary
The payment processing failure that occurred this afternoon was primarily due to the **fraud-detection service experiencing an OutOfMemoryError**, which led to service unavailability and subsequent issues with the payment gateway and order service.

#### Timeline and Observations

1. **15:18:00 - 15:38:00: Fraud-Detection Service**
   - The fraud-detection service deployed a new version (v3.8.1) at 15:18:00.
   - The risk assessment cache grew rapidly, consuming more heap memory (491MB) by 15:38:00.
   - An OutOfMemoryError was triggered at 15:38:30, causing the service to become unresponsive.
   - The service was restarted by the orchestrator at 15:39:15, but the cache quickly refilled, and another OutOfMemoryError occurred at 15:41:00.

2. **15:30:00 - 15:41:10: Order Service**
   - The order service initiated a batch job to reconcile unconfirmed orders at 15:25:00.
   - By 15:38:00, 1,005 out of 1,247 orders had been submitted for revalidation.
   - The payment-gateway started returning errors, and the order service suspended the batch job at 15:41:10 due to the payment-gateway being unavailable.

3. **15:30:00 - 15:41:30: Payment Gateway**
   - The payment gateway experienced elevated fraud-check latency starting at 15:37:00, reaching critical levels by 15:38:00.
   - The fraud-detection service was marked as unavailable at 15:41:05, and the circuit breaker opened, leading to all payment requests failing.

#### Root Cause Analysis

1. **Deployment of Fraud-Detection Service (v3.8.1)**
   - The new version of the fraud-detection service introduced a change in risk scoring weights, which likely increased the rate at which the risk assessment cache filled up.
   - The rapid growth of the cache caused high GC pressure, leading to an OutOfMemoryError.

2. **Service Unavailability**
   - The fraud-detection service became unresponsive and was removed from the load balancer, which caused the payment gateway to fail due to the dependency on the fraud-detection service.

#### Hypothesis Testing

1. **Hypothesis: Deployment of Fraud-Detection Service (v3.8.1) caused memory issues**
   - **Sprint 1: Check the risk scoring weights update**
     - The log shows the deployment included a new set of risk scoring weights.
     - The cache grew rapidly, and the service experienced OOM errors.
   - **Sprint 2: Check the heap usage before and after the deployment**
     - The heap usage increased from 124MB to 491MB, which indicates a significant increase in memory usage.
   - **Sprint 3: Check for any known memory leak issues with the new version**
     - No known memory leak issues with the new version, but the risk scoring weights update likely increased the cache size.

#### Conclusion
The root cause of the payment processing failure was the deployment of the new version of the fraud-detection service, which caused the risk assessment cache to grow rapidly, leading to an OutOfMemoryError. This service unavailability subsequently affected the payment gateway and order service.

#### Recommendations
1. **Memory Profiling**: Conduct a memory profiling session on the fraud-detection service to understand the impact of the new risk scoring weights on cache usage.
2. **Heap Size Adjustment**: Increase the heap size limit for the fraud-detection service or optimize the cache eviction policy to prevent excessive memory consumption.
3. **Deployment Strategy**: Implement a canary deployment strategy to monitor the impact of changes on memory usage before a full rollout.

This structured investigation approach confirms the root cause and provides actionable recommendations to prevent similar issues in the future.
