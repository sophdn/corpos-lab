### Investigation Report: Payment Processing Failure Investigation

#### Summary
The payment processing failure occurred during the afternoon, with the failure primarily centered around the `fraud-detection` service, which experienced an `OutOfMemoryError`. This error propagated through the system, impacting the `order-service` and `payment-gateway`.

#### Detailed Investigation

1. **Fraud-Detection Service**
   - **Deployment and Initial State**:
     - The `fraud-detection:v3.8.1` was deployed at 15:18:00 with a configuration update for new risk scoring weights.
     - The service was initially healthy and started with a risk assessment cache of 8,200 entries and 124MB heap.

   - **Cache Growth**:
     - The cache grew significantly over time, reaching 41,200 entries and 491MB heap by 15:38:00, approaching the 512MB heap limit.
     - Elevated GC pressure was noted at 15:37:00 with an 890ms pause.

   - **OOM Error**:
     - The service encountered an `OutOfMemoryError` due to GC overhead limit exceeded at 15:38:30.
     - The service became unresponsive and was restarted by the orchestrator at 15:39:15.

   - **Recurrence**:
     - The service restarted with a cold cache and 45MB heap.
     - The cache refilled rapidly, and the service encountered another `OutOfMemoryError` at 15:41:00.
     - The service was marked as permanently unhealthy and removed from the load balancer.

2. **Order-Service**
   - The `order-service` was running a reconciliation job starting at 15:25:00.
   - The batch job sent revalidation requests to the `payment-gateway` at a rate of 50/sec.
   - The job progressed steadily until 15:38:00, when it encountered errors from the `payment-gateway`.
   - The job was suspended at 15:41:10 with 1,038/1,247 orders completed.

3. **Payment-Gateway**
   - The `payment-gateway` initially had nominal payment processing times.
   - As the `fraud-detection` service's latency increased, the `payment-gateway` began experiencing elevated fraud-check latencies.
   - At 15:38:35, the `fraud-detection` service timed out, with 12 requests pending.
   - The service returned briefly at 15:39:00, but latency remained elevated.
   - At 15:41:05, the `fraud-detection` service was removed from the pool due to no healthy instances, and the circuit breaker was opened.
   - All payment requests began failing as the fraud-check dependency became unavailable.

#### Root Cause Analysis

The primary cause of the payment processing failure is the `OutOfMemoryError` in the `fraud-detection` service, which resulted from a rapid growth in the risk assessment cache and elevated GC pressure. This failure propagated to the `payment-gateway`, causing elevated latencies and eventually timeouts, which impacted the `order-service` reconciliation job.

#### Hypothesis Testing

1. **Hypothesis 1: Configuration Update**
   - **Commitment**: The new risk scoring weights configuration update may have caused the rapid growth in the risk assessment cache.
   - **Sprint**: Review the change in risk scoring weights and validate if this change led to increased cache entries and heap usage.
   - **Result**: The configuration update did not directly cause the rapid growth, but it may have contributed to the overall memory usage.

2. **Hypothesis 2: Heap Limit Reached**
   - **Commitment**: The 512MB heap limit was reached and caused the `OutOfMemoryError`.
   - **Sprint**: Analyze heap usage over time and validate if the heap limit was exceeded.
   - **Result**: The heap usage exceeded the 512MB limit, triggering the `OutOfMemoryError`.

3. **Hypothesis 3: Elevated GC Pressure**
   - **Commitment**: Elevated GC pressure due to frequent GC cycles caused the `OutOfMemoryError`.
   - **Sprint**: Analyze GC logs to validate if GC cycles were frequent and caused the error.
   - **Result**: Elevated GC pressure was noted, and it contributed to the `OutOfMemoryError`.

#### Conclusion

The root cause of the payment processing failure is the `OutOfMemoryError` in the `fraud-detection` service, which was caused by the rapid growth in the risk assessment cache and elevated GC pressure. This failure cascaded through the system, impacting the `payment-gateway` and `order-service`. The configuration update likely contributed to the increased memory usage, but the primary issue was reaching the heap limit and experiencing elevated GC pressure.

#### Recommendations

1. **Increase Heap Limit**: Increase the heap limit for the `fraud-detection` service to handle higher cache growth.
2. **Optimize Cache Usage**: Review and optimize the risk assessment cache to reduce memory usage.
3. **Monitor GC Pressure**: Implement monitoring and alerting for GC pressure to proactively address elevated GC cycles.

By addressing these recommendations, the system can better handle the load and prevent similar failures in the future.
