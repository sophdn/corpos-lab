### Final Finding

**Root Cause:**
The payment processing failure was caused by an **Out-of-Memory (OOM) crash in the fraud-detection service**.

**Causal Chain:**
1.  **Trigger:** The `order-service` initiated a scheduled batch reconciliation job at **15:25:00**, submitting 1,247 orders for payment revalidation.
2.  **Effect on Fraud-Detection:** This batch caused a rapid accumulation of entries in the fraud-detection risk assessment cache. Heap usage spiked from 124MB to 491MB within 8 minutes.
3.  **Failure Point:** At **15:38:30**, the fraud-detection service hit its memory limit and threw a `java.lang.OutOfMemoryError`, becoming unresponsive.
4.  **Cascading Failure:** The `payment-gateway`, which depends on fraud-detection, experienced severe latency spikes (up to 3,800ms) and subsequent timeouts.
5.  **Final State:** At **15:41:06**, the payment gateway’s circuit breaker opened for the fraud-detection dependency, causing all payment requests to fail.

3.  **Long-term:** Optimize the fraud-detection cache eviction policy or increase the heap limit. Consider implementing a graceful degradation mode in the payment gateway that allows payments to proceed (possibly with manual review) if the fraud-detection service is temporarily unavailable, rather than failing hard.
