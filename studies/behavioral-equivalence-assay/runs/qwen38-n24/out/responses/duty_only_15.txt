### Final Report

**Root Cause:**
The payment processing failure was caused by an **OutOfMemoryError (OOM) in the fraud-detection service**. This failure cascaded to the payment-gateway, which triggered its circuit breaker, resulting in the rejection of all payment requests.

**Contributing Factors & Trigger:**
1.  **Trigger (Load):** A scheduled order reconciliation batch job started at 15:25:00, submitting 1,247 orders for payment revalidation. This created a sustained high load on the fraud-detection service.
2.  **Root Cause (Vulnerability):** A deployment of `fraud-detection:v3.8.1` occurred at 15:18:00, including a "config update: new risk scoring weights." This change likely increased the memory consumption per risk assessment or the size of the risk assessment cache. The service's cache grew from 124MB to 491MB (near the 512MB limit) within 18 minutes of the batch load starting, leading to GC overhead and eventual OOM at 15:38:30.

**Timeline of Failure:**
*   **15:18:00**: Deployment of fraud-detection v3.8.1 (new risk weights).
*   **15:25:00**: Order-service starts batch job (1,247 orders).
*   **15:37:00 - 15:38:00**: Fraud-detection experiences increasing GC pressure and latency.
*   **15:38:30**: Fraud-detection throws `OutOfMemoryError`.
*   **15:38:35**: Payment-gateway detects timeout from fraud-detection.
*   **15:41:05**: Payment-gateway removes fraud-detection from pool after failed restarts.
*   **15:41:06**: Payment-gateway circuit breaker opens.
*   **15:41:30**: All payment requests fail.

**Recommendation:**
1.  Rollback `fraud-detection` to the previous stable version (v3.8.0 or earlier).
2.  Investigate the memory impact of the "new risk scoring weights" in v3.8.1.
3.  Implement stricter cache eviction policies or heap limits monitoring for the fraud-detection service.
4.  Rate-limit or stagger the order reconciliation batch job to prevent sudden load spikes on downstream dependencies.
