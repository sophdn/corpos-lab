### Alternative Elimination

*   **Alternative A: Network Partition.**
    *   *Elimination:* `payment-gateway` successfully communicated with `fraud-detection` until the OOM crash. The logs show latency increasing due to service degradation, not immediate connectivity loss. The `order-service` was able to send requests until the gateway paused them due to errors, indicating the network path was intact.
*   **Alternative B: `payment-gateway` internal bug.**

### Final Verdict

The root cause of the payment processing failure is an **OutOfMemoryError in the `fraud-detection` service**, triggered by a **scheduled batch job in `order-service`** that generated a high volume of revalidation requests.

**Causal Chain:**
1.  `order-service` initiated a batch job at 15:25:00, sending 1,247 revalidation requests.
2.  This load caused the `fraud-detection` cache to grow rapidly, exceeding the 512MB heap limit.
3.  At 15:38:30, `fraud-detection` crashed with `OutOfMemoryError`.
4.  `payment-gateway` experienced timeouts and elevated latency from `fraud-detection`.
5.  After a failed restart of `fraud-detection`, the circuit breaker in `payment-gateway` opened at 15:41:06, causing all payment requests to fail.

**Recommendation:**
1.  **Immediate:** Restart `fraud-detection` with a larger heap limit or optimized cache eviction policy.
2.  **Short-term:** Throttle the `order-service` batch job to a lower rate limit or stagger execution windows to avoid peak load.
3.  **Long-term:** Implement backpressure mechanisms in `fraud-detection` to reject requests when cache is nearing capacity, or auto-scale the service based on load metrics.
