The concurrent `order-service` batch job acted as a stress test that triggered the OOM error. ### Final Report

**Incident:** Payment Processing Failure (INV-002)
**Time Window:** 15:18:00 – 15:41:30

**Root Cause:**
The deployment of **`fraud-detection:v3.8.1`** at 15:18:00 introduced a defect related to **risk assessment cache management**. The update, which included "new risk scoring weights," caused the service's in-memory cache to grow at an unsustainable rate under load. This led to an **OutOfMemoryError (GC overhead limit exceeded)** in the `fraud-detection` service at 15:38:30.

**Cascading Failure Sequence:**
1.  **15:18:00:** `fraud-detection:v3.8.1` deployed.
2.  **15:25:00:** `order-service` initiates a batch revalidation job, increasing traffic to the payment stack.
3.  **15:20:00 – 15:38:00:** `fraud-detection` cache grows rapidly (from 124MB to 491MB heap) due to the new logic/config. GC pressure increases.
4.  **15:38:30:** `fraud-detection` crashes with OOM.
5.  **15:38:35 – 15:39:00:** `payment-gateway` experiences timeouts and elevated latency (>3,800ms) waiting for `fraud-detection`.
6.  **15:39:15:** `fraud-detection` restarts but immediately begins refilling the cache rapidly due to sustained traffic (including the `order-service` batch).
7.  **15:41:00:** `fraud-detection` crashes a second time.
8.  **15:41:06:** `payment-gateway` trips its circuit breaker for `fraud-detection` as no healthy instances remain.
9.  **15:41:10:** `order-service` suspends its batch job as `payment-gateway` becomes unavailable.
10. **15:41:30:** All payment requests fail due to the open circuit breaker.

**Contributing Factor:**
The `order-service` batch job (1,247 requests) provided a significant traffic surge that accelerated the memory exhaustion in the vulnerable `fraud-detection` service. However, the service would likely have failed under normal load eventually, or at least degraded significantly, due to the cache leak/defect in v3.8.1.

**Recommendation:**
1.  **Rollback:** Immediately rollback `fraud-detection` to the previous stable version (prior to v3.8.1).
2.  **Investigate Cache Logic:** Analyze the code/config changes in v3.8.1 related to "new risk scoring weights" to identify why cache entry growth is unbounded or excessive.
3.  **Add Monitoring/Alerts:** Implement tighter monitoring on `fraud-detection` heap usage and cache size, with alerts triggered well before the 512MB limit is reached.
4.  **Load Testing:** Validate new fraud-detection versions against peak traffic scenarios (including batch jobs) in a staging environment before production deployment.
