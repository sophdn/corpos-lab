### Phase 3: Synthesis and Final Verdict

**Eliminative Coverage Check:**
*   **H1 (Fraud-Detection Crash):** Confirmed as the immediate technical failure point.
*   **H2 (Batch Job Trigger):** Confirmed as the causal trigger for the resource exhaustion.
*   **H3 (Payment-Gateway Independent Failure):** Eliminated. The gateway functioned correctly until the dependency was lost.

**Causal Chain:**
1.  **15:18:00**: `fraud-detection` v3.8.1 deployed with new risk scoring weights (likely increasing memory usage per cache entry).
2.  **15:25:00**: `order-service` initiates a batch job to revalidate 1,247 orders, submitting them at a rate of 50/sec.

6.  **15:41:30**: `payment-gateway` fails all payment requests because the fraud-check dependency is unavailable.

**Final Report:**

The root cause of the payment processing failure is **resource exhaustion in the `fraud-detection` service**, triggered by a **scheduled batch job in `order-service`**.

Specifically:
1.  The deployment of `fraud-detection:v3.8.1` at 15:18:00 introduced configuration changes (new risk scoring weights) that increased the memory footprint of the risk assessment cache.
2.  The `order-service` initiated a batch revalidation of 1,247 orders at 15:25:00.

4.  This resulted in an `OutOfMemoryError` at 15:38:30, making the `fraud-detection` service unavailable.
5.  The `payment-gateway` subsequently opened its circuit breaker for the fraud-detection dependency at 15:41:06, causing all payment requests to fail.

The `payment-gateway` and `order-service` did not suffer from independent internal failures; their issues were downstream consequences of the `fraud-detection` service crash.
