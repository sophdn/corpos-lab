### Phase 3: Consolidated Finding

**Root Cause:**
The payment processing failure was caused by **memory exhaustion (`OutOfMemoryError`) in the `fraud-detection` service**.

**Mechanism of Failure:**
1.  **Trigger:** The `order-service` initiated a scheduled batch job at 15:25, submitting 1,247 revalidation requests to the payment gateway at 50/sec.
2.  **Load Increase:** This batch job caused a significant increase in risk assessment requests, driving the `fraud-detection` service's in-memory risk assessment cache to grow rapidly (from 124MB to 491MB between 15:20 and 15:38).
3.  **Failure Point:** At 15:38:30, the `fraud-detection` service hit its 512MB heap limit, triggering a `java.lang.OutOfMemoryError: GC overhead limit exceeded`.
4.  **Cascade:**
    *   The `fraud-detection` service became unresponsive and was restarted.
    *   The restart did not resolve the issue because the high request rate caused the cache to refill rapidly, leading to a second OOM error at 15:41:00.
    *   The service was marked permanently unhealthy and removed from the load balancer.
    *   The `payment-gateway` detected the timeout/unavailability of `fraud-detection` and opened its circuit breaker at 15:41:06.
    *   With the fraud-check dependency unavailable, the `payment-gateway` could no longer process payments, causing the `order-service` batch job to suspend with 209 orders remaining.

**Contributing Factors:**
*   The `fraud-detection` service's heap size (512MB) was insufficient to handle the peak load generated by the batch reconciliation job.
*   The lack of graceful degradation or caching eviction strategy in `fraud-detection` allowed the heap to reach critical limits before failure.

**Recommendations:**
1.  **Immediate:** Increase the heap size of the `fraud-detection` service or optimize the risk assessment cache eviction policy to prevent OOM under high load.
2.  **Short-term:** Implement rate limiting or backpressure mechanisms in the `order-service` batch job to prevent overwhelming the `fraud-detection` service.
3.  **Long-term:** Review the architecture to ensure `fraud-detection` can scale horizontally or handle cache misses more gracefully without crashing.
