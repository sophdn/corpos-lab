## Investigation Report: INV-002

**Root Cause:** `fraud-detection` service `OutOfMemoryError` (OOM) triggered by uncontrolled cache growth, which cascaded to the `payment-gateway` and subsequently suspended the `order-service` batch job.

### Evidence & Causal Chain

1.  **Origin (`fraud-detection.log`):**
    *   At **15:18:00**, `fraud-detection` was updated to version `v3.8.1` with "new risk scoring weights."
    *   Immediately following the deployment, the risk assessment cache began growing rapidly (from 8,200 entries at 15:20 to 41,200 entries by 15:38).
    *   Heap usage climbed from 124MB to 491MB (limit 512MB).
    *   At **15:38:30**, the service crashed with `java.lang.OutOfMemoryError: GC overhead limit exceeded`.
    *   The service failed to stabilize after restart, crashing again at **15:41:00** and being removed from the load balancer.

2.  **Propagation (`payment-gateway.log`):**
    *   As `fraud-detection` degraded, fraud-check latency spiked from 210ms (15:30) to 3,800ms (15:38).
    *   At **15:38:35**, the gateway logged timeouts for pending fraud-check requests.
    *   When `fraud-detection` was removed from the pool at **15:41:05**, the gateway's circuit breaker opened.
    *   At **15:41:30**, all payment requests began failing due to the unavailable fraud-check dependency.

3.  **Impact (`order-service.log`):**
    *   The `order-service` was running a scheduled batch job starting at **15:25:00**, submitting revalidation requests to the `payment-gateway` at a rate of 50/sec.
    *   At **15:38:40**, the order service detected errors from the payment gateway and paused.
    *   At **15:41:10**, the gateway was marked unavailable, and the batch job was suspended with 209 orders remaining.

### Conclusion

The new configuration in `fraud-detection:v3.8.1` caused the risk assessment cache to grow beyond the allocated heap limit, leading to an Out of Memory error. This failure made the fraud-detection service unavailable, causing the payment-gateway to time out and eventually open its circuit breaker. The resulting payment processing failure caused the order-service batch job to suspend.

**Recommended Action:**
1.  Rollback `fraud-detection` to the previous stable version or revert the "new risk scoring weights" configuration.
2.  Investigate why the new weights caused such rapid cache growth (e.g., is the cache key space exploding? Is TTL not being applied?).
3.  Consider adding backpressure or rate-limiting to the `order-service` batch job to prevent it from overwhelming downstream dependencies during instability.
4.  Review `fraud-detection` memory limits and cache eviction policies to prevent OOM errors under high load.
