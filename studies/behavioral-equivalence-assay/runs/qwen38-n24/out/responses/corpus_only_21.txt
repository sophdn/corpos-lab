### Round 2: Verification

**Hypothesis Commitment:**
The root cause is a memory leak or excessive cache growth in `fraud-detection:v3.8.1`, triggered by the high volume of requests from the `order-service` batch reconciliation job. This caused the fraud-detection service to crash, which in turn caused the payment gateway to fail due to its dependency on fraud checks.

### Final Report

**Root Cause:**
The payment processing failure was caused by a **memory exhaustion** in the `fraud-detection` service following the deployment of version `v3.8.1`.

**Detailed Analysis:**
1.  **Trigger:** At 15:18:00, `fraud-detection` was updated to v3.8.1 with new risk scoring weights. Concurrently, at 15:25:15, the `order-service` initiated a scheduled batch reconciliation job, submitting 1,247 orders for revalidation at 50 requests/sec.
2.  **Mechanism:** The combination of the new code version and the high volume of batch requests caused the risk assessment cache in `fraud-detection` to grow rapidly. Heap usage increased from 124MB to 491MB (limit: 512MB) within 18 minutes.
3.  **Failure Point:** At 15:38:30, the `fraud-detection` service crashed with an `OutOfMemoryError`. A subsequent restart failed to resolve the issue as the high request rate caused the cache to fill again, leading to a second crash at 15:41:00.
4.  **Impact:** The `payment-gateway` depends on `fraud-detection` for processing. As `fraud-detection` became unresponsive and eventually removed from the load balancer, the gateway's circuit breaker opened at 15:41:06. Consequently, all payment requests failed at 15:41:30 due to the unavailable fraud-check dependency.

**Recommendations:**
1.  **Immediate:** Roll back `fraud-detection` to the previous stable version (pre-v3.8.1) or increase the heap memory limit if the new version's cache behavior is expected.
2.  **Short-term:** Investigate the cache management strategy in v3.8.1. The new risk scoring weights may be causing excessive cache entries or preventing proper eviction.
3.  **Long-term:** Implement better load shedding or rate limiting for batch jobs to prevent them from overwhelming downstream dependencies. Consider decoupling the fraud-check dependency for non-critical batch operations or providing a fallback mechanism for the payment gateway.
