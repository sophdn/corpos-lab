2.  **Load Propagation:** `payment-gateway` forwards these requests to `fraud-detection` for risk assessment.

### Final Report

**Root Cause:**
The payment processing failure was caused by **resource exhaustion (OutOfMemoryError)** in the `fraud-detection` service. This was triggered by a **scheduled batch reconciliation job** from `order-service`, which generated a high volume of fraud-check requests, causing the `fraud-detection` risk assessment cache to exceed its 512MB heap limit.

**Contributing Factor:**
A recent deployment of `fraud-detection:v3.8.1` (new risk scoring weights) may have increased the memory footprint per cache entry, lowering the threshold for OOM under load. However, the service remained stable for 10 minutes post-deployment, indicating the batch job was the proximate stressor.

**Impact:**
1.  `fraud-detection` crashed and was removed from the load balancer.
2.  `payment-gateway` tripped its circuit breaker for `fraud-detection`.
3.  All payment requests requiring fraud checks failed.
4.  The `order-service` batch job was suspended with 209 orders remaining.

**Recommendations:**
1.  **Immediate:** Restart `fraud-detection` with a cold cache. Manually resume the suspended batch job in `order-service` at a lower rate limit to avoid re-triggering the OOM.
2.  **Short-term:** Increase the heap limit for `fraud-detection` or implement cache eviction policies to prevent unbounded growth.
3.  **Long-term:** Implement rate limiting or backpressure mechanisms in `payment-gateway` to prevent overwhelming `fraud-detection` during batch processing. Investigate the memory impact of the new risk scoring weights in `fraud-detection:v3.8.1`.
